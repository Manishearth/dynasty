Make it at home

Everything you need to build a playable copy of Dynasty: what to print, what to cut, and what to borrow from other games. Most of it prints from this site; the rest is paper and a few tokens.

Choose a size

Every piece is sized in map squares, so pick the square first. One inch suits most pawns and meeples; a soldier and a piece of realia should both fit on one square.

The board

The board prints in tiles that break on grid lines, so each seam lines up with a printed square. Trim each sheet to its outline, then tape the tiles together from the back, left to right and top to bottom, in the order numbered here. A print shop can also print the full map as one poster; scale it so one square comes out at the size you chose.

Print at 100% (“actual size”), not “fit to page”, with background graphics on.

Pieces to print and cut

Everything below is drawn at real size for the square you chose. Glue the sheets to thin card before cutting, and the tokens will stand up to a whole game. If you use real pawns for the soldiers or real objects for the realia, skip those groups.

Each group fits together on as few sheets as it can.

Borrowing from other games

Soldiers: pawns from any game, in two colours. Risk infantry or Catan settlers are the right size for a one-inch square.

Realia: six kinds of anything, four of each: chess pieces by type, coloured cubes, Lego bricks, beads. Label which is which on a card beside the board, using the icons above.

Wall and Canal: matchsticks or craft sticks for the twelve Wall links, blue pipe cleaner or string for the eight canal sections. The map prints where each one goes.

Grain: the money of the game. Poker chips are easiest. Every number in the rules is a whole number of millions of bags.
