Dynasty (1969)

A recreation of the 1969 board game of imperial China: eight provinces, a population that keeps growing, and an Emperor who has to feed everyone and build the public works before the country stops coping.

Play

  • RulebookThe rules, rewritten as one modern rulebook, with a worked round and every editor's note.
  • Reference cardsPrintable two-sided cards: one for everyone and one per province.
  • I Ching cardsThe twenty event cards, as a table and as print-and-cut cards.
  • The boardThe map, redrawn on a 30 × 22 grid.

Watch a game

  • PlaythroughsFive annotated games on the real board, with every player's reasoning and every move's cost.

Sources

  • Original scansThe 1969 booklets, instructions and cards this recreation is based on.
  • RulingsEvery rules question settled so far, and why.
  • Source codeThe scripts that generate the board, the cards and the playthroughs.
