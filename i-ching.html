I Ching Cards

Dynasty · the twenty event cards, reworded

How they are used

The Scholar shuffles all twenty cards face down at the start of the game. From Round Two on, the Scholar turns one card at the start of every round and reads it aloud, and everyone does what it says. The deck is not reshuffled. When a card calls for a Personal Disaster, each named player plays the next disaster from their own booklet, in order. Province 8 is the Warlord.

The cards' effects follow the original deck. The titles and the italic lines are new and are there for flavour only. Fifteen different cards make up the deck; some come in several copies.
