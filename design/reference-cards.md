# Reference cards

`reference-cards.html` holds the printable table aids: one **general card** for every player, one **province card** per role, and a **Running the round** card for whoever runs the round. Each card is two-sided and half a letter sheet. The page is generated by `tools/build_reference.py`, so never edit the HTML by hand; change the script and rebuild:

```sh
python3 tools/build_reference.py   # writes reference-cards.html, then runs add_glyphs on it
```

The page stands alone, like `i-ching.html`. Manish plans to link to it from the rulebook and elsewhere later.

## What Manish asked for

- **Two sets of cards**: one general card that everyone gets, and one per-player card for each province.
- **Two-sided cards**, at half letter size (5.5 × 8.5 in): two per letter sheet, printed double-sided and cut in half.
- **A separate HTML page**, like the I Ching page.
- **On the general card**: the turn order (always), plus the extras he picked from a list: realia ownership, money at a glance, the Harvest Pile in and out, and the public works table.
  - He was also offered, and **did not pick**: a population track or I Ching deck tracker, climbing seats (who may replace whom), revolt and Chaos, and game end and scoring. Leave those off the general card unless he asks.
- **On each province card**: the income (harvest) table, the realia to collect, what to do each round, and the public works owed to that province.

Later corrections from Manish, all applied:

- The new harvest squares section opens with **"Only available once your population has doubled (×2)"**, in bold.
- The line under each income table says **"+ 20 per new harvest square you own"**. The Warlord's card leaves that out, since he can't buy squares.
- The Warlord's raid rules are **two separate lines**: raiding realia (5, the piece, or its cost), and raiding the Emperor's buildings (the Emperor pays the full cost, 20). A third line says any other square, canal and reservoir squares included, is an ordinary raid that leaves the work undamaged.
- The Warlord's toll line says the Great Wall counts as a public work for the toll, and that it can't be raided.

Rules settled on 2026-10-26 (see `RULINGS.md`), all applied:

- **Round One skips only the I Ching and the harvest**, so it starts at step 3 (rent). Its taxable income is the 60 everyone starts with.
- **Tax is on harvest income only** (harvest + works + new harvest squares − consumption), not rent, salaries, commissions or tolls. The Emperor pays none. The province backs say "This is what you pay tax on" under the income table (not on the Emperor's card).
- **The Marble Pagoda** is built in any round the Emperor builds nothing else; a palace round is fine. The Emperor's duty line and the works table say so.
- **The Warlord may refuse tax**; his card says only soldiers or politics can make him pay. **Deals aren't binding** and **grain may be kept secret**: both are in the general front's "at any time" box and the Running the round card.

Added on 2026-10-26 at Manish's request: a **Running the round** card, a script for whoever runs the round.

## Layout

**General card.**
- **Front, "The round":** the nine steps of a round, numbered, each with who acts. A note says Round One starts at step 3 (rent) and its taxable income is the 60. An "at any time" box covers deals and revolt. Below that, the realia table: which province sells each piece.
- **Back, "Money and building":** money at a glance (every fixed number, as a definition list), the Harvest Pile in and out, and the public works table. This is the densest card. It uses the `.dense` styles to fit, so check it first after any edit.

**Running the round** (ink header, like the general card). For whoever runs the round: usually the Emperor, or whoever is teaching.
- **Front:** the nine steps, each with who acts, a line in quotes to read aloud ("Scholar, turn the top card and read it to us.") and what to check before moving on. This face is tight (about 9 px free).
- **Back:** Round One, what changes during Chaos (Note 17 and the Chaos clarifications in `RULINGS.md`), a checkbox list for the end of the round (population counter, works pay from next round, debt, realia waiting for auction, is every work built), and an "any time" box.

**Province card.**
- **Front:** a one-line character sketch in italics, what to do each round, the objective by player count (4–5, 6, 7+), the province's starting realia (including the pair it never needs at 4–5 players), and who the player may replace. Then a card-specific extra, if any: "Beware the Warlord" for the Emperor, "Your army" for the Warlord. Last, the three personal disasters as a short title plus the cost.
- **Back:** the income table (larger type: it's the most-read thing on the card), the public works owed, with cost, who pays and the yearly bonus, and when and where new harvest squares can be bought.

**Header colours by class.** General: ink. Emperor: vermilion. Official, Gentry and Scholar: dark. Peasants: walnut. Warlord: slate. The colour is a thin bar above the title, and the title and the corner mark are in the same colour. On a province card the corner mark is a large glyph of the realia that province sells; the Emperor's is the Winter Palace and the Warlord's is the soldier (Manish: "a larger realia symbol on the top of the cards"). The general and Running the round cards leave the corner empty. There are no FRONT or BACK tags: once printed double-sided, the two faces are one card (Manish: "They're unnecessary"). The headers used to be solid bands; they were thinned on 2026-10-26 to save ink and to keep thin paper from soaking (Manish: "I'm mostly thinking of solid colors"). The screen and print versions look the same.

**Glyphs.** Every piece name gets its glyph from `images/` via `add_glyphs.py`, the same as the rulebook and the playthrough.

## Printing

- **Screen:** each card shows its front and back side by side.
- **Print:** landscape letter, no page margins (each card keeps at least 0.2 in of its own padding, so nothing runs to the paper edge), two cards per sheet, six sheets. Two general cards come first, then two Running the round cards (one for the person running the round, one spare for whoever teaches), then Provinces 1–2, 3–4, 5–6 and 7–8. On each back sheet the two cards are swapped, so the backs line up when the page is printed **double-sided, flipped on the short edge**. The page tells people to switch to the long edge if the backs come out upside down. **This hasn't been tested on a real printer.**
- **Themes:** apply to the screen only, unless "Print it too" is ticked in the picker; then they print as well, and must still fit each card on its page. A theme that changes the card faces sets `--card-body` / `--card-mono` and must pass the fit check below in every theme.
- **Fit:** every card must fit its 5.5 × 8.5 in box with no overflow. To check, render the page in a headless browser and compare each `.screen .card`'s `scrollHeight` with its `clientHeight`. Also measure the gap between the last section and the footer. Then print to PDF with `preferCSSPageSize` and look at the sheets. The tightest faces are Running the round's front (about 9 px free), the general back (about 28 px) and the general front (about 30 px). Free space is the footer gap less the last section's bottom margin (about 11 px, 8 px on the dense general back).

## Where the content comes from

Every number mirrors the rulebook and `RULINGS.md`, but **the script hardcodes them**. When a ruling or a rulebook number changes, update the matching table in `build_reference.py` and rebuild:

| Table in the script | What it holds | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `TURN` | The nine steps of a round | Section 5, Note 5 |
| `SCRIPT`, `ROUND_ONE`, `CHAOS`, `END_CHECKS`, `ANYTIME` | The Running the round card | Sections 5, 8–16, Appendix C, Notes 5, 7, 8, 13, 17; `RULINGS.md` |
| `MONEY`, `PILE_OUT`, `PILE_IN` | Fixed sums; the Harvest Pile | Sections 7–14 |
| `WORKS`, `OWED`, `OWED_NOTE` | Public works, costs, who pays, bonuses | Section 12, each province's booklet section |
| `STATS`, `income_table()` | Population, consumption and harvest; the income rows ×1 to ×6 | Section 7, Note 6 |
| `NEED45`, `STUCK` | Objectives at 4–5 players; the pair each province never needs | Sections 10–11, Note 9 |
| `DUTIES`, `REPLACE`, `NEWLAND`, `EXTRA` | Each round's duties; seat changes; new land; the extras | Part Two, Sections 11, 13, 14, Note 11 |
| `CHARACTER`, `DISASTERS` | Character sketches; personal disasters | Part Two |

**Copyright.** The character sketches and disaster titles are short paraphrases in our own words, not booklet text. Keep them that way: no quoting the booklets on the cards.
