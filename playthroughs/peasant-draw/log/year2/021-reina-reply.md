diary: Two years on the throne and the crown has put up the Summer Palace and the whole northern canal, so the canal pays me twenty a year now and nothing half-dug is left for the river to take. That cost the reserve: I started at sixty, went as high as a hundred and twenty-two, and now I'm down to seventy-nine. That's thinner than I'd like with the land still to open, and I need to start building that cushion back. I've held the tax at forty, which they've grumbled about but taken. Paddy is the one I'm watching, at twenty-nine and a half after paying seventeen for the Jar and thirteen for a scroll, and a bad card could turn him against the crown. Graham and Paddy are already tangled up over who promised which Customs House, and Silke is quietly picking up whatever the two of them don't guard. Next comes the Winter Palace, which opens the coastal canals, and then Graham's Reservoir before his patience runs out. If incomes stay this thin I'll have to ease the tax, because I'd rather give up a few grain than face a revolt.
