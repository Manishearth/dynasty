## At the table since your last turn

Paddy collects the tax at 30%: 3 from Graham, the only positive income, to the crown. Silke builds the Winter Palace on H21 in Province 1, paying the 20 herself, into the Harvest Pile; the canals in Provinces 5 and 6 can now be built. 340 of the works are still owed.

Paddy: "The Bowl's come home to me again, so up it goes. Six to start. Graham went to twelve for it last year, so I don't think six is asking much. And Reina, I'm sorry about the throne. That Customs House of yours at two is the first good news I've had in a year."

Graham: "The Vase again. It's still the only one in China, and last year it went for twenty-five. It opens at ten."

This year's lots: Reina's Customs House, unsold last year, must go up again at 2; Paddy puts up the Wooden Bowl, minimum 6, and keeps both Bamboo Brushes back; Graham puts up the Vase, minimum 10, and keeps the Land Deed Scroll back.

Reina: "Well. Silke played the long game and played it beautifully, and I'll say so on the record. The coast is mine now, so I'm starting over like everyone else: my Customs House is up at two, cheap, and I'll be bidding on the pottery, but nobody's going to see me burn my grain to get it."

Paddy: "Reina, I'll take that Customs House off your hands, and gladly. Graham, ten's a fair start for the Vase. I'll say it plainly to everyone: if I get the House and the Vase both, I've got my set, and I'll still have my twenty grain kept by."

Graham: "The Customs House is Paddy's business, not mine. I'll bid for the Bowl, though, because I need it, and I won't lose it by one grain twice."

Reina: "Paddy, you told the whole table that the Vase finishes your set, and I've just come off the throne, so I know exactly what that sentence means. Sorry, the Vase is coming to the coast."

Paddy: "Twenty-two and a half, and that's every grain I can spare and still keep my twenty. I won't pretend I've got more, Reina. If you go past it, it's yours, and I'll shake your hand."

The auctions settle. Paddy wins Reina's Customs House for 2, unopposed (Reina keeps 0.5). Graham wins Paddy's Wooden Bowl for 9 over Reina's 8 (Paddy keeps 2). The Vase was close, so Reina and Paddy each had one raise: Reina to 26, Paddy to 22.5. Reina wins the Vase for 23.5 (Graham keeps 5.5).

Paddy: "Right next to the Deed, there. Well played, Reina, I said I'd shake your hand and I will. But the Vase only has to come back round once, and when it does, I'll be the one waiting for it."

Graham: "Nine. I lost by one grain last year, so it's a pleasure to win by one this time. It goes next to my Customs House. Paddy, those two brushes in your stock: I'll be bidding on one when it comes up."

Reina: "Back on the shelf where Silke had it, P22. Paddy, I'll take that handshake, and I mean it. You bid honestly and you stopped where you said you'd stop."

Paddy puts the Customs House on R24, beside his Scroll and Jar; Graham puts the Wooden Bowl on Q16, beside his Customs House; Reina puts the Vase on P22. Silke pays Graham 5 for reading the I Ching.
