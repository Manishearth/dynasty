diary: Years 3–4. I kept my word to Graham. I taxed the good year at fifty and built his Reservoir with him, and that same year I lost the throne to Silke, the woman I'd been paying five a year to read the cards. While I was busy watching Paddy and Graham fight over the Customs House, she quietly put a whole set together on the coast, and I never counted her shelf. That's the lesson, and it hurts. I came off the throne with sixty, lost ten to the bad harvest and then spent twenty-three and a half on the only Vase in China to stop Paddy finishing his set, so I'm down to twenty-seven, the poorest at the table. I still think it was the right buy: anyone who wants the throne now has to go through me, and Paddy is one Vase short and knows it. Next is the long climb: a scroll, a brush and a bowl, and enough grain kept back to claim with. Meanwhile I'll press Silke for the Province 5 canal, now that the Winter Palace is up, because she promised the coast those canals in front of everyone.
