## At the table since your last turn

(Storyteller's note: At 6 a square for Paddy's three squares the crown would have kept 6 (1 after Graham's salary), so Graham's squares could still have had a crown share.)

Paddy: "Five a square it is, Silke, and fair enough. Graham's got to eat as well. Three squares go into the south-east, and I'll be back to four and a half grain, same as when I started out."

Province 6 buys three squares of sea, R25, R26 and S26, for 60: Paddy pays 45 and the crown 15, 5 a square. Paddy has 4.5 left; the crown's reserve is 9, with Graham's salary of 5 still to pay.

Graham: "Four squares for the south centre, Silke. One a square from the crown, four in all, and that still leaves your chest enough for my five. I've done the sums. I don't need your eight, and I'm not leaving you short to pay me."

Province 7 buys four squares of the Warlord's land, T14 to T17, for 80: Graham pays 76 and the crown 4, 1 a square. Thirteen squares bought this year in all. Grain: Silke (the crown) 5, Reina 1, Paddy 4.5, Graham 27.5.

The auctions. Paddy holds only one type beside the board, so his last Bamboo Brush goes up, at a minimum he names in his bids. Reina and Graham have nothing left in stock. The Emperor doesn't bid.

Reina: "One grain in my purse and the minimum is two, so the brush is safe from me this year, Paddy. Keep it on your shelf if nobody bids. I'll be good for it once the coast fields come in."

Paddy: "Two grain, and not a grain more, so it's open to anybody who wants it this year. If nobody does, Reina, it'll be on my shelf waiting for you once the coast comes in."

Graham: "Pass. I've got a brush, and I know what it looks like when someone buys a second one just to keep it from somebody. I won't do that. Paddy, the piece I'm waiting on is your Jar. I stood aside for your Customs House, and when you're ready to talk about that Jar, I'll be the one at this table with grain."

Paddy's Bamboo Brush opens at 2 and nobody bids, so it stays beside the board and must go up again next year at 2. Silke pays Graham 5 for reading the I Ching. End of Year 6: Silke (the crown) 0, Reina 1, Paddy 4.5, Graham 32.5. Thirteen squares of new land were bought this year (Province 1 four, 5 two, 6 three, 7 four), each yielding 20 from next year. Nobody is in debt. Still owed: the Great Wall (160) and the Grand Canal (160). Still beside the board: Paddy 1 Bamboo Brush.
