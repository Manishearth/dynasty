diary: Years 5–6. I took Graham's scroll for six, lost the brush to him by one grain, and told myself I could wait, and then the population doubled and I lost all the waiting in a single card. A province that was poor turned into one that would lose forty-five a year, so I put twenty-four of my last twenty-five grain into two squares of sea. Silke only paid eight a square after I'd asked for ten, but she still gave me more than anyone else got. That leaves me with one grain, the Vase and a scroll, and I've never been this poor or this close to the bottom of the table. It was still the right call: I'm out of debt and my losses are down to five a year, and nobody is afraid of a woman with one grain. Paddy's brush is still unsold and he's promised it to me at two. Graham has made it plain that he's after Paddy's Jar and not my pieces. Next I need the brush, then Graham's Bowl, then twenty grain to claim, all before the Wall and the Grand Canal end the game. That's at most two years, so I can't make a single wasted move.
