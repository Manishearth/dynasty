move: counter 8 a square
say: "Eight a square, Reina. That's sixteen from the crown and twenty-four from you, and you've got twenty-five, so both squares go in the ground. I'm not tipping my last grain into the sea when Paddy and Graham haven't asked yet and Graham still needs paying for the cards. The coast gets its fields and the crown keeps its feet."
aside: "Ten a square would bring me down to fifteen before Graham's five and the others' shares, and a broke crown can't pay anyone's share next year, Reina's included. At eight she still gets both squares, so nobody can call it a refusal, and the four grain I keep might be what keeps me out of debt."
