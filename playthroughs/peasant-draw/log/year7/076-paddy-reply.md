Ruling: the Brush can't be sold this way. It is unsold stock beside the board, and unsold stock is sold only at auction (R: Unsold stock is sold only at auction); it must go up this year at 2. So the first offer can't stand; Paddy's fallback, a loan of 6 from Graham repaid out of next year's harvest, is legal and goes to Graham. A note to the table says so. The say's "the Pile would take three of my pieces" is right: he is 5.5 short, so two pieces (4) wouldn't cover it.
