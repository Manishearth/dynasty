move: Appointments: Paddy stays tax collector; Reina banks the Harvest Pile and reads the I Ching. Salaries this year: 2 to Paddy for this year's collection, nothing to anyone else (the new appointees start next year, at 3 each for the jobs they do). No land; the crown keeps 15.5.
say: Paddy, you keep the tax. You collected it honestly and you'll get two for it tonight. Reina, the bank and the I Ching are yours from next year, three a year each, and I'll pay it on time. Silke, you've sixty in hand, which is more than the crown has, so you'll forgive me for not paying you anything.
aside: Fifteen and a half in the reserve and three hundred and twenty still owed on works, so every grain I hand out tonight has to buy me goodwill. Silke paid six once for a brush she didn't need, and tonight I needed to pay her nothing at all.
whisper: none
