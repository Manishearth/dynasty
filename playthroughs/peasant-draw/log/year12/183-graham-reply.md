diary: Years 11 and 12, and the end. I went into Year 11 with sixty grain and five squares of sea, and two harvests later I was nineteen and a half under, holding nothing, with no piece left to sell. The Customs House went to Silke for two. Paddy's brush went to her for nine, one grain over my eight, which makes two brushes at this table I've lost by a single grain, and I counted both. I wouldn't borrow what I couldn't show the numbers to repay, so I took the debt in the open and sat out the auctions. Then Silke finished her Wheat set and took my seat to reach the coast, and that took my nineteen and a half of debt off me. I drew sixty clean grain from the Pile and sat down in Province 7, the province I drew at the start of the game. I finish there, holding my seat, with sixty grain and nothing owed to anyone, which is more than any other peasant has. Reina wins on a throne that's two hundred and ninety-four grain under, and she knew it was that deep before any of us did. Twelve years, the Wall and the Canal never built, and the board went all the way round and put me back where I started with more grain than I had then. Next time I take my first brush at whatever it costs, and I'll be quicker about it.
