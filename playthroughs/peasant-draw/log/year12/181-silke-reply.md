diary: Twelve years, and I ended up exactly where I started, in the Silk seat on the coast. I'd still call it a victory of a kind. In Year 11 I bought the Customs House for two and Paddy's Brush for nine. In Year 12 I lent Paddy eighteen to keep his Deed off the Pile, and the Pile took it anyway, along with his buffalo. He gave me back the Bowl for two, and that finished the Wheat set I'd sworn four years ago to build quietly. This time I did build it quietly, and then I spent it on a ditch three hundred grain deep. I took Graham's nineteen and a half of debt to get back the coast I drew, and I'd do it again, because the Wheat seat was a loss and the Silk seat is a hold. Reina wins. She kept the throne through two collapses and never once lied to my face, and I've stopped being angry about that. Paddy owes me twenty-three and a half that I'll never see, the crown owes me six it will never pay, and the Great Wall and the Grand Canal were never built. What I take home is forty-eight and a half grain I didn't borrow, a Brush, a Bowl and a Customs House on the coast, and the knowledge that I was the one who kept this game going two years longer than anyone wanted. That cost me the throne once and it nearly cost me everything. Next time I'll take a deal from Reina when she offers one.
