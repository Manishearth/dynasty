diary: Years 11 and 12, and the end of it. I turned the cards fair to the last. The last one was Fortune and Misfortune, a good harvest for everyone and trouble for one province, and the one province was mine. A buffalo broke its leg in the middle of planting, and that one animal cost me the Land Deed and the Customs House, two grain apiece back into the Pile, after twelve years of keeping them. Silke lent me eighteen to try and save them, and I sold her the Bowl at two, because a high price would only have fed the Pile and not her. She used it to finish her wheat and walk onto the coast, so my old pots have now moved four people into new seats, and none of them was me. I'm finishing where I started, the Rice Peasant in Province 6, with no grain, no pieces, and seven new squares of paddy that weren't there when I came. What sits worst with me isn't the set. It's the twenty-three and a half I still owe Silke, after all those years of telling myself I'd never owe anybody a thing. The crown owes me twelve, and every grain of it is hers if it ever comes. Reina won it fairly and kept her word to me the whole way, and I'll take that, and next time I'll keep a few grain back for the buffalo.
