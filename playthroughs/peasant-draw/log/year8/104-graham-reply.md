diary: Years 7 and 8. I started Year 7 as the Wheat Peasant with thirty-two and a half grain and finished it on the throne. Paddy sold me his Jar for ten because I'd stood aside for his Customs House, and that finished the Wheat set. I used it to take Reina's seat, which cost me twenty-five of her debt and the Jar itself. Then the Pile sold me the Vase for seven and the Scroll for two, and with those I held the Silk set and twenty and a half grain. I paid three to clear the crown's debt and sat down in Silke's chair. She had paid six for a brush she didn't need to keep me from a set. It cost me about forty grain all told, and that's the cheapest thing I've bought at this table. As Emperor I taxed at thirty and paid five a square to everyone, which was what the last crown paid. I paid Reina exactly what I promised her. Silke wouldn't put twenty into her own canal, so nothing got built, and everyone heard her refuse. Now Reina is back on the coast with sixty-two grain and two of the four Silk pieces, and the Vase and the Scroll she needs are on Silke's shelf. Next year I watch what Silke asks for them, I keep the tax low enough that nobody has a reason to revolt, and I count every grain before it leaves the crown's chest.
