diary: Years 9 and 10. I sat on the throne with one and a half grain and borrowed to keep it out of debt both years, thirteen from Silke and then twenty-nine from Reina. I paid every grain back in full and on the day I said, which meant taxing at forty and building nothing. The north never got its square, and I'll be counting that against me until the population card comes. Then Silke sold the last Scroll to Reina for fifty-three, which was the exact price of the Vase she'd let go at two, and Reina walked into my chair with twenty grain and the Silk set I'd built for her by keeping the crown solvent. I'm on the coast again with sixty grain, the same seat I took from her in Year 7, and the board has gone all the way round once. Reina paid my appointees what I'd promised them, and I'll give her credit for that, but I lent to no one, broke no word, and still lost the throne to a woman who borrowed nothing from me. Next year I buy sea squares for Province 5 before the population doubles, I watch what the Bowl and the Brushes cost out of Paddy's stock, and I keep track of who at this table prices things to the grain.
