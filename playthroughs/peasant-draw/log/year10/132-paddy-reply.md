diary: Years 9 and 10, and I read my first two cards, and both were bad for the rice. Even so I've come through them with fifteen grain, no debts, and two more squares of sea at Q27 and R27, which is more paddy than this province has ever had. I sold my last Brush to Reina for sixteen, though really Silke made her pay that, and it was the last of my stock. Then I sat and watched Reina gather every piece of the silk set, finish it with Silke's Scroll at fifty-three, and climb back onto the throne. That makes three different people up there in front of me, and none of them ever needed my Vase as badly as I did. I'm not bitter about it. Reina's paid me the six she owed me, and she's kept me on the tax and the cards, and a crown that keeps its word is worth more to a rice farmer than a crown that doesn't. What I keep turning over is that Reina's set has gone back on the shelves: the Bowl and both Brushes to mine, the Vase and the Scroll to Silke's, and Silke's sitting on the Jar. With the Wall and the Canal still owed, there may only be a year or two left, and fifteen grain won't buy the Jar and the Vase and keep twenty back besides. So I'll do what I've always done. I'll sell the pots that have come home to me at a fair price, save every grain the new fields bring in, and if the set's out of reach, I'll at least finish this game the way I started it, as the Rice Peasant who never owed anybody a thing.
