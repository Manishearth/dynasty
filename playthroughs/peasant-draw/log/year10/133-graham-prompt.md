## At the table since your last turn

Graham taxes at 40%: Reina 24, Paddy 12, Silke 20, 56 in all. He repays both loans in full from it, 15 to Silke and 33 to Reina. Grain: Graham (the crown) 8.5, Reina 92, Paddy 28, Silke 95.5. Nothing is built this year. The crown pays 1 a square on new land bought by any other province, and buys none for Province 1.

Reina: "One square of sea at N28, Graham, nineteen from me and your one from the crown, as you offered. I'm not going to stand here with ninety grain and let the coast go hungry, but I'm not going to pave it either, because there's a Scroll still to come this year."

New land: Reina buys N28 (sea) for 20, paying 19 with the crown's 1. Grain: Graham (the crown) 7.5, Reina 73.

Paddy: "Same as Reina, Graham: one square of sea at R27, nineteen from me and your one from the crown. I've twenty-eight grain, and I'd sooner it went into a field that feeds the south-east than sat in a sack while the two of you bid over a Scroll I can't use."

New land: Paddy buys R27 (sea) for 20, paying 19 with the crown's 1. Grain: Graham (the crown) 6.5, Paddy 9.

Silke: "Two squares for the wheat, Graham, U15 and U16, nineteen each from me and your one a square, as you offered. The rain sulked on Province 7 this year, so I'm digging deeper before the next population card arrives, not after."

New land: Silke buys U15 and U16 (the Warlord's land) for 40, paying 38 with the crown's 2. Nothing is built this year. Land: 22 squares. Grain: Graham (the crown) 4.5, Reina 73, Silke 57.5, Paddy 9.

The auctions. Provinces 5 and 6 have no stock left, and the Harvest Pile holds none. Province 7's Land Deed Scroll was held back last year, so it must go up this year; Silke sets its minimum in her bids. The Emperor doesn't bid.

Reina: "Everyone knows what this one is for, so I won't pretend otherwise. Silke, set your price where you like. I've kept my twenty for the claim, and whatever's left over that is yours to name."

Paddy: "I've nine grain and a Scroll already on my paddies, so I'll not push the price up on a piece I can't use. Bidding just to feed the Pile isn't my way. Reina, if it's yours, you'll have got it fair, and I'll say so."

Silke: "You told me to name my price, Reina, so here it is: fifty-three. That leaves you exactly the twenty for your claim and not a grain over. You said in Year 8 you'd never forget the Vase at two. This is me remembering it for you."

The auction: Silke's Land Deed Scroll goes to Reina at Silke's minimum of 53, uncontested. Silke keeps a quarter, 13; 40 goes to the Harvest Pile. Grain: Reina 20, Silke 70.5, Paddy 9, Graham (the crown) 4.5. Reina now holds the Customs House, the Bowl, two Brushes, the Vase and the Scroll: a complete Silk set, with 20 in hand.

Reina: "Fifty-three, Silke, and that's fair: you priced it to the grain, and I'd have done exactly the same. Graham, the Silk seat is yours again, and so is the coast I just put a new square on, so you won't be taking over an empty sack. The crown's coming back to where it started, and the first thing it does is pay its appointees and look north."

Reina takes the throne with the Silk set and 20 in hand. Every piece she held goes back to stock: the Customs House to Province 5's, the Bowl and both Brushes to Province 6's, the Vase and the Scroll to Province 7's. She takes the crown's 4.5: 24.5. Graham draws 60 from the Harvest Pile and becomes the Silk Peasant, with Province 5. Grain: Reina (the crown) 24.5, Graham 60, Silke 70.5, Paddy 9.

Reina: "The crown pays what the crown promised, even when the promise was Graham's: six to Paddy and three to Silke. Same hands on the same jobs next year at the same three a job, because nothing worked last year that needs fixing. Paddy, I've known a crown that would give you nothing for reading a card. I wasn't that crown before and I'm not going to be now."

Reina pays Paddy 6 for the tax and the I Ching, and Silke 3 for the bank, and keeps the jobs where they are for next year at 3 a job. End of Year 10: Reina (the crown) 15.5, Graham 60, Silke 73.5, Paddy 15. Nobody is in debt. Land: 22 squares. Nothing built; the Great Wall (160) and the Grand Canal (160) are still owed. Paddy holds a Scroll and a Customs House; Silke holds the Dragon Jar.
