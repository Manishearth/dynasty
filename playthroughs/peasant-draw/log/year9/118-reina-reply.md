move: 6:BB P26; 7:VA P22

say: "Silke, two for the Vase, and that's generous of you, I won't forget it. The Brush I paid for in fear, sixteen grain against a shelf I counted wrong. Fair enough, Paddy, it went into your pocket and not into the river. That leaves one Scroll and twenty grain, and everyone here knows where the Scroll is."

aside: "Silke was four pieces away and I spent sixteen grain blocking a door that was never open. That's the price of finally paying attention, and I'll take it, because I've got fifty-two grain and the Vase is home, and all that's left is the Scroll on Silke's shelf next year."
