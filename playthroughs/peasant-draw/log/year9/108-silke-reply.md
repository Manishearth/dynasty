move: Lend the crown 13 grain now on Graham's terms: 15 back out of next year's tax. Her one condition, made in public: no crisis tax on Province 7 this year, since the crown won't be in debt.
say: "Thirteen it is, Graham, and fifteen back next year. I asked you for thirty once and you said no, so I'm being kinder than you were. One condition, out loud so everyone hears it: a crown that isn't in debt doesn't need ninety percent. I know, because I'm the last one who charged it. Keep the tax decent and we're square."
aside: "Thirteen grain keeps the crown out of the red, and a crown in the red taxes its richest province until it bleeds, which this year is me. It also gets me two grain of interest and puts the Emperor in my debt while I quietly rebuild the Wheat set."
whisper: none
---
Ruling: a loan, legal at any time (§5), not binding; scripted as a deal before the harvest: Silke 60 to 47, the crown 17.5 to 30.5. The harvest then leaves the crown at 0.5, out of debt. Her condition on the tax is a public ask, not binding.
