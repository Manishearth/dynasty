diary: I spent two years sitting on the last Scroll in China while Reina circled it. In year nine I lent the crown thirteen to keep Graham's tax off my wheat. He kept the rate at thirty and paid me back to the grain, so he's as honest as I feared. I got the Jar for two. Reina paid sixteen for a Brush she didn't need just to keep it from me, and I gave her the Vase for two, which may be the kindest and stupidest thing I've done at this table. I put the rest into the ground: three fields of the Warlord's land, and Province 7 now earns more than a hundred a year in an average one. Then I set the Scroll at seventy-five, she stopped at seventy, and I bought one year. Next year it goes up at two, I can't bid on my own lot, and Reina has ninety in her chest, so unless Paddy's Scroll or some new trick turns up, she climbs. My answer is to be the richest peasant in China when she does. Whoever she climbs over, I'll have the grain to climb back after her.
