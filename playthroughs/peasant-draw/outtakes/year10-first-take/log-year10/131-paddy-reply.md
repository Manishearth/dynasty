diary: Two more years, and I've spent nearly every grain I had on mud and sea: Q27 the first year, when Graham found me four, and R27 the second. Both times I went down to nothing or close to it, but the rice will bring in seventy-five in an ordinary year now, which is more than I ever thought these paddies could feed. I read the cards for the first time, and I couldn't turn up anything but rot for my own fields, while Reina's coast had a fine year. The Jar went to Silke for two and the Vase to Reina for two, and I had nothing to bid for either one. Reina did pay me sixteen for my last Brush, though, only to keep it away from Silke, and that's four grain I didn't expect. Then Silke asked seventy-five for the last Scroll and Reina wouldn't go past seventy, so it's still sitting on Silke's shelf. Everybody at that table forgot there's another one, and it's mine, on R23. Reina needs one Scroll to finish her set, and she's got ninety grain. I'm not going to sell the thing I've held since the first year cheap, but I hate the thought of standing in anyone's way either, so I'll have to think hard about what it's worth to me and to her. Fourteen and a half grain, no debts, and I'm still the Rice Peasant.
