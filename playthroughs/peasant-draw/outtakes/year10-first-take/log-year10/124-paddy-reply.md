move: canal: no; land R27; I pay 19.5, asking the crown for 0.5

aside: I couldn't cover the canal anyway, and every work that gets built brings the end of the game closer, which doesn't suit a man who's this far behind. One more field means another twenty a year if that times-six card ever turns up.
