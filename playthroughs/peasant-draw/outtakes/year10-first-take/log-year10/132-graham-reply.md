diary: Years 9 and 10. Both years the crown's harvest came in at minus thirty, and the crown has no fields of its own, so twice I had to borrow just to stay out of debt. Silke lent me thirteen and Reina lent me twenty-nine, and I paid back fifteen and thirty-one, every grain on the day I said I would. I named the forty percent before the harvest, so nobody can say it surprised them. I ended the reign at nothing and owing nobody. That's poor, but it isn't in debt, and I've learned at this table that the difference matters. I put four of the crown's grain into Paddy's first square of sea and a grain or a half on every other square, and the table now holds twenty-one squares that I helped pay for. Nobody would dig the canal at thirty-nine of their own, so no work was built either year. The Wall and the Canal still stand between this table and the end, and I can't pay for either one. Silke asked seventy-five for the last Scroll and Reina stopped at seventy. Next year it goes up at two, and Paddy has a second Scroll on R23, so I'll watch who works that out first. Whoever takes this chair from me will find the books balanced. I mean to keep it long enough for somebody else to pay for the Wall.
