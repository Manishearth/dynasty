diary: Years 9–10. I counted every shelf at the table and still got one wrong. I paid Paddy sixteen for a second Brush to stop Silke finishing a set, and she was four pieces short of one. Twelve of that sixteen went into the Pile because I was afraid. Then Silke let me have the Vase for two, and a year later she put her Scroll at seventy-five, five over everything I could spend and still claim. She'd learned that from me, and I watched her do it with ninety grain in front of me and nothing I could spend it on. I'm not angry. I lent the crown twenty-nine and got thirty-one back the same year, I kept the coast out of debt, I turned down two canals that would have cost me the claim, and I'm the richest woman at the table, one piece short of a set. But there are two Scrolls in China. Silke's has to go up at two next year, unless she finds a way to keep it back, and Paddy has the other one on R23 and reminded me tonight that he keeps his word. So next year I'll ask Paddy before I ask Silke. Nothing has been built for two years, so the game runs on, and every year it runs gives me another chance at the crown.
