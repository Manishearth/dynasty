# Character ledger

Kept current at the end of each round. What each character has promised, offered, refused and resented, their running bits, and their best lines so far. Then the Storyteller's rulings, as precedents. Line numbers like (009) are the log entries in `log/year1/`.

*Last updated: end of Year 1.*

## Reina Osorio — Emperor (Province 1)

- **Holds:** 89 grain. Built the Winter Palace on G21. No soldiers.
- **Promises:**
  - "Graham's Reservoir is next on my list" (002). Graham has written it down. **Year 2's build is the test.**
  - Pays Graham 5 a year as card reader; she paid on time in Year 1.
- **Offers declined:** a loan of 20 to Paddy, interest-free, for a guard on the Jar (018). Paddy declined (019): "I'd rather not start the year owing the Crown."
- **Policy:** 30% tax in Year 1; "every bag goes back into stone." She appointed Paddy and Graham "because a revolt needs two peasants".
- **Running bits:** answering Rob's campfire ("Deserts get cold, and that's the only warmth you'll get from this court"); "Business is business, isn't it?"
- **Best lines:**

  - Aside: "twenty grain to protect it is cheap insurance for my crown. It also leaves Paddy owing me, which helps if anyone starts talking about a revolt." (018)

## Silke Weber — Silk Peasant (Province 5)

- **Holds:** 7.5 grain. Wooden Bowl (L24), Land Deed Scroll (L25). Still needs a Brush and the Vase. Two Customs Houses in stock.
- **Promises:** **next year's Scroll is Paddy's, "no fight from me"** (015), made in public. Paddy asked Graham to record it, and Graham did.
- **Grudges:** she bought the Bowl at 25 against Graham. Rob bid the Scroll up to hold it hostage, and she paid 11 to stop him.
- **Running bits:** counting other people's grain ("I've counted my six and a half, Graham, and I've counted yours too"); the far north as her vault.
- **Leverage she's named:** Graham needs her Customs House; she wants to "talk about that Vase".
- **Best lines:**
  - "Seven and a half grain and two treasures in the far corner, Rob, so come and fetch them if you've got the legs." (018)
  - "Rob, you don't need a Scroll, you need someone to sell it back to at three times the price." (015)
  - Aside: "Whatever they spend fighting each other now is grain they won't have when I'm the one bidding on their Vase and Bowl." (003)
