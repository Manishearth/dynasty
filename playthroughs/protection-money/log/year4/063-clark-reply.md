diary: Years Three and Four, in my own hand. Rich kept his word to the letter both years, thirty and thirty, and even quoted my own book back at me, which tells me he knows exactly what the book is for. I spent forty-four on a Jar and a Bowl I didn't need, outbidding Reina by a single grain each time. It was dear, but she still has no set, I'm the only loaded gun at this table, and I've got spares now that Rob's man is standing on H13, one square from my Jar. Things to note: Reina's "the coast's business" lasted one year. Silke carried the empire through a lean harvest. Rob has taken ten from my fields and smiled each time. And worst of all, the crown's reserve is down to nineteen and a half after Rich paid my thirty and kept building pagodas. A reserve under twenty and a thirty-a-year promise can't both last, so next year I'll find out whether Rich would rather break his word to me or find the grain somewhere. I'll be watching Reina's purse for the moment she offers a better rent than his. Ninety-nine grain, set complete, and my loyalty is still rented by the year.
