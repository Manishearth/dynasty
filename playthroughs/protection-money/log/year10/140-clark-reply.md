diary: Years Nine and Ten, in my own hand. Rich kept his word for an eighth and a ninth year, thirty and thirty, and both times he paid it out of a treasury that couldn't spare it. In Year Nine he finished the Wall over his own roof, all four links. In Year Ten, after three years of my blank line, he put two links over mine, and I've written that down as plainly as I wrote down the delay. A book that won't credit a payment isn't worth quoting. The river took thirty of my grain, a gale took ten, and I paid nothing in tax in a flood year, so I end on two hundred and twenty-eight with the set complete. Silke paid nearly three hundred and fifty in one year and asked me to file it under the people who pay for the Wall, so I have, and her name is the first I'd call on. The main event was Silke's soldier taking W2 off the board, which made me the only loaded gun at the table again. Rob hired another man on T24 straight away, so now two soldiers are four rounds from that Scroll, and my monopoly has a clock on it. Next year I watch three things: whether the last two links over Province 2 go up, whether W2 and W3 close on P25 together, and whether Rich's thirty survives the first lean year since the treasury filled. Loyalty is still rented by the year. The landlord has paid on time for nine years and has started fixing my roof, and the rent goes up the day a second gun appears.
