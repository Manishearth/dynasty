diary: Two good harvests in a row, and I've had to count my grain twice to believe it. A hundred and ten in Year 7 and a hundred and seventy in Year 8, so I put seven new fields into the sea with the crown paying five on each, and now there are eleven of them, all down the far end away from Rob's soldiers. It cost me a water buffalo and a flooded paddy, ten grain of rent to Reina this year that she'd fairly earned, and every grain of the crown's thirty percent. I paid all of it and I owe nobody. Rich finally put two links of Wall up, though it's over his own province and Silke's purse paid for most of it, and I don't think she'll let anyone forget that. I'm going into Year 9 with a hundred and nine grain and still nothing in front of me. But Rob says to bring the big purse round for tea over the Customs House at T23, so I'll go and pay him what's fair. What worries me is that Rob needs only Silke's Scroll to have a whole set, and W2 is sitting one square from it.
