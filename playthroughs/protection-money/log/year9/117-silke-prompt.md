## Silke's diary so far, in her own words

## Years 1-2

Two years, one Land Deed Scroll in the far corner of P25, and I still don't own a Customs House or a Bowl. Both times I stopped a single grain short, and both times the piece went to someone with a fuller purse and less sense. Count what went out: rent to Rich twice, tax to two different Emperors, and two of Rob's "courtesy calls," and the only thing that came back was a quarter of my own Dragon Jar. I told Reina her cushions were in danger, and she lost them the same afternoon. Now Clark holds a full set beside Rich's throne and gets paid thirty to keep it there, which is a bribe, whatever he writes in his ledger. Reina whispered promises of canals on the coast, but she whispered them too late to matter, and a Gentry who just lost her own throne makes no promises that hold. Next year I hold my grain, bid only where the price is honest, and buy land the moment the people double, because land is the one thing nobody can bid away from me.

## Years 3-4

Two years of counting, and here's the total: five fields of my own out by the sea, still no Customs House and no Bowl, and fifty grain in the purse. I lost the Customs House to Paddy by one grain. Then Clark bought my own Jar and Paddy's Bowl, pieces his set doesn't even need, just so the rest of us can't have them, and three quarters of what he paid went into the Pile. I offered Reina an honest ten for one honest promise and she kept the promise and took the five. Fair enough, but now I know her word is good for exactly one year. This year I paid twenty-four of the crown's thirty-three, the biggest share at the table, and Clark wrote it down in his book as if that pays me back for it. Rich kept his word on the fields, I'll give him that, but his reserve is down to nineteen and a half with a Pagoda and a Winter Palace to show for it, so the canal he promised the coast will be paid for out of my harvests or it won't be dug at all. Next year the fields come in at a hundred and more, and I mean to get a Bowl off Clark, one way or another, before Rob's soldier on P21 decides my coast is the next stop on his walk.

## Years 5-6

Two years, and I have ten fields along the sea where I had five, and eighty-eight grain in the purse after paying for every one of them. The crown put in eleven grain across all those fields, half a grain at a time at the end, and I paid sixty-one and a half in tax for that over two years. Rich keeps his word, I'll grant him that every year, but his word comes to exactly what's in the treasury, and the treasury ends every year at zero, while Clark is paid thirty to write it down. I gave Reina ten when she had seven, and she repaid me honestly by not asking again, which is more than the crown can say. The first section of the Canal went to Paddy's coast, and the same year Rob walked off with Paddy's Customs House, so now he sits on two of them out in the desert, and Clark still has two Bowls and only needs one. Count the board and you'll find my Scroll on P25 is the only piece Rob hasn't walked off with, and W2 is three rounds from it. Next year I buy the spare Bowl off Clark or wait for Rob to put a Customs House up for sale, whichever comes first, because one grain short of a set is still short, and my fields need someone at the table who'll speak for them.

## Years 7-8

Two good years, and the coast finally got paid back for something. I started Year 7 with eighty-eight grain and ten fields, and I end Year 8 with two hundred and forty-three and twenty-four fields, a canal section with salt water in it, and a hundred and seventy-one paid to the crown in tax across the two years. Rich kept his word again, the canal on my coast and two links of Wall, though the Wall sits over his own province and was paid for out of my harvest, and Clark wrote the province number down as if that settles it. I gave Reina ten both years because she asked honestly, and a Gentry with sixty-six in her purse won't forget who paid her when she was short. Fire in the drying sheds and a storm on the boat cost me fourteen, which is nothing next to what the fields bring in now. Count what matters, though: Rob holds two Customs Houses and a Jar, he needs exactly one more piece, and that piece is my Scroll on P25, with W2 standing one square from it on Q26 and the lieutenant's elopement the only thing that kept him off it this year. Next year I don't wait for anyone to sell me anything: I find a way to put a soldier or a wall of my own between W2 and that Scroll, and I bring my purse to Rob's tea over T23 before Paddy does, because one Customs House would make him one piece short and make me one Bowl short.
