move: land K25, K26, K27; I pay 54, asking the crown for 6 (the 5 on offer for K25, and half a grain each on K26 and K27)

say: "Three fields, Rich, up the coast at K25, K26 and K27. I'll take your five on the first, and on the other two I'm asking half a grain each, the same as last year, so the crown's last grain does some work too. And I'll say it while Clark's pen is out: the first section of the Grand Canal went to Paddy's province, and I don't begrudge him it, but I paid forty and a half of this year's fifty-seven, so the second section had better have salt water in it."

aside: "Fifty-four grain makes it ten fields by the sea next year, and I'm still holding eighty-eight, enough for the set's twenty and a Bowl and a Customs House the moment either one comes up for sale. Rob's walking Jars around the north for now, and every field I put up at K is another step he'd have to march to reach me."

whisper: none

---
Ruling: K25 bought as offered (15 Silke, 5 crown). K26 and K27 ask the crown for 0.5 each, beyond its offer, so they go to Rich in a free --short prompt. (Paddy's U28, 082, was answered at the same time and is played after Silke's in table order.)
