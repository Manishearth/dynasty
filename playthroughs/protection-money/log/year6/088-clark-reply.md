diary: Years Five and Six, in my own hand. Rich kept his word for a fourth and fifth year, thirty and thirty, to the grain. I've written that down honestly, because a book that only records the sins is no use as evidence. Look at what it cost him, though: the crown finished Year Five on four and Year Six on nothing at all. Four years in a row I've wiped out his reserve with his own promise. Item: the works stood still for a year, and then one Canal section went to Paddy and not to Silke, who pays most of the tax and said so to the whole table. Item: Rob walked off with my spare Jar and then stood on my Scroll and called it restraint, and Rich hasn't moved a single soldier against him, which makes two causes for revolt in one man. Item: Reina has started counting out loud as well, and Silke is carrying the empire and knows it. My set is complete, I'm sitting on well over a hundred, and I'm the only loaded gun at the table, so the throne is mine to take whenever the price is right. That point is coming close now, with an empty treasury, a Warlord nobody controls and the coast feeling unrewarded. Next year I'll watch whether Rich can pay thirty from zero without breaking his word, and if he does break it, I'll read his promise back to him word for word, with two friends standing beside me. Loyalty is still rented by the year, and the landlord's credit has just run out.
