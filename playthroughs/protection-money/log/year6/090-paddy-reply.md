diary: I said no to a field in Year 5 because I'd told the table twenty and fourteen and a half isn't twenty. Rich didn't hold it against me. This year he gave me the ten and ten he'd promised, and the first section of the Grand Canal on top of it, so I've a new field at U28 and a canal both paying from next harvest, and forty-seven grain in my purse, the most I've ever had. But I watched that soldier at R22 for two years, and in the end he walked one step and carried my Customs House off into the desert, and Rob apologised while he did it. Nine grain and seven lots it took me to win that piece, and now I'm back to holding nothing, the same as Year 1, only with more grain. Rob has Reina's piece, Clark's and mine, and Clark has a whole set sitting in front of him. Next year I need to get the Customs House back from Rob, or buy a Jar and a Land Deed off somebody, and I'll have to pay fairly for it, because I won't beg and I won't owe. I'll keep my twenty back like I said, and I'll ask Rob straight out what he wants for it.
