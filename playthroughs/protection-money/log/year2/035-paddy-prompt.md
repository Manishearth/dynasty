## At the table since your last turn

Clark: "My Customs House opens at two again. Last year I lost three lots by a single grain apiece, and I have the receipts. This year the Official has a full purse and a long memory, so anyone planning to bid me up should know I've already written their name down."

Reina: "Well played, Rich. I mean it. One and a half years, and the jewel of the north is yours to enjoy. I'll keep the Pile honest and pay every harvest to the grain. And I'm bidding, everyone. I'd rather not, but I'm not going to pretend I don't want my chair back. Silke, you warned me and I didn't listen. I won't make that mistake twice."

Silke: "Here's the count, since Clark only writes it down. His Majesty has two hundred and twenty-two and a half, and a canal that waters only his own province. Down on the coast I have thirty-five, after Rob's courtesy call, Rich's 'usual five' and the crown's ten and a half. Paddy, Reina, I'm bidding plainly for a Customs House and a Bowl. Anyone who pushes those prices up is just filling the Pile."

Rob: "Your Majesty Rich, congratulations. I always said you had the bottom for that chair. Reina, my condolences, and welcome to Province 3. My man at H10 will be a very attentive neighbour. Buy whatever you like, all of you. I'm only here to watch."

Clark: "Reina, I like you, I truly do, but I've watched one set put an Emperor in the Pile this year already, and I won't watch a second one get built across the table from me. Sixteen apiece on the Jar and the Bowl. Rich, Your Majesty, you can thank me later, and the thanks should come in grain."

Reina: "Clark, write this down in your best hand: I'm not stopping at ten. Silke, Paddy, you'll each get your quarter, and I'm sorry it comes out of my purse and not out of the Gentry's quarrel with the crown."

The auctions, settled: Clark's Customs House goes to Reina at 9 (Silke and Paddy stopped at 8). Reina's Land Deed Scroll goes to Clark at 10 (Paddy stopped at 9). On the Dragon Jar and the Wooden Bowl, Reina raised to 11.5 and Clark to 16, so Clark wins both at 12.5 (Paddy stopped at 8 on the Jar, Silke at 7 on the Bowl). Rob passed on everything. Each seller keeps a quarter of the price.

Clark now holds a Land Deed Scroll, a Dragon Jar and a Wooden Bowl, the three pieces the Official's set needs, and 33 grain: a complete set. He may change places with anyone at any moment, the throne included. Reina holds the Customs House and 43 grain.

Clark: "A Scroll, a Jar and a Bowl, set well back from the sand, and thirty-three grain beside them. Your Majesty, the throne is quite safe for now. I'm a patient man and a reasonable one, and a reasonable Official is paid like one. Ten a year was the price for a man without a set. Shall we talk about the new rate?"

Reina: "M14, deep in the south of my new province, and Rob's attentive neighbour will need a long walk to admire it. Clark, that was very well done. Rich, you'd better count your cushions tonight. I know exactly how that goes."

Clark puts the Land Deed Scroll on I15, the Dragon Jar on I16 and the Wooden Bowl on J16, and holds his set for now. Reina puts the Customs House on M14.

Rob: "Reina, I hear M14 is lovely this time of year, so I've sent a fellow to stand just across the road at M15 and admire it. Five from you and five from Silke for the hospitality. Clark, Your Majesty, I'm not after anyone's throne. I'm just a man who likes to see the sights."

Rob marches: W1 onto I11, in Reina's Province 3, and W2 onto P20, in Silke's Province 5, raiding 5 from each. He hires a third soldier, W3, for 20 to the Harvest Pile, on M15, the desert square beside Reina's Customs House on M14; it moves from next year. His army is full at three.

Rich: "Clark, my most diligent servant, the realm has never had rolls so beautifully kept. Twenty-five for the Official and five for the heavens. A man of your quality is paid like one, and I'm proud to say so in front of everyone. Rob, enjoy the sights, though I'd remind you that M15 is a very long walk from my palace."

Salaries: Rich pays Clark 30, 25 as the Official and 5 for reading the I Ching, three times the 10 he declared. Clark now has 63 grain and still holds his complete set.
