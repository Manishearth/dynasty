move: rent 5 from each peasant; and: change places with Reina
say: "Your Majesty, I've adored every moment of your reign, truly I have, all one and a half years of it. But Rob has shown us all how quickly a pretty collection can be taken, and I'd hate to waste such a fine set. So I'll take the cushions Silke mentioned, with the deepest respect, and Reina, the Harvest Pile is yours, and it's in excellent order. Silke, Paddy, the usual five, and then you'll have a new Emperor who remembers his friends."
aside: "Rob raided me once and took me below twenty, and the tax is still to come, so a set I hold is a set I can lose. The chair came cheap, and I'm taking it while the price is right."
whisper: none

Ruling: legal. Rent 5 from Silke and Paddy first (as Gentry), then swap(3, 1): Rich holds the Customs House, Dragon Jar and Wooden Bowl and 62.5 grain. He takes the crown's reserve, 195, for 257.5; his three pieces return to the stock of Provinces 2, 5 and 6. Reina becomes the Gentry in Province 3, holding nothing, and draws 60 from the Harvest Pile.
