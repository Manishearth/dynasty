# Narration

The narrator's lines, chapter by chapter: the chapter title, each year's opening line, and the turning points, each tagged with the log number it follows. The narrator isn't part of the game, so none of this goes into `game.py` or the playthrough page; the page must make sense without it.

## Chapter 1

- Title: **Chapter One: Neat Handwriting.**

- (002) Year 1: The first year of Reina's reign. Sixty each, no card, and two soldiers set down where each one touches two provinces at once.
- (012) Four lots, four pieces, and none of them sold for more than ten. Silke and Rich each go home with two of their three.
- (013) The desert moves first: five from Rich, five from Silke, and a third soldier pitched below the rice fields. The army is full in its first year.
- (013) Year 2: The second year of the reign. Floods for Clark, Silke and the desert, a fat harvest for everyone else, and the Warlord reads the card that says so.
- (022) The Official's whisper lasts one auction step: the Warlord tells the table who sent it.
- (025) Every piece is sold. Two players hold full sets: one with 60 grain, one with 13. Clark, who sold the last Customs House, holds nothing.
- (026) Three soldiers, three provinces, fifteen grain, and still not one piece taken.

## Chapter 2

- Title: **Chapter Two: Straight Up the Steps.**

- (032) Year 3: The third year of Reina's reign. The seed rots everywhere at once, and three soldiers stand a step or two from three provinces' doors.
- (035) A lean year, a light tax and a new palace. Reina builds as if the throne were going nowhere.
- (038) Before the Winter Palace's mortar is dry, the Gentry climbs its steps. Rich takes the throne and the reserve of 92; Reina takes the granary, and three pieces go back into the stock.
- (042) Year 3 ends with a new Emperor, three soldiers one step from three pieces, and a Customs House, a Jar and a Bowl waiting for next year's auction.
- (043) Year 4: The first year of Rich's reign. An average harvest, an Emperor's Share with nothing to share, and half of every province's income for the crown.
- (052) Three lots, and the Customs House goes to Reina by one grain over Paddy. It is the second Customs House he has lost by a grain.
- (055) Before the Jar and the Bowl have settled on Clark's back row, Silke takes his seat, his purse and the squares he chose. Two seats change hands in two years, and both times the mover took a fuller purse than their own.
- (057) The desert takes its first piece. Paddy's Jar, kept from Clark for thirty grain a year ago, goes to T17 for nothing.
- (058) Year 4 ends with the Emperor at 200, the Warlord at 120, and a Customs House, a Jar and a Bowl back in stock.

## Chapter 3

- Title: **Chapter Three: A Chair So Keen to Be Sat In.**

- (065) Year 5: The second year of Rich's reign. The card asks the crown for two grain a head, and three pieces wait beside the board for three buyers who all want the same Jar.
- (076) Three lots, three buyers, and the Jar goes to Silke by one grain over Paddy. Paddy finally takes home a Customs House; he has now lost three pieces by a single grain.
- (077) Before the Jar has settled on G16, Silke climbs from the Official's book to the throne. Rich takes the book and sixty from the pile; the crown's 257 changes hands, and three more pieces go back into stock.
- (079) Year 5 ends with a third Emperor in three years, the Warlord at 147, and a Land Deed, a Jar and a Bowl back in stock.
- (080) Year 6: The first year of Silke's reign. Fat years and large families: the harvest is good, every mouth doubles, and the sea opens for sale.
- (087) Sixteen squares of new land in one afternoon, and the crown pays for half of everyone else's. Clark beats Paddy to the last square of sea by a moment, not a grain.
- (095) A Land Deed to Paddy at 23, a Bowl to Clark at 16, and a Jar to Reina at 41, which she turns straight into the throne she lost three years ago. Rich is the runner-up on all three lots. The throne has changed hands three times in four years, and the reserve is lighter each time.
- (097) Year 6 ends with the Warlord at 172, richer than anyone but the crown, and Paddy one Jar from a set with eight grain to buy it.

## Chapter 4

- Title: **Chapter Four: A Grain Past Careful.**

- (104) Year 7: The second year of Reina's second reign. Good times make careless people: a fat harvest, a small disaster for everyone, and the coast is suddenly the richest country in the kingdom.
- (111) Twenty squares of new land in one afternoon, and the crown pays for nearly all of them. By the last square the reserve that began the year at 258 is down to 34 and a half.
- (118) Thirteen for a Customs House, sixteen for a Bowl, and a Jar priced one grain above the purse of the only man who needs it most. Nobody buys the Jar.
- (119) Year 7 ends with the crown at fourteen and a half, the Warlord at 187, and two soldiers two steps from Clark's house and one two steps from Rich's Bowl.
- (120) Year 8: The third year of Reina's second reign. The seed rots, the coast's new land carries the kingdom anyway, and one Jar waits in the stock at two grain.
- (131) Forty-six for the Jar, one grain past Silke, and after three Jars lost by a single grain Paddy carries his set up the steps to the Gentry's chair. Silke goes down to the paddies with sixty from the pile. The sixth seat change of the reign.
- (133) Year 8 ends with the Warlord at 192, a soldier one step from Rich's Bowl, another one step from Clark's Customs House, and a Land Deed back in the stock that Clark needs to finish his set.

## Chapter 5

- Title: **Chapter Five: Two Scrolls in the Sand.**

- (140) Year 9: The fourth year of Reina's second reign. The harvest is good and the families are large again: four hundred million mouths, and every field in the kingdom must now feed twice what it fed last year.
- (151) One Scroll, one serious bidder. Thirty-one for Paddy's Land Deed, a grain past Silke, and Clark, who has furnished four other people's sets, finally finishes his own.
- (152) And with the Scroll barely on the sand of O26, Clark carries his set back up to the Official's desk he lost in Year 4. Rich goes down to the coast with sixty from the pile. The eighth seat change of the game.
- (155) Year 9 ends with the reserve at nothing, the Warlord at 172, a fresh Wall above the Empress's province, and a soldier standing one step from both of Clark's pieces.

- (156) Year 10: The fifth year of Reina's second reign, and the first with an empty treasury. The card promises more children; the kingdom has already had all it is allowed, so it gets only the good harvest.
- (166) Sixty-one for the Customs House and thirty-four for the Bowl, both to the man who came to the coast last year with nine grain. Paddy loses a lot by one grain for the fourth time; the Jar stays in the sand at a hundred.
- (169) And the second Scroll of the kingdom goes west into the sand, beside the first. Two names left on the Warlord's list, and both of the pieces one of them needs are his.
- (170) Year 10 ends with the reader's pay doubled to twenty, both Land Deeds in the desert, and the Warlord two names from the throne. Four hundred grain of works are still owed.

## Chapter 6

- Title: **Chapter Six: Grain Doesn't Sit in a Chair.**

- (177) Year 11: The sixth year of Reina's second reign. A utility tax, an average harvest, and four hundred million mouths; for the first time in ten years, nobody has anything left to auction.
- (178) The average year does what the bad ones couldn't. Paddy's fields cost him eighty-five, and the Customs House and the Jar he spent four tries on go back to the pile at two grain each.
- (180) Eight links of Wall in one afternoon, and the kingdom owes one work: an eighty-grain Reservoir in the province of the only man at the table who is in debt.
- (191) Two for the Customs House, a hundred and thirteen for the Jar. Silke loses her sixth piece by a single grain, this time to a man who has no use for it.
- (192) And Rich's Customs House goes west into the sand, beside two Scrolls and a Jar. One name left on the Warlord's list, and her only piece is two rounds from his nearest man.
- (192) Year 11 ends with the reserve at twenty-two, the Gentry in debt, and a single work owed that ends the game the moment it is built.

- (193) Year 12: The seventh year of Reina's second reign, and perhaps the last of anyone's. A drought, an average harvest, and one eighty-grain Reservoir between the table and the end of the game.
- (195) The Empress declares the last stone in China. The game would end in the works step, one march before the Warlord reaches the last name on his list.
- (196) And with the tax barely counted, the Official calls a revolt on the Gentry's debt. The Empress pays the debt in the same breath; four players have already agreed.
- (203) The count: the most grain to Rich, the most soldiers and the most friends to Rob. Twelve years after he stood two soldiers on the Wall road, the Warlord is acclaimed Emperor, and Reina goes west to the desert with his army.
- (204) Rob lays the last stone himself. Every work the kingdom owes is standing, and the reign of the fifth Emperor of the game lasts exactly one afternoon.
