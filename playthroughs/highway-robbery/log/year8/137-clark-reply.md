diary: Years 7 and 8. The crown paid for seven squares of my sea and half of five more, so Province 5 now brings in more on a bad harvest than most of this table brings in on a good one. I've got a hundred and seventy-two grain, and I finally stopped spending it on other people's thrones. I bought Rich's Customs House for thirteen and set it on P23 beside the Bowl, and I'm one Scroll from a set. Then I watched my own Jar go to Paddy for forty-six. I took eleven and a half for my trouble, and the Jar made him the Gentry, which makes four thrones and chairs my pottery has furnished. That's the same lesson for the third time, and the page it's on is getting crowded. Paddy's Scroll is back in Province 3's stock for next year, and it's the one piece I need, so this time I bid like a man with a hundred and seventy grain and nothing left to wait for. Rob has two men one step from my Customs House, and he warned me in public. The book records that as manners, and it doesn't make me forgive him. Silke is a peasant now with sixty grain, so her page gets a footnote: the landlord who wanted twenty from me is now my neighbour on the coast. When the Scroll comes, I'll take whichever chair is weakest, and I don't plan to spend a grain making anybody else's set easier.
