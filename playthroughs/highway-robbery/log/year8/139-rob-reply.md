diary: Two years, and the stars were rude to me twice. First a disaster that kept my men out of three provinces, then a rotten harvest that took twenty out of my own purse. I still came out at a hundred and ninety-two, the richest man at the table, the Empress included, and I still haven't paid a grain of tax or bought a single bowl. Reina keeps paying me ten a year to read cards for her, and I keep reading them aloud and riding anyway. Meanwhile the chairs went round again. Paddy finally got his Jar on the fourth try, climbed into Silke's seat and left her in his paddies, which is funny until you notice the problem. Silke's pieces are back in stock, so she's another name on my list with nothing on the board to take. Paddy, a man I've already finished with, is one Bowl from the throne. Still, look where my men are standing. W1 is one step from Rich's Bowl in his "far corner where nobody's boots go", and W2 is one step from Clark's new Customs House with W3 right behind. Next march I take both, and that leaves Silke as my last name. After that W1 goes north to Paddy's quiet corner, because I didn't spend eight years waiting to watch a rice farmer sit in my chair.
