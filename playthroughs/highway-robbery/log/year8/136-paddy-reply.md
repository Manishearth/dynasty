diary: I got the Jar. Clark opened it at sixty-two the first year to keep it off me, nobody would pay that, and so it came round again at two. I'd saved all year by then, and I took it for forty-six with Silke a grain behind at forty-five. So it was one grain once more, only this time the grain went my way. The crown paid for my sea land, the whole of it the first year and half the next, and by the end my paddies ran right out into the water at the corner, earning nearly two hundred a year. I'd have liked to keep farming them, but I didn't sit down tonight to stay a peasant. So I traded seats with Silke. I'm the Gentry now, with her fifty-nine and my seventy-two, a hundred and thirty-one in all and the Harvest Pile to run, and I feel a bit sorry about it and a bit giddy too. The Scroll had to go back into Province 3's stock, and my Customs House and Jar sit side by side at M9 and M10, about as far from Rob's lads as the north-west goes. The trouble is that these new fields earn nothing on their own, minus five in an average year, so the rent and the pile will have to feed me now. I need a Wooden Bowl and twenty grain, and then any seat at the table is mine to ask for, even the throne. I've no idea how a rice farmer ends up talking about thrones, but I'll bid a grain past careful and find out.
