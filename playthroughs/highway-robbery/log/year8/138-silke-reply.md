diary: Two years ago I said I'd count Paddy's grain every year until I could buy my way back up. I counted it, all right, out loud and to the grain, and he bought a Jar for forty-six with me one grain behind him, then took my chair and my purse to go with it. I lost the Customs House by a grain and the Bowl by a grain, I asked for extra rent four times and got none, and the bad harvest took thirty off fields that never grew anything. I spent the whole two years trying to keep Paddy down, and that was the mistake. I should have been building my own set. So now I'm the Rice Peasant, with sixty grain and nothing on the board, and the strange part is that it's the best land I've ever stood on: two canal sections and sea out to the corner, and it'll be a hundred and ninety-five a year in an average year. Paddy left it in fine order, curse him, and he's sitting in my old corner one Bowl short of a set, with his pieces on M9 and M10 where Rob's men will take years to reach them. I pay rent to him now, and I'll pay the five and not a grain more, exactly as he taught me. From here I bank every harvest, buy pieces cheap and out of Rob's reach, and climb. This time I won't be watching anybody but myself.
