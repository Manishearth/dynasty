## At the table since your last turn

Reina: "Fifty percent and a canal in Paddy's paddies. Rich, you've learned in four minutes what took me two years: build first and explain later. The auctions settle. Clark's Customs House: Reina 39, Paddy stopping at 38. Silke's Dragon Jar and Paddy's Wooden Bowl: Clark 26 each, Reina stopping at 25 on both. Silke and Rob bid on nothing.
