## At the table since your last turn

Reina: "Fifty percent and a canal in Paddy's paddies. Rich, you've learned in four minutes what took me two years: build first and explain later. I'll be bidding on all three of these, friends, and I'll say so plainly. Clark, you and I both want the Jar and the Bowl, and I'd rather not feed three quarters of our purses to the pile fighting over them. If you'd like to talk about splitting them, I'm listening, in public, before the gavel."

Silke: "Eighteen and a half in my purse, friends. I did count it, and so did Rob, twice. I'm not bidding on anything. My Jar opens at six, and that's cheap for the last Jar in the kingdom. Reina, you've got eighty-seven and a half and nothing on the board, so I'd think it's worth six to you. Paddy, love, that Customs House is the one piece you're missing, and Reina's after it too. Mind the bidding doesn't send most of your forty-nine to the pile."

Rob: "Nothing for me, friends. I've never paid for a piece in my life and I don't mean to start now. Go on, bid each other into the dirt. Whatever you buy today, I'll pick up later for nothing. Reina, Clark: when you've settled who gets the Jar and the Bowl, put them somewhere I can find them."

The auctions settle. Clark's Customs House: Reina 39, Paddy stopping at 38. Silke's Dragon Jar and Paddy's Wooden Bowl: Clark 26 each, Reina stopping at 25 on both. Silke and Rob bid on nothing.

Reina: "Thirty-nine, and twenty-nine of it straight to the pile. Paddy, I'm sorry. I'd have taken your word if a word could keep you out of my seat for long, but a Customs House keeps you out for certain. Clark, congratulations on the Jar and the Bowl. You did say you wouldn't lose them by a grain, and you were exactly as good as your word. Rob, I'm putting this one deep in the north-west, where you'll need a long walk and a good map."

Clark: "Twenty-six apiece, and a grain ahead of the Empress-that-was on both. I'll call that the debt repaid, with interest. Rob, you asked where to put them. Back row of Province 2, as far from your boots as the map goes. Come and find them if you fancy the walk."

Silke: "Twenty-five grain and a full set, friends, and I counted it twice, the way Clark taught us. Clark, love, you spent fifty-two today on a Jar and a Bowl to get ahead of me by a grain. I'll take the book, the seat, and your purse with it. Rich, I'll collect for you just as carefully as he did, and I'll want my ten a year as well."

Silke changes seats with Clark and becomes the Official. She takes Clark's 55.5, for 80.5. Her Customs House goes back to Province 2's stock; she keeps her Land Deed and Wooden Bowl, to be placed in Province 2. Clark's new Jar and Bowl go back to the stock of Provinces 5 and 6. Clark becomes the Silk Peasant in Province 5, holding nothing, and draws 60. The returned pieces are auctioned next year.

Silke: "Back row of Province 2, as far from the Wall as the map goes. Clark, love, you picked the spot, I'm only borrowing it. Rob, it's a long walk from I11 and I'll be counting your steps. Now, one Dragon Jar and I'm done, and I've got eighty and a half grain to pay for it."
