move: Change seats: Silke takes the Official's seat from Clark.

say: "Twenty-five grain and a full set, friends, and I counted it twice, the way Clark taught us. Clark, love, you spent fifty-two today on a Jar and a Bowl to get ahead of me by a grain. I'll take the book, the seat, and your purse with it. Rich, I'll collect for you just as carefully as he did, and I'll want my ten a year as well."

aside: "Clark had the biggest purse of the three I could replace, and he'd just bought two pieces that put him close to a set of his own. Now those pieces go back to stock and he's starting again from sixty. I've climbed to the upper class before Paddy, which was the plan from the start."

whisper: none

Ruling: legal (full set, 25 grain; a peasant may replace the Official). swap(5, 2): Silke takes Clark's 55.5, for 80.5. Her Customs House, native to Province 2, goes back to Province 2's stock; she keeps her Land Deed and Wooden Bowl and places them in Province 2 (asked next). Clark returns his Jar and Bowl to the stock of Provinces 5 and 6, moves to Province 5 as the Silk Peasant, holding nothing, and draws 60. No bought soldiers. Returned pieces are auctioned next year. Her new seat needs a Land Deed, a Dragon Jar and a Wooden Bowl; she holds two. "Clark had the biggest purse of the three": Clark 55.5, Paddy 55.5, Reina 48.5, so tied with Paddy; not worth a note.
