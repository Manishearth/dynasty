diary: One grain again. I lost the first Customs House to Rich at twenty-six to twenty-five, and now I've lost the second to Reina at thirty-nine to thirty-eight, so I'm starting to think somebody up there can't stand the sight of me. I turned Clark's thirty for the Jar down to keep my set together, and then Rob walked in the very next year and carried it off to his sand at T17 for nothing, so I've the Deed left and not much else. It did cost me a little less than it might have. I've fifty-five and a half in the purse, more than I've ever had, Rich's canal pays me ten more a year from now on, and next year's block has a Customs House and a Dragon Jar on it, both pieces I need. The lesson I'm taking is that a careful man still loses by a grain, so next year I'll bid a grain past careful. And I'll put whatever I win down in the bottom corner of my paddies, where Rob's lads will need a good long walk to reach it.
