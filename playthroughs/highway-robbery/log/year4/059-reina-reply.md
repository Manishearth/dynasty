diary: I wrote that I was watching Rich, and I watched him take the throne in a single polite sentence, along with the ninety-two I'd saved for the Winter Palace. I didn't even get to lay its first stone. The canals I dug now feed his harvest, and he's already digging one of his own in Paddy's paddies, which tells me he understands the throne better than I'd like. As Gentry I've been honest with the pile and gentle with the rent, and I overpaid for a Customs House, thirty-nine grain, mostly to keep Paddy from finishing his set. That was the right call and an expensive one, and I'm down to forty-eight and a half. Silke did to Clark exactly what Rich did to me, which proves that patience and a purse are worth more at this table than any promise. Next year the Jar and the Bowl I need both come back to the block, and Silke wants that Jar with eighty grain behind her, so I'll have to make a deal or outbid her. Once I hold all three, the throne is the seat I'll take back.
