Clark: "My Customs House opens at fifteen, Reina, since you'll be wanting it and I like the look of a fair start. As for the Jar and the Bowl, I've lost both of them by a single grain before, and both are in the book with the date. I don't intend to lose one by a grain again."

The auctions settle. Clark's Customs House: Reina 39, Paddy stopping at 38. Silke's Dragon Jar and Paddy's Wooden Bowl: Clark 26 each, Reina stopping at 25 on both. Silke and Rob bid on nothing.
