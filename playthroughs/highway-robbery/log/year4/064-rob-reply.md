diary: Two years, and the chairs at this table have changed hands three times. I didn't have to touch one of them. Rich walked up Reina's steps, Reina outbid Paddy into the pile, and Silke took Clark's book and his purse while Clark was still counting his crockery. I sat and watched, and loved every minute of it. My own takings were steadier: fifteen in raids each march, a salary from whichever Emperor wanted the stars kind to him, and at last my first piece, Paddy's Jar, standing in my sand at T17 exactly as I'd promised. I'm up to a hundred and twenty grain, I still haven't paid a grain of tax or bought a single bowl, and only the Emperor is richer than me now. The worry is Silke, who's one Jar short of climbing again with eighty-odd in her purse. W1 is three steps from her Scroll and Bowl, so that's where my next visit goes. After that it's Rich's turn. He's grown fat on that chair, and every palace I burn costs him twenty.
