diary: Two years ago I had eight grain and a set I couldn't use. Tonight I'm the Official with eighty-five and a half grain and Clark's book in my lap. I kept my purse shut through every auction and let Reina, Clark and Paddy throw more than a hundred grain at the pile. Then I took Clark's seat, and his fifty-odd grain with it, the same afternoon he'd paid fifty-two for a Jar and a Bowl to get ahead of me by a grain. It cost me my Customs House, but I've climbed before Paddy, and he's lost his Jar to Rob's sand, so he's further down than he was when we started. I'm short one piece now, a Dragon Jar, and there's one in stock at Province 5. That's Clark's stock now, and he'll want a good price for it, but I can outbid anyone at this table except Rich and Rob. Rob's man on I13 is three rounds from my back row, so the Jar has to be on the board, and I have to use the set, before he gets there.
