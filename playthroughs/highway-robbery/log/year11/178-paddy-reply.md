move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "Rich, Silke, it's five each, same as always, and I'll not pretend I'm in a position to ask for more. Though if either of you fancied making it fifteen, with seven hundred and fifty and five hundred in your sacks, I'd remember it a long while. I sold my Jar and my Customs House back to the pile for two grain each tonight, the two pieces it took me four tries and a grain past careful to get, so I know exactly what being careful costs."
aside: "Ten grain of rent takes me from twenty-nine and a half under to nineteen and a half under, and that's not out of the hole, so unless one of those two is feeling generous I'll be watching my own Jar and Customs House go up for auction with my hands tied. Rich made six hundred and thirty-five this harvest alone, and I paid every grain of it to him out of my own pile, so now we'll see if he's kind or only honest."
whisper: none

---

Ruling: rent 5 each, paid now; the hope for fifteen is a request (105): the extra 10 each is paid only if a peasant agrees at their next turn. The figures in the say and aside are right (-29.5 to -19.5).
