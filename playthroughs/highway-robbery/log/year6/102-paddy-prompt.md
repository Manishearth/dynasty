## At the table since your last turn

Clark: "Sixteen for a Bowl, a grain past Rich, and forty-one for my Jar, Reina, so the quarter coming back to me is ten. Thank you both for being so generous to a humble silk farmer. Rob, it sits down by my new squares on the far shore, so W2 will be wading."

Reina: "Silke, darling, you did tell us that patience and a purse beat any promise, and I took notes. I'll have the throne back, with thanks for keeping it warm. The Gentry's chair is a lovely one; I've left the Harvest Pile in perfect order. And Rob, you won't need to walk to my Bowl now: it's going back to the stock, so your W1 can stay where it is."

Paddy puts the Scroll on S24 and Clark the Bowl on P26. Reina puts the Jar on M13, then, holding a full set and 37 grain, changes seats with Silke and takes the throne back, with the crown's reserve of 165.5: 202.5 in all. Rob marches: W1 raids J13 in Rich's Province 2, W2 raids M25 in Clark's Province 5, W3 raids R19 in Paddy's Province 6, 5 from each. Nobody else has a soldier to move.
