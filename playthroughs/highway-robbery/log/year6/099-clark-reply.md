diary: Years 5 and 6. I sold my own Jar twice and pocketed seventeen and a half for the trouble, and both times the Jar went straight into a set that took the throne, first Silke's and then Reina's. So the kingdom now has had three Emperors in two years, and I've been the one supplying the pottery. Two promises go under "kept": Rich's canal and Silke's, so Province 5 now has two sections and a harvest that, by the engine's count, outruns everyone else below the crown. I also bought four squares of sea at half price, which is the cheapest leverage I've ever had. I lost the Customs House to Paddy by a grain and the Scroll to him by five, so his "honest bidding" gets a page in the book of its own, but I took the Bowl from Rich at sixteen and set it on P26, which Rob tells me W2 can reach in three. Paddy is now one Jar from a set with eight grain to his name, and the Jar is back in my province's stock, so next year I set its minimum and he can't afford it. Rich is the Official with an empty board, two pieces short of me and with forty grain more, and I mean to have his seat. I need a Customs House and a Scroll, I need the Bowl to survive Rob's walk, and I need to stop spending my grain on other people's thrones.
