diary: Two more years, and two more Emperors. Silke took the chair from Rich, then Reina took it from Silke a year later, and each of them paid the Pile handsomely for the privilege while I sat on my hands at every auction. I'm at a hundred and seventy-two grain now, the richest man at the table outside the crown, and I still haven't paid a grain of tax or bought one bowl. I carried Paddy's Scroll into my sand, which keeps him one piece short and down to eight grain, and my men have raided three provinces a march without anyone lifting a sword at them. The annoying part is that every time I ride at someone's set, they climb before I get there and their pieces go back into stock, so Rich and Silke are both on my list with nothing on the board for me to take. Reina doubled my salary to ten and called it the price of a quiet road. Sweet of her, but I've never been a man who stays bought. Next comes Clark's Bowl, three steps from W2 and as good as mine. After that it's the Winter Palace, and twenty out of Reina's reserve for every one I burn. The throne's had three owners in two years, and I mean to be the fourth.
