move: land M26, M27, M28, M29, M30, N30, O30, P30, Q30; I pay 90, asking the crown for 90
say: "Nine squares, Your Majesty, a hundred and eighty in all, ninety from me and ninety from the crown, which is half to the grain. I've written down that you said it was half and not a grain more, so neither of us has to remember it. And Paddy, you'll have your extra fifteen, the whole twenty, under 'goodwill toward the man holding the Scroll'. Rob, count my bowls whenever you like. I've only got the one, and I count it every night."
aside: "The crown pays half of every square, and every square earns its whole price back next year, so that's free money with a palace stamp on it. I've still got two hundred and thirty-odd left after this, enough to outbid anyone here for Paddy's Scroll, or Rob's Deed if he's selling."
whisper: To Rob: Your Deed on O18 is no use to you sitting on the sand. I'll give you sixty for it now, privately, and I'll set it out of your soldiers' reach so neither of us has to argue about it again.

---

Ruling: nine squares, 90 from Clark and 90 from the crown. He pays Paddy the extra 15 of rent (a deal). The whisper to Rob is filed for Rob only; Rob answers it at his next turn. Paddy is the seller of the Scroll in stock, not its holder; a fair description, no note.
