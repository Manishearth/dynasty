## Paddy's diary so far, in his own words

## Setup–Year 2

Two years in, and I've a Land Deed and a Dragon Jar sitting up in the top corner of my paddies, which is more than I'd have bet on when I sat down with thirty-seven grain. The floods were good to the south-east, and I spent nearly all of it in one night: twenty for the Deed, fifteen for the Jar, and I lost the last Customs House to Rich by a single grain at twenty-six, which I'll be chewing on for a while. That leaves me one piece short, and there are no more in the stock, but there are two Customs Houses on the board, Rich's and Silke's, and both of them have their full sets already. So one of them might part with a spare for the right price, or for a friend's word, and Silke owes me a kindness for that Bowl. Rob's man is camped at S20 and already took five off me. If he walks two squares further, my little collection is in trouble, so next year I need to make a deal for that Customs House before the Warlord makes his house call.

## Years 3–4

One grain again. I lost the first Customs House to Rich at twenty-six to twenty-five, and now I've lost the second to Reina at thirty-nine to thirty-eight, so I'm starting to think somebody up there can't stand the sight of me. I turned Clark's thirty for the Jar down to keep my set together, and then Rob walked in the very next year and carried it off to his sand at T17 for nothing, so I've the Deed left and not much else. It did cost me a little less than it might have. I've fifty-five and a half in the purse, more than I've ever had, Rich's canal pays me ten more a year from now on, and next year's block has a Customs House and a Dragon Jar on it, both pieces I need. The lesson I'm taking is that a careful man still loses by a grain, so next year I'll bid a grain past careful. And I'll put whatever I win down in the bottom corner of my paddies, where Rob's lads will need a good long walk to reach it.

## Years 5–6

I finally won one. Silke's Customs House went to me at twenty-five, a grain past Clark, and the next year I took the Land Deed back at twenty-three, a grain past Rich, so going a grain past careful did what I hoped it would. Rob's lad still carried my old Scroll off to the desert, and I lost the Jar to Silke by a single grain, which makes three of those now, but my new pieces sit side by side at S24 and S25, about as far from his soldiers as my paddies go. I spent nearly everything to get here. Thirty went on three squares of land and ten on the fourth, with the crown paying half, and the Deed took most of the rest, so I'm down to eight grain and a five-grain toll to Rob. Still, the land and the canal should bring in eighty-five in an average year. The thrones keep changing hands, Silke's, then Reina's again, and each time a Dragon Jar goes back in the stock. One is sitting there for Year 7, and it's the only piece I'm missing. Next year I'll have a full purse for it, and if I can keep twenty grain on top, I might be sitting at the upper table before I've quite worked out how I got there.

## Years 7–8

I got the Jar. Clark opened it at sixty-two the first year to keep it off me, nobody would pay that, and so it came round again at two. I'd saved all year by then, and I took it for forty-six with Silke a grain behind at forty-five. So it was one grain once more, only this time the grain went my way. The crown paid for my sea land, the whole of it the first year and half the next, and by the end my paddies ran right out into the water at the corner, earning nearly two hundred a year. I'd have liked to keep farming them, but I didn't sit down tonight to stay a peasant. So I traded seats with Silke. I'm the Gentry now, with her fifty-nine and my seventy-two, a hundred and thirty-one in all and the Harvest Pile to run, and I feel a bit sorry about it and a bit giddy too. The Scroll had to go back into Province 3's stock, and my Customs House and Jar sit side by side at M9 and M10, about as far from Rob's lads as the north-west goes. The trouble is that these new fields earn nothing on their own, minus five in an average year, so the rent and the pile will have to feed me now. I need a Wooden Bowl and twenty grain, and then any seat at the table is mine to ask for, even the throne. I've no idea how a rice farmer ends up talking about thrones, but I'll bid a grain past careful and find out.
