diary: Two years ago I said I'd add two grain to every limit, and I did. I set a hundred and twelve on the Jar and Rich paid a hundred and thirteen for a pot he didn't need, which makes six pieces lost by a single grain. The two extra grain made no difference, because Rich was willing to go to three hundred and fifty-one. I got a Customs House for two grain, the cheapest thing I ever bought, and Rob's man was two steps from it before the paint dried. Then Clark called his revolt, I said yes and claimed the throne, and I found out that grain doesn't buy friends. Reina, Clark and Paddy all stood behind Rob, nobody stood behind me, and Rob laid the last stone in China before I could spend a single grain of my six hundred and seventy-five. I sat down at this table a peasant on the coast and I get up a peasant on the coast, with more grain than anyone but Rich and one Customs House on the board. The lesson isn't the two grain. You don't win a revolt with grain or a set, you win it with friends, and I spent twelve years counting everyone's purse out loud when I should have been making friends.
