diary: I built the whole Wall in a single year, eight links and three hundred and twenty grain, and then I set out to end the game with one last stone laid for Paddy. I forgot the one thing a builder should never forget: a debt left standing is a cause. Clark saw it before I did, I paid Paddy's ninety-four and a half a breath too late, and the table pulled me off the throne before the Reservoir went in. I made the best of the fall by backing Rob, and the bandit I'd paid twenty a year for ten years took the chair with my friendship, Clark's and Paddy's, and spent my reserve on the very Reservoir I'd planned. So the game ends with every work in China standing, nearly all of them built by me, and I'm the Warlord with sixty grain and three soldiers I never wanted. By the rules a seat that low counts as a win, and I'll take it with good grace. But I'll remember that every bandit has a price, and that a Gentry in debt has one too, and his comes due first.
