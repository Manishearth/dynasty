aside: The Reservoir is the last work owed, so the moment it goes in the game ends, with me on the throne and Rich's nine hundred grain still in his sack. I said I came for the throne and meant to keep it, and the quickest way to keep a chair is to end the game while you're sitting in it.

Ruling: the crown builds the Province 3 Reservoir alone (80), the last work owed, so the game ends at once, in the works step: survival. Every seat had its one turn to respond to the declared last work earlier this round (the revolt round, Director's precedent), so there's no second round. Nothing after the works step is reached: no land is bought, no auction, no march, and no salaries (Clark's 20 is never paid). "Four Emperors in two years" is a flourish, not a claim about the board (Rob is the fifth reign of the game: Reina, Rich, Silke, Reina, Rob).
