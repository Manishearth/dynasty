diary: Two years, and I finally got to be the fourth Emperor in three, which in this kingdom is practically a dynasty. Rich's Customs House came into my sand the year after I said it would. W3 was two steps from Silke's new house when Clark called his revolt, and the funny thing is that the Empress paid me twenty a year to keep her safe and then backed me for her own chair. I read Clark's whisper out to the table the way I read his first one in Year 1, he backed me anyway, and Paddy followed the Empress, so three friends and three soldiers beat nine hundred and seventy-five grain. It cost me every piece in the desert, the two Scrolls, the Jar and the Customs House, all back to stock, plus the Scroll I'd promised Clark and couldn't sell him. It also cost me Silke, the last name on my list, whom I never did collect. Then I laid Paddy's Reservoir myself, eighty from the crown, and ended the game with me sitting in the chair. The others can argue over what a Warlord on the throne counts for in the rulebook. I said at the start I'd have the throne, and I named every target before I rode. Nothing comes next. That's the point of finishing it.
