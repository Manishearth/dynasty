diary: I said I'd find the figure that makes the Warlord sell, and the figure turned out to be a throne, which wasn't mine to offer. Rob took my Customs House off L25 before I could even ask his price. I paid a hundred and thirteen for a Jar I didn't need, just so Silke wouldn't finish her set, and I'd do it again, because seven pieces lost by one grain would have been one too many for her and one too many for my conscience. Then Clark raised his revolt, and I offered to clear Paddy's debt to the grain. Reina cleared it first, Paddy paid what he owed, and the friends went to the only man with soldiers. So I end the game with nine hundred and seventy-five grain, the fattest purse in China, and not one person who would stand beside it, which is the lesson of twelve years in a single count. And yet the rules say a man who drew the Gentry and finishes on the coast has won by going down, so the coast Clark threw me onto has paid me one last time. I kept every promise I made, and I never once got to sit in the chair I was after.
