diary: Years 11 and 12, and that's the end of the book. I stopped at fifty-two for the Jar, and Rich paid a hundred and thirteen for it only so Silke couldn't have it, which is how the rich play and why I'm not one of them. Then I did the cleverest thing I've done in twelve years: I called the revolt, I was the only vote that counted, and I sold that vote for a Scroll and a doubled salary. I spent it on a man who'd read my whisper aloud twice. The Scroll went back to stock the moment he sat down, so he couldn't sell it to me, and he laid the last stone in China himself before I could collect the salary either. That's five thrones and chairs I've furnished now, and this one I furnished with the throne itself, so it goes under "lessons", in capitals, with a note that Rob kept every promise he made in public and not one of the ones he made to me. I finished where I started, the Official with eighty-six and a half and an honest count, which the rules call a hold and I call twelve years of setting other people's prices. Paddy backed me when I hadn't claimed anything, so the "friends" page has one entry, and I mean to keep it. The throne is still the only chair I haven't sat in, and by my count I'm the one who handed it to Rob.
