## At the table since your last turn

Paddy: "Empress, you've been kinder to me than I've any right to, and it's a lovely Reservoir, but it's a lovely Reservoir to be buried under. I'm ninety-four and a half under, and that's a cause, so I'll say yes, and I'm sorry. Clark said it out loud when nobody else would, so if it comes to counting friends, he's got me."

Rich: "Empress, I've bowed to you every year and I'd bow again, but a crown that lets its Gentry sink ninety-four under hasn't kept its promise to Province 3, and a promise to a province outlasts whoever is living in it. So I agree there's a cause, and if it comes to counting, I claim the throne, and I'll say my terms out loud so they go in Clark's ink: any man who backs me, Paddy first, has his debt paid off by my own hand the day I'm acclaimed, and the Official and the Warlord keep their salaries to the grain."

Silke: "Agreed. Ninety-four and a half under is a cause by any count, and I can count. Empress, you bought the last stone in China to end the game with me holding one Customs House and six-hundred-and-seventy-five grain I never got to spend. So I'm claiming the throne. Clark, Paddy, I've held that seat once already and I paid half of everybody's land while I sat on it, so back me and you'll get the same this time."

Rob: "I agree, the cause is plain: the Gentry is ninety-four and a half under and the crown wants to bury him under a Reservoir. And in keeping with tradition, here's Clark's whisper read out to the room: sixty for my Scroll and his backing for whoever I pick. I'll take the backing, Clark, and I pick me. I'm the only one at this table with a single soldier on the board, so the soldiers count is mine. Rich has the grain, but grain doesn't sit in a chair. Friends decide it, so anyone who'd rather not have my men on their doorstep next year knows where to stand."

Reina gives Paddy 94.5 from the reserve, clearing his debt; the gift stands, but it came in the same round as the other answers and doesn't undo them. The revolt carries: Paddy, Rich, Silke and Rob agree with Clark. Reina is deposed and Chaos begins: no tax, salaries or works until an acclamation, so the Reservoir isn't built. Reina stays in Province 1; her 289.5 is the reserve the new Emperor takes. Claimants: Rich, Silke, Rob. Backing so far: Reina backs Rob; Paddy backs Clark, who hasn't claimed.

Clark: "Well, look at that. Six of us in the room and the only vote that decides it is the Official's. Rob, you read my whisper out, so I'll read your answer back so it's in ink: the Scroll at M15 to me at sixty the day you sit down, and my salary doubled to twenty. On those terms I back Rob. That gives him the soldiers and the friends, and Rich gets the grain, which, as Rob says, doesn't sit in a chair. And Rob, I'll offer the same again, publicly: if you'll throw in the Jar on T17, my last thirty is yours for that as well. Paddy, thank you. You're the first entry on the 'friends' page in twelve years, and I'll not forget it."

Clark claims nothing and backs Rob, on Rob's terms (the M15 Scroll at 60 once Rob sits down, the Official's salary doubled to 20). Friends so far: Rob 2 (Reina, Clark); Rich and Silke none. Paddy's backing of Clark lapses, since Clark doesn't claim.

Paddy: "Empress, you cleared every grain of my debt in one breath, and I'll not forget it as long as I live, and you've backed Rob, so I'll stand where you're standing. Rob, I'm backing you, and all I ask in Clark's ink is that your lads leave Province 3 be, since there's nothing left up there but me. Rich, Silke, thank you both for the offers, truly, but I was always taught you pay what you owe first, and the Empress just paid mine."

Paddy backs Rob. The count: most grain Rich (975.5); most soldiers Rob (3); most friends Rob (3: Reina, Clark, Paddy). Rob leads two of three and is acclaimed Emperor. Everything he held goes back to stock (the Dragon Jar, both Land Deed Scrolls, the Customs House), for next year's auction. He takes Reina's 289.5 as the reserve. Reina is now the Warlord, with 60 from the pile and the army of three where it stands (W1 I14, W2 L25, W3 Q23), and a fresh objective. This year's tax was collected before the revolt and stays in the reserve.
