## Setup–Year 2

Two years in, I'm the richest man at the table after the Empress, and I haven't paid a grain of tax or bought a single trinket. The others paid the Harvest Pile for crockery while I collected twenty-five in raids and spent twenty on a third soldier. The best moment was Clark's whisper. He wanted me to knife Rich for him in secret, so I read his offer out to the whole table, and now those two can watch each other instead of me. Rich has his set and the grain to move up, and that worries me. Silke has her set but only eight grain, and my man on her doorstep keeps it that way. Next year my men get close enough to take pieces off the board, starting with Paddy's Jar, Silke's Deed and Rich's Jar. Once Rich's set is broken, nobody at this table climbs faster than I ride.

## Years 3–4

Two years, and the chairs at this table have changed hands three times. I didn't have to touch one of them. Rich walked up Reina's steps, Reina outbid Paddy into the pile, and Silke took Clark's book and his purse while Clark was still counting his crockery. I sat and watched, and loved every minute of it. My own takings were steadier: fifteen in raids each march, a salary from whichever Emperor wanted the stars kind to him, and at last my first piece, Paddy's Jar, standing in my sand at T17 exactly as I'd promised. I'm up to a hundred and twenty grain, I still haven't paid a grain of tax or bought a single bowl, and only the Emperor is richer than me now. The worry is Silke, who's one Jar short of climbing again with eighty-odd in her purse. W1 is three steps from her Scroll and Bowl, so that's where my next visit goes. After that it's Rich's turn. He's grown fat on that chair, and every palace I burn costs him twenty.

## Years 5–6

Two more years, and two more Emperors. Silke took the chair from Rich, then Reina took it from Silke a year later, and each of them paid the Pile handsomely for the privilege while I sat on my hands at every auction. I'm at a hundred and seventy-two grain now, the richest man at the table outside the crown, and I still haven't paid a grain of tax or bought one bowl. I carried Paddy's Scroll into my sand, which keeps him one piece short and down to eight grain, and my men have raided three provinces a march without anyone lifting a sword at them. The annoying part is that every time I ride at someone's set, they climb before I get there and their pieces go back into stock, so Rich and Silke are both on my list with nothing on the board for me to take. Reina doubled my salary to ten and called it the price of a quiet road. Sweet of her, but I've never been a man who stays bought. Next comes Clark's Bowl, three steps from W2 and as good as mine. After that it's the Winter Palace, and twenty out of Reina's reserve for every one I burn. The throne's had three owners in two years, and I mean to be the fourth.
