move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "Silke, Paddy, my dears, just the customary five apiece, the smallest rent in the Middle Kingdom, and the Harvest Pile pays you back many times over. Your Majesty, the gentry of the north-west are, as ever, your most humble servants. Rob, if you're riding on Clark first, I'll keep a lamp lit for him."
aside: "Five from each of them is ten a year, and none of it is taxed. That goes toward the twenty grain my set needs. Meanwhile Rob has just told the whole table he'll lean on Clark, and I won't spend a single grain stopping him."
whisper: none

---

Ruling: legal. Rent 5 from Silke and from Paddy (Round One rent is asked; R: Rent in Round One). No harvest is paid in Year 1: the 60 dealt was the harvest.
