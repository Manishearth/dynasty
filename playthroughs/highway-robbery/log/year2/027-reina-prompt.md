## At the table since your last turn

Reina declares 30% again: Clark 3, Silke 3, Rich 18, Paddy 18, 42 in all. She'll dig both sections of the Province 1 canal from the crown's grain (80), and pay Clark 5 and Rob 5 at the year's end.

Clark: "Three from me, three from Silke, eighteen each from Rich and Paddy, and forty-two to the crown, all counted twice. Thank you, Your Majesty. It's nice to be right in public. Rob, that's your second refusal, and the page is filling up. Rich, you wanted it written down, so here it is: twenty grain for two pieces, five lost to a raider, sixty in from the floods, and both your heirlooms sitting in the north-west where anyone can see them."

Clark collects 42: 3 each from Clark and Silke, 18 each from Rich and Paddy. Rob refuses his. Reina digs both sections of the Province 1 Grand Canal for 80 from the crown; they add 20 to her harvest from next year.

Clark: "Correction noted, Storyteller, and noted in the book as well. Rich, it turns out the piece you're short of is mine, the very last Customs House in the kingdom, and it opens at twenty. A gentleman doesn't fight over crockery, you said, so this is your chance to prove it. Paddy, you and I want the same Deed and the same Jar, and there's one of each left. Bid high if you like, but I keep the receipts on everyone."

Rich: "Your Majesty, a canal through your own province, how splendid; may it carry nothing but tribute. I'll be bidding on one piece only tonight, Clark's lovely Customs House, and I'll leave the crockery to those who still need it. My last Land Deed is the last one in the stock, though, so it goes up at twenty. Good earth is worth what it's worth."

Silke: "Bad harvest, a raid, rent and tax, and I'm down to eighteen and a half. Clark, you've got fifty-six and three pieces still to buy, and I need exactly one, the last Bowl in the Kingdom. Stand aside on it and the silk coast backs you on whatever you need next, in public, starting now. Fight me on it and you'll pay every grain I have, and three quarters of that goes to Rich's Pile."

Paddy: "Right, I'll be plain with you all, since it's the last of each piece: I stopped at eight last year and I've had a whole flood season to regret it. The rice came in well, and I mean to bid like a man who'd like to be invited upstairs someday. Silke, love, my Bowl goes up at two, like I promised, so it's in reach of an honest pocket."

Rob: "Rich, I'll confess something, because I'm a villain and not a sneak. A little bird with very neat handwriting has told me exactly where your Jar and Bowl are sitting, and he offered to lose my tax refusals in exchange. I'll be riding there anyway, Clark, so you can keep your clerical oversight. And none of you finishes your set while I'm still at the table."

The auctions: Rich wins Clark's last Customs House at 26 (Paddy stops at 25). Paddy wins Rich's last Land Deed Scroll at its minimum of 20, the only bid, and Silke's last Dragon Jar at 15 (Clark stops at 14). Silke wins Paddy's last Wooden Bowl at 9 (Clark stops at 8). Every province's stock is now sold.

Rich: "Twenty-six grain, Clark, and worth every one. Your Customs House will sit in the north-west like a guest of honour. Paddy, you bid like a gentleman, and I don't forget a gentleman. Rob, I'm touched that you'd ride so far to see my little collection, but do mind the neat handwriting. Some clerks sell their maps to anybody."

Paddy: "Well, would you look at that, a rice farmer with a deed and a jar. I'll keep them up in the quiet top corner of the paddies, Rob, a good long walk from any boat. Rich, it was a fair price and a fair fight on the Customs House, and I won't pretend I'm not sore over that one grain."

Silke: "Nine for the last Bowl, and I'll thank you for keeping your word, Paddy. That's Customs House, Land Deed and Wooden Bowl all on the silk coast, so I hold a full set. All I need now is the grain. Rob, you said nobody finishes a set while you're at the table. Come and try, it's a long walk from P25 to my back corner."

Rich places the Customs House on K16, Paddy the Land Deed Scroll on Q19 and the Dragon Jar on Q20, Silke the Wooden Bowl on L25. Rich now holds a full set and 60 grain; Silke holds a full set and 13 grain, 7 short of the 20 a seat change needs.

Rob: "Three men, three doors, and fifteen grain for my trouble: five from Rich, five from Silke, five from Paddy. Silke, darling, you're short seven for your seat change, and now you're short twelve. Paddy, the quiet top corner of your paddies is two steps from my man. Don't think of it as a raid. Think of it as a house call."

Rob's W1 steps to I11, W2 to O24, W3 to S20: 5 each from Rich, Silke and Paddy. Salaries: Reina pays Clark 5 and Rob 5.

End of Year 2. Grain: Reina 94, Clark 67.5, Rich 55, Silke 8, Paddy 38.5, Rob 75. Rich holds a full set (Customs House K16, Dragon Jar L12, Wooden Bowl M13) and the grain to change seats. Silke holds a full set (Customs House L24, Land Deed M24, Wooden Bowl L25) but is 12 grain short. Paddy holds the Land Deed Q19 and Dragon Jar Q20 and needs a Customs House. Clark holds nothing, and no stock is left. Rob's three soldiers stand on I11, O24 and S20; he has raided no piece yet.
