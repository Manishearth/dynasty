diary: Two years, and the set is whole: Silke's Jar and Paddy's Bowl for ten apiece, and Clark's own Customs House for twenty-six, bought out from under him with a smile and a thank-you. It cost me the better part of my grain, and Rob's riders took two more fives off me. Still, I end the year with fifty-five, and twenty is all a seat change needs. Clark tried to sell my squares to the Warlord, and the Warlord was vulgar enough to say so in front of everyone. I couldn't have paid for a better turn of events, and from now on I'll treat Clark with exaggerated kindness. Silke has a set too but no grain, so I must keep the rent gentle and the lady friendly until I've moved. Next comes the throne. I'll bow to Reina all the way up the steps, and I'll want somebody else's soldier between my fields and that desert when I'm sitting on it.
