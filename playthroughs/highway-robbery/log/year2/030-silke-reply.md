diary: Two years in, I have three pieces sitting in the back corner of my coast and eight grain in my purse. I went in with thirty-seven. Rent, tax, a flood that missed my fields, and two of Rob's "house calls" took the rest, and I paid nine apiece for every piece of the set, which was the right call. I took the last Bowl in the Kingdom off Clark by a single grain, and Paddy ended up with no Customs House at all, so for once I'm climbing and he's stuck at the bottom. But a set without twenty grain is just pretty crockery, and Rich has a set and sixty grain, so he gets upstairs before I do. This year I keep my head down, push every grain toward that twenty, and keep an eye on Rob's man at O24, because he's three steps from everything I own.
