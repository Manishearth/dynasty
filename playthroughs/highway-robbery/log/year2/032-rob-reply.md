diary: Two years in, I'm the richest man at the table after the Empress, and I haven't paid a grain of tax or bought a single trinket. The others paid the Harvest Pile for crockery while I collected twenty-five in raids and spent twenty on a third soldier. The best moment was Clark's whisper. He wanted me to knife Rich for him in secret, so I read his offer out to the whole table, and now those two can watch each other instead of me. Rich has his set and the grain to move up, and that worries me. Silke has her set but only eight grain, and my man on her doorstep keeps it that way. Next year my men get close enough to take pieces off the board, starting with Paddy's Jar, Silke's Deed and Rich's Jar. Once Rich's set is broken, nobody at this table climbs faster than I ride.
