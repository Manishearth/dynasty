diary: Two years in, and the Summer Palace stands at G26, and next year my canal will add twenty to every harvest. Those are the things that last. I embarrassed myself over the tax arithmetic, twelve instead of eighteen, and Clark corrected me in front of everyone; I'll let him enjoy it, since a proud Official is a loyal one. Rob has taken ten grain in salary from me and given back nothing but raids on everyone except me. Maybe that is exactly what I'm paying for, but I'll watch my palace all the same. What worries me is Rich: he smiles like a servant, he holds a full set, and he has the grain to change seats, so I'll be watching to see which chair he wants. Silke has a full set too but no grain, so I could cheaply buy her gratitude with a gentle tax. Ninety-four in the reserve. The Winter Palace is next, and then the coast canals.
