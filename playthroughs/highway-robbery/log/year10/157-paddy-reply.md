move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "The pile pays as the cards say: Rich four hundred and sixty, Silke two hundred and twenty, Reina eighty, and Clark, Rob and I pay in, sixty from me. Rent's five apiece as owed. Rich, you came to that coast with nine grain and you've five hundred and ten now, so I'm asking you for forty and I think that's modest. Silke, a fine year deserves fifteen, and I'll not be cross if you say no again. I'm saving every grain for next year, because I hear there's a Wooden Bowl going back in Province 6's stock."
aside: "Rich's old Bowl went back to Province 6's stock when he changed seats, and that Bowl is the last thing I'm missing, so whatever rent I squeeze out this year is my bid for it. I'm down to forty-three and a half, and I'd like to walk into that auction with a good deal more."
whisper: none

Ruling: as (105), the 5 each is paid now; the extra (35 more from Rich, 10 more from Silke) is a request each answers at their next turn. Say slip: Province 6's Bowl is auctioned this year, Year 10, not next; a table note.
