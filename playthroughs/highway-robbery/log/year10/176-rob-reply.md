diary: For two years the table played musical chairs around my men, and this time the music stopped in my favour. Clark finished his set and climbed back to Official just before I reached his Customs House, then walked his Bowl and Scroll north and set them down one step from W1, which I'm still laughing about. I took the Scroll. Now both Land Deeds in China are standing in my sand. Rich holds his Bowl and Customs House but can't climb without a Scroll, and Clark can't either. So the pieces I want are stuck on the board, and for once nobody can climb out from under my boots. It cost me a toll here and there and forty a year into Paddy's pile. I still haven't paid a grain of tax or bought a single bowl. Clark offered sixty for the Deed and then a hundred for the Jar, and Reina has doubled my reading money to twenty to keep both Scrolls in the desert. Everyone wants to buy the man they swore they'd never pay. I'm at a hundred and fifty-seven grain, and the crown has fifty-two and four hundred in works still owed. Every palace I burn from here comes out of a purse that's nearly empty. Two names left. W2 is one step from Rich's Customs House and W3 is closing on his Bowl. After that it's Silke, who still has nothing on the board, so I'll have to wait for her to buy something and then come and collect it. The game can end in Year 12 at the earliest, so I have two marches to finish this, and I mean to finish it.
