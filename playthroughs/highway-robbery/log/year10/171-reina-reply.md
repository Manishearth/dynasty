diary: Two years of building, and the north-east has what I promised it: a Wall all along its border, a Marble Pagoda at F17, and six new squares of field behind the stone. Last year I spent the crown down to nothing, and I had to tell Clark to his face that his salary was a grain and a half short. I paid it back this year in full, because a man who keeps a book needs to see the debt closed in it. Half of every square on the coast has come out of my reserve, three hundred grain or more, and I'm glad of it, because a stingy Empress hands the table its revolt clause. What frightens me now isn't a Bowl. Rich bought one and a Customs House as well, and the only two Land Deeds left in China are sitting in Rob's sand. So the throne is in the hands of the one man at this table who has never sold anything, and I've doubled his pay and whispered him a promise to keep it that way. Every bandit has a price, and I just hope Rob's is still twenty and not whatever Rich offers him. Next year comes either the Reservoir or the last of the Wall, and the game ends when the final stone goes in. I mean to still be sitting here when it does.
