diary: Years 9 and 10. I finally bid like a man with grain and got Paddy's Scroll for thirty-one. That gave me a full set, and I spent it on the chair I'd wanted since Year 4. I traded the richest farm in China for Rich's nine grain and a ledger, and Rich went home to my coast and came back with five hundred and ten. Then he bought my own Customs House off me for sixty-one, and now he's one Scroll from a set, so this time the pottery I furnished him was a whole farm. I left my Scroll beside Rob's man after he'd told me, in public and twice, exactly what he'd do, and it's in his sand at M15 now. That page goes under "lessons", in my own handwriting, because the grievance is mine as much as his. So I'm the Official with a hundred and ninety-one and a half, a Bowl, and the honest count, and I'm short a Jar and a Scroll. Every Scroll left in China is in Rob's desert, and so is the only Jar anyone's selling, if he ever sells. Rob has turned me down twice, but he's a man who loves to talk about prices, and Rich needs a Scroll from that same desert. So I'll make it worth Rob's while to sell to me and not to the coast, and I'll keep Reina's tax so clean that she can't afford to replace me. For the record, the throne is still the only chair I haven't sat in.
