diary: One grain again. Rich took the Bowl at thirty-four to my thirty-three, and I'd capped myself there on purpose so I'd still have the twenty grain the set needs, which means my own carefulness lost it as much as he did. That makes four Bowls, Customs Houses and Jars I've lost by a single grain, and I've stopped thinking it's bad luck. Being the Gentry is harder than it looked from the paddies. My new fields cost me sixty a year to the pile, and the peasants pay their five and not a grain more, just as I taught Silke, so I've gone from a hundred and thirty-one down to fifty-three and a half in two years. I did get twenty out of Clark and seven and a half for my old Scroll, and that Scroll finished his set and put him back in the Official's chair, so at least it did someone some good. Rich told me afterwards he'd set his limits before my offer reached him, and I believe him, he's not a liar, but he's one Scroll short now and Rob has both of them in the sand. That makes Rob the man I'll need to talk to if there's ever another Bowl. My Customs House and Jar are still sitting safe at M9 and M10, a long walk from any soldier, and if the game ends in Year 12 I've two more auctions to get it right. Next time I'll keep more than twenty in the purse beforehand, so a grain past careful doesn't have to come out of the twenty itself.
