move: land K25, K26, L26, L27, L28, L29, L30; I pay 70, asking the crown for 70 | Paddy's extra rent: I pay the 5 already owed and decline the other 35
say: "Seven squares, Your Majesty, K25 and K26 and L26 through L30, a hundred and forty to the pile. Seventy from me and seventy from the crown, which is half to the grain, and I have it in writing that the sum is half. Paddy, you'll have your five, as owed, with my warmest regards, but a man who came to the coast with nine grain will be forgiven for remembering how it felt."
aside: "Each square costs me ten and pays twenty even if the game ends next year, so the crown is paying me to farm, and I'll bow to her for every one of them. The rest of the purse stays shut, because this year the Bowl and the Customs House both come up, and Paddy's forty would only go to outbidding me for the Bowl."
whisper: none
