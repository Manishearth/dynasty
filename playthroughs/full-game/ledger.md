## Rulings (precedents)

- Batched auctions (the Director, after Year 1): no live raises on cheap lots. `resolve` prints CLOSE only when the winner's limit is within one step of the runner-up's and the price is 10 or more; otherwise the ceilings stand as settled. On CLOSE, each of the top two gets one raise-or-pass message covering all their close lots, and the lots are re-settled once (as in 013–016, 108–110).
- Two raids on one province in a march take 5 each (Rich paid 10, 021).
- A cut-out built by the crown in another province goes where that province's player chooses (Graham chose O16:P17 for the Province 7 Reservoir, 031).
- A complete set may be shown right after the auction that completes it; the seat change runs before the march (034). The Emperor at the end of the year pays the salaries, whatever the old Emperor announced (038); a declared salary goes to whoever holds the seat at year end (Year 5, Year 6).
- A public offer, once accepted in public, governs the offerer's own limits: Clark's "leave me the Vase and I'll stay out of the Jar" turned his 18 on the Jar into a pass once Reina and Noah both accepted (046).
- A raided piece goes on an empty desert square the Warlord names; if he names a square that isn't desert, it goes on the nearest desert square in the spirit of his choice (E8 for "B15", 060; F8 beside the Deed for "E9", 076). `march(..., take={soldier: (code, square)})`.
- A careless year's personal disasters are played straight from the booklets as `disaster()` lines, without a prompt per seat (Year 4; likewise the Wall breach in Year 6).
- A piece won by a player who is displaced in the same auction is placed and then returned to stock with the rest of their pieces (Clark's Deed, set on J16 and returned, 072). The mover names squares in the new province for the pieces that move with him (073, 093, 095, 113).
- **Sets completed in one auction are shown in the order they were completed; each holder is told what the later lots will do before deciding** (the Director, Year 5, 092).
- **A limit is cut to the grain its bidder holds when its lot comes up**, so a seat change mid-auction re-settles the later lots from the same limits (Graham's 125 on the Vase cut to 60, 094). `resolve`'s `step` is the bid increment: keep it at 1 (a step of 2 priced the Year 5 Vase at 62; corrected to 61 in Year 6).
- The Emperor's Share is scripted as one `act()` from the engine's bonus figures; the Emperor's own works pay him nothing (Year 5).
- A seller may set any minimum (Noah's 365 on the Deed, 102); a lot that draws no bid stays in stock.

- **Unsold lots are offered again at 2** (Manish, Year 8; in force from Year 9, since Year 8's auctions had already run): a lot that goes unsold at its minimum must be put up again the next round, the same piece, with the minimum fixed at 2 (`briefs.py ... auctions --floor <seat>:<code>`). A seller may hold a piece back for one round, never two in a row. Announce it once, as an `act()` and a note to all, before the first auctions it governs. No lot went unsold in Year 7 or Year 8.
- **A public deal across two years** (Clark and Paddy, Year 7, 127–128): an offer answered in public with a counter, accepted in public, governs both players' limits in each year it names.
- **Two sets in one chapter: the first throne change** (Silke, Year 7). The Emperor at year end paid the salaries the old Emperor declared (precedent 038).
- **A negotiated work's split can be asked inside another prompt** (Graham agreed to the Province 4 canal in his auction prompt, 140).
- **A placement on a raided square goes on the nearest legal square** in the spirit of the choice (Paddy's Customs House, S22 to S23, 145), as with raided pieces on the desert.

- **Two seats asking for the same square of new land in one step:** the earlier seat in table order gets it; the later buys the rest (Silke's K25 over Clark's, Year 12, 222).
- **With no positive income, a declared tax is owed by no one,** and the Official's collect prompt is folded into the next one (Year 12, 220).

- **Unsold stock is sold only at auction** (Section 10; the Director, Year 13): a seller can't sell a piece from their stock by private trade. Kept by Manish; in RULINGS.md (Readings). Raided realia the Warlord holds may still be sold privately.

- **The crown may build a negotiated work alone if it pays the whole cost** (Manish, before Year 13): a province can refuse its share, but can't stop the work. Announced at the start of Year 13 as an act() and a note; Reina built the last work under it.
- **A crown in debt can't pay for a work** (Manish, confirming Note 7).
- **A crown in debt pays only the 5 each that salaries require** (Manish, confirming Note 7): anything above 5 is a gift, and a player in debt can't give.
- **The game ends the moment the last work is built** (Section 18): nothing after it in that round is played, so the year's declared salaries went unpaid (Year 13, 248).
