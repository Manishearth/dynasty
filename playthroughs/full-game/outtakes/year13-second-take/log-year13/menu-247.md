## Your move: collecting the tax, and the Province 2 Irrigation System

**The tax.** The Emperor has declared **20%**. Owed on this year's harvest income, rounded down to the half million: Graham 10, Rich 2, Silke 0, Clark 18, Noah 14, Paddy 6; 50 in all. The Warlord may refuse to pay (and usually does). You collect it and deliver it to the crown, and you make certain nobody cheats.

**The Irrigation System** (30, +10 harvest to Province 2 from next year) is a negotiated work: Province 2 and the crown must both agree the split. The Emperor offers to pay all 30, with nothing from you. You may accept, refuse, or ask for other terms. It is the last work owed: the game ends the moment it is built, in this year's public works step, before the auction (§18).

The Emperor owes you a salary of at least 5 at the end of the year.

Reply with "collect as declared; irrigation: accept | refuse | <your terms>" as your move (and any land, below).
