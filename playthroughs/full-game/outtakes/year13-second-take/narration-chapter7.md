## Chapter 7

- **(244)** The thirteenth year, the second of the Empress Reina. The rains come late everywhere, the counter holds at three hundred million, and one ditch of thirty stands between the table and the end of the game.
- **(247)** The crown offers to pay for the last work in full, and the man whose province it would water says no. Thirty grain, refused.
- **(254)** Free land for every province, and in a single season the crown spends four hundred and eighty on it. The treasury that could have paid for the last ditch three times over now holds four.
- **(265)** Eighty-six for a Deed, thirty-three for a Jar, and Noah, who lost by one grain for a decade, takes back the Scholar's chair he drew in Year One. Silke's four pieces won by a grain each go back into the carts.
- **(269)** The desert walks into the Empress's garden and the crown's last four grain go with it. The thirteenth year ends with the treasury sixteen in debt and the ditch still a line on a list.
