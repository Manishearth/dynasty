## Chapter 7

- **(Year 13, before 244)** The thirteenth year, the second of Reina's reign. The seed rots everywhere, and for the first time last year's new land is what feeds the table. One Irrigation System, thirty grain, stands between the players and the end of the game.
- **(252)** "Every square, in full." Graham takes six, Clark eleven, Noah five, and the treasury that held 474 at the harvest holds 4. The last work owed costs 30.
- **(257)** Graham declines to pay twenty-six for the last ditch, and the game that could have ended at the works step goes on to the auction. The works owed stay at 30.
- **(265)** Seventy-three for the Deed, one grain over Silke, and seventeen for a Jar: Noah completes his set and takes the chair of the woman who needed that Deed. He has lost four chairs by a grain; this one he won by one.
- **(268)** W4 walks off with Clark's Vase, and Clark, one Deed short a moment ago, is two pieces short again. W3's raid and the salaries leave the crown 22 grain in debt.
