diary: Year Thirteen. Silke whispered me seventy-two grain for Clark's old Deed, and I'll admit I was flattered. But that Deed is a name crossed off my list, and it was also the last piece she needed for the chair, so I kept it on the mantel and let her go to auction. Noah outbid her by a single grain, took her chair and her whole purse, and Silke is a rice farmer now with no pots in her garden, which is a shame, because my lads had only just got there. Clark finished the evening needing one Deed, so W4 strolled down to his "unreachable" coast and brought his Vase home to M18. It doesn't count for me, but now he needs two pieces, and every set at the table is a little further off than it was. The crown is twenty-two in debt and the ditch still isn't dug, and Reina owes me ten she didn't have, so somebody at this table is going to start saying "revolt" very soon. I've a hundred and ninety-eight and a half grain, the fattest tent in China, and I still haven't paid for a pot. W1 is three steps from Graham's pots on the G column, and three of my lads are closing on Rich's Bowl at J17. Two names to go. The desert doesn't buy, it collects, and next year it closes the book. I mean it this time.
