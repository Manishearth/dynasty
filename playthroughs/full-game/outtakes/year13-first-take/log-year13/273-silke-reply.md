diary: Year 13. I read Bad Harvest, and it turned out to be about me. I tried to buy my Deed privately, first from Rich and then from Rob, and learned that the rules won't let Rich sell me one and that Rob doesn't sell anything. Then Noah stood up and did the sum out loud, as he always does: I could write seventy-two, so he wrote seventy-three. That's one grain again, the same thread from Year 3 and Year 10, and this time it cost me everything. He took my chair and my ninety-five, my four pieces went back to stock, and I'm the Rice Peasant on sixty-four and a half with an empty cart. I can't even say he cheated me. He told me plainly he wouldn't stand aside, and I'd told the whole table exactly how much I had. I won't make that mistake again: from now on nobody hears my sum but me. Two things to watch. Reina is twenty-two in debt, which is a cause for revolt, and a peasant can raise one. And Clark needs only a Deed and a Vase, Rich still has two Deeds, and Noah is already watching him. Fine. I've been at the bottom before, and it's where I see most clearly.
