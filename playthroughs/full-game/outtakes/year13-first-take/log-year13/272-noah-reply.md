diary: Diary of Noah Tall, the Scholar again, the end of Year Thirteen. Reina promised to pay for every new square in full, so I took five for nothing and said out loud that her treasury had a hundred and four left. That was the last of it, and by the end of the year the crown was twenty-two in debt. At the auction I did the sum I'd promised myself: Silke could write seventy-two for the Deed and still keep her twenty, so I wrote seventy-four. She went out at seventy-two and I had it for seventy-three, and nobody else wanted Clark's Jar past sixteen. I took Silke's chair because taking it sent her set back to stock, and because it was the fattest purse the rules let me take. I'm sorry about it all the same, as she'd offered me a fair deal and I'd turned it down to her face. That's the first seat I've taken in thirteen years that I'd worked out for my own chair before I sat down, and I'm on 153.5. The trouble is that a full purse at this table is exactly what gets stolen. Clark needs only a Deed and a Vase now, after W4 walked off with his old one, and Rich still has two Deeds to sell. So next year I'll be watching Clark and those Deeds, and I'll be counting the Empress's debt out loud, because a crown in the red is cause for a revolt and somebody ought to say so.
