diary: Year Thirteen. A bad harvest, and I bought no land, because the crown had four grain left and I wanted my purse for the market. I lost the Customs House to Clark by one grain, eighteen to his nineteen, and the Jar to Noah by one, so that's the same old song with a new verse. Noah wrote seventy-three on the Deed, one more than Silke could give, and he showed his set and took her chair right there at the table. I'll say this for him, he told her plainly beforehand he wouldn't stand aside, and he was sorry about it afterwards and meant it. My Vase went to Graham for nine, so I got a couple of grain back, and the Bowl and Brush are still safe on S16 and S17 with no soldier near them. I finish on fifty-four and a half, and I still need a Customs House, a Deed and a Jar. Reina's twenty-two in debt now, and Clark needs only a Deed and a Vase, so he's the one who's close this time and I'll be watching him. Next year the harvest should be good again, and I'll go to market with a fuller purse and bid more than a grain past where I stopped this year.
