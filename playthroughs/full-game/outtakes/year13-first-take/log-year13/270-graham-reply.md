diary: Year 13. Reina said "every square, in full," I wrote it down word for word, and six squares of new land in Province 2 cost me nothing. Everyone else read the same sentence and held her to it, and by Noah's turn the treasury had four grain left. Then she asked me to pay twenty-six for her ditch, which would have ended the game with my shelf empty, and I said no. Noah paid seventy-three for the Deed while Silke stopped at seventy-two, one grain short, the way I used to lose. He took her chair the same hour. I bought a Brush for ten and a Vase for nine while the table spent its money on one scroll, so those are two pieces on the G column, out of reach. Rob's lad took five off me for standing on I12, which is what he always does. The crown ended the year twenty-two in debt, and the law calls that a cause for revolt. Clark needs a Deed and a Vase, and I need a Deed, a Jar and a Bowl, and I've eighty-six and a half grain. Next year I keep the figures honest, I watch Rich's two Deeds, and I watch Clark, because he's closer than I am.
