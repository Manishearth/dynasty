diary: Year Thirteen, from Province 3, in a bad harvest that somehow left me richer than I started. I kept the rent at five and called it kindness, and the peasants paid it without a murmur. The Empress promised every new square "in full", so I took F9 at her expense. Then the rest of the table took her at her word too, and she went from five hundred grain to four in one afternoon. Silke whispered me sixty-five for a Deed, the rules wouldn't allow it, and in the open auction Noah paid seventy-three. My quarter of that was eighteen and a quarter, and it put Noah in Silke's chair with her purse in his pocket. The Pile took the rest. I bid a grain over and never two and came home with nothing but my Bowl. Graham beat me to the Vase by one grain. I've lost by one grain often enough to know I'll stop paying for that lesson sooner or later. Clark needs one Deed and a Vase, and I hold both remaining Deeds, so next year he'll be very polite to me. The real news is at the top of the table: the crown is twenty-two grain in debt, and the rulebook calls that a cause for revolt. I've sat on that throne and I know exactly how thin the cushions are. I have sixty-six and a half grain, a Bowl, two Deeds to sell and a long memory. I'll keep smiling and keep counting.
