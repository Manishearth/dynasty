diary: Diary of Noah Tall, the Gentry, the end of Year Six. In Year Five I bought a Customs House for fifteen and a Bowl for nine, and for the first time I held a full set. I worked out, correctly and out loud, that taking the throne would last one auction, because Graham was about to buy his Vase. So I took Graham's chair instead, and his purse, and sat down with 260.5 grain feeling very clever. Then Reina showed her set and took the chair off me by exactly the same reasoning, and I went from 260.5 to sixty in about a minute. I'd done the sum for the throne and never thought to do it for the seat I was sitting in. That was my arithmetic, and it's no one else's fault. In Year Six I paid out the harvest and took five rent from each peasant. I also held the only two Land Deeds left, and Reina needed one. She offered thirty, and I set the minimum at 365, half a grain more than she could pay and keep her twenty, and I told the whole table why. Nobody bid. It didn't matter much, because Silke showed her set after the last lot and took Reina's chair and her 391 anyway. So this table punishes anyone who sits on a fat purse with nothing in hand, and next year that's Silke, who needs only a Jar. I have 115 grain, no pieces and five to collect, plus two Deeds in my cart that Paddy needs. I still owe Paddy a favour, and a fair price on a Deed may be the honest way to pay it.
