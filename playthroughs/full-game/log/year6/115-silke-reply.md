diary: Years 5 and 6, and I can hardly believe I'm writing it. In Year 5 I stopped bidding small and honest, just as I said I would. I put everything on the last Deed in Reina's cart and got it for twenty-three, because Paddy's nerves gave out at twenty-two. I took a Brush for thirteen on top, and Reina paid the fifteen I asked for my Jar, as she'd promised. I watched three chairs change hands in one auction that year, and I learned what Noah learned: a full set is only worth showing if nobody can take your seat off you two minutes later. This year a flood took my ditch and I was down to ninety-five. Then I took a Customs House for twenty-three and a Bowl for seventeen, beating Clark by a single grain both times. Graham beat me by one grain in Year 3, so the thread came back round. I showed the set and took Reina's chair and her three hundred and ninety-one, and she's on my coast now with sixty grain and my old cart. I'm the Official with four hundred and fifty-nine, which is more than the Emperor has, and I'm one Dragon Jar short of a full set again. Reina has the two Jars to sell, and she'll remember who sold her the last one for fifteen. Rob's lad is two steps from my column. So I'll collect the tax honestly, count it twice, and keep a very close eye on Rich's chair.
