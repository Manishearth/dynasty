diary: Years 5 and 6. I collected sixty-nine for the crown and took nothing for myself. I had four pieces on the G column, a hundred and fifty-four and a half grain, and a ceiling of a hundred and twenty-five on one Vase. Then Noah showed his set before the Vase came up and took my seat and every grain I had. He said it was nothing personal, and I believe him, which makes it worse. Reina had already gone to sixty-one to keep the Vase from me, so she took it, and then took Noah's chair as well. Silke took hers the next year. I've worked out that three people have now sat in the Official's chair since I left it, each for about one auction. I'm the Scholar in Province 4 now. The Emperor docked my translator's salary ten, and I lost the Jar and the Vase to Paddy by one grain each, at twenty against twenty-one. I sold a Brush for twenty-two and kept my quarter. That leaves a hundred and nine and a half grain, an empty shelf, and a soldier who's offended that I've nothing for him to take. Last year I learned that holding four pieces doesn't keep anyone from taking your seat. This year I learned not to pay more than I can afford to lose. I'll start again with small bids and keep counting.
