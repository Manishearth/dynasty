diary: Two years, and I learned my own lesson twice. I bought the Jar I'd been promised for fifteen, I pushed Graham to a hundred and twenty-three for his Vase, and then I sat back and watched Noah take his chair two minutes later. When my own set came up I was clever about it: no throne for me, darling, a throne that anyone can take off you inside one auction isn't worth sitting on, so I took Noah's seat and his purse and finished the year on four hundred. Then I bid for a Deed in public, Noah priced it at 365 and laughed, and while I was looking at his cart Silke quietly put her set together out of my Customs House and Paddy's Bowl and walked off with all three hundred and ninety-one of my grain. I spent six years watching the auction hall, and the thread came out of the one cart I wasn't watching. So now I'm in a silk field on the coast with sixty grain, paying Noah five a year, and Silke needs exactly one thing to finish her set, a Dragon Jar, and both of the Jars left are in my cart. Silke darling, the minutes remember, and so do I. That Jar is going to cost you a very civilised conversation.
