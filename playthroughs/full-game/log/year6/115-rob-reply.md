diary: Years Five and Six. Two more refusals in the crown's ledger, and the ledger has had three different keepers in two years. The chair passes round this table faster than the gravy. Noah put his treasures on the top row and told me my lad couldn't reach them, which was true, and then he was out of the chair before the ink dried. Then Reina was in it, and then Silke was, and every one of them walked off with the last one's purse. Meanwhile W4 stepped onto Q25 and picked up Paddy's Dragon Jar like he was collecting the post. It's on T22 now, the third ornament on my mantel, and Paddy is crossed off. Year Six a lieutenant ran off with my daughter, so the lads spent a round on compassionate leave. I watched the shelves fill up and didn't spend a single grain. Silke has stood her whole set in a column down the west edge, where Graham kept his, and W2 is two steps away and very rested. Graham and Reina have empty shelves, so W3 is strolling toward Rich's new Winter Palace in case either of them is shy about buying. Three crossed off, three to go, a hundred and seventy grain, and I still haven't paid for a single pot.
