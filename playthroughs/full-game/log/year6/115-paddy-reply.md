diary: Two years, and I've lost more and gained more than in all the rest put together. In Year Five I bid sensible on everything, and I came second by one grain on the Customs House, the Deed and the Brush, all three in the same afternoon. Then I watched three chairs change hands in a single auction, which I'd not have believed if I hadn't been sitting there. Rob's lad finally took the one step to my Jar, and it's sitting out on T22 in the sand now, and that was my own fault for putting it by the sea. Year Six started with a fire that took the house and ten grain, and then Rich dug both canal sections through my fields at the crown's cost, so there'll be twenty more a year from now on. I don't know what he wants for it yet, but I'll take the ditches. At the market I went past Clark and Graham by a grain or two, and I came home with a Brush, a Jar and a Vase for sixty-four grain, all three at the back on S19, S20 and S21 where no soldier can reach them. That's three pieces out of five. I need a Customs House and a Deed, and Noah has both Deeds and still owes me that favour. Silke's in Reina's old chair now and needs only a Jar, so I'll be watching her. I'm down to sixty-eight and a half grain, but those canals will help.
