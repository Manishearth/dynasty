diary: Year Thirteen, and the last page. Graham wrote down my last refusal and then the Empress put thirty grain into a canal on G12 and closed the book before my lads could take another step. W3 and W5 were one march from Silke's pots and W2 was three from Rich's Bowl, so I finish two names short of my collection, still the Warlord, and holding a hundred and seventy-eight and a half grain, which is more than anyone else at the table except Reina. I never bought a single pot in thirteen years. The Deed, the Customs House and the Dragon Jar on my mantel were all taken on the march, and the Winter Palace paid the crown's twenty for having me round. It cost me nothing but the ending, and Reina earned that by reading the treasury when the rest of us were reading the shelves. Next time I march a round sooner. The desert doesn't buy, it collects, and it'll be back for the rest.
