## Paddy's diary so far, in his own words

## Setup–Year 2

Two years gone, and I've a Dragon Jar to show for them. It's sitting out at Q25 by the sea, and I paid eight grain for it, which is more than I ever paid for a pot in my life. The first year I bid small on everything and came home with an empty cart. Between Rich's rent, the crown's tax and Rob's lads walking through my paddies, I've gone from thirty-seven grain down to twenty-five. Then Rich, who smiled at everyone and fought over nothing, went and bought the whole set and took the throne out from under Reina before the rest of us had two pieces between us. So the man who was collecting my rent is now the man collecting my tax, and I'm not sure that's an improvement. Rob's soldier is standing on R23, two steps from my Jar. I'll be watching him closer than the auctions next year, and I'll keep my purse shut unless a piece is really worth it.

## Years 3–4

Two more years and I've still only the one Jar out at Q25, but I'm not crying over it. The first year I bid small and stood aside on the Deed for Silke, and then Graham walked in and bought the Customs House, the Deed and the Brush in one go. This year I put forty grain on the last Deed in Reina's cart, more than I've ever risked on anything, and Clark beat me by one. Then Graham showed his set and took Clark's seat, and that Deed went straight back to stock, so Clark paid forty-one for nothing and the Deed's for sale again. I won't say I laughed, but I didn't cry either. My Bowls did me proud: Reina paid nine and Graham twenty-three, and my quarter of those went in the purse. With two good harvests I've gone from twenty-five grain to near eighty, which is more than I've ever had. Rob's lads had sore legs this year and left my paddies alone, but W4 is still on Q24, one step from my Jar, and I've not forgotten it. Noah still owes me a favour. Next year I'm going after that Deed again, and I'll be keeping a purse for the new land when the people double.

## Years 5–6

Two years, and I've lost more and gained more than in all the rest put together. In Year Five I bid sensible on everything, and I came second by one grain on the Customs House, the Deed and the Brush, all three in the same afternoon. Then I watched three chairs change hands in a single auction, which I'd not have believed if I hadn't been sitting there. Rob's lad finally took the one step to my Jar, and it's sitting out on T22 in the sand now, and that was my own fault for putting it by the sea. Year Six started with a fire that took the house and ten grain, and then Rich dug both canal sections through my fields at the crown's cost, so there'll be twenty more a year from now on. I don't know what he wants for it yet, but I'll take the ditches. At the market I went past Clark and Graham by a grain or two, and I came home with a Brush, a Jar and a Vase for sixty-four grain, all three at the back on S19, S20 and S21 where no soldier can reach them. That's three pieces out of five. I need a Customs House and a Deed, and Noah has both Deeds and still owes me that favour. Silke's in Reina's old chair now and needs only a Jar, so I'll be watching her. I'm down to sixty-eight and a half grain, but those canals will help.


## Years 7–8

I'm writing this from Noah's chair, and I still can't quite believe it. In Year Seven Clark offered me a deal in front of everyone: he'd take the Customs House and I'd take the Deed, and next year we'd swap. I shook on it, and I got Noah's Deed for fifteen and put it on R22, well back from the sea. Silke bought Reina's Jar for a hundred and fifty and took the throne from Rich in one go. This year Clark and Noah both kept their word and stayed out of the Customs House, but Reina wasn't part of any handshake and pushed me all the way to fifty-one. I paid it, and that gave me the whole set, Brush, Jar, Vase, Deed and Customs House, from a man who came home with an empty cart in Year One. I took Noah's seat because it had the most grain and the Harvest Pile with it, and I'm sorry it had to be him, as he was always fair with me. So now I'm the Gentry, with two hundred and thirty-five and a half grain, and my pieces are lined up on M10 to M13, about as far from Rob's lads as I can get them. All I need now is a Wooden Bowl, and Noah sells those now, so I'll be keeping rent at five and being very nice to him.


## Years 9–10

Years 9–10. I started Year Nine in the Gentry's chair with two hundred and forty-five grain and every piece but a Wooden Bowl, and by the end of the same market I was a wheat farmer. Graham showed his set and took the throne off Silke, and while I was still clapping Clark showed his and took my seat and every grain I had. I drew sixty from the Pile and bought Noah's Bowl for thirty-one anyway, out of stubbornness mostly, and put it on S16 at the back of Clark's old fields. I'd been one piece short for a whole year and I never thought to worry about anyone else being one piece short, and that's the lesson I'll keep. This year I bid small on everything and only got the Brush, for twenty-one, after Noah stood aside at twenty like the decent man he is, and it's on S17 beside the Bowl. Then Reina did to Clark exactly what Clark did to me, two lots after he'd done it, so I'm not the only one on the floor now. I've sixty-seven and a half grain, a Bowl and a Brush, and I still need a Customs House, a Deed and a Jar. Next time I get close I'm showing straight away.


## Years 11–12

Years 11–12. I went into Year Eleven with a hundred and four grain and three pieces to buy, and I came out of it with nothing new, second by a grain or a few on the Customs House, the Deed and the Jar all over again, while Reina put five hundred behind a Jar and took Rich's chair. Then the people doubled, and at times three my fields were losing twenty-five a year, so I put seventy-two grain into four new squares at N13, N14, O12 and O13, with the crown paying two a square because it had nearly nothing left to give. That's the most I've ever spent on anything that wasn't a pot, and it left me with thirty-two and a half. This year I lost the Customs House to Silke by one grain, twelve against her thirteen, and Reina bought her Deed and took the throne off Graham, so the woman who lost the crown to Rich in Year Two is sitting on it again. I've still only the Bowl and the Brush on S16 and S17, and thirty-three and a half grain, but the fields should come out at plus fifty-five a year now, and they'll be bringing in grain long after this auction's forgotten. Silke needs only a Deed and Noah's close behind her, so this time I'll be watching who's one piece short. Next year I'll have a purse again, and I'll spend it on a Customs House, a Deed or a Jar before I spend it on anything else.
