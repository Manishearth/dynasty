diary: Year 13. Silke read a bad harvest, and I collected fifty for the crown at twenty percent, ten of it my own. Reina spent thirty on a canal for my province, I put it on G12 and G13, and the game ended before anyone could bid on anything. She gave me a gift and I paid for it with the last auction, which is the most expensive thing anyone's given me in thirteen years. I sat down a wheat farmer with nothing on my shelf, and I get up the Official with ninety-eight grain and a ditch with my name on it, and the minutes call that a win. I'll take it, quietly, because I know what each chair cost me: two lost by a grain at the auction, one to Noah's full purse, one to Reina's patience. I kept count of every grain anyone took from me, and she worked out that the count only matters until somebody stops the clock. Next time, I'll watch the clock as well as the count.
