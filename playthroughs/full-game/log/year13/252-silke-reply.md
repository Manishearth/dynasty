move: none
say: Majesty, I read the card that said the harvest would be bad, and I never thought to read the treasury. Thirty grain to close the book, and you closed it with four of my five pieces on the table. A well-run reign, and I mean it. I drew the Scholar and I'm finishing as the Scholar, but in between I sat on the throne and in the Official's chair, and I paid a hundred and fifty for a Jar without blinking. Rich, fifty was the price, and you kept your word to the letter, as you said in Year 1. I'll remember the letter.
aside: One Land Deed short, for the second time in this game. The thread came round my way at last, and Reina cut it before the auction began. She had the sharpest eye at the table tonight, and I hope I was a close second.
whisper: none

Ruling: last words. Silke misstates her drawn seat: she drew the Silk Peasant (Province 5), so finishing as the Scholar is a win by one seat. The fact goes into the hover of her line on the page; the game being over, no note to the table.
