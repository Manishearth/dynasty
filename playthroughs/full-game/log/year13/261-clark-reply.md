diary: Diary of Clark Grafton, Silk Peasant of Province 5, end of Year 13, and the last page. The Empress paid thirty grain for a canal on G12 and closed the book with it, before the auction ever opened, so my hundred and a half, my Vase on L25, my Brush on P26 and seven squares of sea that were supposed to pay a hundred and fifteen next harvest all stop counting at the same moment. I paid my eighteen in full, the way I have every year since I held the pen. Rob refused again, so page one closes on line thirteen and nobody is ever going to settle it. I drew the Official's chair and I'm leaving as a silk farmer, and the record shows that as a loss. I won't argue with it, because I sat in four seats and every one I lost went to a set somebody showed while I was still waiting on a last lot. The lesson I wrote three times turned out to be the whole game: Reina showed hers first, and in the end she showed the treasury too, and the one who decides when the game stops never has to wait on a lot. "Paddy, settled" holds, "Graham, generous" holds, and under "Reina, admired and owed" I've struck out "owed" and underlined "admired" a third time. The ledger is closed, but I'm keeping it, because next game I mean to read it before the first lot instead of after the last.
