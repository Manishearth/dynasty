diary: Year 13. The seed rotted and the rains came late, so I paid six in tax and five to Rich and waited for the auction, and there wasn't one. Reina put thirty grain into a canal for Graham on G12, and that was the last work the country was owed, so the game stopped there with her on the throne and four hundred and ninety-four grain in her lap. I finish with fifty-two and a half, a Bowl and a Brush on S16 and S17, and four squares of land that'll never bring in a harvest now. I sat down in Noah's old chair as the Rice Peasant and I'm getting up as the Wheat Peasant, so the book calls it a loss, and I suppose it is. But for one year I was the Gentry with the whole set bar a Bowl, and I never thought to look at who was one piece short, and this year I never thought to look at the treasury. Reina did, and that's why she's sitting where she is. Next time I'll count the works still owed as hard as I count the pieces, and I'll still put my pots at the back, away from the sea.
