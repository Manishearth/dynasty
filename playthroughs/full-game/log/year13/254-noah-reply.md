I drew the Scholar's chair with fifty-three and a half grain, and I'm leaving as the Rice Peasant with a hundred and forty-five and a half, three pieces nobody could reach on S23 to S25, and a Deed and a Jar I never got. That's down one chair and up ninety-two grain, and most of the lots I lost, I lost by exactly one grain. Silke, neither of us got that Deed, so my diary's last promise is the only one at this table that was never tested. Thank you all. It was an honest game, and I mean most of you."
aside: "Three times I sat on a full purse and lost the chair under it, so this time I sat on a full purse with no chair to lose, and the Empress closed the book before anyone could take it from me. It wasn't clever, it was arithmetic, and for once the arithmetic was on my side."
