say: "Majesty, a lovely canal, and a fair finish. I sat down a wheat farmer with an empty cart, and I'm getting up a wheat farmer with a Bowl, a Brush, four squares of new land and fifty-two and a half grain. But for one year in the middle I had the whole set bar a Bowl and Rich's chair to show for it, and nobody can take that out of the minutes. Thank you all. Noah, we're square on that favour, and Clark, I'll take 'settled', it's more than my da ever got written about him."
aside: "I said next time I got close I'd show straight away, and the game ended before I got close, so I suppose I kept my word. Reina read the treasury while the rest of us were reading the auction list, and that's the lesson I'm taking home with the Bowl."

Ruling: last words. Paddy misstates his drawn seat: he drew the Rice Peasant (Province 6) and finishes as the Wheat Peasant (7), a loss by one seat. "Rich's chair" is the Gentry's seat, Rich's drawn seat, which Paddy held in Years 8–9. The fact goes into the hover.
