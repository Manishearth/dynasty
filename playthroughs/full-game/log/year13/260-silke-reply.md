diary: Year 13. I turned the card myself and read out a bad harvest, and I spent the whole morning thinking about Rich's Deeds. Reina spent it thinking about the treasury. Rich wanted fifty for a Deed in the cart, because he'd promised Graham that and keeps his word to the letter. I was getting myself ready to pay it when Reina put thirty grain into Graham's canal and closed the book before any of us reached the auction hall. So I finish one Land Deed short, with four pieces tucked inland and ninety-two and a half grain, and Rob's lads were close enough to read the price tags. When I was writing the minutes I nearly forgot one thing: I sat down as the Silk Peasant with thirty-seven grain and a single Vase, and I'm getting up as the Scholar, having been the Official and the Emperor along the way. Noah says neither of us ever got that Deed, and he's right. I've learned the thread you watch closest isn't always the one that gets cut. It's a win, a thin one, and I'll take it.
