move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "A thin year, my friends, and a thin year needs a gentle landlord. Five apiece, as always, and not a grain more. Clark, Noah, Paddy, you'll find me the most reasonable man in the north-west. As for the Deeds, Silke, they go in the cart like everything else, and the price is what it was."
aside: "Fifteen in rent puts me at fifty and a half if the game ends this year, and every grain counts as a tiebreaker. Silke is one Deed short of a set, and I'm not going to sell her Reina's four hundred and seventy-four for twenty-five."
whisper: To Silke: "You're very kind, but I told Graham in so many words that no Deed of mine opens under fifty, and I keep my promises exactly as I make them. Meet fifty in the cart and the Deed is yours, and I won't say a word about it."
