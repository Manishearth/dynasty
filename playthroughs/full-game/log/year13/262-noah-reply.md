diary: Diary of Noah Tall, the Rice Peasant, the end of Year Thirteen, and the end of the game. The harvest was bad, I paid fourteen in tax, and then Reina spent thirty grain on a canal for Graham and closed the book before the auction, so the Deed Silke and I were both waiting for never went on sale. I finish third in grain on a hundred and forty-five and a half, ninety-two more than I drew, but I drew the Scholar's chair and I'm getting up from a rice paddy, so the minutes call it a loss, and they're right. Thirteen years taught me the same thing three ways: lose a lot by one grain, lose a chair by not doing the sum for it, and lose a game by not counting the treasury. Reina counted thirty grain where the rest of us were counting Deeds. Paddy says we're square, and I'm glad I paid that favour, even if I paid far more than I meant to. If there's a next game I'll do the sum for my own chair first and the throne's last, and I'll write my figure one grain higher from the very first year.
