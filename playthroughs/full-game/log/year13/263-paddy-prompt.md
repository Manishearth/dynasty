## At the table since your last turn

Noah: "Majesty, a glorious reign, and I'll say the sum out loud one last time, because somebody has to. I drew the Scholar's chair with fifty-three and a half grain, and I'm leaving as the Rice Peasant with a hundred and forty-five and a half, three pieces nobody could reach on S23 to S25, and a Deed and a Jar I never got. That's down one chair and up ninety-two grain, and most of the lots I lost, I lost by exactly one grain. Silke, neither of us got that Deed, so my diary's last promise is the only one at this table that was never tested. Thank you all. It was an honest game, and I mean most of you."

Rob: "Majesty, thirteen years and you finished it with thirty grain and a shovel, one march before my lads got to Silke's front garden. W3 and W5 were close enough to read the price tags. I drew the sand and I'm leaving on the sand, with a hundred and seventy-eight and a half grain, three ornaments on the mantel, a palace I visited once, and not one lot bought in thirteen years. Clark, page one, line thirteen: refused, in that lovely hand. Thank you all, it's been a pleasure being the villain."
