diary: Years 11–12. Twelve years, darling, and I'm finally sitting in the chair. I paid seventy-one for a Jar and could have taken the throne that very afternoon, but I could see Rich's Bowl coming on the next lot and I'd learned in Year Five what one auction on a throne is worth. So I took Rich's seat instead, which sent his set back to stock before it was finished, and I built it all again in Province 2 out of his own filing system. The next spring I bought my Deed at Rich's fifty with nobody bidding against me and took the crown from Graham while no other set at the table could be finished, so it cost me nothing but patience. It's an empty treasury, mind: six grain, a times-three population and salaries to pay, and I'm on four hundred and sixty-four with Province 1's new fields only paying from next year. Silke has four pots in her garden and needs one Deed, and Rich sells the Deeds, so Rich darling, you and I are going to be very good friends this spring. And this time it's the Empress who's watching every cart.
