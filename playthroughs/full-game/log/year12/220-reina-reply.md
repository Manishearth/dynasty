move: land F11, F12, F13, F14, F15, F16; I pay 30, asking the crown for 90
say: Your Majesty, I'll take you up on fifteen in every twenty, with thanks for the minutes: all six squares from F11 to F16, thirty from me and ninety from the crown. An Official whose fields pay their own way collects a much happier tax, darlings, and Rich, I'll still be wanting one of those Deeds.
aside: At times three my seat loses 45 a year, so six squares at a quarter of the price turns that into a profit from next year and covers half of the times-six card if it comes. I still keep 531, far more than the 20 I need with a Deed to take the throne.
