move: wall agree; land G9, G10, G11; I pay 15, asking the crown for 45
say: "Your Majesty, that's the most generous twenty I've ever been asked for, and I'm glad to pay it. Four links over Province 3, and since the crown is covering fifteen of every twenty, I'll take all three squares under the Wall, G9, G10 and G11. That's fifteen from me and forty-five from the treasury, and you have the Gentry's thanks in the minutes."
aside: "Thirty-five grain buys me a Wall and three fields, which turns a province losing twenty-five a year into one that makes money from next year on. I'm down to thirteen grain, but I'm solvent, and Graham has just shown me what a Deed is worth to him."
whisper: To Graham: "Agreed, and I'll put it the way I make all my promises: this spring no Deed of mine opens under fifty, whoever is bidding. I'll keep that to the letter. Anything above fifty is up to the table."
