diary: Years 11–12. I went into Year Eleven with a hundred and four grain and three pieces to buy, and I came out of it with nothing new, second by a grain or a few on the Customs House, the Deed and the Jar all over again, while Reina put five hundred behind a Jar and took Rich's chair. Then the people doubled, and at times three my fields were losing twenty-five a year, so I put seventy-two grain into four new squares at N13, N14, O12 and O13, with the crown paying two a square because it had nearly nothing left to give. That's the most I've ever spent on anything that wasn't a pot, and it left me with thirty-two and a half. This year I lost the Customs House to Silke by one grain, twelve against her thirteen, and Reina bought her Deed and took the throne off Graham, so the woman who lost the crown to Rich in Year Two is sitting on it again. I've still only the Bowl and the Brush on S16 and S17, and thirty-three and a half grain, but the fields should come out at plus fifty-five a year now, and they'll be bringing in grain long after this auction's forgotten. Silke needs only a Deed and Noah's close behind her, so this time I'll be watching who's one piece short. Next year I'll have a purse again, and I'll spend it on a Customs House, a Deed or a Jar before I spend it on anything else.
