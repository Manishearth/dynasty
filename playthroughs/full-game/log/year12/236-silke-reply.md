diary: Years 11–12. In Year 11 I read the card that made the villages noisier, and then I lost every lot I bid on again. I pushed Reina's Jar to seventy so she'd pay seventy-one for it, and she took Rich's chair the same afternoon. Then the population doubled, nobody's fields could feed them, and Graham emptied the treasury buying new land for the whole table, mine included. I took two squares on my coast for ten grain, and then I sat in the auction as the second-fattest purse at a table of paupers. Reina paid fifty for her Deed and took Graham's throne with six grain in it. Meanwhile I took a Customs House, a Jar, a Bowl and a Vase for seventy-one between them, and I beat Noah by one grain for the Jar and Clark by one grain for the Bowl, so the thread finally came round my way. Rob's lads came straight to my gate and charged me ten for it, but the pieces are tucked inland where his boots can't reach them, with the Jar on K19 where they can't reach it next year either. I have a hundred and two grain, and I'm one Land Deed short of a full set. Rich sells the Deeds and has twenty-five grain to his name, and Noah wants the same Deed I do. So next spring Rich and I are going to have a very polite conversation, and I mean to have Reina's chair before Noah has mine.
