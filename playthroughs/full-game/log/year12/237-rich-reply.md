diary: Year Twelve, from Province 3, under four new links of Wall that cost me twenty grain and cost the crown a hundred and forty. In Year Ten I wrote that I'd be very polite to Reina this spring. I was, and she was polite back, and then she took my chair and my hundred and fourteen with a Jar she bought the very same afternoon. One Bowl short. I won the Deed by one grain and the Bowl a lot later, and the Bowl was one lot too late. A grain over and never two is a fine rule until the other side has five hundred grain and a memory as long as mine. So I'm the Gentry again, back in the fields where I started, with thirty-three grain and a province that lost money in every average year. I begged a Wall and three fields off Graham for thirty-five, and now the land pays its own way from next harvest. I promised him no Deed under fifty. Reina paid exactly fifty and walked off with his throne inside the same auction, so I kept my promise to the letter and it did him no good at all. I'll write that down as a lesson in how narrow a promise ought to be. Silke sits one Deed from any chair she likes, and I hold the Deeds. She's hoping the price comes down from fifty, and I can't think why it should. I have twenty-five and a half grain, one Bowl with two soldiers walking towards it, and fifteen a year in rent. I've started from the bottom before. The difference is that this time everyone at the table learned the trick from me.
