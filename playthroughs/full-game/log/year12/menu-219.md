## Your move: the Wall over Province 3, and new land

**The Wall.** Graham offers to build all four links of the Great Wall above Province 3 this year, the crown paying 140 and you 20. It's negotiated: you may agree, refuse, or make a counter-offer. With all four links standing, your strip below the Wall opens for new land at once.

**New land (Section 13), if the Wall goes up.** Each square costs 20 to the Harvest Pile and adds 20 to your harvest every year from next year. Graham has said the crown pays 15 of every 20 on every other province's squares this year, so a square costs you 5. Squares you could then buy: G9–G11 (the strip below the Wall touching Province 3; each new square opens the next ring). Buying nothing is also a choice.

Your grain now: 48 (28 after your 20 toward the Wall).

Reply with `move: wall agree | wall refuse | wall counter <what you'd pay>; land none | land <squares>; I pay <n>, asking the crown for <n>`.
