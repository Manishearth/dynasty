move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "Harvest's out of the Pile, friends: the fields come out even this year, and His Majesty, Reina and Rob pay in what the times-three row says they owe. Rent is five each from Clark, Noah and Paddy, as it was in my old minutes and in Reina's, because a promise made in Year Two is still a promise. Your Majesty, there are four links of Wall over Province 3 waiting to be built, and the Gentry would be very glad to discuss how we share them."
aside: "I have thirty-three grain, a single Bowl and a province losing twenty-five a year, so the fifteen in rent and the price I charge Reina for a Deed are all that's keeping me afloat. Silke's right that there's one buyer who matters, and that buyer has five hundred and eighty grain and a very good memory for minimums."
whisper: none
