move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "Right so, a bad year all round, and here's the harvest as the tables have it: ten to Silke, Rich and myself, thirty each to Graham, Reina, Noah and Clark, and nothing to Rob. Rent's five apiece, the same as I promised, and it'll stay at five while I'm in this chair. Noah, I've not forgotten you were always fair with me, and I'd sooner pay an honest price for one of your Bowls than see either of them sit beside the board all year."
aside: "Five's the least I can ask, and I'm keeping it there on purpose, because the only thing between me and the whole set is a Wooden Bowl and Noah's the man selling them. I'll be watching Silke as well, though. Six hundred grain buys a lot of Bowls if she decides she doesn't want me to have one."
