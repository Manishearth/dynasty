## Rob's diary so far, in his own words

## Setup–Year 2

Diary of Robert Land, Warlord of the Western Waste. Years One and Two.

They gave me sand and four lads, and nobody else at the table had a single soldier. I parked my boys one step from everyone's border and called them scenery, and everyone laughed. Nobody's laughing now. I've refused the crown's tax twice. Clark has me on page one, line one, in very neat handwriting, and I've never been so proud. My raids have brought in forty grain without paying a single toll. Rich, bless him, bought every jar and bowl in China and walked up to the throne with them, which crosses him off my list. I'll miss visiting his wheat. Poor Reina is now the Gentry in Province 3, with two of my lads standing in her back field. The pieces are on the board now: Clark's strongbox, Noah's Customs House, Paddy's Jar and Silke's Vase out by the sea. Every one of them is a few days' march from a soldier of mine. I've hired a fifth lad and stood him beside Graham's new reservoir, and he's waiting for Graham to finally buy something worth taking. I'm eighty grain up, the villain of the piece, and I've never had a better time in my life.

## Years 3–4

Years Three and Four. Two more refusals for the crown's ledger, three grain and then six, and I'd have paid both twice over just to watch Clark write them down. Then I took his Land Deed Scroll right off J15, the desert's first real trophy, and set it on my sand like a mantelpiece ornament. Last year I told them to put their treasures somewhere pretty, and they went and did it, bless them. Graham stacked his whole set in a row and climbed into Clark's chair with it, so the man who keeps my page is new, but the page hasn't changed. Year Four the gods broke my legs and kept me out of the south, so I did my shopping up north instead. Noah told the whole table he'd noticed W3 at his door, and the very next march W3 walked out with his Customs House. It's on F8 now, next to the Deed. That's two names crossed off. W1 is a step from Reina's Bowl, W2 is standing in Graham's new garden, and I've a hundred and twenty-five grain and not one pot I paid for. The rest of them spend their grain at auction, and I get mine on the march.

## Years 5–6

Years Five and Six. Two more refusals in the crown's ledger, and the ledger has had three different keepers in two years. The chair passes round this table faster than the gravy. Noah put his treasures on the top row and told me my lad couldn't reach them, which was true, and then he was out of the chair before the ink dried. Then Reina was in it, and then Silke was, and every one of them walked off with the last one's purse. Meanwhile W4 stepped onto Q25 and picked up Paddy's Dragon Jar like he was collecting the post. It's on T22 now, the third ornament on my mantel, and Paddy is crossed off. Year Six a lieutenant ran off with my daughter, so the lads spent a round on compassionate leave. I watched the shelves fill up and didn't spend a single grain. Silke has stood her whole set in a column down the west edge, where Graham kept his, and W2 is two steps away and very rested. Graham and Reina have empty shelves, so W3 is strolling toward Rich's new Winter Palace in case either of them is shy about buying. Three crossed off, three to go, a hundred and seventy grain, and I still haven't paid for a single pot.


## Years 7–8

Years Seven and Eight. I didn't bid on a single lot in two years and I came out of both of them richer. The rest of them took turns pouring grain into the Pile while I sat back with my hands folded. The chair went round twice. Silke climbed onto the throne with a full set and Rich's whole purse, and a year later Paddy took Noah's seat, purse and all, so poor Noah has been caught holding a fat purse and nothing else for the third time. Every new owner moved their treasures as far from the desert as the map allows. I take that as a compliment. The trouble for them is that "far back" in Province 4 is two steps from W3, who is standing on K20 looking at Graham's Bowl the way a cat looks at a fishmonger. W4 is two steps from Reina's new Brush in her bottom corner, and since Rich hasn't got a single pot to his name, W2 is walking down to pay my respects at the Winter Palace instead. My lads came home with five grain every march and paid no tolls. Three names crossed off and three to go, all of them within a few steps, and two hundred and thirty-two grain in the tent. The desert doesn't buy, it collects, and next year it collects in bulk.
