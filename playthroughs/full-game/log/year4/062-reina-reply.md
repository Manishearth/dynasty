move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "A good harvest, friends: sixty to every province, eighty to Graham for his water, sixty to His Majesty for his canal, and twenty to Rob, who has very sore legs this year, poor darling. Rent stays at five. I said it would stay there while the furrows and I stay friends, and we're still friends. My chef got a lovely send-off, by the way. I cried into the soup."
aside: "A good year is exactly when a greedy landlord would squeeze, so the peasants will notice that I didn't. That five is buying me a quiet countryside for the year Rich gets careless with his tax."
