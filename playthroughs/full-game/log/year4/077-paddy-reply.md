diary: Two more years and I've still only the one Jar out at Q25, but I'm not crying over it. The first year I bid small and stood aside on the Deed for Silke, and then Graham walked in and bought the Customs House, the Deed and the Brush in one go. This year I put forty grain on the last Deed in Reina's cart, more than I've ever risked on anything, and Clark beat me by one. Then Graham showed his set and took Clark's seat, and that Deed went straight back to stock, so Clark paid forty-one for nothing and the Deed's for sale again. I won't say I laughed, but I didn't cry either. My Bowls did me proud: Reina paid nine and Graham twenty-three, and my quarter of those went in the purse. With two good harvests I've gone from twenty-five grain to near eighty, which is more than I've ever had. Rob's lads had sore legs this year and left my paddies alone, but W4 is still on Q24, one step from my Jar, and I've not forgotten it. Noah still owes me a favour. Next year I'm going after that Deed again, and I'll be keeping a purse for the new land when the people double.
