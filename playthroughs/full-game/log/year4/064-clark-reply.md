move: collect as declared
say: "Eighteen from me, paid first as usual, and eighteen each from Reina, Noah, Silke and Paddy. Graham, twenty-four. A hundred and fourteen for the crown, counted twice. Rob, six grain, and since you asked so nicely, page one now reads: 'Year Three, three grain unpaid. Land Deed Scroll taken from J15.' I've kept a line free for 'returned'. And Your Majesty, a fine Wall, all four links, right over your own palace again. The province beside it has a Wall too, you know, but it's only a line on a map. When you get round to building that one, I'll write you down on the credit side."
aside: "Rob took my Deed, so I'm back to two pieces short. Rich has built the canal and the Wall on his own doorstep, and I'm going to keep saying so in public until it costs him more to ignore me than to build my four links."
whisper: none
