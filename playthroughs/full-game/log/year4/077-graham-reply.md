diary: Years 3 and 4. I stopped being polite and it worked, though it cost me. Ten for the Customs House, eighteen for the Deed after Silke and I tied at fourteen. I wasn't going to lose a second tie to where I sit, so I raised, and I'm sorry it was her. Then ten for the Brush. The next year I bid nineteen for the Jar while Reina tried to keep it from me, and twenty-three for the Bowl, one grain over Noah. Five pieces and forty-five and a half grain, and I showed the set right there in the hall. I took Clark's seat, the lowest one I was allowed, and his seventy-eight grain with it. He's in my furrows now, with my Reservoir, paying Reina five and probably paying the Emperor's tax to me. I've got the pen he used to write me down with. My pieces sit at the back of Province 2 on the G column, out of reach, although Rob's lad stepped onto I15 the moment I arrived and took five off me to say hello. Now I need one Vase and twenty grain, and I've a hundred and twenty-odd. I said I'd remember the price, and I've paid it.
