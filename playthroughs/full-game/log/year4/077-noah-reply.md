diary: Diary of Noah Tall, Scholar, the end of Year Four. In Year Three I took Silke's Jar for ten, the number I'd said out loud, and agreed in public to leave Clark the Vase and Reina the next Jar. Neither cost me much, and both kept the grain out of the Pile. In Year Four I bid hard for the first time, went to twenty-two on the Bowl and lost it to Graham by a single grain, then took the Vase for fifteen. For about an hour I was one Bowl from a seat. Then Rob did what I'd told the whole table he would: his lad on M21 stepped onto L22 and carried my Customs House off to the desert, where it sits on F8 beside Clark's old Deed. So I need a Customs House and a Bowl again, but I've 102.5 grain after Rich's six, which is more than double what I started the year with, and the Jar, Deed and Vase are all tucked in the corner at J21, K22 and J22. Graham is the Official now and Clark is growing wheat, which shows how quickly this table turns over. I still owe Paddy his favour, and next year I'll put my pieces where the arithmetic says the boots can't reach, not just where they look tidy.
