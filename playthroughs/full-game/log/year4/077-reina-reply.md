diary: Two years in the back field, and I've learned what Rich knew all along: the throne is bought in the auction hall, not in the fields. I kept the rent at five through a lean year and a fat one, so not one peasant has a reason to sign anything against me, and I let Noah have his Jar for a promise in the minutes, which is cheaper than grain and lasts longer. Then I stopped being polite. The Bowl went up on I8 under the Wall, and the Customs House and the Brush on L9 and M9, where Rob's boys can't reach them, which makes three of five. I lost the Jar to Graham by one grain, and I watched him walk off with Clark's seat and Clark's pen the same way Rich walked off with mine, so I'm not the only one at this table who learns quickly. Rob has started carrying pieces off to his dunes now, so I'm glad I built behind the Wall. One Jar is left in Silke's cart, and it's promised to me. After that I need a Vase and twenty grain, and then, Rich darling, we'll have a civilised conversation about that chair.
