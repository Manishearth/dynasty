diary: Years Three and Four. Two more refusals for the crown's ledger, three grain and then six, and I'd have paid both twice over just to watch Clark write them down. Then I took his Land Deed Scroll right off J15, the desert's first real trophy, and set it on my sand like a mantelpiece ornament. Last year I told them to put their treasures somewhere pretty, and they went and did it, bless them. Graham stacked his whole set in a row and climbed into Clark's chair with it, so the man who keeps my page is new, but the page hasn't changed. Year Four the gods broke my legs and kept me out of the south, so I did my shopping up north instead. Noah told the whole table he'd noticed W3 at his door, and the very next march W3 walked out with his Customs House. It's on F8 now, next to the Deed. That's two names crossed off. W1 is a step from Reina's Bowl, W2 is standing in Graham's new garden, and I've a hundred and twenty-five grain and not one pot I paid for. The rest of them spend their grain at auction, and I get mine on the march.
