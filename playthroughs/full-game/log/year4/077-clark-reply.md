diary: Diary of Clark Grafton, late Official to the Crown and now Wheat Peasant of Province 7, end of Year 4. I collected a hundred and eighty-three grain for the crown over these two years, paid my own share first each time, and Rob paid nothing, which takes page one down to line four. In Year 3 I settled the Vase in public and got it for four grain, the cheapest thing I've ever bought, and then Rob's lad walked onto J15 and carried my Land Deed off to a dune. In Year 4 I stayed out of the Jar as I'd promised and paid forty-one for the last Deed in the cart, and before the ink was dry Graham laid down a full set and took my seat, my pen, my seventy-eight grain and every piece I owned, the forty-one-grain Deed included. I'd forgotten the Brush tie and he hadn't: that's the lesson, and it's written in my hand this time. Rich built his canal and his Wall over his own palace and never built a link over mine, and that's noted too. I've sixty grain from the Pile, a reservoir on O16 and no pieces at all, and I still have the ledger. Graham has the pen now; let's see how he likes it when Rob doesn't pay him either.
