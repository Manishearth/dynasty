move: counter: I'll stand aside on Silke's Customs House this year and you stand aside on Noah's Deed, and next year we swap the favour: you keep out of the next Customs House for me, and I keep out of the last Deed for you.

say: "That's a fair offer, Clark, and I'll shake on it with one small addition so neither of us ends up a piece short. You take the Customs House today and I take the Deed. Next year you leave the next Customs House to me and I leave Noah's last Deed to you. That way we both finish, and none of it goes into the Pile."

aside: "Clark's got near twice my grain, so I'd likely lose the Customs House to him anyway. This way I get the Deed at Noah's eight, and Clark's promise gets me first go at the next Customs House."

whisper: none

Ruling: a counter. Clark answers it (128) before the lots are settled.
