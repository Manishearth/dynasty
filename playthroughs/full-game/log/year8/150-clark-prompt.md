Rob: "The desert passes again, friends. Rich, Reina, you're both shopping from the bottom, and I'm rooting for you. Buy something lovely and put it on show. Graham, give my regards to that Vase. Someone will be by to see it shortly."

Paddy places the Customs House on S22, then PADDY TAKES NOAH'S SEAT. Noah holds nothing, draws 60 from the Harvest Pile and is now the Rice Peasant, in Province 6; Province 6's Bowls are his to sell. Paddy now pays out the harvest and asks the rent; his new objective is a Wooden Bowl and 20 grain.

The rest of the Year 8 lots, settled from the same limits (none of Noah's is above the 60 he now holds): Paddy's (formerly Noah's) Land Deed to Clark for 19 (Graham stopped at 18, Reina 16, Rich 14); Graham's Brush, Reina 19 against Noah 18: close; Reina's Jar to Graham for 21 (Rich and Noah stopped at 20); Noah's (formerly Paddy's) Bowl, Graham 20 against Reina 20, Graham ahead by seat order: close; Clark's Vase to Reina for 19 (Noah stopped at 18, Rich 14). The two close lots get one live raise each.

Paddy: "Four pieces in a row on M10 to M13, well back from the desert. Rob, you can admire them through a telescope. Noah, the Bowls are yours to sell now, and they're good ones. I should know, I've been selling them for eight years."

Reina: "Well. I learned something about the rules this year, and it cost me fifty grain, which is about my usual rate. Noah darling, I'm sorry, and I'm sorry it was my push that put the sums in front of him. I'm not leaving empty-handed twice, though. Twenty-four for the Brush and twenty-six for the Bowl, and then I'll stop, I promise."

Noah: "I said eighteen and not a grain more, so it's yours, Reina. Paddy, there's nothing to apologise for. I told you I owed you a favour, and the arithmetic says I've just paid it at a hundred and eighty-one grain, which is about a hundred and sixty more than I'd planned. That's the third time I've been caught sitting on a full purse with nothing in hand, and I've only got myself to blame."

Graham: "I've lost two lots at twenty against twenty-one, Reina, both of them in the last two years. I'm not losing a third by a single grain. Twenty-six."
