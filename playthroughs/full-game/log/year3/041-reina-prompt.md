Two years, and I planned all of them except the one that counted. I kept the tax gentle, put my palace where Rob's boots couldn't reach it, and gave Graham a reservoir so no peasant would ever sign a revolt against me. I was watching the fields for a rebellion and never once looked at the auction hall, where Rich was quietly buying a whole set, one polite grain at a time. He handed over five trinkets and twenty grain and walked off with everything I'd saved, all eighty of it, and he said "civilised" while he did it. Rich, my dear, I learn quickly, and the Land Deeds are mine to sell.
