move: (diary)
Years 9–10. Two years, and for once I watched every cart. In the rotten year I paid fifty to keep a Customs House from Graham and lost it by a grain. Then I sat and watched him take the throne off Silke with it, and watched Clark take Paddy's chair with the Jar I'd sold him for a hundred. That made three seats gone inside one market, and I learned the lesson without paying for it. So in the good year I went to market for three pieces and paid over everyone's fourteens and sixteens: twenty-nine, forty-one and thirty-three, and I came home with a full set on my coast. I didn't wait a round, because this table has shown me twice what waiting costs. I could see Paddy's Vase was going to Clark, and a Gentry with a full set can take any chair here, the throne included, so I took his seat one lot early, and his three hundred and eighty with it. Clark called it civilised and wrote it under "admired", and I'll take that. Now I'm back on the M row with four pieces, where I built in Year Three. I hold the Pile and the rent book again, and I'm one Dragon Jar from a set that reaches Graham's six hundred. Rob's W1 is two steps from my row, Rich has the only Jar on the board, and Clark sells the Jars now. Clark darling, I'll be very polite to you this spring.
