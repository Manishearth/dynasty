Years 9 and 10. I read a bad harvest aloud and then had the best year of my life in it. The Customs House cost me fifty-one, because Reina went to fifty to keep it from me, and the Deed cost thirty-one. That left sixty and a half grain and five pieces, and I didn't wait one more lot. I showed the set and took Silke's throne and her four hundred and ninety. I'd do it again, and I'm still sorry it was her. Within the hour Clark had Paddy's chair, and a year later Reina had Clark's, four hundred and seventy grain and all. She's one Jar short of mine now. I taxed thirty percent and paid Rich and Silke eight each, so nobody has a docked salary to hold against me. I built Province 3 a Reservoir for eighty to win Clark's good will, and Reina has the use of it now. My Prince broke an ivory figure for ten, and Rob's W3 walked across the river to stand by the Winter Palace, took ten and paid me a five toll, as if that made it civil. The reserve is six hundred and eighteen. For years I kept count of what everyone took from me. Now I count who could take it. Reina needs one Jar, Rich needs two pieces, and Silke has nothing yet and knows exactly what this chair is worth.
