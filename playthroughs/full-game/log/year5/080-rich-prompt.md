Graham took Clark's seat tonight with exactly the set I once used myself, and Reina told the whole table he'd played the auction the way Rich taught us. That was a compliment, and it was also a warning. Clark is in the wheat now with sixty grain and a long memory, and the ledger and its pen have gone to Graham. That means I'll have to start over on someone else's credit side, at eight a year. Reina has her Customs House and her Brush tucked under the Wall, and she has wanted my chair since the day I took hers. Noah needs only a Bowl and twenty grain, and he says so out loud, which is braver than I'd be. Rob still owes the crown nine grain, has two pieces of other men's treasure on his dunes, and has yet to look at my palace. I'll keep smiling and keep paying, and I'll keep a full granary for the day someone comes to the door with a full set.
