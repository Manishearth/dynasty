## Years 3–4

That same year he took the Customs House and the Brush as well. This year I went to thirty-five for the last Deed in Reina's cart. Clark paid forty-one for it, and four lots later Graham showed a full set and took Clark's seat, so the Deed Clark bled for went straight back to stock. So the last Deed is back in the cart, and that is the one thread I'm watching. My Jars at least paid me: Noah gave ten, Graham nineteen, and Reina pushed the second one up herself trying to keep it from him. I lost every lot I bid on and kept every word I gave. I set a minimum of twelve on that Jar and the table paid it, which tells me my purse is worth more than they think. The harvest has filled me back up to eighty-odd grain. Rob's legs keep him off my coast this year, though not for long. Next year I stop bidding small and honest. I bid where it counts, and I make the rich pay the Pile to get past me.
