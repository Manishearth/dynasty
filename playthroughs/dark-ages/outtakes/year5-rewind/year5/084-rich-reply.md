move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: Paid out to the grain, friends: fifteen to Her Majesty, thirty-five to Clark, Silke and Paddy, fifty-five to the Gentry's humble granary, and ten to Rob. And the rent stays the customary five, a dry year being no excuse for a thirsty landlord. Paddy, I hope that buys the Gentry a little goodwill on the coast. Her Majesty has kept her word in ordinary years, and so has the Gentry.
aside: Rob's soldier is one round from my Bowl and Paddy's vote is for sale, so this is the year to keep both peasants fond of me and not squeeze them. Five grain each costs me nothing next to having friends on the day someone calls a revolt.
whisper: none
