The Director had moved the tip to Silke and the pay-or-revolt round to Year 2, but the change arrived after both had already gone out in Year 1. Rob then called a revolt on "the Emperor can't control the Warlord", with Silke and Paddy agreeing, and Reina answered it: the tax kept at 50%, 10 each to the coast, the Winter Palace instead of the Summer.

This folder holds the cut lines of game.py, the cut table talk, seen.json from before the rewind, and log 005–010 (prompts and replies). In the game, log numbers from 005 on are reused. Every seat that heard any of it gets a fresh agent at its next prompt.
