move: pay
say: Thirty, Your Majesty, gladly, and may the Summer Palace have a window facing north-west so you can see your most loyal province. Half is a brave number for Year One, but a brave crown is a strong crown, and I'm always for strength. Clark, count it twice. I'd hate for anyone to say the Gentry came up short.
aside: I pay, and I make a show of it, because a revolt in Year One hands the reserve to whoever shouts loudest, and right now that's probably the man with the soldiers. But half the harvest is steep, and I want everyone to remember I paid it smiling. Loyalty is cheapest to buy early and worth the most later.
