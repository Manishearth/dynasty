# Narration

The narrator's lines, chapter by chapter: the chapter title, each year's opening line, and the turning points, each tagged with the log number it follows. The narrator isn't part of the game, so none of this goes into `game.py` or the playthrough page; the page must make sense without it.

## Chapter 1

- Title: **Chapter One: Agreeing Is Not Deposing.**

- (002) Year 1: The first year of Reina's reign. Sixty each, no card, two soldiers at the edges of the map, and an Emperor who keeps the stars herself, unpaid.
- (004) Half the harvest, in the first year. A hundred and twenty grain goes north, and a palace goes up on G20.
- (011) Six, seven, seven: three lots to the same bidder, and the Official holds a complete set in his first year. He has sixteen grain and a half; the set wants twenty.
- (013) Year 1 ends with the reserve at 155, one complete set, one soldier two steps from it, and the coast five grain lighter.
- (014) Year 2: The second year of Reina's reign. Spring floods: the north-west, the crown and the rice fields drink well; the Official and the silk coast drown. A complete set sits in Province 2 with twenty-six grain beside it.
- (023) The Warlord names himself as the cause, and two hands go up beside his. For the first time in this dynasty, the table stops.
- (028) Forty grain, a postponed palace and half a reservoir. The Gentry's hand comes down, and so does Silke's. The Warlord's stays up, alone.
- (035) Eleven and eleven. Two years, two complete sets, both north of the river; the throne has stayed where it was.
- (038) Year 2 ends with the reserve at 220, two sets on the board, two soldiers bought from the man they guard against, and a reservoir promised to the province that decided the vote.

## Chapter 2

- Title: **Chapter Two: Paid in Full and in Public.**

- (045) Year 3: The third year of Reina's reign. Careless Prosperity: a full harvest for everyone, a small misfortune for everyone, and two complete sets still sitting on their shelves.
- (055) Two hands go up on the third fifty percent: the coast and the tent. The other three stay on the table, and the Official collects.
- (059) Two steps, straight on, at the same moment. The Warlord ends the year richer than anyone at the table but the crown, and without a single soldier.
- (060) Year 4: The fourth year of Reina's reign. Imperial Charity: two grain to every subject, from a reserve of three hundred and thirty-six. The Warlord has no army; the table still has its grievance.
- (069) The tent calls it, the coast backs it, and the Gentry names his price in the same breath as his vote. For the second time in this dynasty, the table stops.
- (074) Forty grain, the Reservoir's cost to the grain. The Gentry's hand comes down again; two stay up. The rate has not moved in four years.
- (076) Year 4 ends with two palaces standing side by side, a reserve of 341, one new soldier on M15, and a rice farmer's deadline on the record.
