move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "The Pile pays out, friends, and what a year to pay it! Her Majesty forty, sixty apiece to Clark, Silke, Paddy and my humble self, twenty to our friend in the tent. Silke, Paddy, the customary five and not a grain more. A fat harvest is no excuse for a fat landlord, whatever certain people may be writing down."
aside: "Five from a peasant with ninety in hand is nothing, and that's the point: if this table ever comes down to counting friends, I want both of them remembering the year the Gentry could have squeezed and didn't. My set's complete, so I can afford to look generous."
whisper: none
