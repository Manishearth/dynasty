diary: Two years on the throne, and I nearly lost it to a man in a tent who has never paid me one grain. The Summer Palace stands, the reserve is up to 220, and I held the rate at fifty without bending. But the price was the Winter Palace, forty grain handed to Rob for his own soldiers, and a promise of forty more for Rich's reservoir. Rich decided that vote, and he knows it. Now he and Clark both hold complete sets, and the way Rich bid up those trinkets tells me he is buying a claim, not ornaments. Clark held the line for me and wrote it in his book, and a loyal man who keeps a ledger will expect to be paid for it eventually. Next year I keep my word on the reservoir, keep the coast guarded so Silke stays mine, and watch the man with the Customs House. The monuments will have to wait a season, but they are only waiting.
