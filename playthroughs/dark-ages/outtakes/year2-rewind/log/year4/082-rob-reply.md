diary: Two years, two revolts, and both soldiers lost on a single night. Clark walked onto I14 and Silke onto Q24, and I'd more or less walked my lads into their hands, so I'll take that one on the chin. I still haven't raided a single piece, and I've still never paid the crown a grain. A hundred and two in the tent, which puts me second only to the reserve and Rich. Rich has now been paid twice for the same Reservoir and has sold my revolt twice, and he's the reason the new lad stands on M15, a step or two from his Bowl and his Jar with no guard of his own anywhere near. Silke stood by me to the end this time, and Paddy has promised the whole table he'll agree next year if nothing gets built on the coast. So next spring I'll ask again, and this time I'll have Silke and Paddy, and Reina can try buying Rich a third Reservoir.
