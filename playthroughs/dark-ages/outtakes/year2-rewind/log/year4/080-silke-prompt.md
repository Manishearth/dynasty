## At the table since your last turn

Rob: "I'm standing by it, of course. Forty to Rich and not a grain off the rate for anyone else. Silke, that answer was for him, not for you. Rich, you've been paid twice for the same Reservoir now. Lovely work if you can get it. Let's see whether it buys you twice."

Rich: "Paid in full and in public. Your Majesty is a woman of her word, and so am I. I take it back, Rob, just as I said I would. Clark, enter it beside last year's: the Gentry's price was posted, paid and honoured, every grain."

Rich takes the revolt back; Rob and Silke stand by it. With only two behind it, the revolt is dropped and play resumes. Clark collects the 50%: 27.5 from Rich and 17.5 each from Clark, Silke and Paddy, 80 to the crown; Rob refuses his.
