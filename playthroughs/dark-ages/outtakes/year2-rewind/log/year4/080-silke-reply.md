diary: Four years in, I've paid the crown eighty-two and a half and Rich twenty in rent, and I'm sitting on fifty-nine and a half while the reserve sits on three hundred and forty-one. I called the excessive-tax revolt in Year 3 and nobody backed me. I backed Rob's in Year 4, and that time Reina paid Rich forty to walk away and paid me nothing. That's twice now my landlord has been bought off a cause he admitted was real. The one good night was the beach: my soldier, bought with the crown's own twenty, took Rob's man on Q24, and for a whole season mine and Clark's were the only soldiers on the board. Now I've learned that calling it alone makes me look shrill, and that Reina will always buy Rich before she buys me. So next year I won't be the one calling it. Paddy will be. He's promised it in public and he's promised me in private, and if nothing gets built south of my beach, I'll be standing right behind him with the only soldier on the coast.
