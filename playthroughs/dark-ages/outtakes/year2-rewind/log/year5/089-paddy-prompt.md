## Paddy's diary so far, in his own words

## Setup to Year 2

Two years in, and I've paid the crown sixty, Rich ten, and Rob five off my own front field. It hurts more than the sixty did, because nobody asked for it. Heaven gave us a good harvest in the floods, and I'm thankful, but I've learned that a good year mostly means more people know your address. I wouldn't back Rob's revolt and I'm still glad of it. A man doesn't hand the keys to the fellow trying the lock. Still, I noticed the crown only found its purse once the shouting started, and none of that money came south. I've a Customs House and a Land Deed tucked away at the back of the rice, and every Dragon Jar is now in Clark's hands or Rich's. Clark counts too cleverly for my liking, so if a jar ever comes my way, I'd sooner it came from Rich, honestly traded. Next year I'll keep my grain close, watch that boat on Q24, and see whether the crown means to build anything on the coast or only talk about it.

## Years 3–4

Two more years at half, and that's another forty-seven and a half to the crown and ten to Rich, though I'll grant a fat harvest and an average one made it easier to bear than it sounds. Twice I was asked to pull the roof down and twice I said no. The first time it was because Rob would have counted the soldiers. The second time it was because I'd given Reina a year, out loud, and a man doesn't take that back in the same evening. Then I watched Rich agree, name his price, get paid forty in front of us all and walk it straight back. That taught me more about this table than four years of ledgers did: the crown has grain enough to feed the coast, and it feeds whoever makes the most noise in the north. Clark got a raise, Rich got his reservoir twice over, Reina got a Winter Palace, and the south got two grain of charity. Rob's lost both his soldiers and hired one back, and Silke's boat-thief is in her keeping, so for now the coast is quieter than it's been. Next year I'll pay my rent and watch what the crown builds. If nothing goes up south of Silke's beach, I'll call the cause myself, the way I said I would, with Silke beside me and not Rob. And I still want a Dragon Jar from an honest hand. Rich's is the only one I'd trust, and now I know what his price looks like.
