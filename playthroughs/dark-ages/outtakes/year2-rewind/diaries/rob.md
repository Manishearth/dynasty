# Robert Land's diary

## Setup to Year 2

Two years and I've never paid the crown a grain, and I've finished up richer than anyone at the table except the reserve itself, eighty-five in the tent. My revolt nearly took. Silke and Rich both signed it, and then Reina bought them back with a promised Reservoir and twenty grain apiece, and two of those coins came to me when I sold my own lads to guard against me. Best joke of the night, and I got paid for it. I haven't raided a single piece yet, and that's the real bill. Clark's put my old soldier on his Deed, so W1 goes for the Bowl at I15 and then turns east to Rich's lovely new set at L14 and L10, while W2 creeps south toward Paddy's Deed at S20 and stays out of reach of Silke's boy. Rich went back on the revolt the moment a Reservoir was offered, so I'll remember that when his crops come in, and I'll call the next one the moment that Reservoir's late.

## Years 3–4

Two years, two revolts, and both soldiers lost on a single night. Clark walked onto I14 and Silke onto Q24, and I'd more or less walked my lads into their hands, so I'll take that one on the chin. I still haven't raided a single piece, and I've still never paid the crown a grain. A hundred and two in the tent, which puts me second only to the reserve and Rich. Rich has now been paid twice for the same Reservoir and has sold my revolt twice, and he's the reason the new lad stands on M15, a step or two from his Bowl and his Jar with no guard of his own anywhere near. Silke stood by me to the end this time, and Paddy has promised the whole table he'll agree next year if nothing gets built on the coast. So next spring I'll ask again, and this time I'll have Silke and Paddy, and Reina can try buying Rich a third Reservoir.
