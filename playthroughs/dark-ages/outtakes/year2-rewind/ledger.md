# Character ledger

Kept current at the end of each round. What each character has promised, offered, refused and resented, their running bits, and their best lines so far. Then the Storyteller's rulings, as precedents. Line numbers like (009) are log entries in `log/yearN/`. A launch prompt carries each character's entry (`briefs.py` finds it by the full name at the start of the heading), so keep entries short and current.

*Last updated: end of Year 4 (end of Chapter 2).*

## Reina Osorio — Emperor (Province 1), drew the Emperor (1)

- **Playing it** (a note on playing your character, from Year 4): Proud, and in a hurry to build the dynasty's own monuments: the palaces, the Pagoda and the Great Wall over the north. She taxes very hard while the fields are full, and the provinces' canals can wait for the day the crown is rich. The Warlord's raids are the provinces' business, not the crown's. A crown that bends to a complaint is a crown that breaks, so she answers a grievance with dignity, never with a cut.
- Setup/Year 1: reads the I Ching herself, unpaid: "the crown will read Heaven's book itself" (002). Taxed 50% on the first harvest ("an empire that waits for lean years to build will never build at all"), built the Summer Palace on G20, paid Clark 5 (004). Aside: watching Rich, "his flattery is too smooth". Reserve 155.
- Year 2: read Spring Floods (014); taxed **50% again** ("so nobody can say the crown keeps changing its mind"), declared the Winter Palace (016). **Answered Rob's revolt** without cutting the rate: put off the Winter Palace, paid Silke and Clark 20 each for soldiers against Rob, and **promised the Province 3 Reservoir in Year 3, crown paying 40 of 80** (025). Silke and Rich took the revolt back. Paid Clark 5. Reserve 220.
- Year 3: read Careless Prosperity (045); taxed **50% a third time** ("in a year as fat as this nobody can call it hard") and **kept the Reservoir promise**: built it on K11:L13, crown 40, Rich 40 (047, 056). The Winter Palace waits again (aside: "next year it gets built"). Silke and Rob called a revolt on the tax; nobody else agreed, so she never had to answer it. Paid Clark 5. Reserve 331.
- Year 4: read Imperial Charity and paid everyone 2 (060). Taxed **50% a fourth time**, built the **Winter Palace on G21**, raised Clark to 15 (062, 075). **Answered Rob's called revolt by paying Rich 40** (his Reservoir share back) and keeping the rate (071): "A crown that bends whenever someone complains will snap." Aside: Rich has learned his vote has a price. Reserve 341.
