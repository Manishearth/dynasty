diary: Two Emperors in two years, and neither of them took the throne with a sword. Rich took it with a set halfway through the tax, and Clark took it off him the same way a year later, and both times the rest of us paid our twelve or our seven and said thank you. I paid every grain I owed again: rent to Rich and Reina, tax to the crown. The canal is finally being dug through Province 6, eighty grain of it, and Clark kept that promise when it would have been easy to let it die with the old chair, so I count him a man who feeds the coast until he shows me otherwise. I stood aside on the Bowl twice and kept it at the floor so Silke could finish her set, and when the Deed came up both she and Reina kept their word and let me have it for eleven. So now I'm sitting on the bottom of the coast with a Deed, a Customs House and a Jar, a hundred and one grain, and nobody holding a debt over me. I can't believe I'm writing that down. I could take Rich's chair, or Reina's, or even Silke's, but Silke has been fair with me and I won't be the one to turn her out. I'll hold the set quietly, watch where Rob walks, and only move up when the move puts more rice on the table in Province 6 than staying put would.
