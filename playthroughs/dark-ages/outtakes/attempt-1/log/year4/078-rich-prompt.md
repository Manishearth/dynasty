## At the table since your last turn

Reina: "The Deed goes up at the floor, two. Paddy, you said you'd bid honestly for it, so I won't make it cost you more than that. The Jar is the one I'm after, and I'll say so as plainly as Rich did. The Bowl I leave to whoever wants it."

Reina sets her Land Deed Scroll's minimum at 2.

Silke: "The Deed's Paddy's. I'm not bidding on it, and that's your fair word, Paddy, given in public as you asked. The Jar starts at eight; Reina, you said you're after it, so I've counted it for you. And the Bowl: Rich, you've fifty-three grain and three pieces to buy. Spend it all on the Bowl if you like, but then you'll have no Deed and no Jar, and I'll still have a set. I'm going as high as my twenty-grain reserve allows."

Silke sets her Dragon Jar's minimum at 8.

Paddy: "Thank you, Reina, and thank you, Silke. That's two fair words kept, and I'll remember both. Rich, I'm not out to cost you anything, but I've waited three years on this Deed and I'll go as high as I have to for it. The Jar's not mine to want, and the Bowl goes at the floor again, two, same as last year."

Paddy sets his Wooden Bowl's minimum at 2.

Rob: "Pass on all three. Clark, I walked halfway across Province 3 to visit your set, and you went and sat on it. Very rude. Go on, the rest of you, buy them up and put them on the board. I'll be by shortly."

Paddy takes Reina's Land Deed Scroll for 11 (Rich stopped at 10); Reina takes Silke's Dragon Jar for 15 (Rich stopped at 14); Silke takes Paddy's Wooden Bowl for 11 (Rich stopped at 10). Rob passes on all three. Each seller keeps a quarter of the price; the rest goes to the Harvest Pile.

Paddy: "Eleven grain, and I'll not pretend I'm not pleased. It goes on S23, right next to the Jar and the Customs House, down on the bottom of the coast with the rest of my things. Rob, it's a long walk, and the canal's going to be in your way."

Reina: "K17, next to the Bowl and well back from the desert. Fifteen grain, and thank you, Silke, for the price. Rob, you're welcome to come and look at it, if you can walk that far."

Silke: "P24, in a row with the Customs House and the Deed, so the coast finally has a full set. Deed, Customs House, Bowl and well over twenty grain, so count it if you like, Clark. I'm not presenting it yet. Rich, Reina, a peasant with a full set is a very polite guest, until the day she isn't."

Paddy puts the Land Deed Scroll on S23, Reina the Dragon Jar on K17 and Silke the Wooden Bowl on P24. Paddy (Customs House, Dragon Jar, Land Deed Scroll, 101 grain) and Silke (Land Deed Scroll, Customs House, Wooden Bowl, 58.5) both hold complete sets: each may replace the Official, the Gentry or the other peasant at any moment, though not the Emperor. Both hold them for now.

Rob: "Five from Province 2, Rich, a little welcome to your new chair. And Reina, you put the Jar and the Bowl next to each other. That's very thoughtful of you: I'll only have to make the one trip."

Rob marches: W1 onto J12 in Province 2, raiding 5 from Rich; W2 to M16, one step from Reina's Bowl; W3 to G11 on the strip under the Wall.

Clark: "Fifteen, Rich. You set it, and I've been on the receiving end of a promise that died with the chair, so I'm not going to do that to you. Here's next year in public so nobody has to guess: the Province 3 Reservoir, for Reina, who built the first three works and deserves a fourth. Silke, Paddy, two full sets on the coast is noted. I'm glad you're polite guests, because this crown means to be a good host."

At the year's end Clark pays Rich 15 as the Official. Clark has promised the Province 3 Reservoir next year.
