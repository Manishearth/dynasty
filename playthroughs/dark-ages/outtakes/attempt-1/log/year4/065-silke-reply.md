move: Pay the 7.

say: "Seven, paid. Twenty is fair, and I'll say so as easily as I'd say forty wasn't. Three Emperors in four years, and none of them from the coast. Now let me count the auction: a Wooden Bowl's going back on the block. Reina already has hers, Clark is sitting on the throne, and Clark gave his word on his own page not to bid on the next Bowl. That Bowl is the last piece I need. Anyone who bids against me had better want it more than I do, and nobody here does."

aside: "I pay the seven because a fair tax isn't worth a revolt, and I'm saving my grain for that Bowl. Seventy-three in hand less seven leaves me sixty-six, so I can go to forty-six and still keep the twenty the set needs. The only person who might bid against me now is Paddy, and he asked me for a fair word."

whisper: none

---

Ruling: pays 7; no revolt.
