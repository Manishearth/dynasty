diary: Three Emperors in four years, and every one of them bought the chair with a cupboard of crockery. Rich took it in Year 3 and Clark took it off him a year later, before the tax was even in. I spent most of Year 3 walking towards Clark's set under the Wall, and the moment I got close he climbed onto the throne and his pieces went back in the box. That's a whole march wasted, and I'll admit I was a little impressed. Still, I passed every auction, let the rest of them pour their grain into the Pile, took five off Reina and five off Rich, refused my tax for the fourth year running, and I'm sitting on eighty-odd grain with three soldiers on the board, which is more than any of these chair-swappers can say. Next year W2 steps onto Reina's Bowl, or her Jar if she moves the Bowl, and I take it into the desert. W1 comes in behind to finish the job, and W3 strolls on to the palaces to send Clark a bill for twenty. Silke and Paddy are both holding full sets and smiling politely. That's two more people who might like the chair, and two more who might back a revolt if I can show the table that this Emperor can't control his Warlord. I'd rather they all stopped trading the throne and started counting grain and soldiers, because on that count the desert does very well.
