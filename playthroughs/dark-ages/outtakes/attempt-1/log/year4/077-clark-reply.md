diary: Years Three and Four, entered in full, and the best two pages I've written. Rich took the throne halfway through Reina's tax year, just as I said he would, and in doing it he put his own Dragon Jar back on the block. I bought it from Silke for six grain. Three years of chasing, and in the end it cost me less than the Deed did. Then I waited one round, and no longer, because Rob was three steps from my Deed and Rich's chest had grown fat on his own generosity. I presented the set and took a crown with 444 in the reserve. It cost me one auction, one promise to Reina (no bid on the next Customs House or Bowl) and a second promise in private (the Province 3 Reservoir next year), and I mean to keep both, because a crown the table gives is held by friends, and Reina's name is the first one on my page. I've kept the tax at twenty, built Paddy's canal and paid Rich his fifteen, so there isn't a single cause for revolt anyone can honestly name against me, and I know every one of them by heart. What comes next: both peasants hold full sets and can't touch the throne. Rich has fifty grain and a grudge, and Rob has three soldiers and nobody who can control him, which is the one cause still open in my own book. So I build the Reservoir, I keep my palaces out of Rob's reach, and I give Rich no grievance he can write down. Three Emperors in four years. I intend to be the last.
