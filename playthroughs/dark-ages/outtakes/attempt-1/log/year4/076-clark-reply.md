move: Pay the Official, Rich, 15 for this year, the amount he set himself. Silke's appointment to read the I Ching at 5 a year stands from next year.

say: "Fifteen, Rich. You set it, and I've been on the receiving end of a promise that died with the chair, so I'm not going to do that to you. Here's next year in public so nobody has to guess: the Province 3 Reservoir, for Reina, who built the first three works and deserves a fourth. Silke, Paddy, two full sets on the coast is noted. I'm glad you're polite guests, because this crown means to be a good host."

aside: "Paying an Official I've just pushed out of the throne fifteen grain costs me less than one bad rumour, and an Official who isn't broke can't be turned into an 'in debt' cause for a revolt. The Reservoir buys me Reina's signature, and keeping the tax at twenty and getting the canals built keeps both coastal sets aimed at each other's chairs and away from mine."

whisper: none
