diary: Rich took my throne in the middle of my own tax year, smiling, with my reserve of two hundred and sixteen in his arms, and then cut the rate I'd just eased so that he'd be the one people loved. A year later Clark did the same to him. Three Emperors in four years. The lesson I take is that the chair belongs to whoever holds a set, so I'm building one: a Bowl at twenty-two and a Jar at fifteen, side by side at L16 and K17, with only the Customs House still to find. It has cost me most of what I came down with, and Rob has a soldier one step from the Bowl, so I'll have to watch my own fields as closely as I used to watch everyone else's. I asked the peasants for the floor rent and nothing more, and I told Clark I'd stand with him against any revolt for the Reservoir in Province 3. A revolt would turn my two pieces back into crockery, so I want this table quiet. Silke and Paddy both hold full sets now, and neither can touch the throne, but either could take my chair. Next year: get the Reservoir built and hold Clark to his word, buy the first Customs House that comes up, keep twenty grain in hand, and sit back on the throne with a set that nobody can argue with.
