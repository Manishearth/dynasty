diary: Two years in, and I've paid the crown nothing while everyone else paid forty percent twice. Reina's sitting on a mountain of grain, building palaces and canals, and Clark has started counting to three out loud, bless him. I've spent twenty on a third soldier and taken ten off Rich at the doorstep, and the box is empty now, so every piece I need is out on the board where I can walk to it. Next is Rich's Customs House, two steps away, then Clark's scroll under the Wall, then the long stroll down to the coast for Paddy and Silke, who think distance is a kind of armour. And if Clark ever finds two friends for his revolt, the desert will happily make three. A table with no Emperor suits me fine, and an empty throne suits me even better.
