diary: Two years in, and I'm the only man at this table holding a complete set. Twenty-one grain at the first auction bought me a key to any chair in the room, and I'd call that the best bargain since the Wall went up. Reina has taken forty percent twice now and sunk her grain into a Palace and a Canal on her own soil. Let her. Every work she finishes is furniture I'll inherit, and every forty percent puts Clark and Silke one step nearer saying "consistently" out loud. The second auction cost me nothing, since I let my Bowl bid lapse at nine and Clark got his porcelain. He's still a Jar short, though, the box is empty, and Paddy is a Deed short with Silke sitting on the last one. So I'm still the only one who can walk into the throne. Rob's man is standing on my doorstep at J10, raiding five a time. That's a nuisance today and an argument for me tomorrow: "the Emperor can't control the Warlord." Next year I keep the rent at five, keep smiling at Her Majesty, keep my grain above twenty, and watch for the moment the crown wobbles. When it does, I won't need a revolt or anybody's permission, only the right afternoon.
