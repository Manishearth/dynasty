diary: Two years in and I've paid every grain I owed: five to Rich each year, twenty-four to the crown twice, and twenty-six at auction for a Customs House and a Dragon Jar that now sit at the bottom of the coast, as far from Rob as I could put them. The flood year was kind to Province 6 and I spent most of what it gave me, which sits badly with me, but the box was emptying and it was buy then or never. I lost the Deed to Silke by a single grain because she'd sooner pay double than watch me climb, and I can't blame her much, since the water hit her and not me. So I'm one scroll short, she holds it, and next year I'll have to go to her and ask her price without owing her more than I can stand. Rich has a full set and smiles too much, Rob is creeping along the Wall, and Clark writes everything down. I'm keeping my head down, my rice dry and my word good.
