diary: Started with sixty, and I'm down to seventeen and a half. Twenty-eight went to the crown, ten to Rich, and nineteen for a Land Deed that finishes nothing for me. The floods took my harvest while Rich and Paddy got fat, and Rich walked away from the first auction with a full set like it was nothing. Reina built a Palace and a Canal in her own province, and none of it reaches the coast. Clark has started saying "consistently" out loud, and Rob says the desert votes, so I've counted three people who might back a revolt the next time the tax is forty. My set is dead, but Paddy is one Deed short and I'm holding the only one, so for once the coast has something somebody has to bid for. Next year I keep my grain, I don't pay for anyone's palaces, and I make Paddy name his price.
