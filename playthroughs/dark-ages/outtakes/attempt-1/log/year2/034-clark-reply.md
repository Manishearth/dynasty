diary: Years One and Two, entered in full. Forty percent twice running is one year short of "consistently excessive", and I've marked the page. Rob pays nothing, keeps three soldiers on the board and walks through Rich's fields for sport, which is "the Emperor can't control the Warlord" in all but name; that page is marked too. The Deed cost me 6 and the Bowl 10. I lost the last Jar to Paddy by a single grain, and I won't forget it. Rich is the danger: a full set, a fat granary and a smile for everyone, and he can step into any chair he likes, the throne included. So I don't call a revolt that only hands him the crown. I get a Jar first, from Paddy, who now owes the whole table, or from Rich, if his shelves can be made to feel crowded. I keep twenty in hand, keep paying on time, and keep the ledger. When I do move, I want three friends behind me who have already signed it.
