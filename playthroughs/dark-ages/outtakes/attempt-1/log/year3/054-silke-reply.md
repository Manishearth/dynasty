move: Raise my limit on the Wooden Bowl to 21.

say: "Twenty-one, and that's every grain I can spare, Reina. You know it, because I've just counted it out loud. Take the Bowl off me and you've paid twenty-two for a quarter of a set you can't finish. Let it go and you'll have a peasant on the upper benches who owes you one."

aside: "Forty-one in hand, twenty kept back for the set, so twenty-one is my ceiling to the grain. If Reina outbids that, she's burned her grain on spite and I'm still holding the Deed and the Customs House for next year."
