move: Pay the 12.

say: "Twelve, paid, Your new Majesty, and thank you for the raise. Ten a year buys a lot of ink. I'll enter it all: Year Three, the crown changes hands in the middle of the tax, the rate goes down and the Official's pay goes up. A very good page. And I hear there's a Dragon Jar back on the auction block this year, so nobody get too attached to it."

aside: "Rich has the crown, which means his set is gone, and that Jar he gave back to stock is the one piece I'm missing. I'll buy it, then hold the Deed, the Bowl and the Jar while Rich sits on the throne thinking he's safe."

whisper: To Reina: You were thrown off your own throne in the middle of your own tax year, and I wrote it down. When I make my move, I want your name next to mine. Back me and the Gentry's chair stays with you, with no questions about rent.

Ruling: pays 12; no revolt. Whisper to Reina filed.
