diary: Years five and six. I paid my seven and my four, collected every grain for the crown, and took my fifteen twice with a smile. Nobody called a revolt, and I'm glad I didn't, because Paddy outweighed me in grain and I had no soldiers and no cause anyone would have believed. Then the population doubled and I was five a year in the red, with my land shut behind four Wall links nobody has built. Meanwhile Clark spent three hundred and forty of the crown's grain in one afternoon on the coast: canals for Silke and land for both peasants. They each now hold a complete set and six squares of new land, which makes them the two most dangerous friends I have. The reserve is down to sixty, and that's interesting. A poor crown can't buy friends, and "the reserve is too low" is a cause that names itself. I'm sitting on about a hundred and fifty grain with nothing on the board, and Rob's man raids me every year at the Wall. Next year I press the Wall links (Reina is on my side for that), stay sweet with Silke and Paddy, and count. If the crown wobbles again, I'll want to be the richest friend in the room when it does.
