diary: Five years of walking and I finally have something to show for it. Reina's Wooden Bowl is sitting on M16 in my desert, and she's promised to remember how long the walk took, which I'll take as a compliment. I left her the Jar on purpose. One piece settles my business with Province 3, and a Gentry who's been robbed twice stops being a neighbour and starts being a crusade. Meanwhile Clark has been the best Warlord's assistant I could ask for. In two years he poured the crown from four hundred and twenty-nine down to sixty: reservoirs, canals, the coast's land paid three-quarters by him, and a little seaside estate for himself. He kept every promise, and he spent the treasury doing it, so the reserve is too low and he can't control the Warlord, and either one is a cause anyone at this table can name. I didn't call a revolt, because nobody calls one alone, and Silke and Paddy are too busy admiring their new beaches to back it just yet. The cost so far is a few tolls and four more years of everybody muttering my name, against a hundred-odd grain in hand and three soldiers on the board. Next comes the long walk to the coast, W2 to Paddy's scroll first and Silke's pieces after, with W3 working down towards the palaces to send Clark a bill for twenty he can no longer afford. The first time the crown misses a salary, I'll be standing there ready to make the third voice.
