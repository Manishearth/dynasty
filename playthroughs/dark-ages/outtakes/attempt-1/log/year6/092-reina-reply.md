move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "A good harvest: forty to Province 3, forty to Paddy, twenty each to Rich and Silke, and nothing for the crown or the desert. Rent stays at five each. Silke, I know your sums went red with that card, and I'm not about to make them redder. Rob, enjoy the Bowl. I'll remember the walk you took to get it, and I'll remember how long it took."
aside: "Losing the Bowl puts me two pieces from a set again, so for now my real currency is friends, and squeezing a coast that's just gone into deficit would be foolish. Rob has had his fun; next I find out what he wants for it, and whether a Customs House ever comes free."
