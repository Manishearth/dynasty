say: Four, Rich, counted. Clark, you said you'd pay the coast, and now both canal sections and fifteen of every twenty are on the page, so I'll take you at your word, all six squares of it: N26, N27, O27, P27, Q26 and Q27. That's thirty from me and ninety from the crown. Six squares at twenty a year is a hundred and twenty a year from next harvest, enough to cover an x4 card and still eat, and a peasant who isn't in the red has no cause to call. That's what you're buying.
aside: Clark took away every cause I had in my pocket, so I'm taking his money while it's on offer and leaving my set where it is until it's worth more than it's worth today. Rich and Reina are lining up for Wall links, and the next Emperor who wants the coast's backing will have to outbid ninety grain of land.
