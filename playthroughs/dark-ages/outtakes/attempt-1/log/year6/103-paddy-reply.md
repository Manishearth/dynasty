diary: Two more years and I've paid every grain I owed: five rent to Reina each year, eleven and then eight to the crown, and thirty for six squares along the bottom of the coast that the crown paid three parts of. The canal came in at last, both Clark's promise and the twenty it adds to my harvest, and in the dry year that was the only reason Province 6 ate better than anyone else. Clark kept every word he gave: Reina's Reservoir, Silke's canal, land for the coast. But keeping them has emptied the reserve from over four hundred down to sixty, and Rob noticed before I did. Somebody whispered to Rich that a revolt hands the whole treasury to whoever's acclaimed, and now there's next to nothing left in it, so the only prize in a revolt is the chair itself. I don't like how Rob talks about pages and Warlords in two provinces at once. I have a hundred and forty-odd grain, a full set, six new squares coming into harvest next year, and still no debt to my name, which is more than a rice farmer has any right to. I'll keep paying, keep watching Rob's men on the walk south, and if the table turns on Clark I'll stand with the man who actually fed the coast, not the one who only talks about it.
