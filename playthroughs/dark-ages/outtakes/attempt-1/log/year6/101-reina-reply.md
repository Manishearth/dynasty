diary: Clark kept his word, and I give him that without grudging it: the Reservoir stands in Province 3, built with the crown's eighty, and it will pay me twenty a year. So I kept mine, paid my twenty percent twice, held the rent at five and stood beside him while Silke and Paddy did their sums. What it cost me was the Bowl. Rob walked it into the desert after four years of telling me he would, and I was too busy watching the throne to watch my own field. That's the lesson, and it's an expensive one. Now I hold a Jar and nothing else, and every soldier step through Province 3 takes another five from me. The coast has done well out of the crown, with six squares each and full sets, while I can't buy an inch until four Wall links go up above me, and the reserve that stood at four hundred has gone down to sixty in a single afternoon. A treasury that empty gives the whole table a cause, and Rob has started saying so out loud. Next year I want the Wall links on Clark's page in return for my support, and I want to know what Rob would take for my Bowl. I'll keep my grain dry, because if this table falls into an acclamation, grain in hand and friends are what will count, and I mean to have more of both than anyone expects.
