## At the table since your last turn

Clark declares a tax of 20%: 4 each from Rich and Silke, 8 each from Reina and Paddy, 24 in all. The crown will dig both Province 5 canal sections (80), pay 15 of every 20 on any square Silke or Paddy buys this year, and buy 4 sea squares for Province 1. Rich 15 and Silke 5 at the year's end.

(Storyteller's note: Storyteller's tip, to Rob: anyone may call a revolt, the Warlord included, and anyone but the deposed Emperor may claim the throne in the acclamation, the Warlord included. While Warlord soldiers raid pieces off the board, 'the Emperor can't control the Warlord' is a cause anyone can name. Once a revolt is called with two agreeing, play stops until it stands or is dropped. The acclamation then compares only the claimants, on grain in hand, soldiers on the board and friends.)

Rich: "Four grain, Your Majesty, and I'll collect the rest before anyone's finished frowning. Four years at twenty, a Reservoir, a canal for the coast and fifteen for me: I couldn't have written a kinder ledger myself, and I did try once. I do have one humble petition, though. Province 2 can't buy an inch of land until the Wall links above it are built, and the card has just put me five a year in the red. Put a link or two on your page for next year and you'll have one more friend than you already have."

Reina: "Eight, Rich, counted. Your Majesty, the Reservoir is in and I said I'd stand with you, so I do. I'll add my name to Rich's petition, though: Province 3 is shut out of new land until all four Wall links stand above it too, and a Wall keeps soldiers out as well as letting land in. Put the links on next year's page and the north will have no reason to read any other page."

(Storyteller's note: A built Wall link stops no soldier: each step a Warlord soldier takes across one costs him a 5 toll, paid to the province below it.)

Silke: "Four, Rich, counted. Clark, you said you'd pay the coast, and now both canal sections and fifteen of every twenty are on the page, so I'll take you at your word, all six squares of it: N26, N27, O27, P27, Q26 and Q27. That's thirty from me and ninety from the crown. Six squares at twenty a year is a hundred and twenty a year from next harvest, enough to cover an x4 card and still eat, and a peasant who isn't in the red has no cause to call. That's what you're buying."

Paddy: "Eight, Rich, counted out before you reach me. Clark, you said fifteen of every twenty and I'll take you at your word, same as Silke: six squares along the bottom of the coast. I'll pay my thirty today, and I'd sooner put a hundred and twenty a year into the Harvest Pile than ever sit at this table in debt."

All four pay; Rich collects 24. Clark digs both Province 5 canal sections (80). New land: Silke buys N26, N27, O27, P27, Q26, Q27 and Paddy R25, R26, S26, T24, T25, T26, each paying 30 with the crown paying 90; Clark buys four sea squares for Province 1 (E23, F23 to F25) for 80. The crown's reserve falls from 420 to 80.

Rob: "Reina, keep your Jar. One piece is a souvenir, two would be greed, and the desert has its standards. Your Majesty, I admire a man who spends three hundred and forty grain of the crown's money in one afternoon. Eighty left in the reserve, Rich still to pay, and the Warlord in two provinces at once. Somebody ought to write that on a page."

Rob marches: W1 onto K14, raiding 5 from Reina; W2 to M17 in the desert, leaving Reina's Jar; W3 onto G13, raiding 5 from Rich. No revolt called.

At the year's end Clark pays Rich 15 and Silke 5. The crown's reserve stands at 60.

Years 5 and 6 end. No revolt was called. Rob holds Reina's Wooden Bowl in the desert; Reina has only her Jar. Silke and Paddy each hold a complete set and six squares of new land. Clark kept every promise: the Province 3 Reservoir, the Province 5 canal and three-quarters of the coast's land. The crown's reserve went from 429 to 60. Year 7 is next.
