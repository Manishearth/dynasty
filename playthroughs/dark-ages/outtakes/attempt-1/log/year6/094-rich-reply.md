move: pay
say: "Four grain, Your Majesty, and I'll collect the rest before anyone's finished frowning. Four years at twenty, a Reservoir, a canal for the coast and fifteen for me: I couldn't have written a kinder ledger myself, and I did try once. I do have one humble petition, though. Province 2 can't buy an inch of land until the Wall links above it are built, and the card has just put me five a year in the red. Put a link or two on your page for next year and you'll have one more friend than you already have."
aside: "With the population doubled, my province loses grain every year until the Wall goes up, so I need the crown spending on me, not on a revolt. I also saw the Storyteller lean over to Rob, so I'm paying quietly, keeping my hundred and twenty-odd in hand, and if a revolt does come I want to be the friendliest rich man in the room when the counting starts."
