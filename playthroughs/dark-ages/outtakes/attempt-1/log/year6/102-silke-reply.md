diary: Two years ago I said Rich was the man who ought to be nervous. Instead Clark spent the whole crown buying my silence, and I let him. Twenty percent both years, a canal dug in Province 5 at last, and ninety grain of the crown's money in six squares of coast, so my thirty buys a hundred and twenty a year from next harvest. I'm going home with seventy-two and a half, a full set, and no cause in my pocket, and I'm not sorry about any of it. But the reserve has gone from four hundred and twenty-nine to sixty in two years, Rob is walking through the north with Reina's Bowl under his arm, and Rich and Reina are both waiting on Wall links that Clark can't pay for now. The next chair that changes hands won't go to whoever has a set; it'll go to whoever holds grain and friends when the reserve runs dry. So I keep the set back, I let the land pay, and when Clark finally has to say no to somebody, I want to be the one with a full belly who gets to choose a side.
