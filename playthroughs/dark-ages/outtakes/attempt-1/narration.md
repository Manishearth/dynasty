# Narration

The narrator's lines, chapter by chapter: the chapter title, each year's opening line, and the turning points, each tagged with the log number it follows. The narrator isn't part of the game, so none of this goes into `game.py` or the playthrough page; the page must make sense without it.

## Chapter 1

- Title: **Chapter One: I Can Count to Three.**

- (002) Year 1: The first year of Reina's reign. Sixty each, no card, two soldiers resting at the edge of the sand, and the Emperor keeping the stars herself, unpaid.
- (013) Four lots, three to the same buyer, at seven apiece. By the time the Bowl is set down on M12, the Gentry holds a complete set with six and a half grain to spare, and a soldier one step from his border.
- (014) A third soldier on G8, and the army is full in its first year. The Gentry is raided and repaid in the same step; his set survives the visit.
- (014) Year 2: The second year of Reina's reign. Spring floods: the Emperor's, the Gentry's and the Rice Peasant's fields drink well, the Official's and the Silk Peasant's drown. A set sits in the Gentry's south, and a soldier sits three steps from it.
- (021) The rules of revolt are read out, and four players hear them and pay. Each says the same thing in a different voice: a revolt now only clears the path for the man with the set.
- (028) Last of the box. Silke spends nineteen on a Deed that finishes nothing of hers, and Paddy is left one paper short. Three quarters of the price, as ever, goes to the Pile.
- (032) Year 2 ends with one set on the board and two near-sets beside it, forty percent two years running, and three soldiers in the sand.

## Chapter 2

- Title: **Chapter Two: The Right Afternoon.**

- (038) Year 3: The third year of Reina's reign, and a careless one. Every field is full, every household has its small misfortune, and the Gentry's set has sat on its shelf for two years.
- (043) The rule is read out at last: a revolt would turn the set into three pots on a shelf. The Gentry hears it, pays his eighteen, and presents the set before anyone else can speak. The third year of Reina's reign ends in its third act, and the first of Rich's begins.
- (055) Six grain for a Jar, and the Official's shelf is full. One year, two complete sets: the first now sits on the throne, the second sits under the Wall, four steps from a soldier.
- (058) Year 3 ends with a new Emperor, a tax of twenty, a second palace, and the Pile three hundred grain lighter than its keeper's purse. Nobody has called a revolt.
- (062) Year 4: The first year of Rich's reign, and nearly the last line of it. The chest is opened, the canal is promised to the coast, and the Official presents a set before the tax is gathered. Two thrones taken by the same door in two years; the chronicle notes that it was left open.
- (074) Eleven, fifteen, eleven: the cheapest auction of the dynasty, and it finishes two sets on the coast. Four complete sets have been made in four years; none has yet been raided.
- (076) Year 4 ends with a third Emperor, a fourth promised work, both coastal canals paid for in one province and owed in the other, and a soldier one step from the Gentry's Bowl. Nobody has called a revolt.

## Chapter 3

- Title: **Chapter Three: Fifteen of Every Twenty.**

- (083) Year 5: The first full year of Clark's reign, and a dry one. The reserve stands at 464, the Reservoir is promised to the north-west, and two soldiers stand within a step of the Gentry's crockery.
- (089) Four answers, four payments. The tip about a sealed reserve of 464 is heard by the whole table, and nobody reaches for it.
- (090) And so the first treasure of the dynasty goes west into the sand: the Gentry's Bowl, from L16 to M16, one square and a whole world away. Year 5 ends with the Reservoir dug, the reserve at 396, and no revolt called.
- (091) Year 6: The third year of Clark's reign. The population doubles, every province eats twice, and for the first time there is new land to buy.
- (097) Fifteen of every twenty. Twelve squares of sea go to the coast, four to the crown, two canal sections to Province 5, and the reserve goes from 420 to 80 in one afternoon. Every promise is kept.
- (098) The desert leaves the Jar where it stands. Year 6 ends with the reserve at 60, a lean year next on the deck, and no revolt called.
