# Chance and steering in the Dark Ages

The Director nudges this game towards the arc in `scenario.md`. Any steering is the Director's, made before the game or between chapters through a handoff, and is listed below when it happens. A Storyteller never steers on its own.

- **Seed:** `350`, recommended in `scenario.md` and confirmed by the Director with `deck_scout.py` before Chapter 1.
- **How the deck is shuffled:** the twenty card keys from `cards.py`, sorted alphabetically, then `random.Random(350).shuffle(keys)` (Python 3).
- **Deck order, top first** (Year 1 draws no card, so the first of these is Year 2's):

  1. blight · 2. double2 · 3. heaven · 4. dribble1 · 5. utility · 6. breach · 7. careless · 8. repairs · 9. rainflood · 10. double3 · 11. dribble2 · 12. canal · 13. half2 · 14. share · 15. chest · 16. winds · 17. dribble3 · 18. half1 · 19. double1 · 20. goodevil

  The players are never shown this list, and the narrator never hints at it. Population cards past their caps are still drawn in order; the engine treats a capped one as a good harvest.

- **Booklets:** not drawn by chance. Each player sits in the province their name puns on: Reina 1, Clark 2, Rich 3, Silke 5, Paddy 6, Rob 8.

## Steering log

- **Before Chapter 1, the deck: seed 350 kept.** Scouted against 200, 446 and 513 (`deck_scout.py --seats 1,2,3,5,6,8 --years 12`). 350 is the only one shaped like the arc: an average Year 2, land open from Year 3, then three lean years (Year 4 heaven, Year 5 dribble1, Year 6 utility; the crown's bare income −70, −70, −45 and most provinces negative), which is when a hard tax or an empty reserve can become a revolt. Years 7–10 are good harvests at ×2 (+20 a province, −20 for the crown), room for a new Emperor to rebuild through land, though personal disasters fall in Years 7–9. The second doubling waits until Year 11 (×4, then a bad Year 12), so the Director decides by Year 10 whether to stop there or let the last two years bite. The others: 200 opens land in Year 2, then has five losing years in a row (3–7) and half-cards in 10–11, so no clean recovery; 446 scatters its bad years (2, 5, 8) with no lean stretch to revolt in; 513 is lean only in 4–5 and turns bad again in Year 8, with ×3 in Year 9, leaving one good year to recover in.
- **Before Chapter 1, the actor's notes** (`ledger.md`, **Playing it**), from `scenario.md`'s levers and the booklet personalities, stances only: Reina a builder in a hurry who taxes hard while incomes are positive; Clark the paper trail who knows the revolt clauses by heart; Rich fawning on the crown while it stands and counting his friends when it wobbles; Silke sharp and unwilling to starve quietly; Paddy hating to owe; Rob the opportunist who'd rather be nearby when the throne falls than push it.
- **Before Chapter 6, the length: the game stops after Year 12** (`LAST_ROUND = 12` in `game.py`, Note 19), so `briefs.py` puts the round count, the scoring and the late-land warning on every card; the Chapter 6 Storyteller tells the table at the start of Year 11. Why: `scenario.md` plans about 12 years, and 430 is still owed in five separate works, which at one project a year can't be finished before Year 15; Years 11–12 (×4, then a bad harvest) are the scenario's last chance at revolt and Chaos, so the game plays them rather than stopping at 10. No other steering: notes unchanged, no tips. **Also before Chapter 6, a rules clarification (not steering):** Storyteller 5 proposed refusing by hand a single raider's capture of a free guard. The rulebook (§14, "Guarding a piece") makes that a consequence of move order, not a separate rule: the raider must end a move straight beside the guard, which may then capture it (leaving the piece). The engine plays it correctly, so nothing is enforced by hand.
- **Before Chapter 3, one Storyteller's tip, to Paddy** (in `handoff/chapter-3.md`): a public note after Year 5's harvest stating the seven causes for revolt, how a revolt is called off, and the acclamation's three counts. Rules only, no advice. Why: `scenario.md` names this tip for when a revolt is in the air, and it is: the reserve is 19.5 against Clark's promised 30, Year 5 is a bad harvest, and every Year 4 diary names the pressure. Paddy, because he's the most hard-pressed (14.5) and the least likely to know the clauses already; the note is public, so the whole table hears it. Actor's notes unchanged: all six still fit their seats.
- **Before Chapter 2, three notes rewritten** after Rich took the throne in Year 2: Rich now an Emperor who got the chair cheaply and pays dangerous friends rather than other provinces; Reina the deposed Empress turned landlord, with a grievance; Clark the paper trail with a loaded set, his loyalty rented by the year. Silke, Paddy and Rob unchanged. No tips. Why: the old notes named seats they no longer hold; the new ones keep each character's temperament and point it at the seat they have.
- **Before Chapter 1, the setup line** (`game.py`, R(0)): the appointee's pay is the Emperor's choice, not a fixed 5 (today's ruling), and the Warlord's one hired reinforcement is mentioned.
