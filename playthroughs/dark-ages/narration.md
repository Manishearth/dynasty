# Narration

The narrator's lines, chapter by chapter: the chapter title, each year's opening line, and the turning points, each tagged with the log number it follows. The narrator isn't part of the game, so none of this goes into `game.py` or the playthrough page; the page must make sense without it.

## Chapter 1

- Title: **Chapter One: The Knife Held Up.**

- (002) Year 1: The first year of Reina's reign. Sixty each, no card, two soldiers on the sand, and the Official now keeps the stars as well as the tax rolls.
- (013) Four lots, and three of them go to the same buyer. By the time the last Bowl is set down, the Gentry holds a complete set with two and a half grain to spare.
- (014) The desert moves first. Five from the Gentry on H10, and his set, complete for one march, is two and a half grain short of usable.
- (014) Year 2: The second year of Reina's reign. Drought on the card, an average harvest in every field, and the Gentry's set two and a half grain from usable, with a harvest to be paid out by the Gentry himself.
- (016) The rent is collected, and then the man who collected it climbs the steps. Rich takes the throne and a reserve of 195; Reina takes the granary and sixty from the Pile. One and a half years.
- (027) The second set of the year, and the second year in a row the set goes to the man who bid hardest on its last two pieces. Clark pays 12.5 twice and keeps 33; Reina, a step short, keeps a Customs House.
- (028) A third soldier, pitched one step from the Gentry's only piece. The army is full in its second year.
- (029) Year 2 ends with two Emperors in two years, a second set held a seat away from the throne, and its holder on thirty a year. Three soldiers, three provinces, and still not one piece taken.

## Chapter 2

- Title: **Chapter Two: Not One Grain Short.**

- (035) Year 3: The second year of Rich's reign. The card doubles the people and fills the fields, the Official holds a loaded set at thirty a year, and a soldier stands one step from the Gentry's only piece.
- (049) The Official, who needs neither, buys the last Jar and the last Bowl for 44 grain. Every Dragon Jar and Wooden Bowl at the table now sits in Province 2, and the Gentry's set has nowhere to come from.
- (050) The desert takes its first piece: the Gentry's Customs House, carried from M14 to N14. Year 3 ends with seven new squares in the sea, a second Winter, and a reserve of 76.5, down from 192.5.
- (050) Year 4: The third year of Rich's reign. Heaven favours the Official and the silk coast and frowns on everyone else; the crown has 76.5 in hand and no lots to sell.
- (054) The Gentry asks the silk coast for ten and is offered it, on a condition. She takes five instead and keeps her hands free.
- (059) Year 4 ends with the reserve at 19.5. The Official has been paid thirty, to the grain, three years running; the crown has 19.5 left to keep paying him with.

## Chapter 3

- Title: **Chapter Three: Owing Nobody.**

- (065) Year 5: The fourth year of Rich's reign. The seed rots in every province, the reserve holds 19.5, and the Official is owed thirty at the year's end.
- (066) The tip is read to Paddy, and the whole table hears the seven causes for revolt. Nobody remarks on it.
- (075) The crown pays out every grain it has between the coast's fields and the Official's thirty. Year 5 ends with a reserve of 4, the Official paid to the grain a fourth time, and a second piece on the desert, the Official's spare Jar.
- (076) Year 6: The fifth year of Rich's reign. An average harvest and a new levy, the crown holds 4, and the Official has a Jar fewer and the same set.
- (083) The crown buys the coast's fields with its last grain, half a grain at a time. Silke now farms ten squares of sea.
- (084) The desert takes a third piece, the Rice Peasant's Customs House, and steps onto the Official's Scroll to take five grain instead. Year 6 ends with the reserve at zero, the Official paid thirty for the fifth year, and no revolt called.

## Chapter 4

- Title: **Chapter Four: A Grievance with a Number on It.**

- (090) Year 7: The sixth year of Rich's reign. A good harvest, a breach in a Wall nobody has built, and a crown that begins the year with nothing and owes the Official thirty at its end.
- (094) The crown digs on the silk coast for the first time in the reign. Silke says so in front of the Official's book, and adds that the Wall line is still empty.
- (099) Year 7 ends with the reserve at 14, the first year in three that the crown keeps anything. The desert's last soldier stands two squares of sea from the silk coast's Scroll, with his legs bandaged.
- (099) Year 8: The seventh year of Rich's reign. Good times and careless people: every player at the table pays for a misfortune, and the desert is forbidden to take anything at all.
- (105) Two links of the Great Wall go up over the Emperor's own province. The Official's empty line now has ink on it, and a province number.
- (108) Year 8 ends with the reserve at 48 and the desert's soldier one square of sea from the silk coast's Scroll. The coast farms thirty-five squares between two peasants; no revolt has been called.

## Chapter 5

- Title: **Chapter Five: A Very Short Rental.**

- (114) Year 9: The eighth year of Rich's reign. A fine crop and a fine season for raiders; the desert's last soldier stands one square of sea from the silk coast's Scroll.
- (123) The last two links go up over Province 1. The Wall over the Emperor's province is finished; over the Official's and the Gentry's, nothing yet.
- (125) The coast buys a soldier from the desert for twenty, and the desert's soldier walks up to it. Year 9 ends with W2 off the board, the Scroll unguarded on P25, and the crown on 23.
- (125) Year 10: The ninth year of Rich's reign. The river floods the Official's fields and the silk coast's, waters the Emperor's, and the crown begins the year with 23 and owes 30.
- (130) Two links of Wall go up over the Official's province, three years after he opened the line for them. He writes them down as paid.
- (135) The Emperor buys a soldier from the desert and stands it beside the desert's own. It lasts until the march. Year 10 ends with the crown on 188, the Scroll guarded again, and two soldiers four rounds from it; no revolt has been called in ten years.

## Chapter 6

- (142) Year 11: The tenth year of Rich's reign, and the table agrees it will be the second-to-last. The villages double again, to four hundred million, and every mouth at the northern tables doubles with them.
- (145) The tea is poured at last. Thirty for the Customs House, four years after the purse first came round; then the desert's last two pieces go back to the Pile at two apiece, to feed an army on sand. Rob keeps his record and a grain and a half.
- (153) The last two links go up over Province 2, eight years after the first stone over Province 1. The Official writes the line closed.
- (159) The Pile auctions what the desert sold it for two. The Customs House goes for two; the Jar goes for three hundred and one, to a bidder who doesn't need it, against one who did.
- (162) Year 11 ends with the crown on 443, the silk coast one Bowl short of a set, and the desert's soldiers four rounds from anything they want, with one march left.
- (162) Year 12: The eleventh year of Rich's reign, and the last the table has agreed to play.
