move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: A golden harvest, friends, sixty to nearly every house, paid out of the Pile in full and on time, as always. And the rent stays at the customary five. Heaven calls us careless, but nobody will ever say the Gentry was greedy in a fat year. Paddy, Silke, I'd call that good neighbours. I'd remember it, too, if I were counting friends.
aside: Five on a good harvest costs me nothing and buys me goodwill I can call in when the fields go bad. Meanwhile I'm sitting on a hundred and fifty-two, the biggest granary of any claimant, so whoever wants the chair will have to come and see me first.
