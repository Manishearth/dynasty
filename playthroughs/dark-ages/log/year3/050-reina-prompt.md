Two years, one palace, and a throne I lost on a flooded spring. I taxed half because the fields were full and an empire that waits for lean years never builds. They called that a habit, and when the vote came all five stood together, Rich loudest of all after years of honey in his mouth. I won't pretend I'd answer differently. A crown that cuts its tax because the table shouts has stopped being a crown. Now the reserve sits sealed beside me, a hundred and ninety-five that I can't touch.
