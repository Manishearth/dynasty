diary: Two more good years on the rice coast. Two hundred and fifty in Year 9 and three hundred and seventy in a flood year that drowned Clark and Silke, so I've put ten more fields into the sea and now there are twenty-one of them, all down the far end. I paid Reina her ten both years because she kept the Pile honest. I paid the crown a hundred and eighty-six between the two years. I paid my share of every field, and I still owe nobody and have four hundred and one grain, which I'd never have believed in Year 1. But I've nothing in front of me, the same as then. The Customs House is still sitting in Rob's tent, and so far "bring the purse round" has meant tea and talk about the weather, with no price named. I watched Silke's soldier knock W2 off the board, and I watched Rob take Rich's soldier the same way a year later, so I've learned not to buy a soldier and park him beside the Warlord. Next year I'll put the purse on the table and ask Rob for a number, plainly, and if he won't name one I'll ask Clark about his Jar and his Scroll instead. Rob has a new man on T24 now, right beside my old Customs House, and I don't like how close that is to the coast.
