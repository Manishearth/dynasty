## At the table since your last turn

Clark collects 315 for the crown. Public works: the first two links of the Great Wall above Province 2 go up, 80 from the crown. The crown has 398.

Silke: "Ten more fields, Rich, at your five a square. That's fifty from the crown and a hundred and fifty from me, and that's on top of a hundred and ninety-eight in tax. So count it properly, Clark: this year the coast put three hundred and forty-eight into the crown and the Pile, and got fifty back. Write that under whatever heading you keep for the people who pay for the Wall."

New land: Silke buys G27-G30, H30, I30, L30, M29, N29, O29 (15 hers, 5 the crown's on each); forty-four sea squares.

Paddy: "Fifteen and five is fair, Rich, and I'll not argue with it. Four more fields, every one of them down the far end beside the ones I bought last year. Sixty from me, and Clark can count it. And well done on the Wall over Clark's roof. I'd say that's the most patient count I've ever seen kept at a table."

New land: Paddy buys S28, R29, R30, V25 (15 his, 5 the crown's on each); twenty-one squares.

Rich: "Four more fields up in the north-east corner, eighty grain into the Pile, counted. And Rob, you told us your lads have to eat somewhere. I'll buy one of them at your price, twenty, and you keep the five, and he can tell W1 dinner's over in my garden. Nothing personal, as Silke says. I'm a customer, and a very polite one."

New land: Rich buys C24, D25, D26, E26 in the sea for Province 1 (the crown pays 80); eleven sea squares. Rich buys a soldier from Rob (20; Rob keeps 5): Rich1 stands on H17, straight beside W1 on I17. It may move (Province 1 has been raided), but only after the Warlord's march. No auctions: no stock. The crown has 228.

Rob marches: W1 steps straight onto Rich's soldier on H17 and captures it, raiding H17 for 10 from the crown; W3 to L21 in empty Province 4. Rob hires a new soldier, W2, for 20 on T24 in his southern desert, beside T23, four rounds from Silke's Scroll; it moves from next year. Army 3 of 3.

Rob: "Rich, I sold you that lad at a fair price, and you put him right next to W1, so W1 has gone over to shake his hand. Ten for the garden, and I'll call the twenty a very short rental. Silke, I learned that one from you, so thank you for the lesson. Paddy, the Customs House is still in the tent and the kettle's still warm. Bring the purse round, I always like hearing it."

Silke's soldier steps back onto P25 and guards her Scroll; W3 on L21 and W2 on T24 are each four rounds from it.

Silke: "Back on the Scroll he goes. Rob, your new man on T24 is four rounds out and so is W3, and one raider can't break a free guard, so you'll need to bring both of them, and I'll see them coming. Rich, the lesson wasn't buying a soldier. The lesson was not parking him in front of the Warlord."

Salaries: Rich pays Clark 30, as promised, a ninth year. The crown ends Year 10 with 188.
