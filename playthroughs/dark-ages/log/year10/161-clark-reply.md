diary: Years 9 and 10. The Wall went up over the north, ten links in the first year and the last two in the second, from Province 1 all the way to the desert, and it was never moved a third time. I asked fifty percent for it once and went back to thirty, and nobody said the word revolt, because everyone could see the stone. Rich paid his forty before Reina had finished reading the line, Reina paid twenty twice for stone over her own roof, and Silke and Paddy each turned down half my land money and told me to put it toward the works. I've written every one of those on the credit side, and that's more than I ever expected to write for a landlord. It cost the drawer down to nothing once. I had to pay Reina five and owe her the other five, and I cleared that before a single link was ordered in Year 10, so "settled" is now in her hand and not just mine. Rob carried off Rich's Customs House, then put Reina in debt over a Bowl, and still wants paying not to do it. That's page six, and the answer hasn't changed. Four squares under the Wall are mine now, eighty a harvest from next year. Still owed: 210, the Province 1 canal, the reservoir, the irrigation and the Pagoda. Reina's already reading the Pagoda to me, and I mean to give her something to strike off.
