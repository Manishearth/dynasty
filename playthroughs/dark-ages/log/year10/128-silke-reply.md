move: Pay Reina the extra 5, so 10 in all, as in each of the last four years.

say: "Ten, Reina, the five you have and five more, counted out loud. A bad year on my coast still pays six hundred and sixty, so I won't plead poverty to a landlord whose Pile is paying it. And Rich, twenty-three in the crown against thirty owed to Clark is a number I'll be watching when you name the tax."

aside: "Five grain keeps the one honest purse in the north on my side of the ledger. The real fight this year is the tax, because the crown is nearly empty, and I've got eleven hundred grain that Rich will want a share of."

whisper: none

---

Ruling: Silke pays 5 more freely (deal): 10 in all. Her say misstates the crown: 23 was its end of Year 9; after this year's harvest (+140) it holds 163. Filed as a public note.
