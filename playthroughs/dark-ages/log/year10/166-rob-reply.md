diary: Well, they built the thing. Twelve links of Wall from Province 1 to the sand, and every province put its own grain in the mortar, even Reina, who hadn't two coins to rub together by the end of it. I took Rich's Customs House to L8 for my trouble, which makes six pieces on my desert, and then Heaven took half my granary in a single night. I'm sitting on sixty-three and a half, which is the poorest I've been since Year 1, and I can't say I enjoyed that part. The real joke was on me, though. I walked W3 all the way up to Reina's Bowl, and my last five sent her into debt and the Bowl went off to the pile before my lad could lay a hand on it. So Reina's still on my list with nothing left on the board to take, and Silke's coast is fatter than the treasury ever was. Clark told me my figure was nothing, then put seventeen and a half in a drawer and told me that wasn't mine either. Fine. There's a Pagoda and a reservoir still to go up, and the palaces haven't moved. When Reina buys that Bowl back, and she will, I'll be standing beside it.
