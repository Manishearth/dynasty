diary: In Year 9 I read the Emperor's Share and paid Clark ten for my own ditch. Then he asked fifty, my part of it was a hundred and sixty-seven and a half, and ten links of Wall went up that same year. I said I'd count it into the mortar and I did. In Year 10 the canals gave way everywhere except where they'd been finished, so mine cost me two for holding. The tax went back to thirty, a hundred and thirty-six and a half from me, and the last two links went up over Reina's roof, Year 8 to Year 10, exactly as written. In those two years I bought fourteen squares and asked the crown for almost nothing, because a crown sitting on two grain or on zero pays Rob for scenery and never pays for stone. I'm on four hundred and eighty-four and a half with thirty squares of coast. That's more grain than I ever saw in the years I was paying thirty to a palace I'd never sit in. There's still two hundred and ten owed: Clark's canal, Rich's reservoir, Reina's irrigation and the Pagoda. The drawer has seventeen and a half in it, so Clark will need the coast again. He'll get it, at my price, and I'll be counting every grain.
