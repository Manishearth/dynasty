move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "The pile pays out as read: Clark fifteen, Silke four hundred and fifty-five, Paddy three hundred and ninety-five, Province 3 fifty-five, the first fat harvest it's ever had, and Reina and Rob put theirs back in. Rent is the customary five each, and yes, Silke, the same five in the year Heaven took half my granary. I said five and I meant five. Rob, I hope that toll booth gives you splinters."
aside: "Half my grain is gone to Heaven and the Customs House to the desert, so I'm a man with eighty-eight grain and one Jar, and asking for more rent now would only look like panic. Those three new squares will fill the granary back up, and the first grain I spare goes on a sword to guard them, and then on the Reservoir."
