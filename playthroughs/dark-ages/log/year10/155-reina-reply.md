move: land F11; I pay 10, asking the crown for 10
say: "One square, F11, under my own Wall. Ten from me and ten from the crown at the rate Clark named, and I'm asking for nothing more. I put my last full twenty into the stone, so that's all Province 2 can do this year. Next year it'll be more, and the year after that the ring goes all the way along the Wall. Clark, the Pagoda is the next line on your page. I'll be reading it to you."
aside: "Ten out of twelve leaves me two grain and a salary on the way, and I'd rather have one square of my own under the Wall than a fat purse and a bare strip. F11 is as far as I can get from Rob's lad on I14 for now."
whisper: none
