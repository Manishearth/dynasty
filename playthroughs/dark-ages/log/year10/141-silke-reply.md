diary: Two years, and the coast has stopped asking for anything. I started Year 9 with twenty-four fields and end Year 10 with forty-four, and seven hundred and sixty-five grain in the purse after paying for every one of them. Count what went out: three hundred and fifty-one in tax, two hundred and fifty for my half of the land, twenty to Reina, twenty to Rob for a soldier, two for repairs and five to W2 on his way out. The crown put a hundred and fifty into my fields, the most it has ever given back, and it still built four links of Wall over Rich's roof and two over Clark's before it built anything else on mine. The best twenty grain I've spent in ten years bought Rob's own man, and he took W2 off the board for me. Rob's paid me back by hiring a new W2 on T24, four rounds out, with W3 four rounds off on L21, so my guard stands on P25 again, and he'll need both of them to shift it. Rich bought a soldier the same year and parked it in front of W1, which tells you everything about who at this table actually reads the board. Next year I go to Rob's tent with my purse before Paddy does, because Rob is sitting on two Customs Houses, Clark is sitting on two Bowls, and I'm the one with the grain to buy them both.
