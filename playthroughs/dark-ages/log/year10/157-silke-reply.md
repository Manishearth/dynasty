move: land G27, G28, G29, H29, I29, J29, K29, L28; I pay 120, asking the crown for 40
say: Eight squares on the north edge of the coast, a hundred and sixty. I pay a hundred and twenty, the crown pays forty, five a square, and I'm not waving it off, Clark, I'm halving it. Count it up: two twenty-five and a half in the drawer, eighty on your own strip, forty to me, and Reina's ten, so you've still got about ninety going into next year. There's two hundred and ten still owed, and I'd sooner my other forty went into that than into my fields. The Wall's whole, Reina, and I'll say it out loud: three years, on the page, not moved.
aside: That's thirty squares of coast now, all on the north side and well away from Rob's lads. Next year the coast pays a hundred and sixty more, and that crown has the purse to start on what's left of the two hundred and ten.
whisper: none
