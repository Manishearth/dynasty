diary: The Wall stands. It runs from Province 1 to the desert, four links over my own roof, laid in Years 9 and 10 just as the page said after it slipped twice, and I read every line of it aloud as I swore I would. It cost me nearly everything I had. I put forty of my own grain into the stone, ten into one square of land under it, and then Rob's lad took his five twice from my field until my Wooden Bowl went back to the pile for two grain and I sat in debt for the first time in my life. One grain, and Clark's salary cleared it within the hour, but I'll remember it. Clark kept his word on the Wall, and on the five he owed me as well, paid first just as he said, and I wrote "settled" beside both. Even Rich behaved like a neighbour over F11, and I'm not used to that. Silke and Paddy each took less from the crown than they were offered so the drawer would keep something for what comes next, and I think better of the coast for it. Two hundred and ten is still owed: the Province 1 canal, Rich's reservoir, my own irrigation and the Pagoda. I have nine grain, one square, and no Bowl. But F11 harvests next year behind a wall I fought ten years to see built, and I'll keep reading the ledger to Clark until the Pagoda stands over this land too.
