diary: The Wall stands. Every link of it from Province 1 to the desert, and four of them are mine. I paid my forty to the grain, exactly as I offered it, and Reina read it out while the mortar was wet, just as she swore she would. I never thought I'd be grateful to that woman's ledger, but there it is. It cost me dearly. Heaven took half my granary in one bad page of the booklet, and Rob's lad walked off with my Customs House and then came back for five more off my new fields, so I end the year with thirty-seven grain and a single Jar, the poorest the Gentry has been since this game began. But I have seven squares of land behind the Wall, the first ground Province 3 has ever farmed, and from next spring they'll pay me a hundred and fifty-five in an average year. Land is land, and stone lasts longer than grain. I kept the rent at five through both years, the fat one and the one that halved me, and nobody at this table can say the Gentry broke his word. Next comes the Reservoir. It's my stone again, so I'll put my share toward it as I did for the Wall. Then I'll buy a sword or two before Rob decides my new fields are his larder.
