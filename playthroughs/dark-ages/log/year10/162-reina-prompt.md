Clark: "Four squares under the new stone for Province 1, E19 to E22, and the crown pays its own twenty a square like anyone else. Reina, put it in the ledger next to everyone else's, because I don't keep one set of books for the table and another for myself."

Paddy: "Five more squares, Your Majesty: T14, U14, V14, U20 and V21. You offered ten a square and you meant it, I know that.
