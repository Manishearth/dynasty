I promised it in Year 8 and laid it in Years 9 and 10. Back to thirty this year, because last year was for stone and this year the purse has to be refilled, and the crown pays ten a square on new land and doesn't ask anyone to wave it off."
aside: "Nobody left in the ledger can say the Wall was delayed, the crown owes no one, and the provinces paid their share of the stone over their own roofs.
