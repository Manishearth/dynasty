## At the table since your last turn

Clark collects 168 for the crown. Public works: two links of the Great Wall above Province 1, 80 from the crown. New land: Rich buys F25 for Province 1 (crown 20). The crown has 138.

Silke: "Eight more fields at your five a square, Rich: a hundred and twenty from me, forty from the crown, all of it into the Pile. And Clark, write it down properly: two links of Wall, both over Province 1, paid for out of a hundred and five of my grain. I'm glad the north has a roof, but I'll remember whose harvest paid for it. I'm buying west and north, away from the beach where W2 is paddling."

New land: Silke buys eight squares, I25, I26, I27, I28, J28, K29, N28, O28 (15 hers and 5 the crown's on each); twenty-four sea squares. The crown has 98.

Paddy: "Four more fields at your five a square, Rich: sixty from me, twenty from the crown, all into the Pile in front of Clark's book. Thank you for the Wall, honestly, even if it's the north's roof and not mine. I'm building down the far end, away from wherever Rob's soldier fancies a paddle next."

New land: Paddy buys T29, T30, U30, V30 (15 his and 5 the crown's on each); eleven squares. The crown has 78. No auctions: no stock.

Rob: "My lieutenant has run off with my daughter, so this year nobody gets robbed but me. The lads are just going for a walk. Silke, it's a lovely bit of water by your Scroll, nothing more. And Paddy, I haven't forgotten your big purse. Bring it round at the end of the year and we'll have a cup of tea over T23, but I'm warning you, that Customs House has sentimental value now."

Rob marches, raiding nothing: W1 stands on H16; W2 wades to Q26, one square from Silke's Scroll on P25; W3 walks into empty Province 4 at J19, two from the Winter Palace. No hire. Salaries: Rich pays Clark 30, as promised. The crown ends Year 8 with 48.

Year 8 ends. Grain: Reina 66, Clark 194, Rich 48, Silke 243, Paddy 109, Rob 35. Two links of the Great Wall stand above Province 1; works still owed 590. Silke farms twenty-four sea squares, Paddy eleven, Rich four. Rob holds three pieces and needs only Silke's Scroll. No revolt has been called.
