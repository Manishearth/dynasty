diary: Years 7 and 8. Two canals in two years, Province 6 in the lean year and Province 5 in the fat one, both at thirty percent, and both written "built" in hands other than mine. Paddy's fifty came back in the year I named, and then something I hadn't budgeted for happened: Silke and Paddy took two and a half a square instead of my ten and told me to keep the rest for the Wall. I've written that down too, as kindness and as leverage both, because a province that refuses its due has lent the crown something, and I mean to repay it in stone. It cost the reserve down to half a grain at one point, and it stands at 21 now with 690 still owed. Rich has put a quarter of every Province 3 link in Reina's ledger, which is the first page I've ever opened for a landlord on the credit side. Reina moved the Wall to Year 9 in ink and swears she won't move it again, and she's right not to. I moved it once in the open and I won't do it twice. Rob has taken Paddy's Customs House, made a fifth page under his name, and promised me a bill next year. A crown with 21 in the drawer can't pay scenery money, so the real answer is the Wall, which is the one work that keeps swords out of the north.
