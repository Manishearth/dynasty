## At the table since your last turn

The crown's year: Clark declares 30%. One work: both sections of the Province 5 Grand Canal, 80 from the crown. The crown will pay Paddy the 50 left owed on last year's land. Land this year: the crown pays 10 a square. Salaries: Reina 10, Silke 3. The Wall over the north moves to next year.

Reina: "Six from my own bowl first, so nobody says the collector spares herself. Then Rich six, Silke seventy-eight, Paddy sixty-six: a hundred and fifty-six, counted twice and delivered whole. Clark, that pays Silke's canal, Paddy's fifty and our salaries, with something left over in the reserve. Paddy's debt goes down as settled the moment it's paid. And Rich, your quarter is in the ledger beside the Wall. But Majesty, in Year 6 you wrote the Wall for Year 8, and this is Year 8. I've written Year 9 next to it in ink, and that's the last time I move it."

Reina collects the 30%: Reina 6, Rich 6, Silke 78, Paddy 66, 156 to the crown. Clark builds both sections of the Grand Canal in Province 5 for 80, and pays Paddy the 50 owed on last year's land. The crown's reserve is 56.5, with 13 in salaries still to pay this year.

Silke: "My canal's in. Year 4's page, read back and kept, and I'll write 'built' next to it myself. Four more squares, I25 to I28: eighty in all, seventy from me and ten from the crown, two and a half a square, the same as Paddy took last year. And nothing written down as owed, Clark. I don't want that thirty back. I want it to stay in the reserve so the Wall goes up in Year 9 and Reina never has to move that date again."

New land: Silke buys four more squares, I25 to I28, paying 70; at her asking the crown pays 10 and owes nothing. The crown's reserve is 46.5, with 13 in salaries to pay.

Paddy: "Clark, the fifty came back the year you said it would. Reina, write it settled, and I'll write it in my own book too. Five more squares up the top of the coast, and I'll take the same as Silke, two and a half a square and nothing owed. Keep the rest in the reserve for the Wall over the north. I lost my House to Rob this spring, so the crown's not the one I'm worried about now."

New land: Paddy buys five more squares, T16, U16, V16, V17 and V18, paying 87.5; at his asking the crown pays 12.5 and owes nothing. The crown's reserve is 34, with 13 in salaries to pay.

No auctions this year: every province's stock is sold.

Rob: "Heaven's told my lads to keep their hands in their pockets this year, so they're taking a stroll and nothing more. Reina, that one in your field is only sightseeing. Clark, you're right, the crown doesn't pay for scenery. It's a good thing the scenery's free this year, then. Next year I'll send you the bill."

Rob marches, but may raid nothing this year: W1 and W2 stand; W3 walks onto J14 in Province 2. Salaries: Clark pays Reina 10 and Silke 3. Year 8 ends: the Province 5 canal built, the 50 owed to Paddy paid, nine more squares of land, the crown's reserve 21, 690 owed; the Wall over the north promised for Year 9.
