diary: Two good years, and the coast finally got paid back for something. I started Year 7 with eighty-eight grain and ten fields, and I end Year 8 with two hundred and forty-three and twenty-four fields, a canal section with salt water in it, and a hundred and seventy-one paid to the crown in tax across the two years. Rich kept his word again, the canal on my coast and two links of Wall, though the Wall sits over his own province and was paid for out of my harvest, and Clark wrote the province number down as if that settles it. I gave Reina ten both years because she asked honestly, and a Gentry with sixty-six in her purse won't forget who paid her when she was short. Fire in the drying sheds and a storm on the boat cost me fourteen, which is nothing next to what the fields bring in now. Count what matters, though: Rob holds two Customs Houses and a Jar, he needs exactly one more piece, and that piece is my Scroll on P25, with W2 standing one square from it on Q26 and the lieutenant's elopement the only thing that kept him off it this year. Next year I don't wait for anyone to sell me anything: I find a way to put a soldier or a wall of my own between W2 and that Scroll, and I bring my purse to Rob's tea over T23 before Paddy does, because one Customs House would make him one piece short and make me one Bowl short.
