diary: Years 7-8. Two good harvests and I didn't get a grain from the Pile either time. The desert grows sand and sympathy, and the gods sent me bad legs one year and a runaway lieutenant the next, so for two years I could hardly raid anybody. I still got ten out of it, five off Clark and five off Reina, before the lieutenant ran off. I'm on thirty-five, which is poor, but it's enough. The real work was walking. W2 waded round the coast and now he's standing in the sea at Q26, one step from Silke's Scroll, the last piece I need. Once he takes it I'll have something from every set at the table. W3 is in empty Province 4, two steps from the Winter Palace, and Rich has only forty-eight in the treasury. He's finally started paying for the Wall, so a palace raid costing the crown twenty would hurt him now. Paddy wants to buy back his Customs House, and I'll keep smiling at him and pouring the tea, but nothing leaves my tent until the collection's complete. Silke is the rich one now, and Clark has nearly two hundred and still writes everything down. Next year the lieutenant's gone, my legs are fine, and every soldier I've got is standing exactly where I want him.
