move: collect as declared (Reina 6 from my own grain first, Rich 6, Silke 78, Paddy 66; Rob owes nothing. 156 delivered to the crown)
say: "Six from my own bowl first, so nobody says the collector spares herself. Then Rich six, Silke seventy-eight, Paddy sixty-six: a hundred and fifty-six, counted twice and delivered whole. Clark, that pays Silke's canal, Paddy's fifty and our salaries, with something left over in the reserve. Paddy's debt goes down as settled the moment it's paid. And Rich, your quarter is in the ledger beside the Wall. But Majesty, in Year 6 you wrote the Wall for Year 8, and this is Year 8. I've written Year 9 next to it in ink, and that's the last time I move it."
aside: "A good harvest, and for once the crown can pay for everything it promised in a single season. That's what a tax does when it's actually collected. The Wall has slipped twice now, and I'm letting it go this once because the canals are real, but next spring I'll read that page out loud to the whole table."
