diary: Two good harvests, and I've finally finished a year with something left in the jar: fourteen after the first, forty-eight after the second. That isn't a fortune, but it isn't zero either, and I slept better for it. I gave Silke her first canal section, and she thanked me in front of Clark's book, which is worth more than the forty it cost. Then I spent eighty of her grain on the Wall over my own province, and she made sure the whole table knew whose grain it was. She now farms twenty-four sea squares to my four, she pays most of the crown's tax, and she has started saying "I'll remember." I don't like the sound of that. Clark paid for the empty Wall line in breach damages, and now that it has something on it he's written my province number next to it, which tells me he's still building his case. Paddy is still my quiet friend, but he's talking to Rob about a Customs House, and that worries me. What really worries me is Rob. He has three pieces and only needs Silke's Scroll, his W2 is one square from it, and W3 is two steps from my Winter Palace. Next year the remaining two links of the Wall go up, the coast keeps its five a square, Clark gets his thirty to the letter, and I keep more in the jar every year. Then I'll quietly start working out what it would cost to make Rob look somewhere other than Silke's beach.
