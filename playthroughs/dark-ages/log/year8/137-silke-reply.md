diary: Clark gave me the I Ching to read, three grain for the ink, so for two years I've been the one telling the table what Heaven says. The first year was a bad harvest. I got ninety from the pile and paid twenty-seven in tax and five to Rich, and I still bought six squares of sea for forty, because land that pays for itself in a year is cheapest when everyone else is too frightened to buy it. The second year was a good one: two hundred and sixty in, seventy-eight to the crown, five to Rich, and my canal went in, both sections, in the order Clark wrote down in Year 4. I read that page back to him and he kept it. I bought four more squares and asked the crown for only ten. I didn't do it for Clark. A crown with half a grain in its purse ends up paying Rob for scenery, and I've already lived four years with nobody in that chair. I'm on about two hundred and fifty now, with sixteen squares of coast and a canal running through it, and that's more than I ever had under Chaos. The Wall over the north is written in for Year 9, Reina has sworn she won't move the date again, and I'll be counting to make sure it goes up.
