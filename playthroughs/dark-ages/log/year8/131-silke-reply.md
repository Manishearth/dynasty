move: land I25, I26, I27, I28; I pay 70, asking the crown for 10
say: "My canal's in. Year 4's page, read back and kept, and I'll write 'built' next to it myself. Four more squares, I25 to I28: eighty in all, seventy from me and ten from the crown, two and a half a square, the same as Paddy took last year. And nothing written down as owed, Clark. I don't want that thirty back. I want it to stay in the reserve so the Wall goes up in Year 9 and Reina never has to move that date again."
aside: "Sixteen squares of coast and a canal on it, and that's worth more to me than thirty grain of subsidy, especially if the subsidy empties the purse the next work has to come out of. A crown with grain in it doesn't get a Warlord bargaining for its palaces, and it doesn't hand the next wobble to Rich."
whisper: none

Ruling: four squares I25-I28: Silke 70, the crown 10 (less than offered, at her asking; nothing owed).
