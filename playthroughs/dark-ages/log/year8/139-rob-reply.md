diary: Two years under Clark, and the funny thing is the place works. Two canals are dug, Paddy's fifty is paid back, and Reina reads the dates out like a schoolmistress. Every province is spending its grain on fields and stone, and none of it is coming to me. I did get Paddy's Customs House, though. I paid his toll like a gentleman, walked it home to U21, and that's five pieces in my desert now. Then Heaven tied my lads' hands for a whole year, and it cost me five for the privilege. My grain sat at a hundred and seven and didn't move, which is the dullest thing that's happened to me since Year 1. Clark told me to my face the crown doesn't pay for scenery. Fair enough, but he still keeps checking how far my lads are from his palaces, and a man who counts the rounds is a man who'll pay before the count reaches nothing. What he doesn't see is W3 standing in Reina's field, three rounds off her Bowl. Reina and Silke are the last two names on my list, and the Wall goes up over the north next year with everyone's stone on it. I'll let them build it. A finished dynasty with fat barns is a much better thing to raid than a broken one, and I'd rather be paid to keep my distance than sit on a chair someone will only try to take from me.
