diary: Two years of Clark's crown, and it has done what it said it would, which I can't say for many crowns. Both canals are in, Paddy's fifty came back on the day promised, and the tax has sat at thirty in fat years and thin, so for once I've no complaint to make, and I've noticed nobody else is making any either. It wasn't cheap for me. The bad harvest took thirty out of my granary, Rob's lads took another ten on their way through Province 3, and Heaven sent me a disaster of my own. I kept the rent at the customary five through all of it, because I said I would, and Silke can't say a word against me for it. The granary is slowly coming back now that the desert has to keep its hands in its pockets. My land is still shut, and every year that Wall goes unbuilt is a year I pay into the pile and take nothing out of the ground, so I've put my quarter on the table, ten a link out of my own grain. Better it's my stone they remember on the Wall than my name on a petition. Clark has written it for Year 9 and Reina swears she'll never move the date again. For once I'm glad that woman writes everything down, because next spring I'll have her read that line out to the whole table, and I'll have my forty counted out before she's finished.
