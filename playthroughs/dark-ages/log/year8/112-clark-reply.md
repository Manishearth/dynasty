diary: Years Seven and Eight, in my own hand. Rich kept his word for a sixth and seventh year, thirty and thirty, to the grain, and I've written that down honestly. He also did what I'd stopped expecting and put two links of Wall on the board, and I've written down that both of them stand over Province 1, which leaves my own strip as shut as it was in Year One. The coast is fat now, and Silke paid a hundred and five in one year and said out loud whose harvest built the north's roof. That makes her the one I'd want standing next to me when I read Rich's promise back to him. The crown finished on fourteen and then forty-eight, which I'd call thin but not empty, with five hundred and ninety still owed in works. The new item, and the one that matters most, is Rob. He holds three pieces, his only missing one is Silke's Scroll, and W2 is standing one square from it. The day he takes it, I stop being the only loaded gun at this table, and every grain of my leverage gets cut in half. So next year I'll watch Q26 harder than I watch the treasury, and I may have to decide whether the Scroll is worth more in Silke's hands than my patience is worth to Rich. A hundred and ninety-four grain, set complete, and my loyalty is still rented by the year, though I'm now pricing in a competitor.
