move: pay Reina 5 more, freely: 10 in all
say: Ten it is, Reina, and gladly. You took five off me in the lean years and never squeezed, so in a fat year you'll have ten without me haggling over it. The river took ten off me too, mind, so I'll count it out: a hundred and seventy came in, ten went to the flood, ten to you, and the rest waits for Clark.
aside: Five grain is cheap for staying on good terms with the woman who runs the Harvest Pile, and I don't like being the one who paid less than he could afford. I'm saving the big purse for Rob and that Customs House, like he told me to.
whisper: none
