move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "A good year at last, and the pile pays out as written: Silke two hundred and sixty, Paddy two hundred and twenty, twenty each to Reina and myself, forty to the crown. The rent stays at the customary five, fat year or thin. Majesty, you told me to come with my share, so here I am: when the Wall over Province 3 goes on the page, the Gentry will put up a quarter of every link out of his own granary. Write it down, Reina. I'd like to see my name on that stone beside the crown's."
aside: "Twenty in from the harvest, ten from rent, and Rob's lads are stuck at home this year, so I'm finally gaining ground. My land can't earn a grain until that Wall stands, and the crown has half a grain in its purse, so a quarter from me is the cheapest way to move it up the page and get it remembered."
whisper: none

Ruling: rent 5 each. Offer on record: a quarter of each Province 3 Wall link (10 of 40). Aside: the crown holds 30.5 after the harvest, not half a grain; state in his next prompt.
