diary: The canal's in. Both sections, Year 7, the way Clark wrote it down, and I stood on the bank and watched them dig it in a bad harvest. That's twenty more on my harvest every year, and I'll not forget who paid for it. The crown was nearly empty, so I let it owe me fifty on the land instead of going hungry, and Clark paid me back every grain the next spring. In all my years at this table I've never seen a debt come home that fast. Rob's lad paid my toll on the new canal like a gentleman and then walked off with my Customs House, so I've no pieces left at all now, only land, nine squares of it up the coast, and soil can't be carried to the desert. I'm richer than I've ever been and I hold nothing anyone could steal, and I'm surprised to find I like it that way. Next year it's the Wall over the north, and Reina has the date in ink. I'll pay my tax and I'll watch the count, and if the crown keeps that figure the way it kept mine, I'll keep my name on Clark.
