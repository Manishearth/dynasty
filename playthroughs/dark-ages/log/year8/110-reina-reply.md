diary: Two good harvests running, and for the first time since I lost the chair my purse went up. I'm at sixty-six. I asked the coast for ten, said there were no conditions and meant it, and both of them paid it gladly in front of everyone, which matters more to me than the grain does. Rob still had five off me in Year 7, and the horsemen and my chef's send-off cost me a little more, but nothing I couldn't stand. Rich has finally started the Wall, two links, both over his own province, paid for out of Silke's tax, and she has noticed. Clark has noticed too, and he wrote the province number into his book. The man I ought to be watching now is Rob. He has three pieces and needs only Silke's Scroll, and W2 is one square away from it. If the desert takes the throne, every set on this board goes through his hands, and mine will be worth even less than it is. So next year I keep building up grain, I keep the coast thinking of me as the landlord who never squeezed, and I tell Silke plainly to guard that Scroll, because her safety and mine have become the same thing.
