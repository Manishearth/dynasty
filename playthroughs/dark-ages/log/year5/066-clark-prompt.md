A reserve under twenty and a thirty-a-year promise can't both last, so next year I'll find out whether Rich would rather break his word to me or find the grain somewhere. I'll be watching Reina's purse for the moment she offers a better rent than his. Ninety-nine grain, set complete, and my loyalty is still rented by the year.
