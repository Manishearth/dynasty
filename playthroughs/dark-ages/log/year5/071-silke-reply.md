move: land L26, L27; I pay 25, asking the crown for 15

say: "Two more fields, Rich. Fifteen of mine and your five on the first, then ten and ten on the second. That's fifteen from a crown that just took twenty-one off me, so you're getting most of my tax back as a sea wall. Every one of these squares pays forty to the Pile in a good year, and none of them ever asks for a palace. Clark, write that the coast's buying land while the throne's buying time."

aside: "That puts seven fields by the sea and still leaves me sixty-four, plenty for a Bowl or a Customs House when one comes up. I went north up the coast toward L instead of south, because Rob's soldier on R22 is only a few steps from anything I'd put on P or Q."

whisper: none

Ruling: the first square (L26) is 15 hers, 5 the crown's, as offered. The second (L27) asks the crown for 10, more than Rich offered, so it goes to Rich in a free prompt; nothing is bought until he answers. Note filed: a square adds 20 a year to her harvest (her "forty to the Pile" misstates it).
