## Rich's diary so far, in his own words

Two years, and the throne I flattered so faithfully is empty, and I helped empty it. I don't regret it. Fifty in the hundred on drowned fields was never going to last, and I gave my name to the revolt and kept it there, as I always do. The granary stands at ninety-seven and a half, the largest any claimant has, though Silke will go on telling anyone who listens that I built it out of her rice. I have the Customs House and the Bowl now. Paddy outbid me for the Jar by a single grain past my word, and that same Paddy now holds a full set that could put him in my chair at the Harvest Pile, so the quiet rice farmer has become the man I must keep sweetest. Clark has his ledger and his promises, Rob has his swords and his raids, and between the three of us nobody counts ahead, which suits me well enough for now. Next year I keep the rent at five, keep Paddy grateful, and let every claimant learn that the road to the chair runs past my door.

## Years 3–4

Two more years without a crown, and my granary has grown from ninety-seven and a half to a hundred and ninety-nine and a half. No tax, no salary to pay anyone, and the rent kept at the customary five, which I promised and which I kept, though Silke says it through her teeth. Rob's lads finally came to Province 3 and ate ten grain in a single night. It cost me little, but it tells me the desert has noticed where the grain is, and that a granary with no swords beside it is only a larder with the door left open. Paddy turned down my twenty-five for his Jar, and he was right to, since that Jar is all that keeps me out of his seat. I'll remember he guards it, and I'll remember that he'll sell his name to whoever can keep Rob off his coast. Clark has finally put a canal on paper, and that ledger of his has more friends in it than I'd like. Next year I keep my claim and keep the rent at five, and I start thinking about which of Rob's swords could be bought, or a few of my own, because whoever finally wears the crown will have to come through my granary to get there.
