Rent's the customary five, Silke, Paddy. Eleven years now, and I said five and it's still five. And Clark, since the Wall's done, I'll say it before anyone else does: the Reservoir is Province 3's stone, and the Gentry will put his grain toward it the same way he did for the Wall."
aside: "A hundred and sixty out of the ground and ten in rent, and I'm back near two hundred and seventy. That's enough to buy a sword to stand next to my Jar and still pay my share of the Reservoir. Silke and Paddy are sitting on fifteen hundred between them, so I'll squeeze them later, when they come asking a favour. For now my word is worth more than they could pay me."
