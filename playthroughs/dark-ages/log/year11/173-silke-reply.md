move: land H30, I30, J30, K30, L29, M28, M29, N28; I pay 0, asking the crown for 160
say: For nine years I've asked the crown for tens and sixes because it had nothing in the drawer, Clark, and now it has five hundred and forty-five, a hundred and ninety-two of them mine. So this year I'll take your offer at its word: eight squares at twenty, the crown's full share, a hundred and sixty. That still leaves you three hundred and ten. The canal, the Irrigation and the Pagoda come to a hundred and thirty between them, so I've counted it, you can afford them, and I'll be watching to make sure each one goes up.
aside: When the crown was empty I paid for my own sea to keep the stone coming, and now the crown is fat on my tax, so I'm taking some of it back as land. The works are still covered, and every grain I don't spend is one more in my hand at the end.
whisper: none
