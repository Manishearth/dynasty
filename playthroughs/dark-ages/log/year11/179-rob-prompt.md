## Rob's diary so far, in his own words

Two years in and the throne is empty, and I never even had to lay a finger on it. Reina taxed them half twice over, flooded fields and all, and when I called the revolt every one of them stood up beside me, the Gentry and the Official too, the very men who'd been eating at her table. It's cost me nothing yet. I've passed every lot, my grain sits at seventy-five, and with no Emperor there's nobody to pay me off or send men after me, so my soldiers just carry on. ## Years 3–4

The longer they argue over who sits, the longer I get to keep collecting.

## Years 5–6

Well, they finally found someone to sit in the chair, and it's Clark, the little man with the ledger, which makes it the only one of my sins he's ever going to get to do anything about. I held out through the drought, and when the people doubled, the coast went over to him one name after another. In the end I put my own name on his claim too. An empty throne never paid me a grain to stay at home, and an Emperor might. It cost me the count and a soldier Paddy made me sell him, and I took that soldier straight back the same afternoon along with Paddy's Jar, and Rich's Bowl about an hour after he finished his set. That makes four pieces in my desert, and only one of them was ever paid for, by the man who lost it. Clark promised me "my figure" once my lads are back on the sand. ## Years 7–8

Two years under Clark, and the funny thing is the place works. Two canals are dug, Paddy's fifty is paid back, and Reina reads the dates out like a schoolmistress. Every province is spending its grain on fields and stone, and none of it is coming to me. I did get Paddy's Customs House, though. I paid his toll like a gentleman, walked it home to U21, and that's five pieces in my desert now. Then Heaven tied my lads' hands for a whole year, and it cost me five for the privilege. My grain sat at a hundred and seven and didn't move, which is the dullest thing that's happened to me since Year 1. Clark told me to my face the crown doesn't pay for scenery. What he doesn't see is W3 standing in Reina's field, three rounds off her Bowl. Reina and Silke are the last two names on my list, and the Wall goes up over the north next year with everyone's stone on it. I'll let them build it. A finished dynasty with fat barns is a much better thing to raid than a broken one, and I'd rather be paid to keep my distance than sit on a chair someone will only try to take from me.

## Years 9–10

Well, they built the thing. Twelve links of Wall from Province 1 to the sand, and every province put its own grain in the mortar, even Reina, who hadn't two coins to rub together by the end of it. I took Rich's Customs House to L8 for my trouble, which makes six pieces on my desert, and then Heaven took half my granary in a single night.
