say: "Ten more squares, Rich, at your five a square: a hundred and fifty from me, fifty from the crown, and that leaves you plenty to build with. Count the tax I just paid, though: two hundred and forty-nine. With your fifty that makes three hundred, so the coast is still paying for its own mud and your Wall too. Congratulations on the open strip, Clark. It took four links over your roof before the crown remembered there was a sea."
aside: "Each square pays back twenty once, in Year 12, against fifteen out of my purse, so I come out ahead and the crown spends grain it can't use to outbid me. I've kept off Q26 and Q27, where W2 could walk straight in from T24."
