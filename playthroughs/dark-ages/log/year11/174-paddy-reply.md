move: land T13, U13, V13, U22, V22; I pay 90, asking the crown for 10
say: Five more squares, Clark: three up the north end and two beside Rob's lad at U21, so he's got neighbours. I'll pay ninety and the crown pays two a square, because that purse has a canal, an irrigation and a Pagoda still to find, and I'd like the record to say Province 6 paid its own way. Reina, write it down like that.
aside: It's a fat year and nobody can raid the coast, so it's a good year to put spades in. I'll not take the crown's whole twenty when there's two hundred and ten of work still owed, and I'd rather it went into stone than into my fields.
whisper: none
