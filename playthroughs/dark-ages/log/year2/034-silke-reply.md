diary: Two years, one Land Deed Scroll in the far corner of P25, and I still don't own a Customs House or a Bowl. Both times I stopped a single grain short, and both times the piece went to someone with a fuller purse and less sense. Count what went out: rent to Rich twice, tax to two different Emperors, and two of Rob's "courtesy calls," and the only thing that came back was a quarter of my own Dragon Jar. I told Reina her cushions were in danger, and she lost them the same afternoon. Now Clark holds a full set beside Rich's throne and gets paid thirty to keep it there, which is a bribe, whatever he writes in his ledger. Reina whispered promises of canals on the coast, but she whispered them too late to matter, and a Gentry who just lost her own throne makes no promises that hold. Next year I hold my grain, bid only where the price is honest, and buy land the moment the people double, because land is the one thing nobody can bid away from me.
