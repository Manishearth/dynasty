diary: Two years now and I've bid on six lots and won none of them. I lost each one by a grain, and twice to a man with a fuller purse than mine. First I was too careful, and then I was a little less careful and it still wasn't enough. Rich took the throne with pieces bought off the coast, Clark built his set in a single evening, and Reina was Emperor one year and keeping the Pile the next. At least my grain is all still my own. I've paid every rent and every tax I was asked for and I owe nobody, and Rob hasn't been round to my door yet. My Wooden Bowl fetched a fair price for Clark's collection, and I'll take that quarter gladly. Next year the Land Deed stock is empty everywhere but Clark's hand, so I'll need to talk to people rather than just outbid them, and I'll keep my twenty back no matter what, because a set's no use without the grain beside it.
