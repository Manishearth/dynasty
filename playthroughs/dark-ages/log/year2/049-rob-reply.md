diary: Two years in and the throne is empty, and I never even had to lay a finger on it. Reina taxed them half twice over, flooded fields and all, and when I called the revolt every one of them stood up beside me, the Gentry and the Official too, the very men who'd been eating at her table. It's cost me nothing yet. I've passed every lot, my grain sits at seventy-five, and with no Emperor there's nobody to pay me off or send men after me, so my soldiers just carry on. The count for the chair is three ways deadlocked: Rich has the granary, Clark has Silke, and I have the only swords on the board. I'm happy for that to go on for a good long while. Next year W1 takes Clark's Deed and W2 goes down the coast to see Paddy's shiny new set "from a distance". If they want an Emperor badly enough, they'll know whose price has to be met.
