move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: The pile pays out, friends, and gladly: Your Majesty forty, Clark ten, Silke ten, Paddy sixty, and sixty to your humble servant, since the floods were so kind to Province 3. Rent stays at the customary five each. I won't squeeze a flooded field, Silke, a landlord's only as good as his tenants' goodwill. I do hope the crown remembers the flooded provinces when the tax comes round, Your Majesty. Heaven has already taken its share there.
aside: Ninety-odd grain in my granary and two peasants who'll remember I didn't raise the rent in a bad year. Funny how the crown's own province never seems to flood; I'm keeping count of who's paying this tax and who's getting tired of it.
