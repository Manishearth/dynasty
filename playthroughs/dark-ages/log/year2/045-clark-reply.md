diary: Years 1 and 2. I had a complete set by the end of the first year's auctions, Deed, Jar and Bowl, and I've kept them in the drawer ever since. A throne you buy has to be guarded every night, so I'd rather be given one. This year Reina put fifty on a drowned field for the second year running and wouldn't cut a grain of it when we asked her to. Five names stood by the revolt and mine was one of them. The chair has been empty since. The first count went three ways, Rich on his granary, Rob on his two swords and me on Silke's one hand, so nobody was acclaimed. That cost me a salary, and Rob has taken another 5 out of Province 2 and calls it the Warlord's tax, which leaves me at 21.5. New in the ledger: Paddy now holds a full set as well, Rich is already calling himself the friend of "a man with a full set and no chair", and the Gentry's man is precisely the man I won't have on that throne. I've given Paddy my word on the first canal, Silke has my word on the flood rate, and I can wait. Chaos costs the landlord more than it costs me, and I'll be the one keeping count when the table gets tired of waiting.
