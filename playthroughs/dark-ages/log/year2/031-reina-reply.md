diary: I taxed at half, built the Summer Palace, and by the second harvest I was sitting in Province 3 holding the Pile. Silke warned me out loud, and I counted my reserve when I should have counted Rich's set. That's the lesson, and it cost me a throne and a hundred and ninety-five grain I'd meant to put into stone. Then I paid nine for a Customs House and went to eleven and a half for the rest, and Clark outbid me at sixteen. Now he's the one with the full set, and Rich is paying him thirty a year to keep it in his pocket. That arrangement won't last. Rob has put a man at I11 and another at M15 to watch my Customs House, so I'm down to thirty-eight and the soldiers have the measure of my province. Next year I keep the Pile scrupulously honest, I buy the Jar and the Bowl as cheaply as the coast will let me, and I hold twenty in reserve whatever Rob takes. The coast should be my friend, because Silke was right before anyone else was. And the palace on G24 is still mine in every way that matters.
