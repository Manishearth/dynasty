diary: Years One and Two, entered in my own hand. Reina taxed us at half in the first fat year, and I noted it. Rich bought three lots by one grain apiece over me and took her throne within the year, and I noted that too, along with the fact that nobody lifted a finger to stop him. This year I spent thirty-five on a Scroll, a Jar and a Bowl and outbid Reina for the last two. It was dear, but it bought the only thing at this table that's worth more than grain: a loaded set that nobody knows when I'll fire. It's already paying. Rich went from ten a year to thirty, publicly, and gave his word in private: "thirty a year, every year, not one grain short and not one day late." That promise is copied here word for word, so I can quote it back to him the day he breaks it. Next year I'll watch three things: whether Rob's soldiers start walking toward I15, whether Reina comes calling with a better offer than Rich's, and whether I stay above twenty grain, which keeps the set loaded. Loyalty is a fine thing, and so far it has a price of thirty.
