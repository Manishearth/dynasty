diary: Two years in, and somehow I'm the one holding a full set: the House, the Deed and the Jar, all down by the sea for twenty-one grain. Last year I paid thirty in tax and lost the Deed on a tie. This year the whole table stood up against fifty percent, me with them, and Reina answered with a promise and a threat instead of a cut, so now there's no Emperor and no tax, and nothing gets built either. I didn't expect to feel uneasy about that, but I do. Rob's soldier is already standing in Province 6 and took five off me tonight, and with nobody on the throne there's nobody to stop him. Clark has promised me the first canal in writing, Rich is suddenly very friendly, and Rob says he'll keep his swords off whoever backs him. I'll back none of them on a promise. Whoever gets my name will have to show me they'll actually dig the canal, and I'll keep my grain close until then.
