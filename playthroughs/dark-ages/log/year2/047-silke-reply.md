diary: Two years. I paid thirty to the crown and five to Rich, my field went under the floods, and the crown still wanted half of it. So I called it, the whole table stood with me, and now there's nobody in that chair. This year's tax is void, which leaves me with about twenty-nine grain, the first time I've gone up instead of down. I bid small, and I lost every piece I bid on. Paddy has a full set now, and I have a promise from Clark, said out loud: half the rate on flooded fields. Rich wants the throne with a granary we filled for him, and he won't get it while my name's on someone else's claim. My backing is the only thing I own that's worth much right now, and I'll make them bid for it.
