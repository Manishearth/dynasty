diary: Two years, and the throne I flattered so faithfully is empty, and I helped empty it. I don't regret it. Fifty in the hundred on drowned fields was never going to last, and I gave my name to the revolt and kept it there, as I always do. The granary stands at ninety-seven and a half, the largest any claimant has, though Silke will go on telling anyone who listens that I built it out of her rice. I have the Customs House and the Bowl now. Paddy outbid me for the Jar by a single grain past my word, and that same Paddy now holds a full set that could put him in my chair at the Harvest Pile, so the quiet rice farmer has become the man I must keep sweetest. Clark has his ledger and his promises, Rob has his swords and his raids, and between the three of us nobody counts ahead, which suits me well enough for now. Next year I keep the rent at five, keep Paddy grateful, and let every claimant learn that the road to the chair runs past my door.
