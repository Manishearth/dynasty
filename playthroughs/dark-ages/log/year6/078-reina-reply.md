move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "Pile's paid out: a hundred and thirty-five to Silke, fifty-five to Paddy, fifteen to the crown, and the north and the desert pay in. Rent's the floor this year, five each and no more. Silke, you were right to count it out loud. You gave me ten when I had nothing to give back, and the way I'll repay it is by not asking twice. And Rich, a crown that ends the year on four, with the Official holding a full set and a Jar walking into the desert, ought to be counting more carefully than I am."
aside: "If there's a revolt, the next Emperor is chosen on grain, soldiers and friends. I've no grain and no soldiers, so friends are all I have, and the coast is the only place I can make them. Taking the floor from a woman sitting on a hundred and eighty-seven is cheap, and she won't forget it."
whisper: none
