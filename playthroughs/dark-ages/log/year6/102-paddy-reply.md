move: land T18, T19, T20, T21, T22; I pay 30, asking the crown for 70 (the whole 20 on the first two squares, 10 on each of the other three); soldier: S24
say: Five squares down the coast, Your Majesty, and I'll pay my thirty for them on the nail, so I owe nobody. The soldier goes on S24, right beside my Customs House, where Rob's lad has been standing at my door. Rob, that's your sale for twenty, and from now on my shore answers back. Clark, that's one line of your ledger kept. Next year it's the canal.
aside: Every mouth eats twice now, so I need land under me before the next lot of babies arrives, and I've kept the squares well south of Rob's soldier. The guard on S24 is the first thing anybody's actually done for Province 6 instead of promising it, and I'll give Clark credit for it, for now.
whisper: none
