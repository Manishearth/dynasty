## At the table since your last turn

(Storyteller's note: The Emperor's soldier for Province 6: a soldier stands on a square of its buyer's province, so the crown can't place one on Paddy's coast itself. It can pay Paddy to buy one for Province 6 (20, to the Warlord, who must sell). Rich's strip, like Province 2's, opens only once the four Wall links above it stand.)

Reina: "Six from me, six from Rich, six from Silke, six from Paddy: twenty-four to the crown, counted twice and not a grain short. Rob, your share is nothing, so I won't trouble you for it. Your Majesty, thirty percent on a good harvest is gentle, perhaps gentler than the works can afford. And twelve years ago, I mean half an hour ago, you wrote the Wall over the north into your first year. The Winter Palace has to come first, I know the law better than anyone here, but I'm putting the Wall in my ledger for the year after Paddy's canal, and I'll read it back to you that year."

Reina collects 24 for the crown: 6 each from herself, Rich, Silke and Paddy. Clark builds the Winter Palace on H20 (20) and buys three squares for Province 1, F23 to F25 (60), all from the crown's reserve. The crown offers to pay Paddy 20 toward a soldier for Province 6.

Silke: "Six squares, Your Majesty, on the terms you read out yourself: the crown pays the full twenty on the first two and half on the other four. That's forty from me and eighty from you, all counted. I'm putting them at the north end of the coast, as far from Rob's lads as the law lets me. Next year they put a hundred and twenty back in the harvest, and my canal's in your second year, so I'll be reading that back to you too."

Paddy: "Five squares down the coast, Your Majesty, and I'll pay my thirty for them on the nail, so I owe nobody. The soldier goes on S24, right beside my Customs House, where Rob's lad has been standing at my door. Rob, that's your sale for twenty, and from now on my shore answers back. Clark, that's one line of your ledger kept. Next year it's the canal."

New land: Silke buys six squares of sea, K25, K26, L26, M26, N26 and N27 (she pays 40, the crown 80); Paddy buys five squares, T18 to T22 (he pays 30, the crown 70). Paddy buys a soldier from Rob for 20 and stands it on S24, beside his Customs House; the crown pays him the 20. The crown's reserve is now 155.5.

Reina: "The Official starts again with an empty table and seventy-four grain, so I'll bid like it. Silke, Paddy, I'll pay fair for your Jar and your Bowl. Rich, you've had six years of the customary five out of the coast. If you want that Jar, you'll pay for it properly."

Rich: "Your Majesty, I'll bid for that Jar, I won't pretend otherwise, and three quarters of whatever I pay goes into the Harvest Pile, so it feeds every one of you. And let me say it plainly, for the ledger: I put my name to this crown, and the Gentry doesn't tear down what he's just helped build. A full set in my hands is a guarantee for this reign, not a threat to it."

Silke: "My Jar starts at five. It's only clay, but it's coast clay, and nobody gets it for nothing. And I'm bidding on Paddy's Bowl. Reina, I know you need it too, but you're on seventy-four with a salary of ten, and I've got six new squares to protect. If I lose it, I'll lose it at a fair price, not a cheap one."

Paddy: "I've had one Jar carried off to the desert already, so I'll bid honestly on this one, and not past what a jar is worth. Rich, you offered me twenty-five for mine last year, so you know my price on jars. As for my Bowl, it's five or nothing, and it'll be back again next round if nobody wants it."

Rob: "Long live Emperor Clark, then, and look at him, a palace and three fields before he's even warm. Pass on both, as always; I don't buy furniture, I collect it. Paddy, you paid me for the sword you've stood next to my lad, and you built your fields on my sand, so thank you twice. Clark, 'back on the sand' is a lovely phrase. Put a number next to it and we'll see how fast they walk."

Reina: "Twenty-two, Paddy. The Official has to start her set somewhere, and I'd rather start it with a bowl from a coast that answers back."

Silke: "Twenty, Reina. That's my last word on it. Every grain past that goes into Rich's pile, and I've filled that pile enough already."

Rich: "Thirty-six, and twenty-seven of it goes into the Harvest Pile for all of you. Silke, that's a fair price for coast clay, and you won't hear me say otherwise. The Jar goes up in the far north-west corner where Rob's lads would have to walk a long way to admire it. Your Majesty, a full set it may be, but I said it in front of everyone and I'll say it again: it serves your reign, not against it."

Reina: "G14, well back under the line where the Wall ought to stand. Your Majesty, I've put my first piece where your Wall will cover it, so I'll take that as a reason to build it on time."

Auctions: Rich wins Silke's Dragon Jar for 36 (Paddy stopped at 35, Reina at 28) and puts it on I8: he holds a complete set, the Customs House, the Wooden Bowl and the Dragon Jar. Reina wins Paddy's Wooden Bowl for 21 after a live raise with Silke, and puts it on G14.

Rob: "Rich, you had a full set for very nearly a whole afternoon, and I want you to know I enjoyed watching it. Your Bowl's coming to live at N12, and W3 will have his five out of Province 3 as well. Paddy, I sold you that soldier this afternoon and I've just bought him back for nothing, so your shore can answer back from my pocket. Clark, you can see what keeping my lads 'on the sand' costs now. My figure's still waiting for you."

Rob marches: W1 takes Rich's Wooden Bowl from M12 to N12 in the desert, breaking Rich's set; W2 steps straight onto S24 and captures Paddy's new soldier; W3 raids 5 from Rich on L14. Clark pays Reina 10 as the Official. Year 6 ends with Clark on the throne (145.5 in the reserve), the Winter Palace built, new land bought for Provinces 1, 5 and 6, and 850 in works still owed.
