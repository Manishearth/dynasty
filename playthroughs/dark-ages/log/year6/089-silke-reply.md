diary: Two years, and I have ten fields along the sea where I had five, and eighty-eight grain in the purse after paying for every one of them. The crown put in eleven grain across all those fields, half a grain at a time at the end, and I paid sixty-one and a half in tax for that over two years. Rich keeps his word, I'll grant him that every year, but his word comes to exactly what's in the treasury, and the treasury ends every year at zero, while Clark is paid thirty to write it down. I gave Reina ten when she had seven, and she repaid me honestly by not asking again, which is more than the crown can say. The first section of the Canal went to Paddy's coast, and the same year Rob walked off with Paddy's Customs House, so now he sits on two of them out in the desert, and Clark still has two Bowls and only needs one. Count the board and you'll find my Scroll on P25 is the only piece Rob hasn't walked off with, and W2 is three rounds from it. Next year I buy the spare Bowl off Clark or wait for Rob to put a Customs House up for sale, whichever comes first, because one grain short of a set is still short, and my fields need someone at the table who'll speak for them.
