diary: Well, they finally found someone to sit in the chair, and it's Clark, the little man with the ledger, which makes it the only one of my sins he's ever going to get to do anything about. I held out through the drought, and when the people doubled, the coast went over to him one name after another. In the end I put my own name on his claim too. An empty throne never paid me a grain to stay at home, and an Emperor might. It cost me the count and a soldier Paddy made me sell him, and I took that soldier straight back the same afternoon along with Paddy's Jar, and Rich's Bowl about an hour after he finished his set. That makes four pieces in my desert, and only one of them was ever paid for, by the man who lost it. Clark promised me "my figure" once my lads are back on the sand. I'll believe that when I'm holding the grain. Until then Reina's new Bowl is sitting at G14, Silke's coast is still untouched, and there's a brand-new Winter Palace on H20 that costs the crown twenty if anyone so much as breathes on it. The dynasty has an Emperor again, and I'm still the most dangerous man at the table. I've never been happier.
