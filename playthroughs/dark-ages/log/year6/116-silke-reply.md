diary: Year five was another average harvest with nobody in the chair, and nothing changed: thirty-five in, five to Rich, and three claimants who each led one count. Then the people doubled, and suddenly every mouth on my coast ate twice. There was land at twenty a square and nobody could sell it to me, so Chaos stopped costing nothing and started costing me something every year. That's what brought everyone round. Clark wrote it all down, with years beside it, and in the end the whole table backed him, Rich included, right after he'd sworn he never would. I've got six squares of sea on the north of the coast now. They cost me forty and the crown eighty, and they're well away from Rob's swords. My canal is written in for Clark's second year, and I'll read it back to him. Rich bought my own Jar for thirty-six, I got nine of it, and he had a full set for about an hour before Rob walked off with his Bowl. Reina beat me to Paddy's Bowl by one grain. So I'm on a hundred and twenty with no pieces and a crown I helped put up, and next year I find out whether my name bought me a canal or only another promise.
