move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: Long live Emperor Clark! The harvest is paid, twenty to each of you, and Your Majesty's twenty goes into the pile, and I'll keep it safe there for you. The rent stays at the customary five, as I promised the day I backed you, and I keep my word to the letter. Your Majesty, since my strip of land only opens under four links of the Wall, I'll be watching that first stretch over the north go up with great interest.
aside: Five again: I promised it, and a Gentry who keeps his word is a Gentry nobody bothers to replace. The Dragon Jar is back in stock and up for auction this year, and it's the last piece I need, so I'll smile at His Majesty now and bid hard when the Jar comes up.
whisper: none
