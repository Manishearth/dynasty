diary: Years 5-6. Two years, two more pieces in my tent: Clark's spare Dragon Jar and Paddy's Customs House, so now I hold something of Reina's, Clark's and Paddy's, and the only one left is Silke's Scroll, three steps from W2. The bad harvest and the new utility levy took a bite out of me, but five grain here and five there off the road kept me at about twenty, which is all a man with three soldiers needs. I'll admit I got clever with Clark's Scroll. I stood on it and took the five to look restrained, and now the square's been raided, so it's safe from me for good. That's a lesson for the night: a threat only works while you can still carry it out. The real news is Rich. He's ended two years running on zero, he pays Clark thirty every year without fail, and the Wall's still waiting, while Clark writes every grain of it down in ink. Reina's on her last few grain, and she's started dropping hints about counting carefully. Somebody is going to call it soon. When they do, W3 will be standing near the Winter Palace looking helpful, and I'll be holding a piece of everybody's set and smiling at whoever counts the votes.
