say: My claim stands, but Her Majesty is right that the wind has changed, so here are my terms, plain enough for Clark to write down. If I'm acclaimed, Province 6's canal goes in during my first year, and I'll buy soldiers out of my own granary to stand on Paddy's coast against Rob's lads. In my second year comes the Wall over the north, and the Pagoda before my reign is out. Paddy, Madam, that's a figure and a promise, and you all know I keep mine to the letter.
aside: Now the people have doubled, my granary is losing five a year with no land to buy, so a chair I never wanted has turned into the only door to my own fields. If Paddy and Reina put their names on me, I lead on grain and friends together, and I'll happily lay every stone I just promised rather than watch Clark lay them.
