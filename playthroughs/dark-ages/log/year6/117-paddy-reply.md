diary: Rob's lad walked off with my Jar in the drought, and that was the end of my patience with an empty chair. My set was gone, every mouth on the coast was eating twice, and there was land to be had that nobody could buy. So when Clark finally put Province 6's canal into the record with a year beside it, I put my name on him, and by the end of the afternoon the whole table had done the same, even Rich and Rob. He's kept the first few lines already: five new squares down the coast with the crown paying seventy of the hundred, and the twenty for my soldier. Then Rob's man stepped onto S24 and captured that soldier in the same year I bought it, so my shore is back to answering with nothing. I bid thirty-five for Silke's Jar mostly to keep it from Rich, and he paid thirty-six, then lost his Bowl to Rob before supper, so at least nobody at the table is sitting on a set. I'm down to my Customs House and a hundred and fifty grain, but I've got land under me now and a canal promised for next year. I'll take Clark at his word until next year's count, and I'll watch every grain of it.
