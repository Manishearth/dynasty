move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: An average year, so the pile pays out by the book, and I'll count it into every hand myself. Silke, I'll take your thanks and frame it. The rent is the customary five from each of you, the same as Year 1, and fourteen years now without a grain more. If the Pagoda goes up next year, nobody at this table can say the Gentry raised it on his way out the door. And Rob, five again? You've eaten more of Province 3 than its own rice ever did.
aside: Raising it now would gain me a few grain and lose me fourteen years of keeping my word, and I'd rather end the night known as the man who never broke a figure. My fields behind the Wall and beside the Reservoir earn me more than any squeeze would, so I'll keep the grain they bring in and the reputation with it.
whisper: none
