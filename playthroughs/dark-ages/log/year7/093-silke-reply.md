move: pay 5 more
say: Ten, then, Reina, and I'll count it out so nobody says I paid quietly: two hundred and twenty came in, four went to the sea, ten to you, and the crown's hand hasn't even reached my purse yet. You asked honestly with no conditions, so you get it. Rich, Clark, the coast has paid its rent and paid for the Wall you didn't build, so when the tax comes round I expect it to buy something with salt water in it.
aside: Five grain is nothing next to two hundred and ninety, and a Gentry with forty-three in her purse remembers who paid her in full. Still, I've just read out that she's one of the seats I can take once I own a Customs House and a Bowl, so I'd rather she owes me than resents me.
