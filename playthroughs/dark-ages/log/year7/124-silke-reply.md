move: land J25, J26, J27, K27, L27, M27; I pay 40, asking the crown for 80
say: "Six more, all in the north, away from anyone's swords. That's J25, J26, J27, K27, L27 and M27. By Clark's terms the first two are the crown's, twenty each, and on the other four it's ten from me and ten from the crown, so I pay forty and the reserve pays eighty. Counted twice, Reina, the way you like it. Paddy's canal went in today, and I watched it. Mine's next on that page, Clark, and I've got the ink."
aside: "Each square pays for itself in a year, so a bad harvest is exactly when I should be buying, and with a crown back in the chair someone else pays two-thirds of it. Twelve squares of coast by next spring, and I'm still holding back enough grain to bid when the pieces come up."
