move: land J25, J26, J27, K28, L28, M28; I pay 90, asking the crown for 30
say: Six more fields, Rich, at your five a square: that's ninety from me and thirty from the crown, and they all go into the Pile in plain sight. And thank you for the canal, honestly. That's the first stone the crown has laid on my coast in seven years, and I'll say so in front of Clark's book. But the Wall line is still empty, and every grain of salt water I'm given goes back out in tax the next year, so dig the Wall before the horsemen take a second bite.
aside: Each field pays for itself in a year, so ninety grain now comes back as a hundred and twenty a year from next year, and I still keep a hundred and thirty-eight for the day Clark sells a Bowl or Rob gets tired of guarding a Customs House. Sixteen fields and a canal: whoever ends up on that throne will have to talk to the coast.
whisper: none

---
Ruling: Silke buys six squares, J25, J26, J27, K28, L28, M28, at 15 each and the crown's 5 each (90 and 30), as the crown offered. Sixteen sea squares.
