move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: "The pile pays out as written: Silke ninety, Paddy seventy, and the rest of us pay into it, myself included, thirty from the Gentry's own granary. And the rent stays at the customary five, bad harvest and all. I said five while I'm Gentry, and a thin year is no time to make a liar of me. Majesty, a lean year is when the works matter most, and Province 3 sits under four links of Wall that would look very handsome with your name carved on them."
aside: "Five from each of them, ten in all. That doesn't cover the thirty I just paid in, but every year I hold it at five is another year Silke can't call me greedy, and I'll want that goodwill when it comes time to share out the cost of the Wall over my land. The canal goes first, and then I start reading Clark's promise back to him."
whisper: none
