move: land U29, V28, V29; I pay 45, asking the crown for 15
say: Three more fields, Rich, at your five a square: forty-five from me, fifteen from the crown, all into the Pile where Clark can see it. I've lost a buffalo this year and I won't pretend it didn't sting, but a hundred and ten from one harvest is more than I ever thought I'd see. I'm keeping well over my twenty back, and Rob, that's partly because I'd like to talk to you about a Customs House at T23 before the year's out.
aside: I've put the new fields down at the far end, out by U28, and away from that soldier sitting on S24, because Rob's legs will heal by next year. That leaves me seventy grain, enough to pay Rob fairly for my piece back and still have a Jar and a Deed to save for.
