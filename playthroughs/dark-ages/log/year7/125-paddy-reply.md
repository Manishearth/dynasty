move: land T17, U17, U18, U19; I pay 70, asking the crown for 10
say: "Clark, the canal's in. I watched the spades go into the ground myself, and I'll say it in front of all of you: that's a figure kept. Now the land. Your terms say the crown pays sixty on four squares, but I can count, and after the salaries there's ten and a half in that reserve. So give me ten, two and a half a square, and I'll put up the seventy. Write the other fifty down as owed to Province 6, Silke, ink and all. I'm not letting it go, I just won't have the crown going hungry over my fields."
aside: "Four squares at the top of my coast, as far from Rob's lad on S24 as I can get them, and each one pays for itself in a year. I'm paying most of it myself because an empty reserve is how we lost the last Emperor, but that fifty goes in the book and I'll ask for it every spring, same as Reina will."
whisper: none
