## Paddy's diary so far, in his own words

## Years 1-2

Two years now and I've bid on six lots and won none of them. I lost each one by a grain, and twice to a man with a fuller purse than mine. First I was too careful, and then I was a little less careful and it still wasn't enough. Rich took the throne with pieces bought off the coast, Clark built his set in a single evening, and Reina was Emperor one year and keeping the Pile the next. At least my grain is all still my own. I've paid every rent and every tax I was asked for and I owe nobody, and Rob hasn't been round to my door yet. My Wooden Bowl fetched a fair price for Clark's collection, and I'll take that quarter gladly. Next year the Land Deed stock is empty everywhere but Clark's hand, so I'll need to talk to people rather than just outbid them, and I'll keep my twenty back no matter what, because a set's no use without the grain beside it.

## Years 3-4

I finally won something, Clark's Customs House for nine grain, after six lots that went to other people. It's sitting down at S24, and I've been watching Rob's soldier creep toward it ever since, one square at a time. I put three fields into the sea with the crown paying half, because the card told me plainly I'd go hungry otherwise, and I'd rather owe nobody than be proud and broke. But a bad harvest, two marches through my province and Reina's rent have left me with fourteen and a half grain, under the twenty I'd promised myself I'd keep back, and I won't pretend it's otherwise. Clark bought the last Jar and Bowl just to sit on them, so I'll have to get my Jar and Land Deed by asking people across the table now, and I'll do it honestly. Next year the new fields start paying. First I'll get the purse back up past twenty, then I'll go and talk to Clark and Silke, and I'll keep a close eye on that tent at R22.

## Years 5-6

I said no to a field in Year 5 because I'd told the table twenty and fourteen and a half isn't twenty. Rich didn't hold it against me. This year he gave me the ten and ten he'd promised, and the first section of the Grand Canal on top of it, so I've a new field at U28 and a canal both paying from next harvest, and forty-seven grain in my purse, the most I've ever had. But I watched that soldier at R22 for two years, and in the end he walked one step and carried my Customs House off into the desert, and Rob apologised while he did it. Nine grain and seven lots it took me to win that piece, and now I'm back to holding nothing, the same as Year 1, only with more grain. Rob has Reina's piece, Clark's and mine, and Clark has a whole set sitting in front of him. Next year I need to get the Customs House back from Rob, or buy a Jar and a Land Deed off somebody, and I'll have to pay fairly for it, because I won't beg and I won't owe. I'll keep my twenty back like I said, and I'll ask Rob straight out what he wants for it.
