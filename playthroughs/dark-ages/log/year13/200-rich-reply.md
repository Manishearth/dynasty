say: Bad harvest, so the pile pays out as the book says, and I'll count every grain of it into your hands myself. The rent's the customary five from Silke and five from Paddy, the same as Year 1 and every year since, thirteen of them now. And Silke, you'll be wanting to tell me I'm squeezing you in a lean year. You're sitting on two thousand three hundred. I could ask more, and you know I won't.
aside: Thirteen years at five, and that number is worth more to me than any increase, because when the last stone goes up nobody at this table will be able to say the Gentry broke his word. The land behind the Wall and the Reservoir is what fills my granary now, so I don't need to squeeze the peasants for it.
