say: "Three hundred and fifty-six, Clark, with my own eighteen counted in first, as always. The rest is counted twice and comes to you whole, lean year or not. I'll take the Irrigation, and gladly: G13 and G14, right under the Wall, which, I'll remind everyone, was my idea eleven years before anyone here would pay for a stone of it. I'm writing 'settled' beside Year 13 in my own hand. I'll take the five open squares as well, D15, D16, E16, F15 and F16, on the crown's twenty, since the drawer can spare it and Province 2 has gone hungry long enough. After this there's one line left, Clark. The Pagoda, next year. Don't let it slip."
aside: "Water on my own fields while I'm still the one sitting in this chair, and the crown pays for every grain and every square. I've waited thirteen years for this. G13 and G14 sit out of reach of Rob's lads, because I'm not giving him a third thing of mine to admire from his sand."
