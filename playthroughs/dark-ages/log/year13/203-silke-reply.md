move: land F29, F30, G30, O29, O30, P27, P28, P29, Q26, Q27; I pay 0, asking the crown for 200
say: Ten squares, Clark, and this year I'll take your twenty a square at its word: two hundred from the drawer, nothing from me. That still leaves the crown about seven hundred and seventy, and the Pagoda costs twenty, so nobody can say the coast stood between Heaven and its last stone. And Rich, five is five, and yes, I counted it. Thank you for not asking more in a lean year. Write that down, it's the first time I've said it.
aside: For two years I asked the crown for less because its drawer needed the grain for stone. Now there's twenty of work left and nearly a thousand in the drawer, so every square the crown pays for is grain that stays in mine, and grain in hand is how the coast keeps score at the end.
