move: land S12, T12, U12, V12, U23; I pay 90, asking the crown for 10
say: There's the canal in, Clark, and the rate down besides. That's two figures kept in one year, and I'll say so out loud. Five squares from me again: four across the top and one tucked in by Rob's Jar. I'll pay ninety and the crown pays its two a square, so there's plenty left in the drawer for Reina's Irrigation and the Pagoda.
aside: The crown kept its word, so I'll keep mine and pay my own way. The less I take out of that purse, the surer I am the last two works go up on time.
