## At the table since your last turn

Reina: "Three hundred and twenty-four, counted twice: Silke one fifty-five, Paddy one nineteen, Rich thirty-five, and my own fifteen first, as always. Clark, I'll strike the Province 1 canal off when the water's in it. That leaves the Irrigation in Province 2 next year, and I've already written Year 13 beside it in my own hand. Two squares for me, the crown's twenty each. I'll ask modestly, the way the coast has."

Rich: "Twenty percent, a canal paid out of the crown's own drawer, and the stone going in on the very year it was promised. Clark, I flattered you when you were strong, and now I'm only telling the truth. I'll have E14 if Reina leaves it to me. And Rob, here's twenty for one of your lads. You must sell, and I must say it's the best five you've earned off me in years."

Silke: "The canal's going in on time, Clark, so I'll write the ink down as holding. And I said last year the coast would ask for less, so here's what that looks like: six squares, two a square from the crown and the other hundred and eight from me. That leaves the drawer what it needs for Reina's Irrigation and the Pagoda, with room to spare."

Rich: "E14's yours, Reina. Good neighbours make a good Wall, and I said so first. Rob, my lad stands on the Jar itself. Your lad on J9 can admire it from there, the way you promised to admire Reina's Bowl."

Public works: both sections of the Province 1 canal go in, the crown paying 80. Two works are still owed: the Province 2 Irrigation System and the Marble Pagoda, 50 in all.

New land: Reina E14, E15 (crown 40; E14 hers over Rich's on table order, and Rich has no other open square); Silke L30, M30, N29, N30, O27, O28 (Silke 108, crown 12); Paddy S12, T12, U12, V12, U23 (Paddy 90, crown 10). Rich buys a soldier from Rob for 20; it stands on I8, guarding his Jar. No auctions this year.

Rob: "Reina, I did promise to admire it, and I'll admire it every day now. It's coming home to the sand with me, to M16, next to the first one. Silke, Heaven's let go of my wrists, so that's five from the coast, the first you've ever paid me. Rich, a fine guard you've bought there. He's my old lad, so mind you feed him. W1 will take his five off your fields instead."

Rob marches: W1 to J10, raiding 5 from Rich; W2 to P21, raiding 5 from Silke; W3 to J16, carrying off Reina's Wooden Bowl to M16 in the desert.
