diary: I finally won something, Clark's Customs House for nine grain, after six lots that went to other people. It's sitting down at S24, and I've been watching Rob's soldier creep toward it ever since, one square at a time. I put three fields into the sea with the crown paying half, because the card told me plainly I'd go hungry otherwise, and I'd rather owe nobody than be proud and broke. But a bad harvest, two marches through my province and Reina's rent have left me with fourteen and a half grain, under the twenty I'd promised myself I'd keep back, and I won't pretend it's otherwise. Clark bought the last Jar and Bowl just to sit on them, so I'll have to get my Jar and Land Deed by asking people across the table now, and I'll do it honestly. Next year the new fields start paying. First I'll get the purse back up past twenty, then I'll go and talk to Clark and Silke, and I'll keep a close eye on that tent at R22.
