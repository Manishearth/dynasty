diary: Two years of counting, and here's the total: five fields of my own out by the sea, still no Customs House and no Bowl, and fifty grain in the purse. I lost the Customs House to Paddy by one grain. Then Clark bought my own Jar and Paddy's Bowl, pieces his set doesn't even need, just so the rest of us can't have them, and three quarters of what he paid went into the Pile. I offered Reina an honest ten for one honest promise and she kept the promise and took the five. Fair enough, but now I know her word is good for exactly one year. This year I paid twenty-four of the crown's thirty-three, the biggest share at the table, and Clark wrote it down in his book as if that pays me back for it. Rich kept his word on the fields, I'll give him that, but his reserve is down to nineteen and a half with a Pagoda and a Winter Palace to show for it, so the canal he promised the coast will be paid for out of my harvests or it won't be dug at all. Next year the fields come in at a hundred and more, and I mean to get a Bowl off Clark, one way or another, before Rob's soldier on P21 decides my coast is the next stop on his walk.
