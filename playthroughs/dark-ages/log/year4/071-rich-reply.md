diary: Two more years without a crown, and my granary has grown from ninety-seven and a half to a hundred and ninety-nine and a half. No tax, no salary to pay anyone, and the rent kept at the customary five, which I promised and which I kept, though Silke says it through her teeth. Rob's lads finally came to Province 3 and ate ten grain in a single night. It cost me little, but it tells me the desert has noticed where the grain is, and that a granary with no swords beside it is only a larder with the door left open. Paddy turned down my twenty-five for his Jar, and he was right to, since that Jar is all that keeps me out of his seat. I'll remember he guards it, and I'll remember that he'll sell his name to whoever can keep Rob off his coast. Clark has finally put a canal on paper, and that ledger of his has more friends in it than I'd like. Next year I keep my claim and keep the rent at five, and I start thinking about which of Rob's swords could be bought, or a few of my own, because whoever finally wears the crown will have to come through my granary to get there.
