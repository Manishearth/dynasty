diary: Two years of mercy, and the reserve has gone from a hundred and ninety-two to nineteen and a half. I don't regret it, but I feel it in my stomach. I've built the Winter Palace and the Marble Pagoda, put two fields into the sea and paid half of every coastal field Silke and Paddy wanted, so the coast is grateful and nobody can call me a miser. Clark got his thirty both years, to the letter, and he spends it on Jars and Bowls he doesn't even need. That used to look like folly. Now it looks to me like a man shopping for allies. Rob has taken Reina's Customs House, which takes her down a peg, but his soldier is one square from Clark's Jar, and I'd sleep better if he'd hurry up and take it. Next year I need a good harvest and fat taxes from Silke's fields. I'm not raising the rate, though, not yet. A thirty-percent Emperor with an empty purse can still sit on his throne, but a fifty-percent Emperor with enemies can't. If the population card comes up x4 before I've bought more land, I'm in real trouble, so the first spare grain goes into the sea.
