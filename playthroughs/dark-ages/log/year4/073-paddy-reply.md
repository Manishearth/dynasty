diary: Two fat years with no tax, and I've more grain than I've ever had, a hundred and forty and more. You'd think I'd be pleased. Then Rob's lad walked into S24 and carried my Deed off to the desert, and there was nobody on the throne to complain to, so my set's broken again and his soldier is standing right next to my House and my Jar. Rich offered me twenty-five for the Jar, very politely, but that Jar's the only thing keeping his set from my seat, so I told him no. Clark has finally put the first canal for Province 6 into the record, and I'll grant him that it's more than anyone else has given me, but he's still only on paper and I've watched him count before. I'm keeping my name back for now. No chair means no new land and no canal, and one bad harvest with Rob's swords about would cost us more than any tax did, so before long somebody will have to be crowned. When it comes to it I'll back whoever will actually build, and in the meantime I need to find myself another Deed.
