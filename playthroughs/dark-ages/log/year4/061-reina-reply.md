diary: Years 3 and 4 I kept the Pile honest to the grain and asked the coast for nothing past the floor, and in return I lost everything I'd built. I went to twenty-eight for the Jar and fourteen for the Bowl, and Clark took both by one grain apiece. He didn't want them, he only wanted me not to have them. Then Rob's soldier walked my Customs House out into the desert, and I'm thirty-seven grain and no pottery, starting from nothing. Silke offered me five grain to promise away the next Customs House and I turned her down. It was the right call, but Clark wrote it in his book as a broken word, and that will cost me with the coast. The thing to watch now is that Rich has spent his reserve down to nineteen and a half on palaces, pagodas and Clark's thirty a year. Clark has the full set and ninety-nine grain, so the crown is hollow and the man paid to guard it is the one best placed to take it. I can't take it myself this year. What I can do is get my Customs House back from Rob or buy a new one, make it plain to Silke and Paddy that I'm the landlord who never squeezed them, and be standing by with the coast when Clark makes his move, so that whatever's left afterwards comes to me.
