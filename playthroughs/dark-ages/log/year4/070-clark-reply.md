diary: Years 3 and 4. Rob took my Deed from J14 to M15 in a year Heaven called careless, then took Paddy's to T24 in a year it called average. That's two pages under his name now, and I've written both down with the squares. The set is broken, but under Chaos a set can't reach the chair anyway, and with no tax and no salary for two years I've gone from 22.5 to 115.5, which no Emperor would have let me do. I finally put the canals in writing in front of the whole table: Province 6 in the first year I sit, Province 5 the year after, and half the rate on any flooded field. Paddy heard it, and the next day he turned Rich down over the Jar. Reina wants walls over the north before she'll lend her name, Silke wants her terms on paper before the harvests turn, and Rich is still counting grain as if grain had ever made anyone a friend. Rob's lads ate ten out of the Gentry's own fields this year, and I enjoyed writing that down more than I should have. I'll keep Silke and keep my word to Paddy, and I'll wait until the table is tired of feeding swords.
