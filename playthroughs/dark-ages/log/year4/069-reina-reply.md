diary: Two more years with nobody in my chair. My sealed treasury has gone on paying for disasters, and for the charity Heaven sends, to the same five who put me out of it. The first year was fat and the second was average, and Chaos felt cheap to all of them. It won't feel cheap much longer: Rob has broken two sets and eaten out of Rich's granary, and Rich's honeyed "customary five" doesn't fool even Silke. Clark finally put his canals on paper, Province 6 first, then Province 5, with half rate on flooded fields, and I'll admit it was well done. But I didn't hear a word about the Wall over the north or the Pagoda, and I won't give my name away for half of what this land is owed. Rich will never have it. Rob can have it the day his swords build something. The day Clark writes the walls in next to the canals, he may find the chair a good deal closer than he thinks.
