diary: Years 3-4. I passed on every lot, sat back, and watched Clark spend forty-four grain on a Jar and a Bowl he didn't even need, just to stop Reina finishing her set. Then I strolled onto M14 and took her Customs House anyway, so now it's sitting outside my tent on N14 and her set's broken for a good long while. The bad harvest cost me twenty into the Pile, but the walk paid for itself: five here, five there, and I'm still up to forty-two. Rich is the one I'm smiling at now. He's down to nineteen and a half in the reserve after the palaces, the pagoda and all those half-price fields, and he still owes Clark thirty a year. Clark has ninety-nine grain and a full set, and he writes down every step I take, which tells me exactly who's measuring the chair. W1 is one step from his Jar, W2 is two from Paddy's Customs House, and W3 is going for the Bowl. When Rich's purse runs dry, someone will push, and I'll be standing right beside them holding a piece of everybody's set, ready to offer a little help.
