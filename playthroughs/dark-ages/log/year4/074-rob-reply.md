diary: Two more years without a crown and I've never had it so good. Heaven sent a fat harvest and then the sealed treasury paid me two grain for my trouble, and I spent twenty of it on a third lad and got the rest back off Rich's fields in a single night. Clark's Deed is sitting at M15 and Paddy's is sitting at T24, so that's two sets broken, and there's nobody in the chair for them to complain to, only Clark's ledger, and he can write my name in it as often as he likes. Paddy's been watching W2 in his field with a face like thunder, and Rich has started whispering to him about keeping my lads out, which tells me the swords are finally being taken seriously. The count's still stuck three ways: Rich has the grain, Clark has Silke and now a canal on paper, and I've got the only soldiers on the board. Rich and Silke are the two pieces I'm missing, so W1 and W3 are already standing in Province 3, and Silke's coast opens up again next year. The longer they argue over who sits, the longer I get to keep collecting.
