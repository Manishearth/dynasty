say: "Here's your other five, Reina. Ten's what you asked for and ten's fair, and two-fifty off the rice is more than I ever thought I'd see from that province. You've kept the Pile honest, and I'd rather pay my landlord than owe her. Rob, I'm sorry about the sandstorm, truly. The big purse is still coming round to your tent at the end of the year, and maybe it'll sound better to you now."

aside: "Five grain is nothing against three hundred and fifty, and paying it keeps Reina friendly, which I'll need if I'm ever going to climb. Rob's down to seventeen and a half grain, so the Customs House on T23 may never be cheaper than it is this year, and I'll be watching to see if he's hungry enough to sell."
