move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: The pile pays out as the card says: Clark fifteen, Silke three hundred and thirty-five, Paddy two hundred and ninety-five, and Reina, Rob and I each put our little bit back in. Rent is the customary five from each of you, the same as it's been every year, fat or thin. And Silke, that's handsomely said about your half going to the Wall. My quarter is counted and waiting, ten a link, and Reina, I'll be very glad to hear you read that line out when we come to the works.
aside: Five is a small price for nine years of people saying the Gentry keeps his word. With Silke throwing her canal money at the Wall too, this is the year my four links finally get laid, and once they're up my strip opens and I have land to farm at last.
whisper: none
