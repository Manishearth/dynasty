move: collect as declared (Silke 167.5, Paddy 147.5, 315 in all, delivered whole to the crown); Wall share: 20, the full 10 a link on Province 2's two links
say: "Three hundred and fifteen, counted twice: Silke one sixty-seven and a half, Paddy one forty-seven and a half, and not a grain lost on the road. Clark, here's Province 2's twenty for its own two links. I've waited nine years to pay for stone over my own roof, so I'm not going to haggle it down now. And yes, Rich, I'll read your forty out loud when the mortar's wet and not a moment before. You'll hear me read Year 10 beside my last two links as well."
aside: "Fifty percent on a fat year is exactly what I asked for in Year 2, and they burned my chair for it. Now the same table pays it, and I'm putting my own grain under the north. Twenty out of fifty-two hurts, but this is the first year since I lost the throne that the money turns into a wall and not into a promise."
whisper: none

Ruling: collected 315 (Silke 167.5, Paddy 147.5). Province 2 pays 20 toward its two links. Aside's "a fat year": this is an average year (the canals and the land make it fat for the coast); not corrected, a manner of speaking.
