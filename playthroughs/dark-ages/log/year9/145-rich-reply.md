move: land G9, G10, G11; I pay 45, asking the crown for 15
say: "Three squares under the new stone, Your Majesty: G9, G10 and G11. Forty-five from the Gentry and the crown's five a square, which is fifteen, and I'll take it gratefully. Nine years I've paid into that pile and taken nothing out of the ground. From next year Province 3 finally puts something back."
aside: "Sixty grain of land that grows sixty a year, and I'm only paying forty-five of it, so it's paid for before the next drought arrives. I'm watching that lad of Rob's on L11, because new fields are just more grain for him to eat, and some of this granary is going on swords soon."
whisper: none
