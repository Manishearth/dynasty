move: land T15, U15, V15, V19, V20; I pay 100, asking the crown for 0 (if the rules want the crown to put in some part, put it down as 1 grain owed, and I'll take that next spring)

say: "Five squares up the top of the coast, a hundred grain, and I'll pay the lot myself. Your Majesty, you've ten in the purse and Reina's ten still to pay, and I'll not be the man who leaves the Official unpaid in the year the Wall went up. Last year you paid me back fifty the spring you said you would, and that's worth more to me than five a square. Keep your purse for the stone. Two hundred and ninety still owed is two hundred and ninety, and I'd rather see it standing than see it in my granary."

aside: "I'm buying on the north edge, away from Rob's lad on S23, and I'm buying more land rather than pieces because soil can't be carried off to the desert. I'm letting the crown off the share on purpose, too: an Emperor who can pay his Official this year will still be on the throne to finish the Wall next year."
