## Your move: salaries, and the crown's appointments

It's the end of the year, and the salaries are now the Emperor's to pay. Reina appointed Graham to bank the Harvest Pile, Silke to collect the tax, and you to read the I Ching at 5 a year. The jobs stay with the players until the Emperor changes them, and an appointee is paid only what the Emperor chooses (Note 3).

Decide:
- **appointments:** keep them as they are, or name someone else for any of the three jobs (the I Ching reader keeps the population counter and reads the card each year).
- **salaries this year:** what the crown pays, and to whom (none is owed).

The crown's reserve is 118.
