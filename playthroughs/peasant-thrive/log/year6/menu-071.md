## Your move: your set is complete

You hold the Silk Peasant's whole set (a Customs House, a Land Deed Scroll, the Vase, a Bamboo Brush and the Wooden Bowl) and 90.5 grain, more than the 20 a set needs. With no upper class at this table, the Silk Peasant may now (Section 11):

- **take the throne**, replacing Reina as Emperor. You keep your grain and take the whole crown's reserve (27.5), and you return every piece you hold to the stock (each to its native province: the Customs House to 5, the Brush and the Bowl to 6, the Deed to 7, the Vase to 7). You'd become the Emperor of Province 1, with its five squares of new land and its canal section, and the 220 of works still owed. Reina would take the Silk Peasant's seat in Province 5, return what she holds (nothing), and draw 60 from the pile.
- **replace another peasant**, Graham (Rice) or Silke (Wheat): you take their grain with yours, and they take the Silk seat with 60 from the pile.
- **stay where you are** and keep the set on the board for now.

Say which.
