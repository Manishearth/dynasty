diary: I read out the card that doubled the people and told everyone to buy land before the next one, so I bought four squares myself. I'd have felt a fraud otherwise. Graham asked seventy for the Brush and a hundred and twenty for the Bowl, and I paid both without a word, because I'd said I would and because he'd let me have the Jar without a fight. A hundred and ninety grain for two pieces is the dearest thing I've ever bought, and most of it went into the pile, not to him. Silke spent everything she had on the Jar to keep Graham off it, and now she's the poorest of us and needs only the Bowl, which went straight back into Graham's stock the night I climbed. I could have taken Graham's grain or Silke's seat, but they'd both dealt fairly with me, so I took the throne instead, and Reina went down to the coast with good grace. Now I've a hungry province, 103 grain, 220 of works owed and three neighbours who all know exactly what I paid to get here. I mean to keep the tax honest, pay my people even when nobody owes it, and buy enough land before the x4 card comes. I'm still a bit surprised to be writing "Emperor" at the top of this page.
