Ruling: legal. 5:DJ ceiling 45; 6:WB ceiling 77 (keep from Paddy 77). Graham set the Bowl's minimum at 120 in his parallel reply, so her 77 is under it and counts as a pass. "A coastal one with five fresh fields": Graham's Province 6 has five squares of new land (three in Year 5, two this year); Paddy's seat is already coastal. A reading of who Paddy might displace, not a misstatement.
