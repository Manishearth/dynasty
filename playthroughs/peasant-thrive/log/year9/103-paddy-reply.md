Ruling: tax 30% (193.5: Reina 46.5, Silke 82.5, Graham 64.5), collected by Silke; the crown builds the Marble Pagoda alone for 20, and it is the last work owed, so the game ends in survival in the public-works step, before any land is bought and before the auctions (§18). The land offer stands in the record but no square is bought. "The last stone Reina's canal was waiting on" is colour (the canal never needed the Pagoda); not corrected, since the game ends here.
