diary: Year 9. I kept the tax at thirty, the same as I'd promised, and I put up the little Marble Pagoda between the two palaces for twenty grain, and just like that it was over. The dynasty survived, and I'm still the one wearing the crown. Twenty grain was the cheapest thing I bought all game, and it mattered more than anything else I bought. I know what it cost the others. Reina was one Bowl from the throne, and Silke was sitting on more grain than the rest of us put together, and now neither of them gets their turn. I can't pretend I didn't see that. But I said all along the crown's grain was for finishing the reign, and I'd rather end it honestly than hang on to make it last. I started this game as a Rice Peasant counting every grain, and I'm finishing it as Emperor with four hundred and fifty-four in the reserve and nobody owed a thing. That last part is what I'm proudest of. I still can't quite believe it.
