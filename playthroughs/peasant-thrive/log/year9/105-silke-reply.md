diary: Year 9. Paddy put up the Pagoda before a single square was bought, and that was the end of it: no auction, no Bowl to sell, and I was still holding it. I finish with five hundred and seventy grain, the fattest jar anyone has filled at this table all evening, and by the only measure that counts I lost, because I'm sitting in the Rice seat and not the Silk one I drew. I knew a full purse buys nothing once the game is over, I wrote that down myself, and I went on filling mine anyway, while Paddy spent his reign building the thing that ended it. He was honest the whole way through and he's the one on the throne, so I'll grant him that, and I'll grant myself that I never got robbed after Year 3 and that I paid Graham back his single grain. Next time I sit down I'll keep a quieter eye on the last stone of the public works, because it turns out the Pagoda was the knife this year and not anybody's set.
