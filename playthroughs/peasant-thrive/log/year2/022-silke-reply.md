diary: Two years in, and I have the Brush and the last Land Deed for fourteen grain between them. The only fight was the first Deed, which Paddy took off me by a single grain, and then he kept his word to stay out of the second, so I'll give him that. Reina is playing the gentle Empress: thirty percent, waterworks for Graham, canals promised to me. She's sitting on a hundred and twenty grain, though, and a gentle crown can still be a fat one. The problem now is that everyone is short exactly two pieces, and each of us holds the last copy of something the other two need. Paddy holds my Bowl, Graham holds my Vase, and my Dragon Jar is the one thing both of them want. Next year that Jar is the most valuable thing on the board, and I mean to sell it last, slowly, and to whoever pays me with the piece I'm missing. Keep seventy grain in reserve, keep being the reasonable one, and let the men drive the price up for each other.
