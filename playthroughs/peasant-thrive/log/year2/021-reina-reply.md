diary: Two years in and the house is quiet, which is how I want it. I gave each of them a job so each has a reason to keep the peace, I taxed thirty both years, and I put the Reservoir first because it feeds Graham and so feeds the treasury. The Winter Palace came second; it's a cheap gate, and the canals for Silke and Paddy go through it. That tribute horse cost me four, and Paddy's salary costs five a year, and I mind neither. The reserve stands at a hundred and twenty, which is enough cushion for when the population doubles and land starts costing twenty a square. Still 440 owed and I mean to see every bit of it built. Canals next, a section a year, and I'll watch the realia: they've been gentle with each other over the duplicates, but the Jar, the Bowl and the Vase are one of a kind, and that's where the table's good manners will be tested.
