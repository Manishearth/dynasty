diary: Year one cost me fourteen grain in losing bids and bought me nothing. Paddy offered me the Customs House cheap and then outbid me by a grain, and Silke did the same with the Brush. Year two put it right: the Customs House and the Brush came to me at four each, and I'm writing down that Paddy kept his word the second time, as well as that he didn't the first. The Reservoir is a fine thing, twenty a year that I never had to bid for, and it's the reason I'm sitting on 91 and a half. Now every one of us holds the only copy of a piece the other two need. Silke has the Jar, Paddy has the Bowl and I have the Vase, so next year's auctions are where the set gets decided. I'll pay what it takes for the Jar and the Bowl, I won't sell the Vase cheap, and I'll keep my grain dry until then.
