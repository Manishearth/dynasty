## At the table since your last turn

Tax 30%: Reina 54, Graham 66, Silke 54 to the crown, collected by Silke. Paddy raises all four links of the Great Wall above Province 1 (160, the crown's); only the Marble Pagoda (20) is still owed, built in a year when nothing else is. The crown offers 10 of every square the provinces buy this year and buys none for Province 1.

Reina: "No land for me this year, Paddy, kind as the offer is. And congratulations on the Wall, that's a promise I made in Year 1 that you've kept for me. Silke, Graham: a Deed and a Bowl are all I lack, and I'm holding my grain for them. If either of you puts one up, you'll find me a serious buyer and a grateful one."

Reina buys no land this year.

Graham: "Four more squares on your half, Paddy, the same as last year. Ten grain a square that comes back as twenty at the next harvest is the cheapest sum I'll do all year. Reina, I heard you. The Bowl's still in my stock and I haven't forgotten whose it would finish."

Graham buys four squares of sea for Province 6, O27, O28, P28 and S27, paying 40, the crown paying 40.

Silke: "Three more squares of desert on your half, Paddy, and thank you. It's ten a square and it comes back twice that, and even I can't argue with sums like that. Reina, I heard you as well. My Deed's still in my stock, and I know who else at this table holds a piece somebody's waiting on."

Silke buys three squares of desert for Province 7, T12, T13 and T21, paying 30, the crown paying 30.

The auctions, the last two pieces in stock: Graham's Wooden Bowl (Province 6), which Reina and Silke both need, and Silke's Land Deed Scroll (Province 7), which Reina needs. Each is its seller's last piece, so both go up this year. The Emperor doesn't bid.

Reina: "Here we are: two pieces, and two of us who want the same Bowl. Silke, you've more grain than I do, and we both know it. If you outbid me, you'll have earned it, and I'd ask only that you keep Paddy's thirty percent and his half on the land. But if the Bowl comes to me, I'm asking you to price your Deed for the woman who built your Reservoir."

Graham: "The Bowl opens at sixty. It's the last one in the game and two of you need it, and sixty is a good deal less than Paddy paid me for it. After that it goes to whoever wants it most, and I've no favourites either, Silke. The Deed's none of my business. I've got one already."

Graham sets the Wooden Bowl's minimum at 60.

Silke: "Reina, you built my Reservoir and I haven't forgotten it, but this Bowl is the one piece I've been waiting on since Year Three, and I'm going to buy it. The Deed opens at twenty, which is the fair price for it and not a grain more, whoever ends up holding the Bowl."

The auctions settle. Graham's Wooden Bowl: Reina stops at 110 and Silke takes it at 111. Silke's Land Deed Scroll: Graham bids to 90 and Reina takes it at 91. Graham keeps a quarter of 111 (27.5), Silke a quarter of 91 (22.5); the rest goes to the Harvest Pile. Grain: Reina 52.5, Silke 103, Paddy 166.5, Graham 267.5.

Silke: "S15, right next to the Jar. A hundred and eleven grain, Graham, and I paid it to you without haggling, which I'd like you to write down. Reina, you fought fairly, and you've the Deed to show for it."

Reina: "O22, next to the Brush and the Vase. Graham, ninety for a Deed you told us was none of your business. I've noted that, the same way you note everything. And Silke, well bought. You've a Bowl now and I'm the province with the canals, so I'll be watching which coast you fancy."

(Storyteller's note: Graham kept a quarter of the 111 for the Bowl (27.5); the other 83.5 went to the Harvest Pile.)

Silke puts the Wooden Bowl on S15 and Reina the Land Deed Scroll on O22. Silke holds the Wheat Peasant's whole set (a Customs House, a Bamboo Brush, the Dragon Jar and the Wooden Bowl) and 103 grain, so she may replace another peasant: Reina (Silk) or Graham (Rice).

Silke: "Graham, you took my seat in Year Three over a single grain, and you wrote down your reason. I've written mine down too: one Brush, one Jar, and a hundred and eleven for your Bowl. I'll take the Rice seat, thank you, and the Wheat fields are yours. They're good desert, and I bought most of it myself."

Silke replaces Graham as the Rice Peasant of Province 6 and takes his 267.5 grain with her own. The Wooden Bowl goes back into Province 6's stock, for Silke to auction; her Customs House, Bamboo Brush and Dragon Jar go on R23, R22 and R24. Graham returns his Land Deed Scroll and Customs House, draws 60 and becomes the Wheat Peasant of Province 7. Paddy pays Reina, Silke and Graham 5 each for their jobs.

End of Year 8. Seats: Paddy Emperor, Reina Silk (Province 5), Silke Rice (Province 6), Graham Wheat (Province 7); the jobs are unchanged. Grain: Reina 57.5, Silke 375.5, Paddy 151.5, Graham 65. Works: the Grand Canal and the Great Wall are finished; only the Marble Pagoda (20) is still owed, and the game ends the moment it stands. Reina holds a Bamboo Brush, the Vase and a Land Deed Scroll and needs the Wooden Bowl; Silke holds a Customs House, a Bamboo Brush and the Dragon Jar and needs a Land Deed Scroll and the Vase; Graham holds nothing and needs a Customs House, a Bamboo Brush, the Dragon Jar and the Wooden Bowl. In stock: a Customs House (Province 5), the Wooden Bowl (Province 6), a Land Deed Scroll (Province 7).
