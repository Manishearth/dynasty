diary: Two years of buying pieces, and I'm one Bowl short of the throne again. I paid forty for the Brush, forty-one for the Vase and ninety-one for a Deed Graham bid up out of spite, and the Bowl I needed went to Silke at a hundred and eleven. Then the Bowl went back into stock when she took Graham's seat, so the last piece I need is now Silke's to sell, and she has three hundred and seventy-five grain and no reason to hurry. That's what it cost me. What I have is the Vase, which Silke needs, so for once I'm holding something the richest player at the table wants. Paddy has built the Wall I promised in Year 1, and only the Pagoda is left, so the game could end the year it goes up. Whatever deal I make with Silke has to be made before then. I promised to see the Wall finished with every one of them still at the table. It's finished, they're all still here, and I'd like one more year with the crown back on my head before it ends.
