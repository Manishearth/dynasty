## Your move: your set is complete

You hold the Wheat Peasant's whole set (a Customs House, a Bamboo Brush, the Dragon Jar and the Wooden Bowl) and 103 grain, more than the 20 a set needs. A peasant with a set may now replace another peasant (Section 11), never the Emperor:

- **replace Reina as the Silk Peasant** (Province 5, the east coast: two canal sections and seven squares of new land). You take her 52.5 grain with your own, 155.5 in all. The Dragon Jar is native to Province 5, so it goes back into Province 5's stock, for you to auction; your Customs House, Brush and Bowl move to squares of Province 5. Reina returns her Brush (to Province 6's stock), Vase and Deed (to Province 7's), draws 60 from the pile and becomes the Wheat Peasant of Province 7. As the Silk Peasant you'd then need a Land Deed Scroll and the Vase (the Brush and the Bowl you already hold); with that set, the Silk Peasant is the one seat at this table that may take the throne.
- **replace Graham as the Rice Peasant** (Province 6, the south-east coast: two canal sections and thirteen squares of new land). You take his 267.5 grain with your own, 370.5 in all. The Wooden Bowl is native to Province 6, so it goes back into Province 6's stock, for you to auction; your Customs House, Brush and Jar move to squares of Province 6. Graham returns his Land Deed Scroll (to Province 7's stock) and Customs House (to Province 5's), draws 60 from the pile and becomes the Wheat Peasant of Province 7. As the Rice Peasant you'd then need a Land Deed Scroll and the Vase.
- **stay where you are** and keep the set on the board for now.

Graham took the Silk seat from you in Year 3; he has held the Rice seat, Province 6, since Year 4. Say which, and if you move, which square of your new province each piece goes on.
