diary: Year seven I bought the Customs House at twenty-five and four squares at ten each, and I stopped at forty for the Vase because I'd said forty, and Reina took it at forty-one. Year eight I had two hundred and forty grain and a Bowl that had to go up, and I counted Reina. I paid ninety for a Deed I didn't need so she couldn't finish, and I never once counted Silke, who had the Brush, the Customs House and the Jar on her own squares and needed one piece, and that piece was mine. I wrote last year that the Bowl would wait until I'd counted exactly what Silke would do with it. I didn't count it. She paid me a hundred and eleven for it and then took two hundred and sixty-seven and a half off me with it, and she told me why to my face, the way I told her in year three. I'll write that down fairly: she was owed it. I have sixty-five grain, her desert, which she bought well, and nothing in my hand. Only the Pagoda is left to build, and it's twenty grain, so the game could end next spring before I've set out a single piece. I've started counting again from nothing, the second time now. This time I'm going to count every one of them, not just the one I'm watching.
