diary: Years 7–8. I sold the Vase to Reina for forty-one and kept the Deed back because I couldn't yet see whose set it would finish. Then I bought desert on Paddy's half while it was ten grain a square, and let two good harvests fill my jar, because I was too poor for anyone to bother robbing. When Graham finally put the Bowl up, Reina had to keep enough back for my Deed, so I knew she'd stop somewhere short of me, and she stopped at a hundred and ten. I paid a hundred and eleven, which is one grain more, the same single grain he wrote down against me in Year One, and I hope he noticed. Then I took his seat and his 267 grain with it, exactly as he took mine, and gave him back the desert I'd bought. That's 375 grain now, the fattest jar at the table, and I know better than anyone what a full jar draws. Reina is one Bowl from a set and the Bowl is in my stock, Graham has nothing, and the Pagoda can end it all next year, so I'll sell that Bowl very carefully, or not at all, and keep being the reasonable one with Paddy.
