## Your decision: your set is complete

You hold the Wheat Peasant's whole set (Customs House, Bamboo Brush, Dragon Jar, Wooden Bowl) and 69.5 grain, so you may change seats now with another peasant (Section 11). You may not take the throne: at this table only the Silk Peasant may. You may also wait and climb in a later year, so long as you still hold the set and 20 grain.

What each choice does, by the rules:

- **Replace Silke as the Silk Peasant (Province 5).** You take her grain as well as your own: 190. The Dragon Jar is native to Province 5, so it goes back into that province's stock, which you then must auction as its seller. You keep the Customs House, the Brush and the Bowl, placed on squares of Province 5. The Silk Peasant's set is a Land Deed Scroll, a Bamboo Brush, a Wooden Bowl and a Vase, so you'd still need a Land Deed Scroll and the Vase. A Silk Peasant with a full set may take the throne. Silke returns her pieces and draws 60 from the Harvest Pile, and sits as the Wheat Peasant, with the Province 7 Reservoir.
- **Replace Paddy as the Rice Peasant (Province 6).** You take his grain as well as your own: 158. The Wooden Bowl is native to Province 6, so it goes back into that province's stock, which you then must auction as its seller. You keep the Customs House, the Brush and the Jar, placed on squares of Province 6. The Rice Peasant's set is a Customs House, a Land Deed Scroll, a Dragon Jar and a Vase, so you'd still need a Land Deed Scroll and the Vase. Paddy returns his pieces and draws 60 from the Harvest Pile, and sits as the Wheat Peasant, with the Province 7 Reservoir.
- **Stay the Wheat Peasant for now.**

Your table job (banker of the Harvest Pile) is the Emperor's appointment and goes with you, not with the seat.

Reply with `move: I replace Silke`, `move: I replace Paddy` or `move: I stay`. If you move, name a square for each piece you keep in your new province, in the same line (e.g. `move: I replace Paddy; CH Q19, BB Q20, DJ Q21`). Once the seat changes, every square of the new province is free: Province 5 is L24–L25, M24–M25, N20, N23–N25, O19–O26, P19–P26; Province 6 is Q19–Q25, R19–R24, S19–S25.
