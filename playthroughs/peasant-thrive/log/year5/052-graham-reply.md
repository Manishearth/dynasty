move: Buy all three squares (R25, R26, S26); I pay 45.5, the crown pays 14.5.
say: "All three, then. Forty-five and a half from me, fourteen and a half from the crown, and I've written down the rest of what was said. A field is worth more than a grain in the jar when the next card comes."
aside: "The crown promised me ten a square and paid me less than five, so Reina now owes me fifteen and a half, and I've written it down next to Paddy's name. I'll still take the fields, because four more squares is what stands between me and starving at x4, and I can't wait for anyone's promise to come good."
whisper: To Reina: Fifteen and a half, first out of next year's tax, before anything is built. I'll hold you to the exact figure.
---
Ruling: legal. Land: R25, R26 and S26, sea for Province 6; Graham pays 45.5, the crown 14.5. Grain 84 - 45.5 = 38.5. Aside: "four more squares" is his board card's figure for x4 before these three; with them he needs one more.
