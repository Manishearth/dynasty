## Your move: the crown's share of the provinces' new land

Three provinces asked to buy new land this year, and each asked the crown for a share:

- **Paddy**, Province 5: four squares (K25, K26, L26, M26). He pays 60 and asks the crown for 20 (5 a square).
- **Graham**, Province 6: three squares (R25, R26, S26). He pays 30 and asks the crown for 30 (10 a square).
- **Silke**, Province 7: three squares (T16, T17, T18). She pays 45 and asks the crown for 15 (5 a square).

That's 65 asked of the crown. **Your reserve is 54.5**, and Paddy's 5 for the I Ching is due at the end of the year. The crown's share is a voluntary payment, so it can't take the crown into debt.

The crown must pay some part of every square and may pay all of it. Say what the crown pays toward each province's squares. Any square the crown pays less on, that province pays the difference or doesn't buy, and the Storyteller will ask it.
