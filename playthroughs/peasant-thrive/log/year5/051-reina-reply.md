move: The crown pays 20 toward Paddy's four squares (5 each), 15 toward Silke's three (5 each) and 14.5 toward Graham's three (5, 5 and 4.5). That's 49.5 in all, which leaves exactly 5 for Paddy's I Ching salary. Graham pays the other 45.5 on his squares.

say: "Five a square, as I said in front of all of you, and I'll stand by it to the last grain, which is very nearly where it leaves me. Ten new fields, and that's ten more harvests the crown can tax next year. Graham, your third square is half a grain short of five; the crown will make that up and more once the coast starts growing."

aside: "I promised five a square in public and ten to Graham in private, and the crown's purse can only cover the promise everyone heard. Graham gets the bad news quietly from me, along with an IOU, because a sore Graham holding the Bowl is the most dangerous thing at this table."

whisper: To Graham: "I can't pay the ten this year: three of you asked at once and the crown is down to the salary. The fifteen and a half I owe you comes first out of next year's tax, before anything else is built. I'd rather be late in paying you than break my word to the whole table."
