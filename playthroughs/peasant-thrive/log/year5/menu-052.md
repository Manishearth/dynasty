## Your move: your squares, at the crown's share

The crown puts 14.5 toward your three squares (R25, R26, S26), not the 30 you asked: 5, 5 and 4.5. For all three you'd pay 45.5. Or buy fewer: on one or two squares the crown pays 5 each and you pay 15 each. Or none.

Your grain is 84, and the auctions come next.
