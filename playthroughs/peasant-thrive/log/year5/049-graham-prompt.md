## Years 3–4

In year four I bought her Deed at sixty, which was a fair price, and sold Paddy the Jar at sixty, which was the mistake. I knew what a finished set would let him do. I said it to the camera before the auction and then I sold it to him anyway, because sixty looked like a good number and fifteen of it was mine. Fifteen grain. He took 183 and a half off me with it, and the Deed, and the Brush, and the Bowl, and the seat that leads to the throne. He said he wouldn't forget that I didn't fight him. I'll be precise about this: he shook my hand in year three and sat in my chair in year four. I have 65 grain, two canal sections in Province 6, and nothing on the board. Province 6 sells the Brush and the Bowl, and those are the two pieces Paddy needs for the throne. He'll pay what I ask for them, or he'll wait, and every year he waits the rest of us get closer. I've started counting again from nothing, and this time I'm counting Paddy as well.
