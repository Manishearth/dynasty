## Your decision: your set is complete

You hold the Rice Peasant's whole set (Customs House, Land Deed Scroll, Vase, Dragon Jar) and 58.5 grain, so you may change seats now with another peasant (Section 11). You may not take the throne: at this table only the Silk Peasant may. You may also wait and climb in a later year, so long as you still hold the set and 20 grain.

What each choice does, by the rules:

- **Replace Graham as the Silk Peasant (Province 5).** You take his grain as well as your own: 242. The Dragon Jar is native to Province 5, so it goes back into that province's stock, which you then must auction as its seller. You keep the Customs House, the Deed and the Vase, placed on squares of Province 5. The Silk Peasant's set is a Land Deed Scroll, a Bamboo Brush, a Wooden Bowl and a Vase, so you'd still need a Bamboo Brush and the Wooden Bowl. A Silk Peasant with a full set may take the throne. Graham returns his pieces (his Customs House to Province 5's stock, his Brush and the Wooden Bowl to Province 6's, his Deed to Province 7's), draws 60 from the Harvest Pile, and sits as the Rice Peasant, with Province 6's two canal sections.
- **Replace Silke as the Wheat Peasant (Province 7).** You take her grain as well as your own: 170. The Vase is native to Province 7, so it goes back into that province's stock, which you then must auction as its seller. You keep the Customs House, the Deed and the Jar, placed on squares of Province 7. The Wheat Peasant's set is a Customs House, a Bamboo Brush, a Dragon Jar and a Wooden Bowl, so you'd still need a Bamboo Brush and the Wooden Bowl. Silke returns her Brush (to Province 6's stock), draws 60 from the Harvest Pile, and sits as the Rice Peasant, with Province 6's two canal sections.
- **Stay the Rice Peasant for now.**

Your table job (reading the I Ching) is the Emperor's appointment and goes with you, not with the seat.

Reply with `move: I replace Graham`, `move: I replace Silke` or `move: I stay`. If you move, name a square for each piece you keep in your new province, in the same line (e.g. `move: I replace Silke; CH Q13, LD Q14, DJ Q15`). Once the seat changes, these squares are free: in Province 5, L24–L25, M24–M25, N20, N23–N25, O19–O26, P19–P26; in Province 7, O14–O15, P13–P15, P18, Q13–Q18, R13–R18, S13–S18.
