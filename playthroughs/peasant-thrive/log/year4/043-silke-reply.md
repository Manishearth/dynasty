diary: Years 3–4. I set out to sell the Jar slowly and let the men push the price up for each other, and they did, only Graham was the one who won all of it. He took the Jar and my Bowl in the same round, and then he took my seat. Two years of careful buying were gone in one evening, and he said it was for a single grain in Year One, which he had written down. So I'm the Wheat Peasant now, and next year Paddy took Graham's seat in exactly the same way, which taught me something: at this table a finished set is a knife, and whoever holds one uses it on the richest peasant nearby. My Deed went to Graham for sixty, and fifteen of that came back to me. The Brush cost me two grain. I have 111.5 grain now, the Reservoir, one Brush, and I'm the only one of us who hasn't been robbed yet this year. Paddy has 242 and needs only a Brush and the Bowl to take the throne. Graham will be auctioning the Bowl and a Brush, and I already have a Brush, so whatever happens I must not let Paddy have both. Reina should be frightened, and next year I mean to make her pay me to stand in Paddy's way.
