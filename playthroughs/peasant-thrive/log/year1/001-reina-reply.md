move: banker Graham; collector Silke; reader Paddy (pay: 5 a year)
say: Right, one job each, so nobody can say the crown has favourites. Graham, you hold the Harvest Pile and pay it out fairly. Silke, you bring the tax in honestly. Paddy, you read the I Ching, and the crown pays you five a year so the cards stay kind to all of us. Ten years is a long time, friends, and I mean to see the Wall finished with every one of you still at the table.
aside: Giving each peasant a job means each has a stake in the order holding, and the five a year to Paddy is cheap insurance on the man who keeps the population counter. Five out of sixty hurts a little now, but a revolt costs far more than that.
whisper: none
