# Scenario: Peasant thrive

For the Director. Read `playthrough-sim/agents/DIRECTOR.md` first: it says how to start a game, how far steering may go, and how the parked Storytellers work.

**The table:** 4 players, the same cast as the two earlier peasant tables: Reina Osorio (Emperor, 1), Silke Weber (Silk Peasant, 5), Paddy Doyle (Rice Peasant, 6), Graham Garner (Wheat Peasant, 7). No Warlord. Played in two-year chapters until the works are built.

**Working title:** "Peasant thrive", subtitle "Four players · in progress". Write the real subtitle once the game ends (players · years · how it ended), in `game.py`'s `setup()`.

**Target arc: a peasant table that ends in survival.** Every work owed (520) gets built and the game ends by §18, with no collapse. The two earlier tables lost to the deck, not the play: both drew seed 20261027, where land opens only at ×3 and the third population card makes it ×6 in Year 11 (`peasant-collapse/REPORT.md`, `peasant-draw/REPORT.md`). This game's deck was chosen with Manish (2026-10-28) to give success room, so the result shows what the rules do on a fair deck. Nobody at the table is told how to win.

**What it tests.**
- **Whether a fair deck is enough.** The works, the climbs and the land, all at one table, without the Director rewinding years.
- **Land in proportion.** Land opens at ×2 in Year 5, while incomes are still near break-even. The table needs some land, roughly a dozen squares before the second doubling, but not every grain of it. Manish has seen tables buy land "willy nilly" and it feels silly, so the notes and tips present land as one investment among several (the works' own bonuses, the climb), never as the only one.
- **The works as the clock.** The game ends when the works are built, which the crown can do while harvests are good.

## The deck

**Seed 8535**, chosen by Manish from a shortlist of three (8535, 17152, 19860). See `seed.md`.

## Levers

- **Actor's notes:** each character a stance in which land is one good use of grain among several.
- **Storyteller's tips:** Reina's Year 1 tip on the works owed; one tip to each player the year land opens (Year 5), on the rules of land, including that a square is bought alongside the year's project, not instead of it.
- **No rewinds planned.** If the game goes somewhere else, let it and record it.
- **A stop, only if needed:** if the works are far from done before the second doubling, the Director may offer the table an agreed stop (RULINGS, authored stopping points). Never by naming the deck.
