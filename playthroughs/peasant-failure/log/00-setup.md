# Test 1: one round, two players

This is a format test of the reply fields `move`, `say`, `aside` and `whispers`, with the aside phrased as talking the audience through the move. The position is hand-made, not from an engine run. The Storyteller (the coordinating session) wrote the move menus by hand from the rulebook: mercenaries cost 20 and the Warlord keeps 5; a bought soldier can't move until its province is raided; one raider can't take a guarded piece; the Warlord may hire one soldier a round for 20, up to his army + 1.

The table: the peasant game, 5 players, Year 4. Only Rob (Warlord) and Silke (Silk Peasant, Province 5) are played by agents. The order: Rob moves first, then Silke answers, having heard his table talk and any whisper.
