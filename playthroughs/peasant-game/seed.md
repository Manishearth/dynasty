# Chance and steering in the peasant's game

The Director nudges this game towards the arc in `scenario.md`. Any steering is the Director's, made before the game or between chapters through a handoff, and is listed below when it happens. A Storyteller never steers on its own.

- **Seed:** `20261027`, Peasant collapse's own deck, recommended in `scenario.md` and confirmed by the Director before Chapter 1.
- **How the deck is shuffled:** the twenty card keys from `cards.py`, sorted alphabetically, then `random.Random(20261027).shuffle(keys)` (Python 3).
- **Deck order, top first** (Year 1 draws no card, so the first of these is Year 2's):

  1. canal · 2. half2 · 3. dribble2 · 4. half1 · 5. double1 · 6. dribble3 · 7. share · 8. dribble1 · 9. heaven · 10. double3 · 11. goodevil · 12. blight · 13. careless · 14. utility · 15. rainflood · 16. repairs · 17. double2 · 18. winds · 19. breach · 20. chest

  The players are never shown this list, and the narrator never hints at it. Population cards past their caps are still drawn in order; the engine treats a capped one as a good harvest.

- **Booklets:** not drawn by chance. Each player sits in the province their name puns on: Reina 1, Silke 5, Paddy 6, Graham 7.

## Steering log

- **Before Chapter 1, the deck:** seed 20261027 stands, so the rematch draws the same cards as Peasant collapse. `deck_scout.py 20261027 --seats 1,5,6,7 --years 10`: the Canal card in Year 2 (Wheat plays a disaster), ×1.5 in Year 3, a bad harvest in Year 4 (every bare income negative), ×3 in Year 6, and bad harvests in Years 7, 9 and 10 (Silk escapes Year 10's, on Heaven Has Favourites). Land opens in Year 6, and from then every bare income is negative under every card, so a province without land loses grain every year. Past Year 10 the deck gets worse: Year 11 draws the third doubling (×6, every bare income −140 or below), so a game still open then is unlikely to survive. Kept anyway: this is a design check, and a fairer deck (`--where "land<=4 and lean_early<=1"`, which thousands of seeds pass) would only show the rules surviving an easier shuffle.
- **Before Chapter 1, the actor's notes:** the **Playing it** line in each `ledger.md` entry, a stance from `scenario.md`'s suggestions: Reina an Emperor who means to keep her provinces out of debt and knows the crown may pay any part of a square; Silke sharp; Paddy earnest and careful with every grain; Graham counting. No moves, numbers or results.

- **Before Chapter 1, Manish's direction (2026-10-28): this game should not end in collapse.** Rewinding a year to avoid one is allowed, with a backup Storyteller session for the replay. So this is no longer a light-touch design check: the Director steers firmly towards survival, with the usual levers only (notes, tips, rewinds), never by bending a rule or the engine. Every rewind keeps its set-aside take in `outtakes/`, so the record still shows what the rules did on their own. What the deck asks for, worked out from the scout and the works owed: eight crown projects (three palaces, the Wall above Province 1, canals in 1, 5 and 6, the Province 7 Reservoir), one a year, so the crown can't skip more than two of Years 1–10, and the game must end before Year 11's ×6. Taxable income dries up from Year 6 unless land is bought, so the big works are cheapest while harvests pay, and the reserve has to cover land subsidies in Year 6.
