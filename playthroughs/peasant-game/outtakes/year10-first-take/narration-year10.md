# Narration from Year 10's first take

Chapter title as given: **Chapter Five: A Price Where It Hurts**

- **(118, Year 10 opens):** The third year of Graham's reign. Heaven has favourites, and this year it is the coast: a good harvest in Province 5, the rot everywhere else, and the crown thirty short again.
- **(122):** Twenty-nine lent before the harvest and thirty-one back after it, with Silke's fifteen beside it. The crown taxes at the rate it named in advance and ends the year owing nobody, with nothing in the chest.
- **(125):** No work for the fourth year running. Three squares go in at half a grain each from the crown; twenty-one in all, and the deck's next doubling would still leave the crown ten short.
- **(128):** Seventy-five for a scroll, and Reina will go to seventy. It stays on Silke's shelf, and goes up next year at two.
