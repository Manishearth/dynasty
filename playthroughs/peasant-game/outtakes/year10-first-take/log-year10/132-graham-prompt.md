## At the table since your last turn

Graham taxes 40%: Reina 24, Paddy 12, Silke 20, collected by Paddy; the crown 56.5. He repays both loans from it: 31 to Reina and 15 to Silke; the crown 10.5. His offers for the works step: one section of the Grand Canal in Province 5 or 6 (40), the province paying 39 and the crown 1, or nothing built; and 0.5 a square from the crown on new land bought by any province this year, for three squares at most in all. Grain: Graham (the crown) 10.5, Reina 90, Silke 95.5, Paddy 28.

Reina: "Thank you, Graham, the crown's books balance and it paid every grain it owed this year, which is more than I managed in my reign. No canal from me and no sea. The coast is fed and the coast is solvent, and I'm keeping my grain where it is."

Paddy: "Thank you for the offer, Graham, but thirty-nine of mine for a ditch with the crown's name on it is more than my paddies can carry. Reina said it well last year. I'll take one more square of sea at R27, though, with your half grain, and I'm grateful for it."

Silke: "The canal's for the coast to decide, not me. I'll take two of your half-grain squares, Graham, and that leaves the third for whoever wants it. If times six comes, Province 7 will be eating."

No work is built this year; neither Reina nor Paddy took the canal section at 39. New land: Paddy buys R27 (sea), paying 19.5 with the crown's 0.5; Silke buys U15 and V14 (the Warlord's land), paying 39 with the crown's 1. Each yields 20 from next year. Land: 21 squares. Grain: Graham (the crown) 9, Reina 90, Silke 56.5, Paddy 8.5. The crown has said it pays 9 in salaries at the year's end.

The auctions: only one piece is left in stock, Province 7's Land Deed Scroll, so it is the year's only lot; Silke sets its minimum. The Emperor doesn't bid.

Reina: "Silke, it's your Scroll and your price, and everyone here can do the sums: I have ninety, and the claim takes twenty. Set it wherever you think is fair. You gave me the Vase for two, and I'm not going to haggle with you over the last piece."

Paddy: "I've a Scroll of my own already sitting on R23, so I'll not bid on this one. I won't run anyone up for a piece I've no use for. Reina, whatever you do with it, I'd ask you to remember the Rice Peasant has kept his word to everyone at this table."

Silke: "The last Scroll in China, and everyone knows whose shelf it finishes. Seventy-five, Reina. You told me once to name a price where it hurts, and I listened."

(Storyteller's note: There are two Land Deed Scrolls: the one in Province 7's stock, and Paddy's on R23.)

The Land Deed Scroll goes unsold: Silke's minimum was 75 and Reina's limit 70; Paddy passed. It stays in Province 7's stock and must go up again next year at 2. Graham pays Paddy 6 for the tax and the I Ching, and Silke 3 for the bank.

End of Year 10: Graham (the crown) 0, owing nobody; Reina 90, Silke 59.5, Paddy 14.5. Nobody is in debt. Land: 21 squares. No work was built in Years 9 or 10; the Great Wall (160) and the Grand Canal (160) are still owed. Reina holds the Customs House, the Bowl, two Brushes and the Vase, and needs a Land Deed Scroll; Silke holds the Dragon Jar; Paddy holds a Scroll and a Customs House. Province 7's Scroll goes up next year at 2.
