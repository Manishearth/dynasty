diary: Two years ago I had a throne, and tonight I have a ditch full of wheat and a Reservoir someone else paid for twice. Year seven was the one that broke me. The rains failed, my chest was already empty because I'd put it into everyone's fields, and when I asked Graham for thirty he bought Paddy's Jar instead and took the coast with it. I taxed Reina at ninety percent to get out of the red. I knew it was hard when I did it, and a year later she paid me back for it in full. Then Graham walked onto my throne with three short in the chest, and I learned what it feels like to be told I'd be forgiven for not being paid. I fought Reina for the Bowl and lost by one grain, after warning the whole table about her out loud, and then I gave her the reason myself. She took the coast, my nineteen and a half, and the two squares of sea I'd only just paid thirty for. What I've kept is the best land at this table, fifty-five a year, the bank, and the Vase and Scroll sitting on my own shelf, which nobody but Reina and Graham's old seat will want. The throne goes through the Silk seat, and the Silk seat now belongs to the woman who knows exactly how I play. So next year I keep my grain dry, sell the Vase and Scroll dearly to whoever needs them, and build the Wheat set again quietly, piece by piece, until the coast is in reach. This time I won't announce it first.
