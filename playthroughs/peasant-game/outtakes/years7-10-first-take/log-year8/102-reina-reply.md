diary: Years 7–8. The bad harvest took everything I had. The Vase and the Scroll went to the Pile for two each, and Graham passed on my Vase, bought Paddy's Jar instead and took my seat. That left me twenty-five in debt and then holding sixty and a wheat field I'd built for him myself. It's the strangest kindness anyone has done me at this table. I used it the way I should have used the throne: I took the Jar at his minimum and the Brush at Paddy's two, and then I counted everyone's shelves before I counted my own. When Graham finished the Silk set and took the crown, I saw that the only door back up was the Silk seat, and Silke was standing in it with a Scroll in her hand. So I bought the Customs House and the Bowl, made her a fair offer in public, which she turned down, and took the coast back from her. I'm sorry about that, because she played well, but she was the next one coming for Graham, and now she's on the wheat and that road is shut to her. I have sixty-two and a half grain, the Bowl and the Brush, and the Vase and the Scroll are both on Silke's shelf, so whatever minimum she sets is the price I'll pay to go home. The game could end next year, so there's one auction left that matters, and this time I'm not going to be the one who wasn't paying attention.
