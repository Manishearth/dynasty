diary: Two rotten harvests, and the second one nearly took everything I own. I had four and a half grain and owed ten, and the Pile would have had my Jar, my Deed and my Customs House for two apiece. Graham wouldn't lend me six, but he gave me ten for the Jar. I took it and shook his hand, and that same night he finished his set with my Jar and walked up to the throne. I can't call it unfair. He was the only one at the table with grain to spare, and he spent it. Then the Bowl and the Brush I'd sold him came back to me, I sold them both to Reina, and with them she finished the wheat set and took the coast back off Silke. So my old pots have put two people in new seats this year, and neither of them was me. I kept my word on the Brush, I bought a square at Q26 with the crown paying five, and I've nine grain and no debts. I'm still the Rice Peasant, which this game hasn't let everyone say. The Jar's on Reina's shelf now and the Vase is on Silke's, and I need both of them again, so it's further off than ever. But next year I'm reading the cards as well as collecting the tax, the rice will bring in thirty-five in an ordinary year, and I mean to save every grain of it.
