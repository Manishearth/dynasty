diary: Years nine and ten were the years I stopped trying to win the race and started charging the toll on it. I lent the crown thirteen and got fifteen back. I sold Reina the Vase for two and the Brush cost her sixteen out of fear, and in the end she bought my Scroll for fifty-three, the most she could pay and still climb. She walked onto the throne with twenty grain and a smile and said she'd have done the same, and I believe her, which is the trouble with Reina. It cost me what I'd let myself want: the Vase and the Scroll are back on my shelf, but they're stock to sell now, not pieces of a set. All I hold is one Jar, and I'm three pieces short of anything. What I have instead is seventy-three and a half grain, the most at this table. I have two new squares of the Warlord's land, wheat that earns a hundred and fifteen in an ordinary year, and the bank. Reina's crown has fifteen and a half in the chest and three hundred and twenty of works still owed, and Graham is back on the coast with sixty and a long memory. So next year I sell the Vase and Scroll dearly again, I buy the Bowl and the Brushes when they come round in Province 6, and I keep enough grain dry that when the population card doubles, I'm the one who still has a field. I'm not announcing anything this time either.
