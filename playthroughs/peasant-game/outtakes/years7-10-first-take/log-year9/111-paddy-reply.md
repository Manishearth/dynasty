move: land Q27; I pay 16, asking the crown for 4
say: Graham, I've sixteen grain to my name, and the square's twenty. Your one a square won't get me there, so I'm asking four of you, and I'll put in every grain I've got. If the crown can't do four, I'll buy nothing and say no more about it. Times six is still in the deck, and I'd sooner meet it with one more field than with an empty bowl.
aside: That's everything I own going into one square of mud next to Q26, but even a bad year brings me ten, and a square pays twenty every year after. The Vase can wait one more year. What I'm afraid of is the times-six card.
whisper: none
