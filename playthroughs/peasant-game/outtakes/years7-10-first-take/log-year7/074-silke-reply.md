move: Ask Graham, in public, for a loan of 30 grain before the harvest is paid, so the crown lands at exactly 0 and not in debt. Terms: the crown repays 35 out of the first tax it collects, and his salary of 5 stays as it is. If Graham says no, I do nothing and take the debt.

say: "Another rotten year, and the crown has no shelf left to sell. You all know I put every grain into your fields. Graham, lend the crown thirty now and I'll pay you back thirty-five from the first tax I raise, with your five on top as always. A crown in debt can't pay its share of a single square next year, and that hurts every one of you, not just me. Reina, Paddy: if you have to sell, sell the pieces you need least. The Vase and the Jar are the last things I'd let go to the Pile."

aside: "I'm out of grain and out of pieces, so the only thing I have left to trade is my word, and Graham is the only one at this table with grain to lend. If he says yes, the crown stays out of debt and can still buy land. If he says no, everyone gets to watch the Empress go broke, and I'll make sure they remember who let it happen."

whisper: none
---
Ruling: a public request for a loan, with terms (30 now, 35 back from the first tax, salary of 5 as before). Not binding (§5). It goes to Graham with his reply after Reina and Paddy have spoken. The say and aside are accurate to the board.
