Ruling: accepted, and legal once it's put in grain. The crown holds 0, and a share can only be paid from grain in hand (N7), so it can't pay a salary it doesn't have, now or at year's end. The deal is therefore booked as Graham handing the crown 3 (a voluntary payment, act(deal=True)), which the crown pays straight back as its 1 a square. Graham pays 57 directly, 60 in all, and has 43.5 left, not the 46.5 his aside reckoned on (stated in his next prompt). By Silke's word the crown still owes him 2 for the year. With a reserve of 0 it can pay nothing at the salary step, so the year's I Ching salary goes unpaid. An appointee's pay is the Emperor's choice (Note 3), so that's legal; it's recorded as a promise outstanding.
