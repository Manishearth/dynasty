diary: Years 3 and 4. I paid twenty into the Reservoir, and it gave me twenty back in the first bad harvest, the only positive income in China that year. That's the best twenty I've spent. I sold the Vase twice: six grain from Silke, five and a half from Reina, and both times the buyer was left with nothing to show for it. Silke took the throne, and her taking it sent every piece back into stock, which no counting of mine had allowed for. That's how I got the Bowl for nine, a year after losing it at twelve. Now I hold the Customs House and the Bowl and seventy-four grain, the most of any peasant. The brushes are sitting in Paddy's stock, and the Jar is the one thing I can't buy. I still have the Scroll, and Paddy and Reina will both want it. I kept my word to Paddy, and he knows it, so when I ask him about that Jar I want it remembered. I'll keep my grain together until the population doubles. Then land at twenty pays twenty, and I'll stop needing anyone's pottery.
