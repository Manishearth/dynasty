diary: Year three was the year I stopped being the girl who reads the cards. I set my Customs House at fifteen to bleed Paddy, and nobody bought it. I didn't mind, because I took the Bowl from Graham by a single grain and the Vase from Paddy by a single grain, and then I stood up and took Reina's throne with a hundred and sixty-six grain behind me. It cost me thirty-eight grain and every piece I'd collected, since they all went back to the stock. That's the price of the crown, and I'd pay it again tomorrow. As Empress I kept it gentle: a light tax in a rotten year, a Winter Palace built with my own grain, and Graham's five for reading the cards, because Graham is the only one at this table who has nothing to gain from my fall. Now Reina has my old shelf and the Vase sitting on P22 exactly where I put it. Paddy has the Jar, a Scroll and the Customs House, and he's waiting for the Vase to come round again. Graham is quietly collecting too. My reserve is down to a hundred and nineteen, three hundred and forty of the works are still owed, and once the population doubles I'll have to buy land. My job now is to make sure that whichever of them gets close, the others want to stop them more than they want to stop me.
