diary: Two years, and I came close twice, and both times somebody beat me to the Vase: Silke by one grain, then Reina by one step. I can't hold it against either of them. I told the whole table it would finish my set, and Reina's just lost a throne, so of course she wasn't going to let me have it. Silke, meanwhile, played her game quietly the whole way through and now she's sitting on the throne, which taught me that you don't have to shout to win. The Customs House is finally mine, and I paid only two grain for it, so I've got the Jar, the Deed and the House in a row on my paddies with 42 and a half grain, and I don't owe anyone a thing. Next time I won't say out loud what a piece is worth to me. I'll just keep my twenty back, save the rest, and wait for the Vase to come round again, and I'll be ready when it does.
