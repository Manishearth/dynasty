diary: Year five was the good year. I set forty percent, built the Pagoda with grain the table had paid in, and finished with a hundred and eighty in the chest. Then Graham drew Population Doubles and every province went into the red together. That chest is now empty. It all went into the ground: four squares of sea for the north and a share of every square the others bought, thirteen squares across China. I don't regret a grain of it, because next year this empire finally has a harvest I can tax. Graham surprised me. He asked the crown for one grain a square so I'd still have enough to pay him, and I'm starting to think he's the only honest man at this table. Or he wants me to think so while he waits for Paddy's Jar. Reina is down to one grain and told us not to be afraid of her. Those are the words I'd use too. Next year I tax the new fields hard enough to start the Canal, I keep something back in case the population card comes again, and I watch who ends up holding that Jar.
