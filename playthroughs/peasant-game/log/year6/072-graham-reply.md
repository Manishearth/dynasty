diary: Years 5 and 6. I paid thirteen for Paddy's brush, one under my limit, and sold my last Scroll to Reina for six, which was my minimum. That leaves the Jar as the only piece missing from my set. Then I turned the card and it said three hundred million. Every province went to nothing or less, and Silke spent the whole crown on fields. I put seventy-six of my own into four squares of the Warlord's land, and the crown paid four, one grain a square. That turns twenty-five short a year into fifty-five over. I end the year with thirty-two and a half. Silke has nothing, Reina has one, Paddy has four and a half. I didn't bid on the second brush. I've said once at this table what it looks like when someone does that, and I don't need to show them. The game could end with next year's canal or wall, so the squares may only pay back once, but once still covers their cost. The Jar is next. Paddy is the only one holding it, I'm the only one with grain, and I want him to remember that I stood aside for his Customs House when he names his price.
