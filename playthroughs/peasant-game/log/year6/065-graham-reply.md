move: land T14, T15, T16, T17; I pay 76, asking the crown for 4
say: Four squares for the south centre, Silke. One a square from the crown, four in all, and that still leaves your chest enough for my five. I've done the sums. I don't need your eight, and I'm not leaving you short to pay me.
aside: At three hundred million I'm twenty-five short a year, and four squares make that a profit of fifty-five, with twenty-seven and a half still in my purse for the Jar. I asked the crown for its last four grain so that when Silke's chest is empty, nobody can say I left her owing.
whisper: none
---
Ruling: legal. Graham asks the crown for 1 a square, less than its standing public offer of 5, so the offer covers it and no further prompt is needed; the crown pays some part of every square, as §13 requires. T14 to T17 are Warlord's land on Province 7's frontier. Province 7 buys four squares, 80 into the pile: Graham 76, the crown 4. Graham 103.5 - 76 = 27.5; the crown 9 - 4 = 5, which covers Graham's salary. The say and aside are right: -25 + 80 = +55.
