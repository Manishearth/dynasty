diary: Two years ago I had forty-two and a half grain and was dreaming about the Vase. Now I've four and a half, and for once I think I've done the right thing with it. Graham's card doubled the mouths in every province, and the paddies would have eaten me hollow, forty-five grain a year. So I put forty-five into three squares of new land at R25, R26 and S26, and now the south-east just about feeds itself. I sold a brush to Graham for thirteen, and the other one's still on my shelf, since nobody had two grain to spare for it, and I've half promised it to Reina. What I keep thinking about is Graham telling the whole table he's waiting on my Jar, and that he'll be the one with the grain. I didn't give him an answer, and I won't sell the Jar cheap, not after all it's cost me. The Vase feels further off than ever, but I'm not in debt and I don't owe anyone a thing, and next year's harvest finally comes in my favour. If the game runs on, I'll save every grain the new fields give me and be ready when the Vase comes round.
