move: tax 40%; build nothing this year (the reserve goes into land); land: the crown pays 5 grain of every square any province buys this year, and Province 1 buys 4 squares (80)
say: "Three hundred million mouths, and not one province with a grain of income to tax. So no marble this year. Every grain the crown has goes into the ground. I'll pay five on every square any of you buys, and I'm buying four for the north myself. Fill your fields first, then come and ask me for canals. Reina, the coast canal is still coming. It comes once there's a harvest to pay for it."
aside: "A square pays twenty a year and a canal section pays ten for forty, so the Wall and the Canal can wait until somebody here has a taxable income again. Four squares gets the north close to even and leaves me about twenty in reserve for a bad harvest. Reina's down to twenty-five and holds the Vase and the Scroll, so she's the one I'm watching while everyone else plants."
whisper: none
---
Ruling: legal. Tax 40% raises nothing: no province has a positive income (Graham's is 0). No project this year, which is allowed. Province 1 buys 4 squares of sea, 80 into the pile; she named no squares, so the Storyteller places them on F23-F26, the sea along the province's edge (the palace precedent), leaving K25, which Province 5 can also reach. Reserve 120 - 80 = 40. The crown's offer of 5 a square stands only as far as its grain in hand covers it (40 now, and Graham's salary of 5 still to come): up to 8 peasant squares at 5. "No marble" is a figure of speech (the Pagoda is built). The aside's "about twenty in reserve" is an estimate of what the shares will leave; no note.
