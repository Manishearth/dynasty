## At the table since your last turn

Paddy collects the tax at 40%: 14 each from Silke, Paddy and Graham, 42 to the crown. Reina digs both sections of the Grand Canal in Province 1, paying the 80 herself; they add 20 to her harvest from next year. 420 of the works are still owed.

Paddy: "Second brush, as promised, Graham, same as the first, opening at four. And I'm sorry about the frost. Losing two to a cold night is hard on anybody."

Graham: "The other Land Deed Scroll. Minimum five. Paddy, you went to six for the last one."

This year's lots: Silke puts up one of her two Customs Houses (her only type left; she sets its minimum in her bids); Paddy puts up his second Bamboo Brush, minimum 4; Graham puts up his last Land Deed Scroll, minimum 5.

Silke: "One Customs House, opening at three. It's good for trade and a quiet port. As for the brush and the scroll, I already have mine, gentlemen, so I'm only watching. Mostly."

Paddy: "Graham, there are two Customs Houses and two of us after them. Let's not feed the Harvest Pile. You take this one, I'll only put in a small bid, and next year you leave the second one to me. That's fair, isn't it? And yes, I'll be bidding on that last scroll, I won't pretend otherwise."

Graham: "Paddy. Silke has two Customs Houses, and you and I need one each. I won't bid against you on this one if you leave the next one to me. No point feeding the Pile." (A pause.) "The brush I'll take at Paddy's price."

The auctions: Graham wins the Customs House for 5 over Paddy's 4; Silke keeps 1. Silke wins the last Bamboo Brush for 6 over Graham's 5; Paddy keeps 1.5. Paddy wins the last Land Deed Scroll for 13 over Silke's 12; Graham keeps 3.

Graham: "Q15. Thank you, Paddy, the next one's yours, as agreed." (Quietly.) "Silke. You already had a brush."

Silke: "Two brushes on my coast, so I'll be the best-lettered peasant in China. Graham, I'm sorry, but six is cheap for a good brush, and you agreed it was Paddy's price."

Paddy: "Thirteen, and Silke, you made me earn every grain of it, for a scroll you already had. Graham, we crossed our wires on who got which Customs House, but you've got this one now, so by my reckoning the last one's mine next year and you'll stand aside. I'll hold you to that, same as I'd hold myself."

Graham puts the Customs House on Q15, Silke her second Bamboo Brush on P24, Paddy the Land Deed Scroll on R23 beside his Jar. Reina pays Silke her 5 for reading the I Ching.
