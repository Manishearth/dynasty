diary: Two years in, and I've spent thirty grain on pots and paper, more than I've ever spent on anything I couldn't plant. But the Dragon Jar and the Land Deed were the only ones left in all of China, and now they're both sitting on my paddies at R22 and R23. Graham shook on it fair and square: the last Customs House is mine next year, and I mean to keep my side of it as carefully as he keeps his. Silke worries me a bit. She bid twelve on a scroll she didn't need, and now she wants Graham's Vase same as I do, and my Wooden Bowl as well. I've only 29 and a half grain, so I'll have to be careful. I'll save what I can, I won't owe anyone a thing, and I'll keep the Bowl back until it's worth something to me.
