diary: Two years. I started with thirty-six and now have fifty-four and a half, and the only piece I own is one Customs House on Q15. The Jar went to Paddy at seventeen, one grain past my sixteen. Silke paid six for a second brush she had no use for, and called it my price. A frost took two more off me. With both brushes on Silke's coast and the only Jar in Paddy's paddies, I can't finish my set unless one of them gives something back, and neither will. What I do have is the Vase, and both of them need it. Nobody else has one. I'll put it up next year and let them bid against each other for it, and I'll stand aside on Paddy's Customs House as I promised, because I keep my word even when the others don't. After that I save my grain for land. When the population doubles, a square costs twenty and pays twenty a year, and that's the best number on the board.
