move: land C17, C18, D18, E18; I pay 20, asking the crown for 60
say: Fifteen of twenty, Noah, as declared. Four squares, C17, C18, D18 and E18: twenty from me and sixty from the crown. That finishes the ring above my wheat, and I've checked that it doesn't touch anybody's coast either. You've paid my salary every year for ten years, so you'll find yourself on the first page of the good book, in ink.
aside: Twenty grain buys me eighty a year from next harvest on, which is the best trade on the table. And I still have a hundred and fifty-nine in my pocket for the auction where Rich needs only a Jar.
whisper: none

Ruling: legal. C17, C18, D18, E18 for Province 2; Clark 20, crown 60, as declared. "Every year for ten years": Noah has been Emperor in Years 2 to 4 and 9 to 11; colour, not corrected.
