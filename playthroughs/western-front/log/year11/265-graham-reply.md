diary: Years 10 and 11. I asked for the rent and all three paid it, eighty to the grain, and I'd written each of them down for it. Then I lost the Customs House by one grain, the Brush by one, and the Bowl by one, and Rich bought Reina's chair with a Jar and mine with a Bowl, and he said sorry both times before he did it. That's his first two sorries, and Noah's are up to four now. So I came down to the silk coast with sixty grain and an empty shelf. It turned out to be the best field in China, and the crown had paid for most of it when I was the crown. One harvest there was worth a thousand and twenty. I paid Rich sixty rent by my own measure, set the Jar at four hundred because a chair should cost a chair's price, and with his quarter in my pocket I bought the four pieces I was missing for a hundred and fifty-nine. Then he took the throne from Noah with the Jar I'd sold him, and that made it three sorries. I'm sitting on a finished set and six hundred grain, and I didn't get up, because nobody below me is close to my coast and a set doesn't spoil. Next year the silk comes in at twelve hundred and fifty-five. After that I'll pick my chair and climb one rung, then the last one, the same way I did before. This time the man I'm climbing toward is the one who taught me what sorry costs.
