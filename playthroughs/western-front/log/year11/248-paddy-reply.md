move: rent 25; land M15, M16, M17, M18, N12, O12, O13; I pay 35, asking the crown for 105
say: Twenty-five, Rich, as asked. The land paid me four hundred and forty, and I'd be a poor sort of farmer to grudge you a tenth of the fields that did it. Noah, seven squares at your fifteen and my five, M15 to M18, N12, O12 and O13. Every grain of it goes into a Pile that's nine hundred short, so I hope that counts as a kindness to everyone.
aside: Seven squares for thirty-five grain is the best bargain I'll see this year, and it keeps me ahead of the disasters and the rents. I've a Customs House on the shelf and a fat purse now, so this time I'll be able to stay in when the bidding climbs.
whisper: none

---
Ruling: legal. Rent 25 of 25 asked. Seven squares for Province 7; Paddy 35, crown 105, as declared.
