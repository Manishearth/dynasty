move: rent Graham 60, Reina 25, Paddy 25
say: Every bag paid out in full, friends, the Pile's own shortfall noted to the grain where anybody can read it. Now, I said in the very first year that the rent rises with the land, and I'll use Graham's own measure from last year, not mine: a little under a tenth of what the land paid you. Graham, sixty on a thousand and twenty; Reina, twenty-five on four-twenty; Paddy, twenty-five on four-forty. That's the honest ledger he taught me, and I won't argue with it.
aside: Graham set the rate himself last year, so if he balks at sixty he's arguing with his own book, and a man who sells me my Jar this year ought to see I'm asking him less than his harvest says I could. I don't need the grain; I need everyone at this table to remember Rich Baron charges fair.
