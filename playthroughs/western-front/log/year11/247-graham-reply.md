move: rent 60; land F26, F27, F28, F29, F30, N30, O30, P30, Q30, R30, S30, T29, T30; I pay 130, asking the crown for 130
say: Sixty, Rich. It's my own measure, and a man who sets the rent ought to be able to pay it. Noah, I'll take the thirteen squares of coast at ten a square from me and ten from the crown, the way Reina did when this was her coast. That leaves the reserve something for the others.
aside: I set that sixty last year, so paying it in full costs me sixty and leaves Rich nothing to complain about. Thirteen more squares at ten each means the silk coast pays back two hundred and sixty a year from next year, and I'll be the man who didn't empty the reserve, the next time anyone asks.
whisper: none

Ruling: legal. Rent 60 of 60 asked. Thirteen squares for Province 5, paying 10 a square (130), the crown 130 (less than its offered 15). T29 was also on Reina's list; Graham's seat comes first, so it was taken off her list (249).
