Silke: "Debt again, so I'm only selling. The Brush opens at eleven, and every grain of my quarter goes back to Noah's kindness. Thank you, Noah. Clark: "Customs House at three, the toll hasn't changed in eleven years. And Reina's right about the arithmetic. Rich has a thousand and sixty-seven grain and he needs one Jar. Rich: "Reina's arithmetic is quite right, and I won't insult anyone by pretending otherwise: I want that Jar, and I'll pay for it at the counter where you can all watch. Graham: "The Jar opens at four hundred. Rich, you told the table last year the rent rises with the land, so here's the same measure: the price rises with the chair. Everyone else, I'll be bidding on the Customs House, the Deed, the Brush and the Bowl, and I'll be bidding to win them."

Paddy: "I'll say my pieces out loud again, the way I did in Year 5, and it went well for me that year: the Deed, the Brush and the Bowl. I'll pay a fair price at the counter for each, but I'll not put a farmer's whole harvest on a pot. Rich, the Jar's between you and Graham, and I'll stand well back from that one."

The auctions, settled on the limits: Clark's Customs House to Graham at 31 (Reina stopped at 30); Rich's Land Deed Scroll to Graham at 46 (Paddy stopped at 45; Clark's 35 and Reina's 30 were under Rich's minimum of 40); Silke's Bamboo Brush to Graham at 41 (Paddy 40, Reina 30); Graham's Dragon Jar to Rich at Graham's minimum of 400 (Clark's 20 was under it). The Bowl and Vase are still to come.

Graham: "The Customs House, the Deed and the Brush go round the Vase, where anybody can count them. That's four. Rich, four hundred, paid at the counter as you said it would be, and I've written down that you kept your word."

Rich: "Four hundred, Graham, paid at the counter as promised, and the Jar sits at K16 with the other four where everyone can count them. Noah, Your Majesty, I'm not sitting down quite yet. A man who's been unseated from behind once learns to look over his shoulder before he takes the chair, and Graham, you've got four pieces round a Vase and a Bowl coming up at ten."

Rich: "Then I won't sit here waiting to be taken from behind. Noah, Your Majesty, you've been the most honest Emperor this table has seen, paying salaries out of your own reserve and building canals seven years late but building them, and I'm terribly sorry. Rich took the Jar on K16, completing the Gentry's set, and took the throne from Noah after lot 5. He returned all five pieces to stock and took the reserve, 229 (907.5 in all). Noah drew 60 from the Pile and is now the Gentry, in Province 3.

Graham, if the Bowl is your fifth piece, you'll be paying more for it than I've got."
