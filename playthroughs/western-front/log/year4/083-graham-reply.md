diary: Years 3 and 4. I paid fifteen for a Deed and twenty-four for the last Brush in the game, and I've set them side by side in the middle of my land. I bought two squares on the southern edge with the crown paying half, and I told Rich no when he tried to charge me rent on a reservoir he never paid for. That was five grain, and it was worth saying. Then in one auction the throne went round the table three times, Reina, then Rich, then Clark, and all their pieces went back into stock, so the Brush I paid top price for as the last one isn't the last one any more. Noah has gone from Emperor to Scholar with sixty grain and nothing in hand, so he's starting from the same rung I'm on, and I'm two pieces ahead of him. I have fifty-eight grain, and with forty a year of new land and the Reservoir coming in, the south will be the best-fed province at this table. I still need the Customs House, the Jar and the Bowl. They'll cost what this table charges, and I'll pay it, and I'll go on writing down who said sorry.
