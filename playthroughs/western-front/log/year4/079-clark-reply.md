diary: Years Three and Four, and this time the good book got an entry under my own name. I kept my mouth shut in Year Three, took the Bowl at thirty-six, and then watched Rich go to forty-one on the Vase and call it sentimentality. That went straight into the other book, under Reina's three entries. In Year Four I was five grain in the red every year with no land I was allowed to buy. I collected the crown's tax anyway and told Noah to his face that his collector was starving under an unbuilt Wall. Then came the auction where three people sat on the throne in three lots: Reina for a lot and a half, Rich for one, and me last, with the Vase for twenty-three because Graham kept his word and Noah wouldn't go past twenty-two. I paid Noah ten because he paid me ten when I was nobody, and I paid Rich five because he hasn't earned more yet. Now I have two hundred and eighty in the reserve, eight hundred and fifty owed on the works, a Wall that needs building in a province that isn't mine any more, and six people who have all watched how quickly this chair changes hands. Next year I buy land for the provinces that will pay tax on it, I watch every piece that goes up for sale, and I keep both books open, because the man in this chair can't afford to forget anything.
