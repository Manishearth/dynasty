diary: For two years I kept every promise I made from the throne. I built Graham his reservoir, put up the Winter Palace for the canals, paid half on every square of new land, paid Clark and Reina their ten each, and said every sum out loud, including the one I got wrong. Then I bought J25 and K25 without looking, and that boxed Reina in with no coast left, and three lots later she took the throne back from me with a sorry I'd earned. The throne went round three times in one auction, and Clark sits there now with two hundred and eighty, which is more than I ever managed to save. I'm the Scholar again with seventy grain and not a piece to my name, and I owe Reina a "make it right" that I can't pay from this chair, but I haven't forgotten it. Next comes the slow arithmetic: five pieces and twenty to spare, bought at fair prices, one year at a time, and I'll check the map next time before I buy a square of land.
