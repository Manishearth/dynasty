diary: Years 3 and 4. I did what I said I'd do. I picked two pieces and went after them, and I got the Customs House for fifteen and the Brush for ten, cheaper than I'd braced for. Then the land opened and I put most of my grain into three fields by the shore, two of them half paid by the crown and the third paid by me in full, so from next year I'll be feeding myself and not lying awake over it. I'd only thirty-odd left for the auctions after that, and Reina beat me to the Deed and the Jar, fair and square. Then the throne changed hands three times in one evening, Reina, then Rich, then Clark, each of them saying sorry to the last as they sat down, and I sold Rich the Bowl for twenty-one that put him in the chair for about a minute. I've thirty-six and a half grain, two pieces on the board and three fields of my own coming in. The gentlemen hand the crown round among themselves like a teapot. Next year I'll want a Deed, a Jar and a Vase, and with the fields paying I might finally have the purse to stay in when the bidding climbs.
