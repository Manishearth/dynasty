diary: This year I paid Silke the fifty she asked for, and Reina kept her word and made me pay a hundred and fifty-one for the Bowl. This time I didn't tell anybody my top, and I still think I stayed honest. Then the set did a thing I hadn't counted on: I took Paddy's stool because it was the one move where the man I pushed out came off better, the Brush went home, and all at once I needed the very Vase I'd set at ten a minute before. I bought it for twenty-one and took the throne from Silke after she'd held it for one lot. I said sorry to her face, same as I did with Reina in the second year, and I meant it just as much and it did her just as little good. So I'm Emperor again with five hundred and fifty in the reserve and two hundred and ninety owed on the works, and Rich wants one Jar, Silke has seventy and knows every set at this table, and Reina is still counting. Next year it's the same arithmetic I promised the first time: ten for Clark, ten for the Scholar, the works built and how far along they are said out loud every year, and this time I'll check the map before I buy a square for anybody.
