diary: Year 9. I turned up the Bad Harvest card myself, and it hit me harder than anyone: with no fields to my name the pile took seventy off me, and I sat down to the auction with two and a half grain, which isn't a purse, it's lint. I put two on Clark's Customs House and he'd set it at three, so I couldn't even buy a ticket. Then the teapot went round the table faster than I've ever seen it. Silke paid two hundred and one for a Jar and took the throne, Noah bought the Bowl and took my stool off me, and then he needed the Vase, which I suddenly had to sell, so he bought it from me and took the throne off Silke before her tea had cooled. He said sorry to her face, and he said a kind word to me as well, and I believe he meant both. So now I'm a wheat farmer with sixty-five grain and twenty squares of good land with a reservoir on them, which is more ground than I've ever stood on in this game. All I had to do for it was lose my job. Next year I'll let the land feed me, pay my rent and tax without a fuss, and keep my grain back until a piece comes up at a price a farmer can stand. It's the land I've regretted every year, and this year I didn't even have to buy it.
