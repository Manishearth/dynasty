say: Fifty asked and fifty paid, Silke. A bad year's a bad year for the pile too, and I won't be the man who short-changes it the year it's down to four hundred and seventy-seven. aside: The land puts grain back in a pile that's running thin, and it doesn't cost me much of what I'll need when Rich's last two Bowls come up. I've said what I'm holding, and I haven't said what I'll bid with it.
