diary: I said the eighth year and I did it in the ninth: two hundred and one for the Jar, one grain over Rich, and the throne was mine. I held it for exactly one lot. Noah paid a hundred and fifty-one for a Bowl and twenty-one for a Vase and walked up through Paddy's stool to my chair. He said sorry to my face, which is more than Reina, Rich or Clark ever managed, and I can't fault him for it, because it's the same lesson I taught Reina. Whoever waits loses the throne. It cost me everything I'd built: twenty fields given to Reina last year, five pieces back into stock this year, and my province's harvest handed to whoever sits in the Gentry's chair. What I have now is seventy grain, the Scholar's stool, an empty shelf, and a card I turned over myself that doubles the mouths on my table to six hundred million. Graham is running my old Harvest Pile with sixty grain, Rich still needs only one Jar, and Noah has five hundred and fifty in the reserve. Next year I start again from nothing, which is where I started this game, and back then I learned that losing teaches more than winning, so I'll be counting every grain at this table the way I did when I was squeezed the first time.
