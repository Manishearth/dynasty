move: salaries Official 10, Scholar 10; land none
say: Ten for Clark and ten for Silke. Graham declared ten and five, but I told Clark in the second year I'd pay him ten every year I sat here, and I'm sitting here again, and I won't pay the Scholar less than I'm paying the Official. That's twenty out of five hundred and seventy, which leaves five hundred and fifty in the reserve, and two hundred and ninety still owed on the works. Next year I'll tell you how much of that we've built.
aside: These are the same salaries I paid the first time, so my old promise to Clark still holds and Silke can't say I shorted her the year I took her chair. I'm holding the reserve back for the works and next year's land.
