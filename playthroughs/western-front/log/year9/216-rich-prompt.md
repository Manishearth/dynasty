## At the table since your last turn

Clark: "Graham, fifteen it is, and you go into the good book in ink, twice now. As for the auction, I've got twenty-nine and a half grain and a Brush, so nobody needs to lose sleep over me. My Customs House is three, same toll as always."

Silke: "Thirty from Reina, thirty from Rich, fifty from Noah, and thank you all, that's more than I collected last year in a good one. I'll be plain with the table, since every one of you can count: I'm bidding on one lot this year, and it's the Jar. Rich, you've got two hundred and twenty. I've got more."

Paddy: "Two and a half grain, that's my whole purse after the pile took its seventy, so I'll not be keeping anybody up late. I'll put two on Clark's Customs House, the last one he has, and if Reina wants it she only has to breathe on it. Thank you, Silke, for not asking me for rent. There wasn't anything to ask for."

Reina: "The Jar opens at two, as the house rule says, and I can't touch it, so whoever takes it will take it cheap and take a chair with it. I'm not hiding from that. I'm buying pieces for a set I can't finish yet, and I'll say again what I said last year: Noah, the Bowl stays on the shelf if I can manage it."

Noah: "I'm bidding on one lot, the Bowl, and only the Bowl. I won't tell you my top this year. Last year I said it out loud and lost by one grain, and I've learned from that. The Vase opens at ten, same as last time, and it's a fair price for a piece four of you still want."

(Storyteller's note: Three players still need a Vase: Clark, Paddy and Reina.)

Silke: "Two hundred and one, Rich, and I paid for every grain of your sentiment. Graham, you kept your word on the land, and I'll keep mine: in the eighth year I said I'd buy the Jar and then take the throne. Slide the reserve over. Reina: "Three grain for a Customs House. Clark, that's the cheapest toll you've ever charged me, and Paddy, thank you for only breathing on it. Noah: "Well, there's a turn I didn't see coming. Noah: "Twenty-one for the Vase, Paddy, and you keep a quarter of it. Silke, I'm sorry, and I'm saying it to your face, the same as I did with Reina in the second year. You said the eighth year and you did it in the ninth, and I'm taking the chair from you after one lot anyway, because I learned a long time ago that whoever waits loses the throne. Three hundred and ninety and a half in the reserve, two hundred and ninety owed on the works, and I'll tell this table every year how far along we are, same as I did before."

Noah: "Ten for Clark and ten for Silke.
