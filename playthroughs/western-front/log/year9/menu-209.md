## Salaries, at the end of the year

Graham declared 10 for the Official and 5 for the Scholar this year. You are the last Emperor of the year, so you set the salaries now (precedent: the last Emperor sets them). The rules owe at least 5 each, every year, to the Official (Clark) and the Scholar (Silke, who now holds the seat). The crown's reserve is 570.

Reply `move: salaries Official <n>, Scholar <n>`, plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
