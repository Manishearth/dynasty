## You won Paddy's Vase for 21

Clark stopped at 7, Graham at 15 and Reina at 20; your limit was 180. Paddy, now the Wheat Peasant, keeps a quarter.

**1. Place it now** (Note 10), on a square of your province. Free squares: J19–J22, K19, K24, L19–L23, M19–M23, N19, N21–N22. A piece can't go under a work.

**2. Your set is complete.** With the Vase placed you hold a Customs House, Land Deed Scroll, Dragon Jar, Wooden Bowl and Vase, and you have 179.5 grain, more than the 20 the set needs. The auction is over (this was the last lot), so you may now change places with one player, or hold your seat:

- Silke, the Empress (Province 1), whose reserve is 390.5;
- Clark, the Official (Province 2), who has 30;
- Graham, the Gentry (Province 3), who has 60;
- Reina, the Silk Peasant (Province 5), who has 357;
- Rich, the Rice Peasant (Province 6), who has 258;
- Paddy, the Wheat Peasant (Province 7), who has 65.

If you change places, you keep your grain and take all of the displaced player's. Becoming Emperor returns all your pieces to stock, and the old Emperor hands over the whole reserve. Otherwise pieces native to your new seat go back to its stock, and you carry the rest to squares in your new province (you'll be asked which). The displaced player returns everything, draws 60 from the Harvest Pile and plays the Scholar from now on.

Reply `move: 7:VA <square>; replace <name> | hold`, plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
