move: rent Reina 60, Rich 30, Noah 50
say: "Rotten seed, late rain, a lame ox, and the pile still pays out four hundred and seventy-seven. Reina, Noah, you took the two fattest harvests in China this year, so the rent goes up with them. Sixty from the coast, fifty from the wheat, thirty from Rich. Paddy, I've paid your seventy into the pile, and nobody here is going to ask you for rent this year."
aside: "Every Jar in China is sitting beside Reina's hand, and a rent bill is the one lever I have over her price. Sixty in public, and in private I tell her what it would take to make it five."
whisper: To Reina: Put the Jar's minimum under a hundred this year and I'll take five from you, not sixty, and I'll take it next year too. Keep it at three hundred and I'll ask you for sixty every year I sit in this chair.
