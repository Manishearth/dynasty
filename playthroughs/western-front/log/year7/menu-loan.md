## The crown asks for a loan

Graham has asked the table to lend the crown 12 grain now, before salaries, to be repaid with 2 on top (14) out of next year's tax. Without it the crown pays its declared 15 in salaries from 3.5 and falls 11.5 into debt, which is a cause for revolt. No deal is binding.

You may lend the 12 (or part of it, or on your own terms), or decline.

Reply `move: lend <n> | decline`, plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
