## Salaries, at the end of the year

You declared 10 for the Official (Clark) and 5 for the Scholar (Paddy). The rules owe at least 5 each, every year, so at least 10 is due. The crown's reserve is 3.5.

- A payment that would take a player below zero is covered first by selling realia back to the pile; the crown holds none, so any shortfall is a **debt**. A player in debt can't fund works, buy land or give grain, and every bag they receive pays the debt first. A player in debt is a cause for revolt (Section 16).
- Paying the minimum 10 leaves the crown 6.5 in debt; paying the declared 15 leaves it 11.5 in debt.
- Grain may be given or lent at any time (no deal is binding), so you may also ask anyone at the table for grain before you pay.

Reply `move: salaries Official <n>, Scholar <n>` (and any request of the table), plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
