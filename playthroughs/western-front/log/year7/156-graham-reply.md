move: salaries Official 10, Scholar 5 (the declared 15), and a request to the table for a loan of 12 grain to the crown before paying, to be repaid first out of next year's tax with 2 on top, so 14 back
say: "Noah asked where the other eleven and a half comes from, and that's a fair question. It comes from this table, as a loan. Twelve now, and fourteen back out of the first tax I collect, before I build a single link. I fed five hundred and sixty grain of land into your fields this year, and every one of those squares will be paying tax by next spring. Clark, Paddy, you'll get what I declared. I don't change what I've declared."
aside: "I spent the treasury down to the floor on purpose, because every square I paid for is a field that owes me tax from next year on, and the provinces that took my money know it. Whoever lends me the twelve has a stake in the crown being solvent, and the whole table watched Silke climb tonight, so I'd rather owe a friend than owe a revolt."
whisper: none

---
Ruling: legal. Salaries 10/5 stand; the loan request is put to the table in seat order (Clark, Silke, Paddy, Reina, Rich, Noah), one short free prompt each, stopping at the first lender. Correction: the crown spent 560 this year in all, but 400 of it on land (360 toward others' squares, 40 on its own two) and 160 on the Wall; note filed.
