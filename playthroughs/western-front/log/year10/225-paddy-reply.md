move: rent 20; land N13, N14, N15, N16, N17, N18, O18, V19; I pay 80, asking the crown for 80
say: Twenty, Graham, as asked, and gladly. I've stood on more ground this year than in the nine before it, so it'd be a poor man who grudged the rent on it. Noah, I'll take eight squares, all of the N row, O18 and V19, but I'll go halves like Reina: ten from me and ten from you on each. I've wanted my own field every year of this game, and I'd sooner pay my share for it than have it given to me.
aside: Free land is lovely, but if the crown pays every grain, the crown's tired by Year 13 and the Pagoda never gets built, so I'm paying half and keeping the reserve honest. Eight squares and I've still two hundred-odd back for the auction, which is a farmer's purse for once and not lint.
whisper: none
