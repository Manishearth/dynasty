move: land D15, D16, D17, E17; I pay 0, asking the crown for 80
say: "Noah said the whole twenty on any square, so I'll take all four: D15, D16, D17 and E17, eighty from the crown and not a grain from me. Write it down, Noah, because I'm writing it down: an Emperor who keeps a promise in public on the same night he makes it. That's a rare entry in either of my books."
aside: "Four free squares is another eighty a year in every harvest from next year, and I keep all seventy-two grain in my pocket for an auction where the Deed opens at two. Noah's generosity buys my fields, and my grain buys my set."
