## Your new seat: the Rice Peasant, Province 6

You returned your Customs House, Wooden Bowl, Land Deed Scroll and Bamboo Brush to their provinces' stock and drew 60 from the Harvest Pile, so you hold nothing and have 60 grain. As the Rice Peasant you collect every type but the Wooden Bowl: a Customs House, Land Deed Scroll, Bamboo Brush, Dragon Jar and Vase, plus 20 grain. Province 6 comes with Rich's twenty-seven squares of new land and its two canal sections.

**1. The Wooden Bowl** (lot 6) is now your lot: you sell it, at the minimum of 12 Rich set, and keep a quarter of the price.

**2. The Vase** (lot 7, Paddy's, minimum 10). Your limit of 220 was given as the Silk Peasant and lapses with the seat. Give a new one: pass, or a ceiling, with an optional keep-from. Clark and Graham have already given theirs.

Reply `move: 7:VA pass | ceiling <n>; keep from <name> up to <n> (optional)`, plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
