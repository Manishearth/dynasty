move: rent Reina 40, Rich 20, Paddy 20
say: "The Pile's paid out every bag it owed, all eleven hundred and eighty of them, and it's short eighty for doing it. Now the rent. Reina, the crown paid fifteen a square on your new land when I was sitting in the chair, and your harvest this year was six hundred and twenty, so I'm asking forty. Rich and Paddy, twenty each. I'm not asking for more than the land paid you back, and I've written down what each of you got from it."
aside: "I said in my diary I'd ask for all of it, and this is all of it, set against the grain I put into their fields, so a no from any of them will be a no to their own harvest. With a hundred and sixty on top of my eighty I can buy the cheap pieces first and still keep twenty back."
