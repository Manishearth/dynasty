## Your new seat: the Silk Peasant, Province 5

You held nothing, so you return nothing; you drew 60 from the Harvest Pile and have 60 grain. Province 5 comes with fifty-seven squares of new land, two canal sections built this year and four Dragon Jars in stock, which you now sell. As the Silk Peasant you collect every type but the Dragon Jar: a Customs House, Land Deed Scroll, Bamboo Brush, Wooden Bowl and Vase, plus 20 grain. Rich, the new Gentry, needs only a Jar.

**The Vase** (lot 7, Paddy's, minimum 10) is the last lot. Your limit of 30 was given as the Gentry and lapses with the seat. Give a new one: pass, or a ceiling, with an optional keep-from. Clark (12) and Reina (15) have given theirs; Rich holds a Vase.

Reply `move: 7:VA pass | ceiling <n>; keep from <name> up to <n> (optional)`, plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
