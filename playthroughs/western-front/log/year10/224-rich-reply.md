move: rent 20; land T28, U27, U28, V20, V21, V24, V25, V26, V27; I pay 0, asking the crown for 180
say: Twenty, Graham, counted out with a smile. I said in the very first year that the rent rises with the land, and it has, so here it is. And Noah, Your Majesty, you said the whole twenty on any square, so I'll take you at your word, every word of it: nine squares of rice shore, and I'll say it loud enough for Clark's good book. An Emperor who pays for the peasants' fields will be remembered in the south-east long after the rest of us are forgotten.
aside: Nine free squares is a hundred and eighty more grain every harvest, and I'm not spending one grain of my four hundred and fifty before the Jar comes up, because Reina's the one selling it and she just showed the table she can afford to be generous. Let her play the gracious seawall builder; I'm keeping my purse whole for the one lot that matters.
whisper: none
