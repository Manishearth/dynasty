**The Wall above Province 3: the crown's offer.** Clark offers to build two of the four Wall links above your province this year (80 in all): the crown pays 60 and asks you to pay 20. It's this year's one project. All four links open Province 3's strip for new land; two leaves two still to build.

The split is negotiated (§12): you may accept, refuse (the crown may still build it alone if it pays the whole 80), or counter with a different share. You have 45 grain.

Reply `move: accept | refuse | counter <your share>`, plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
