## You won Rich's Customs House for 16, Silke's Dragon Jar for 14 and Paddy's Wooden Bowl for 10

**1. Place each now** (Note 10), each on its own square of your province. Free squares: O14–O17, P13–P14, P17–P18, Q13–Q18, R13–R16, S13–S16. A piece can't go under a work.

**2. Your set.** With the Land Deed and the Brush already on P15 and P16, these three make your set: Customs House, Land Deed Scroll, Bamboo Brush, Dragon Jar, Wooden Bowl. After the Bowl you have 19 grain, one short of the 20 the set needs; your Vase is the last lot, and its quarter (2.5 of Paddy's 10) brings you to 21.5. So the set is complete once the last lot is sold, and the Storyteller asks you now what you'll do then:
- **hold:** stay the Wheat Peasant and keep the pieces;
- **replace <name>:** a peasant may replace the Official (Rich, 39 grain), the Gentry (Reina, 37.5), the Scholar (Noah, 42) or another peasant (Silke 45.5, Paddy 25), never the Emperor. The mover keeps their own grain plus all of the displaced player's grain, and returns any pieces native to the new seat to its stock (the Official's is the Customs House, the Gentry's the Land Deed, the Scholar's the Brush); the displaced player returns everything and draws 60 from the Harvest Pile, and plays the Wheat Peasant from then on.

Reply `move: 2:CH <square>; 5:DJ <square>; 6:WB <square>; then hold | replace <name>`, plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
