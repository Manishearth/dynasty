move: collect as declared
say: "Nine from the coast, nine from the rice, nine from the wheat. Twenty-seven, counted twice and not a grain shaved off, Your Majesty. Silke, Paddy, Graham: I know what a lean year costs, and I'm not taking a half-grain more than the decree says. Write that in the good book too, Clark, the page with my name on it."
aside: "Five a year, and I'm sitting on thirty-five grain with nothing in my hand, so the only thing I've got to sell is being honest, and I'm making sure the man with two hundred and seventy grain sees it. The peasants have the money this year, and the day the Wall goes up over my province I'll want them remembering who was gentle with them in the bad harvest."
