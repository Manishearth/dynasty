- **pass** on a lot, or a **ceiling**: the most you'll pay for it.

**Auctions drain the table.** Three quarters of every price goes to the Harvest Pile and only a quarter to the seller, so a bidding war makes everyone poorer: grain that would have paid tax and built the works.
