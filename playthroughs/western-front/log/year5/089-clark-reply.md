move: land F23, F24
say: "Twenty-seven, counted twice. Very well, Rich, I'll write it down. In pencil, mind, on the good page, and it stays there if next year's count is as clean. The crown takes F23 and F24, forty grain out of the reserve, and from next harvest they pay for themselves. Silke, Paddy, Graham, the offer stands: you lay five a square and the crown lays fifteen."
aside: "Two squares for me is forty grain I'll have back in two harvests, and every peasant square I pay for is a province that owes the crown a bigger tax and a favour. Rich has started asking for the good book, and a man who asks for it can be kept asking."
