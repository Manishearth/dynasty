move: rent 5 from each peasant
say: Bad harvest, so you get the lean rate: five each, Silke, Paddy, Graham, and not a grain more this year. I keep a long book, and I remember who stood by me when the rains came late. And Clark, my province sits under four Wall links with not one stone laid. I'll keep asking.
aside: I'm at thirty grain and losing five a year with no land, so I need every coin, but squeezing the peasants in a famine year only feeds a revolt that nobody here can afford. Fifteen keeps me afloat, the peasants owe me a little goodwill, and the next time I ask for more I'll be asking people I've treated well.
