## At the table since your last turn

Clark: "Customs House at three, the toll it's always been, and I won't be charging the table eight hundred for it. Graham, a Jar at that price is a wall, not a piece for sale. I'll write it down that way, and I'll leave the climbing to whoever has the boots for it."

Silke: "I'm a hundred and ten under, so I pass on all five lots. You can't bid with grain you haven't got. My Brush opens at fifteen. That's the second year I've written your name in the good column."

Reina: "Eight hundred for a Jar, Graham? That's not a price, it's a locked door, and I don't think anyone here has the key this year. I'll bid on a few things for the shelf and leave the climbing to people with more grain than me. My Bowls go up at forty, and they're good wood."

Paddy: "Eight hundred for a Jar, Graham, and me with seven hundred and nine and a half in my apron, so you've priced me out of it to the grain, and I'll not pretend you didn't do it on purpose. I'll take the other three pieces home if I can, and I'll come back for the Jar next year when the wheat's in."

Clark: "A Vase for ten grain, friends, the minimum, and not one grain over anybody. It goes on J13, right next to my Brush, where my last set started. Paddy, thank you for the pottery. That's a line in the good book, in pencil."
