move: rent 100 from each peasant
say: "A hundred each from Noah, Reina and Paddy. The Pile is four thousand eight hundred and fifty-six and a half grain down, and I paid every one of you in full tonight. Noah, you asked me fifty last year with the hole half this size. I'll write down whatever each of you pays, the same as I always have."
aside: "Rent goes into the Pile, not my pocket, but if the Pagoda goes up tonight, grain in hand decides the ties, and Paddy and Noah are both within three hundred of me. Every grain they choose to pay keeps the hole smaller and their aprons lighter, and anyone who pays only the five will see it in my book."
whisper: none
