## At the table since your last turn

(Storyteller's note: Rent is paid to the Gentry, as the Gentry's own grain; it doesn't go into the Harvest Pile.)

Rent: Graham asks 100 of each peasant; the minimum 5 each is paid to him now (rent is the Gentry's own grain, not the Pile's), and Noah, Reina and Paddy answer the rest of the ask when the land is offered, if the year gets that far.

Rich: "Friends, a gentle twenty percent, because Heaven flooded half of you this year and I won't add to it. And the crown lays the last stone: the Marble Pagoda, all twenty from my own reserve, just as I told Clark the mortar was ready. Clark, your twenty, and Silke, a hundred and fifteen, both declared where everyone can hear them. It's been an honour to be robbed by every one of you, and terribly sorry, Graham, but your Jar will have to wait."

Rich declares the year: tax at 20%; the one project is the Marble Pagoda, at the crown's whole cost of 20, the last public work owed; salaries of 20 to Clark and 115 to Silke, paid at the end of the year; and the crown pays 10 of every 20 on new land. The game ends the moment the Pagoda is built, in the works step, before the auction.

Clark: "The last tax bag in China, friends: five eighty-two, and I pay my own forty-four first, the same as every year. Noah, two seventy, Paddy, one sixty, Reina, a hundred, Graham, eight, Silke, nothing. Rich, it's all there to the grain, and you can light the Pagoda whenever you're ready. Thirteen years I've carried this bag, and not one grain of it has gone missing."

Rich: "G20, friends, right in the middle of my own north-east, under the Wall I built, where everyone who comes to the capital will have to walk past it. Thank you all, every one of you: you robbed me by one grain four times, and I'd sit at this table again tomorrow. Graham, Reina, I'm terribly sorry about the Jar, truly."

Clark collects the tax at 20%, 582 (Clark 44, Graham 8, Noah 270, Reina 100, Paddy 160): the reserve is 2,005. Rich builds the Marble Pagoda on G20, at the crown's whole cost of 20 (G20 lies under the Wall above Province 1, which Graham built as Emperor in Year 7). It is the last public work owed, so the game ends here, in Year 13's works step, in survival: every work in the book stands. Nothing else happens this year: no land, no auction, and no salaries, so the 20 and 115 Rich declared are never paid.

The game is over, and the empire survives.
