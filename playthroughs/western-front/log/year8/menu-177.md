## Salaries, at the end of the year

You declared 10 for the Official (Clark) and 5 for the Scholar (Paddy). The rules owe at least 5 each, every year. The crown's reserve is 12.5.

- Paying the minimum 10 leaves the crown 2.5; paying the declared 15 leaves it 2.5 in **debt**. The crown holds no realia to sell, so any shortfall is a debt: a player in debt can't fund works, buy land or give grain, every bag they receive pays the debt first, and a player in debt is a cause for revolt (Section 16).
- Grain may be given or lent at any time (no deal is binding), so you may also ask anyone at the table for grain before you pay.

Reply `move: salaries Official <n>, Scholar <n>` (and any request of the table), plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
