diary: Six years on the coast, and in the seventh I finished the set with one grain to spare on each of the last two pieces, twenty-nine for the Bowl and thirty-two for the Vase, both times one over the man behind me. Then I took Reina's chair, the one I told the camera in the third year I could take. It cost me twenty fields that pay four hundred in a good year, and I handed them straight to the woman I took the chair from. I knew that would happen. I didn't expect her to learn so quickly what to do with them. She set the Jar at three hundred, which is my own lesson from six years behind the counter, and then she spent sixty-six grain just to keep Noah from his Bowl. So now there are two of us one Jar short, Rich and me, and every Jar in China sits with Reina. What she can't do is keep it high: the Jar goes up first next year at two, and I have two hundred and fifty-seven grain against Rich's ninety-five and a half. Graham's treasury is down to half a grain and he's borrowing salaries from the table. The rule says a Gentry with a full set can take the throne. Next year I buy the Jar, and then I take the throne.
