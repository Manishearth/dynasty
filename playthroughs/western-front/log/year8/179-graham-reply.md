diary: Years 7 and 8. I built eight links of Wall, both strips, mine and Clark's, and I paid for every stone with crown grain. I put six hundred and ten into other men's fields and a hundred and twenty into my own, and twice I ended the year with half a grain, borrowing from the men I was paying. Clark lent me twelve and took fourteen back. Noah lent me three and wouldn't take the fourth, and that's the second time I've written Noah down for something, and this time it's to his credit. Silke climbed into Reina's chair, and Reina asked three hundred for a Jar so nobody could climb into hers, and now that same Jar goes up first next year at two grain, with Silke and Rich each one Jar short and Silke sitting on two hundred and fifty-seven. Every field in China is wider than it was, and every one of them pays my forty percent at times three, so the crown will be rich again by spring. I think it's a good trade, but it only counts if I'm still in the chair when the harvest comes in. There are three hundred and seventy of works left. I'll watch that Jar lot closer than I've watched anything since my own Vase, and I'll remember who still owes this crown a kindness and who doesn't.
