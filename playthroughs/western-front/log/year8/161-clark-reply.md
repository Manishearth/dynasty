move: collect as declared (376 in all: Silke 40, Reina 152, Rich 72, Noah 112; Clark and Paddy owe nothing), take the 14 owed on the loan off the top as Graham announced, and deliver 362 to the crown

say: "Three hundred and seventy-six, every grain counted. Fourteen comes off the top, as His Majesty said in front of everybody, and three hundred and sixty-two goes to the crown. Reina, a hundred and fifty-two. The coast is generous, isn't it? I hear it was very generous to the last woman who had it. Graham, four links over my head, all paid by the crown. You said it out loud, so it's in ink now."

aside: "That's the first time anyone at this table has paid me back with interest, so Graham's name moves out of pencil and into the good book, provisionally. Once those four links stand my strip opens, and at times three I'll need every square I can plant just to stop starving."

whisper: none
