## The crown asks you for a loan

Graham has asked you to lend the crown 3 grain now, before salaries, to be repaid with 1 on top (4) off the top of next year's tax. With it he pays his declared 15 in salaries (Clark 10, Paddy 5); without it he pays the minimum 10 and owes the other 5 out of next year's tax. No deal is binding.

You may lend the 3 (or on your own terms), or decline.

Reply `move: lend <n> | decline`, plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
