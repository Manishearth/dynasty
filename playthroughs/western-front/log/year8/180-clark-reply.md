diary: Years Seven and Eight, and the good book finally has an entry in ink. In Year Seven I started with an empty shelf. I lost every lot I bid on, watched Silke buy the Bowl and the Vase and take Reina's chair, and then lent the crown twelve grain so it could pay me ten of it. Graham paid back fourteen off the top, as promised, and built all four links over my head, so his name moves out of pencil. In Year Eight I bought my five squares at half, which takes me from minus forty-five a year to plus fifty-five, and I took the last Bamboo Brush in China for thirteen, one grain over Reina. That's the first single grain at this table that's ever gone my way. It cost me nearly everything, and I end the year with twenty-eight grain and one piece on J12, where my last set started. Reina gets a new entry in the other book, but it's an admiring one: three hundred on a Jar and a hundred and one on a Bowl just to keep two people out of chairs. Silke and Rich both need only a Jar, the Jar reopens at two first thing next year, and whichever of them gets it will start a scramble. Next year I keep collecting honestly, I let the fields fill my granary, I buy the cheap pieces nobody's watching, and I sit on the only Brush in the country until somebody has to come and ask me for it.
