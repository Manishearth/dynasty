Clark: "Three for the Customs House, as always. It's a toll. I've got twenty-seven grain and five new squares, so I'm a spectator with a notebook this year. Noah, Silke, you each need one piece, and you can fight each other for the pile's benefit. I'll be writing down who paid what."
