diary: Years 5 and 6. I'll write it down slowly, because I hardly believe it myself. The seed rotted the first year, but the crown paid three parts of every field, so I put two more squares by the shore and took the Deed and the Vase for ten apiece, with nobody fighting me for the Vase at all. Then the granaries filled, I paid Reina her ten because she asked it kindly, and I paid twenty-six for a Jar that Rich only let go at twenty-five. In one evening I went from rice farmer to Official to Emperor of China. I took Rich's chair because he'd stood against me to my face, and Clark's because a Bowl I'd sold for three years came round and finished the set. I sat on the throne with two hundred and five grain for about as long as it takes to pour a cup of tea, and then Graham bought his Vase and took it off me. I'd told him I'd understand, and I do. So the teapot came round the table to me after all, and I've ended up the Scholar with sixty-five grain, the five-grain rate and nothing on the shelf. I've lost my fields by the shore, and that's the part that stings a bit. Still, I'm better off than the empty cart I started with, and I know the way up now. Next I'll keep the book honest, read the cards plain and save towards a set again, and I'll not make anybody a promise I can't keep, Graham least of all.
