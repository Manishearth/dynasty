- **pass** on a lot, or a **ceiling**: the most you'll pay for it.
- **keep from:** optional, `<name> up to <n>`: a higher limit you'd pay only to stop that player getting it.
- Your limits are settled lot by lot, in order. If you've already won an earlier lot, a later limit above the grain you have left is cut to it.
- Most lots draw one or two serious bidders; passing is normal.

**Auctions drain the table.** Three quarters of every price goes to the Harvest Pile and only a quarter to the seller, so a bidding war makes everyone poorer: grain that would have paid tax and built the works. Settling a contested piece by a public deal before the auction (one of you stands aside for a favour, or for first claim on the next piece) is legitimate (rulebook Section 8).

**Whispers** exist for a genuinely private deal, one that can't be made in public. They're still rare: most turns, write "none".
