**Where the Province 7 Reservoir goes.** It's a 2 x 2 cut-out, and it must lie wholly inside Province 7 (rows O to S, columns 13 to 18: O14–O17 and P13–S18), on squares with no realia. Realia can never be placed under a work, so every square it covers is one fewer for Graham's pieces.

Reply with the four squares as a corner pair, e.g. `move: reservoir R17:S18`, plus your say, aside and whisper as usual.
