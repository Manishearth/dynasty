## Paddy's diary so far, in his own words

## Years 1–2

Two years and my cart's come home empty both times. I bid honest and small the first year and bid firmer the second, and each time somebody with a fuller purse had already decided the piece was theirs. The only thing I've got out of it is my Bowls selling dear, twelve and then fifteen, though only a quarter of that comes back to me and the rest goes into Rich's pile. Then Noah, who kept saying "bid small, bid plain," walked off with the throne, and I'll give him this: he said sorry to Reina's face before he did it. I'm sixty-one grain richer than I'd feared and I've not wasted any of it, which is something, but there's one fewer of every piece now and the gentlemen have all the rest. Next year I'll pick two pieces and go after them properly instead of trying for all five, and I'll keep twenty back for a field when the land opens, because I'd sooner own a field than pay rent on one.
