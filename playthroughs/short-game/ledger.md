## Storyteller's rulings

- **Which piece goes up:** each seller names one piece from their stock and its minimum before the auctions, in a short `free` prompt of its own (or folded into a launch). Putting up the second type instead of one's own isn't a hold-back: a lot was still offered (Reina, Year 6: the Vase up, the Brush kept).
- **The unsold-lot rule, so far:** Years 1 to 5, every lot sold. **Year 6, the first unsold lots:** Noah's Customs House (min 150) and Rich's Wooden Bowl (min 150) drew no bid. **Both must go up in Year 7 at 2** (`auctions --floor 2:CH,3:WB`); neither may be held back again. Year 5: Land Deed (95) 95, Brush (40) 40. Year 6: Vase (10) 21. **Year 7:** Customs House (floor, 2) 2 to Rich; Wooden Bowl (floor, 2) **no bid, unsold for a second year: it must go up again in Year 8 at 2** (a seller can't hold it back; it was offered and nobody bid); Brush (25) 32 to Noah. **Year 8:** Wooden Bowl (floor, 2) no bid again; it ends the game in Province 3's stock. Earlier years' prices are in the log.
- **The minimum stands as first named:** the batched auction prompt asks again for the minimum; the one named in the piece prompt stands.
- **Tie on a lot:** settled by seat order after one live raise each (Year 1 Vase).
- **A site nobody names:** the Storyteller places the work or the land (Year 1: Summer Palace on G26; Year 5: Province 3 Reservoir on K16:L18; Year 6: Marble Pagoda on H22, Province 1's four sea squares on F23, F24, G27, H25).
- **A negotiated work's split** is asked of the province in its next prompt (folded into Rich's piece prompt, Year 5, 057).
- **New land:** from Year 6 only Provinces 1 and 4 can buy (the sea); 2 and 3 need all four Wall links above them first, and none stands. The Emperor's declared share per square (Clark, Year 6: 15 of 20 for Reina) stands for the year's purchases without asking again. Reina gets a `land` prompt each year, and Clark's `crown` carries it.
- **A private trade of a piece on the board** (Year 8): the offer goes to the other party as a `free` prompt (accept, decline or counter); the grain moves as `act(..., deltas={pid(seat): n}, deal=True)` and the piece by `give()`. Rich paid Noah 60 for the Brush (100).
- **A completed set is offered as a choice:** a short `free` prompt asks whether they change seats now (and with whom) or not now, stating what each change returns (039, 064).
- **Seat change mid-year:** the year's tax and works stand as declared; the Emperor pays the year's salaries to whoever holds each seat after the change, in a short `free` prompt (040, 065).
- **Seat changes return pieces to stock:** a new Emperor's pieces all go back to their home provinces' stock. A player moving to Province 2–4 returns only the new seat's native piece; the rest move with him, and the Storyteller places them if he names no squares (Year 5: Noah's Jar, Bowl and Deed on J14–J16). The displaced player returns everything.
- **The one-year limit** applies only to a lot that drew no bid (the Director, Year 4).
- **Whispers on the page:** a whisper goes into the speaker's hover after the aside, as "Whispered to <name>: ..." (042, 044, 055, 067, 069, 076).
- **The Emperor doesn't bid** (§10): Clark's whisper offering to bid for the Deed (055) got a note to Rich and to Clark.
- **Misstatements:** a public line that misstates the board or rules gets a `note` to the table and a plain `act()` (Reina's "next Emperor's Share", 014; Noah's grain, 022; Clark's land for Provinces 2 and 3, 067; Reina's "nobody needs the Vase", 072). An aside's slip goes in the ruling and that player's next prompt (Reina's peasants, 001; Clark on the second Vase's minimum, 009; Rich's raiders, 012; Reina blocking a lot, 020; Noah's "paid on the way in", 033; Clark's "financing", 034; Clark's "nobody else can finish", 039; Reina's "I put him on that chair", 043; Rich's "nobody needs the Bowl", 044; Reina's "the Brush would finish Rich", 059; Rich's "Reina's Brush can't finish anything", 060; Rich's "a Bowl she can't get anywhere else", 071; Reina's "keeping the Brush" and "hurts the people with the most land", 081, noted to her; Rich's "claim with the set next year", 088, in the ruling; Noah's "36.5", 091, noted to the table; Noah's "anyone who wants my chair has to finish a set in them [the auctions]", 094, noted to the table (pieces on the board may be traded) with a plain act(); Rich's "my set dies one piece short", 095, in the ruling; each noted to that player).
