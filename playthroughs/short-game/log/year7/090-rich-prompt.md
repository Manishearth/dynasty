## At the table since your last turn

Noah: "For four years I've said my limit out loud and lost by one grain, four times. So this year I'll give you every number except that one. One more year after this, Rich has 101.5, Reina 61, and I'm at 68.5. If anyone closes a set this year, they have a year left to come for a chair, and I know whose chair is easiest to take. Reina, I'll be bidding on your Brush."

Reina: "I'm out of the shopping this year, gentlemen. One year left and no set in reach, so I'd rather keep my grain than give three quarters of it to the Pile. The Brush stays at twenty-five, and whoever pays it gets my blessing and my close attention."

The auctions settle. The Customs House goes to Rich at 2, the only bid. Nobody bids on the Wooden Bowl, so it stays in Rich's stock for a second year and must go up again next year at 2. The Bamboo Brush goes to Noah at 32: Rich stopped at 31. Reina keeps 8 of it, the Harvest Pile 24.
