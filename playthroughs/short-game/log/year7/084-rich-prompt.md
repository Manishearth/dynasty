## Years 1–2

Two years in and I hold half a set: Clark's Jar for eleven and Noah's Vase for ten, both sitting safe at L12 and L13. ## Years 3–4

Two years, and the board has turned upside down. I stopped at forty on the Customs House, Noah took it for forty-one, and that was the end of my set, because there was only ever one. Clark then bought my Scroll and Noah's Brush, finished his set before any of us had checked our sums, and took the throne from Reina. I promised Clark the Deed wouldn't go up this year, and it didn't. I said nothing about next year, and next year it has to go up. Noah needs that Deed and nothing else, and once he has it he can come for my seat as easily as Clark's throne. Clark will pay well to keep it from him, and Reina wants it too. So the Deed goes up next year with a minimum high enough to make Noah sweat and the Emperor generous. Before it does, I'll have the Reservoir in Province 3 promised in public, and I'll smile at everybody while it's being built.

## Years 5–6

Two years, and I've gone from the man with the Scroll to the man with two Vases and a Bowl nobody can afford. Noah paid my ninety-five without a murmur, as he'd said he would, and then he used it on Reina instead of on me or the crown. That was the cleanest knife-work I've seen at this table, and I had a hand in sharpening it. The Reservoir cost me twenty-five and paid me twenty in its first harvest, which is the best bargain I've made all game. The second Vase cost twenty-one, bought only to keep Noah a piece further from the throne, and I've made sure Clark knows whose grain that was. Province 3 is safe, a hundred and sixteen grain in the barn, and I hold the seat I drew. Next year the Customs House and my Bowl both come up at two, and the Brush is in Reina's box. A cheap House and a Brush would give me a set and the throne in Year 8, so I'll go on smiling at Noah while I count what Reina's Brush will cost me.
