Ruling: legal. Lot 4 is the Vase at a minimum of 10; the Brush stays in stock this year, his choice (§10). The say misstates the rule: the one-year limit applies only to a lot that drew no bid. Putting up the other piece isn't holding one back. If the Bowl sells this year, the Deed is Rich's only piece and must go up in Year 5; if the Bowl goes unsold, the Bowl must reopen in Year 5 at 2 and the Deed waits until Year 6. The same holds for the Vase and the Brush. A note to the table and a plain act().
