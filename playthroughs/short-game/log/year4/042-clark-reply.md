move: tax 50%; build Winter Palace (split: crown pays all 20); salaries Official 7, Scholar 5
say: A bountiful year all round, so the crown asks for half. Fat harvests pay fat taxes, I'm sure you'll agree. The Winter Palace goes up out of the crown's purse, so I'll have somewhere warm to keep the ledgers. Reina, seven again for collecting it. Noah, five, and you can count it yourself.
aside: Noah's only one Land Deed from my throne, and every thirty grain I tax out of him is thirty he can't bid at Rich's auction. And the Winter Palace is the key that unlocks his canal, so he'll be wanting to talk nicely to me soon.
whisper: To Rich: Rich, the Deed going to Noah is the one thing that unseats me. Set your minimum on it high this year, or let it sit in stock, and I'll make the Reservoir in Province 3 the crown's next project with the crown paying the larger share, and you'll have a friend on the throne for four years.

Ruling: legal. 50% on 60 is 30 each from Reina, Rich and Noah: 90 to the crown. The Winter Palace, 20 from the crown (the Storyteller places it). Salaries Reina 7, Noah 5 at the end of the year. The whisper is a deal, not binding (§5); Rich chooses which of his two pieces goes up, so keeping the Deed in stock this year is open to him.
