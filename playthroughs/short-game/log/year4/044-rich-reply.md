Ruling: legal. Lot 3 is the Wooden Bowl at a minimum of 30; the Land Deed stays in stock this year, which is his choice (§10). The aside misstates the table: Reina, now the Official, needs the Wooden Bowl (her set is the Bowl, Vase, Land Deed and Brush). His timing is right only if the Bowl goes unsold: then it must reopen in Year 5 at 2, and the Deed, his last piece, goes up in Year 6. If the Bowl sells this year, the Deed goes up in Year 5.
