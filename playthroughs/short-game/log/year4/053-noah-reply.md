diary: Years three and four. I said my arithmetic out loud, and Clark used every number of it. I told the table I had 88 to spend and split it 46 and 42, and he took the Deed at 43, one grain over my limit, for the third time in this game. Then he took my Brush at the minimum and the throne with it. Province 1 from a draw of 2 is a win if he keeps it, and he has 221 grain to keep it with. I did get the Customs House, so I hold three of the four pieces now, with the most grain of any of us who aren't on the throne. I also got a rule wrong in public, the hold-back rule this time. The Storyteller was right to correct me, and I wrote it down. Next year Rich's Deed has to come up, and so does my Brush. Rich has told me, politely, that a Brush means nothing to him without the Customs House I'm sitting on, so he doesn't need my piece, and I need his. Reina needs the Deed and the Brush as well, and she has 62 grain against my 115.5. My plan is to make one fair offer in public. If Rich asks for more than the Deed is worth, I'll pay the price anyway and say exactly what it cost me. A late seat on the throne is still a seat, and four years is enough time to take it from Clark.
