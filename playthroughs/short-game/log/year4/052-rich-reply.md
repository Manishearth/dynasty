diary: Two years, and the board has turned upside down. I stopped at forty on the Customs House, Noah took it for forty-one, and that was the end of my set, because there was only ever one. Clark then bought my Scroll and Noah's Brush, finished his set before any of us had checked our sums, and took the throne from Reina. That sent my Deed and Bowl home again, and I sold the Deed twice for the price of once, which is the kind of accident I can live with. Reina paid thirty for the Bowl, so I'm sitting on 103 grain, more than I've had all game, and I don't owe anyone a piece I can't deliver. I promised Clark the Deed wouldn't go up this year, and it didn't. I said nothing about next year, and next year it has to go up. Noah needs that Deed and nothing else, and once he has it he can come for my seat as easily as Clark's throne. Clark will pay well to keep it from him, and Reina wants it too. So the Deed goes up next year with a minimum high enough to make Noah sweat and the Emperor generous. Before it does, I'll have the Reservoir in Province 3 promised in public, and I'll smile at everybody while it's being built.
