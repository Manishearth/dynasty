move: Land Deed Scroll, minimum 40
say: "My dear Clark, how right you are that the last of anything deserves a proper price. In that spirit, my humble Land Deed goes up at forty, the very same as your fine Customs House, so nobody can say Rich asked a penny more than the Official. Noah, Clark, you both need it and there is only the one. I'm sure two such gentlemen will be generous with each other."
aside: "That's my only piece, so it goes up whether I like it or not. Forty matches Clark's own price, so he can't call it greed. Whichever of them wins the Scroll buys me the grain to chase their Customs House or their Brush, and I'll remember which one of them was nice to me about it."
whisper: none
