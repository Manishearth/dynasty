Noah, you count beautifully, but this year the man who carried the tax gets the extra."
aside: "With that canal finished, my own fields earn twenty more a year until the end, and all of it comes from building, none from taxing them. I gave the extra two to Clark because Noah is sitting richest and closest to my chair this year, and a gentle tax keeps poor Rich too busy surviving to think about revolt."
