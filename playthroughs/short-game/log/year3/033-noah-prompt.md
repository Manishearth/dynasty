## Your move: which piece you put up this year

Before the auctions, each seller names the one piece from their stock they put up this year, and its minimum bid (at least 2). You must auction one piece every year until your stock is gone. Your stock now holds a single piece (on the board above), so that is the piece that goes up this year; the minimum is yours to set. If nobody meets your minimum, the piece stays in your stock and must go up again next year at a minimum of 2: you may hold a piece back for one year, never two in a row. The bidding itself comes in your next prompt, once all three lots are known.

Reply with `move: <piece>, minimum <n>`.

**Whispers** exist for a genuinely private deal, one that can't be made in public. They're still rare: most turns, write "none".
