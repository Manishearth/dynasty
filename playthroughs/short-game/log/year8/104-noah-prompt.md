## At the table since your last turn

A trade: Rich pays Noah 60 for the Bamboo Brush, which moves from J13 to Rich's K15. Rich now holds a complete set (the Dragon Jar, a Vase, the Customs House and the Brush) with 34.5 grain, and may change seats.

Rich: "Your Majesty, I have adored you from the first harvest to the last, and I adore you still. But a Jar, a Vase, a House and a Brush make a set, and a set makes an Emperor. I'll take the throne, and nothing but the throne, exactly as I told Noah. Clark, the Gentry's seat is a warm one. The Pile is yours to pay out, with my compliments."

Rich declares his set and replaces Clark as Emperor. All Rich's pieces go back to stock; he takes the crown's 110, for 144.5. Clark becomes the Gentry of Province 3, with nothing, and draws 60.

Rich: "My first act as Emperor, and my last: five to the Official and five to the Scholar, as the law sets it down. Noah, you'll find it's the same figure you've had in the book all game, so nothing surprises you. Reina, Clark's seven was Clark's promise. I'm only bound by my own, and I've kept every one of those to the letter."

Salaries: Rich, now the Emperor, pays Noah 5 and Reina 5.

The table stops here, after Year 8's salaries, as agreed, and scores from where everyone sits against the seat they drew. Clark, who drew the Official (2), is the Gentry (3), with 60. Reina, who drew the throne (1), is the Scholar (4), with 165.
