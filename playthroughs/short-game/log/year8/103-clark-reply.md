diary: Years Seven and Eight, and the book is closed. I built Rich two links of Wall at his own price, thirty-five and five each time, so he'd carve my name over the gates. Then he bought a Brush off Noah for sixty, in the last hour of the last year, and carved his own name over my throne. My two informants did their duty. Reina warned me in public, and so did Noah, and I could only sit and watch, because the Emperor doesn't bid and he can't buy what's already on the board. I lost the chair, the hundred and ten I'd saved went to the man who took it, and I've drawn sixty as Gentry of Province 3, the province I spent four years and a hundred and fifty grain improving, Reservoir and Wall links together. I drew Province 2 and I end in Province 3, so it goes in my own book as a loss. And the one man I never wrote down by name turns out to be the one who should have had a page of his own. I did keep every promise I made, which is more than the new Emperor can say for the old Scholar's seven. If there's ever a next game, the first page goes to Richard Baron, and his kind words go in the margin as evidence.
