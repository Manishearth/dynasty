diary: Years seven and eight. I paid thirty-two for a Brush I couldn't use, only to keep it from Rich, and I won it by a single grain, the first time all game that grain went my way. One year later I sold it to him for sixty, which is either very clever or exactly what he'd been planning for me since the Vase. I don't know which, and I'm too honest to pretend I do. Here's the final arithmetic. I drew Province 4 and I'm finishing in Province 2 with 87 grain, so that's a win, and the biggest surplus I've had all game came in its last hour. Rich kept his word to the letter and took the throne, not my chair. Clark went from Emperor to Gentry with sixty grain, and part of that is on me, because I handed Rich the last piece and said so to Clark's face. Clark paid me five every year and wrote it in his book. Rich paid me five once and said it was the law, so there was never any kindness in the figure. I'll take that from a man who kept every promise he made me. Reina drew the throne and finished in my old Scholar's chair with more grain than anyone, and I took that chair from her in Year 5. If there's a next game, I'll still say the arithmetic out loud. I'll just stop saying my limit first.
