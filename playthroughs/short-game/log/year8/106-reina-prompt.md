## At the table since your last turn

Noah: "Last lot of the game, so the last count. Reina has 160, Clark 110, Rich 94.5, and I have 22. The Bowl is no use to me, since I already hold one, but it's the piece Reina still needs, and she has seven times my grain. Reina, last year you said no set was in reach. If that's still true, you won't mind me bidding."

Rich: "My poor little Bowl, up for a third time at two grain. Take it, somebody, for sentiment's sake. It's the last year, gentlemen, and I'd hate to go home with anything on my hands but gratitude."

Nobody bids on Rich's Wooden Bowl at 2, for the second year running. It stays in his stock.

Noah: "I'll say it out loud, because that's how I've played all game. Rich has offered me sixty for the Brush on J13, and I'm taking it. That puts me at 82 and Rich at 34.5 with a full set. He's given me his word he'll take the throne and not my chair, and words aren't binding, so here's the arithmetic either way: I drew Province 4, so Province 2 or Province 3 is still a win for me. Clark, I'm sorry. Reina warned you, and so am I."

A trade: Rich pays Noah 60 for the Bamboo Brush, which moves from J13 to Rich's K15. Rich now holds a complete set (the Dragon Jar, a Vase, the Customs House and the Brush) with 34.5 grain, and may change seats.

Rich: "Your Majesty, I have adored you from the first harvest to the last, and I adore you still. But a Jar, a Vase, a House and a Brush make a set, and a set makes an Emperor. I'll take the throne, and nothing but the throne, exactly as I told Noah. Clark, the Gentry's seat is a warm one. The Pile is yours to pay out, with my compliments."

Rich declares his set and replaces Clark as Emperor. All Rich's pieces go back to stock; he takes the crown's 110, for 144.5. Clark becomes the Gentry of Province 3, with nothing, and draws 60.

Rich: "My first act as Emperor, and my last: five to the Official and five to the Scholar, as the law sets it down. Noah, you'll find it's the same figure you've had in the book all game, so nothing surprises you. Reina, Clark's seven was Clark's promise. I'm only bound by my own, and I've kept every one of those to the letter."

Salaries: Rich, now the Emperor, pays Noah 5 and Reina 5.

The table stops here, after Year 8's salaries, as agreed, and scores from where everyone sits against the seat they drew. Rich, who drew the Gentry (3), sits on the throne (1), with 134.5 in the crown's reserve. Noah, who drew the Scholar (4), is the Official (2), with 87. Clark, who drew the Official (2), is the Gentry (3), with 60. Reina, who drew the throne (1), is the Scholar (4), with 165.
