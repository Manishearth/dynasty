move: replace Clark
say: "Your Majesty, I have adored you from the first harvest to the last, and I adore you still. But a Jar, a Vase, a House and a Brush make a set, and a set makes an Emperor. I'll take the throne, and nothing but the throne, exactly as I told Noah. Clark, the Gentry's seat is a warm one. The Pile is yours to pay out, with my compliments."
aside: "I said I'd take the throne and no one else's chair, and that's exactly what I've done. Province 3 to the crown is a win, and Clark's hundred and ten on top of my thirty-four makes it a comfortable one."
whisper: none

Ruling: legal (Section 11). Rich declares his set and 34.5 grain and replaces Clark as Emperor. All Rich's realia goes back to stock (Jar and Customs House to Province 2's, both Vases and the Brush to Province 4's); he takes the crown's 110, for 144.5. Clark becomes the Gentry of Province 3, holding nothing, and draws 60. The Wooden Bowl stays in Province 3's stock. The year's tax and work stand as declared; the new Emperor pays the salaries.
