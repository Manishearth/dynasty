  3:WB: pass (I can't bid on my own lot. Separately, I'm proposing a trade for the Brush on the board, laid out in my whisper to Noah, for the Storyteller to rule on.)
say: "My poor little Bowl, up for a third time at two grain. Take it, somebody, for sentiment's sake. It's the last year, gentlemen, and I'd hate to go home with anything on my hands but gratitude."
aside: "The Storyteller has just reminded us that pieces on the board can change hands, and Noah is sitting on twenty-two grain and a Brush that's no earthly use to him. Sixty grain buys it, leaves me the twenty I need to claim, and puts Rich Baron on the throne before Clark has finished his sums."
whisper: To Noah: Sixty grain for the Bamboo Brush off J13, paid now and in full, to you, not to the Pile. You go from twenty-two to eighty-two in the last year, which is the best surplus you'll see all game. And my word, to the letter: with the set I will replace the Emperor and nobody else, so you keep the Official's chair, and you keep it richer. If someone outbids me, come back to me first and I'll go to seventy. Say yes and the Storyteller can record it.
if I drop out: "Go on, it's yours. I've always said the Bowl wanted a better home."

Ruling: his own lot, no bid. The whispered offer is a legal private trade of a piece on the board (pieces on the board may be traded at any time; 60 grain from Rich to Noah, a voluntary payment). It goes to Noah as a free prompt. With the Brush, Rich would hold the Jar, Vase, Customs House and Brush with 34.5 grain: a complete set. "Up for a third time": it's the Bowl's third year at auction (Years 6, 7, 8), correct.
