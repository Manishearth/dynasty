diary: Years One and Two, closed and balanced. I collected eighty-five and a half for the crown and didn't skim a grain, and for that Her Majesty pays me five "for now" while the Scholar gets seven for reading a card aloud. Entered on page two, with interest. I paid twenty-three for the Bowl and the Vase, so I'm halfway to the set, and I have 58.5 grain, less than Noah and more than Rich. Very pleasing. Better still, they each need the Customs House and I own the only one. Next year it has to go up, and I mean to watch Rich and Noah decide how much they love each other, while I quietly go after the Land Deed and the Brush. Every gentleman at this table has been very polite. I've written all of it down.
