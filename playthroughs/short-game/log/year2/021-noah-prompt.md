**Auctions drain the table.** Three quarters of every price goes to the Harvest Pile and only a quarter to the seller, so a bidding war makes everyone poorer: grain that would have paid tax and built the works. **Whispers** exist for a genuinely private deal, one that can't be made in public. They're still rare: most turns, write "none".
