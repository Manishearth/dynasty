diary: Two years in, and the crown is down to 78.5 from 99. I spent it on a palace and a ditch, and both will outlast the men at that table. The Summer Palace opens the Wall and the Canal, and the first stretch of canal pays me ten a year until the end. Clark keeps his ledger of my "for now" like a loaded pistol, and I let him: a grudge he writes down is a grudge he isn't acting on. The trouble is that they've sorted the auctions out among themselves far too politely. Each of them sits at two pieces and needs exactly two of the three that go up next year: the Customs House, the Land Deed and the Brush. Next year is the knife fight, and I mean to stand well back from it, keep my tax gentle and my salaries a little uneven, and see who comes out of that auction looking closest to my chair.
