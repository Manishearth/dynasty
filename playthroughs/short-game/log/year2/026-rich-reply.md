diary: Two years in and I hold half a set: Clark's Jar for eleven and Noah's Vase for ten, both sitting safe at L12 and L13. I let Clark have the first Vase rather than throw grain into a pile I pay out of myself, and I passed on the second Jar so that Noah owes me a kindness. Every one of those smiles cost less than a single raise would have. I'm down to fifty grain after two rounds of tax and two pieces, which is lean but enough. Next year the last three pieces all go up at once. I need Clark's Customs House and Noah's Brush, and they each need my Scroll, the only one there is. So the Scroll gets sold for the throne's price, to whichever of them makes me the sweeter promise on the piece I need, and I'll keep my end of it exactly as worded.
