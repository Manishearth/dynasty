diary: Years Five and Six. I taxed at sixty and paid fifty-five toward Rich's Reservoir, which he accepted with both hands and a full heart. Then I promised to bid against Noah for the Deed and was told, in front of everyone, that the Emperor doesn't bid. That's page three, and it's mine. Noah paid ninety-five for the Deed and then didn't come for my chair at all. He took Reina's, and stripped her down to nothing, which handed me the most grateful Scholar in the history of the empire, for seven a year and a few roofs. The doubling cost me dearly: the Pagoda, four squares of sea, sixty for Reina's land, salaries every year, and the reserve dropped from 258 to 106. Fat years make large families, and apparently they're all mine. But Rich paid twenty-one for a Vase he can't use, just to keep Noah off my throne, and Reina has promised to warn me early. So I have two informants and one Official who's two pieces short. The Customs House and the Bowl come back up at two next year, and that's where the danger is. Two more years. Keep the chair, keep Rich hoping for his Wall link, and write all of it down.
