diary: Years five and six. I paid Rich ninety-five for the Deed without a single bid against me, which is what I said I'd do. I had four years of losing by one grain, and in the end the price was simply the price. With the set complete I took Reina's chair rather than Clark's, and I'd make the same choice again. The throne would have handed her a road straight back to me, and Province 2 beats the 4 I drew, so that's a win if I can keep it. It was not kind. She let me have the Deed and I took her seat a minute later, and I said it to her face because the arithmetic deserved saying, but I won't pretend it felt good. Then I lost Reina's Vase to Rich at twenty-one when my limit was twenty. That's the fourth time this game I've lost by one grain, and the second time Rich has thanked me for my figures. I'm beginning to think saying my limit out loud is a gift to everyone except me. Now the count for what comes next. I have 98.5 grain and lose about five an average year. My Customs House and Rich's Bowl both open at 2 in Year 7, Reina's Brush is still in her stock, and two years are left. I can't keep the Customs House out of anyone's hands a second time. So this coming year my job isn't to collect pieces. It's to watch who is closing in on my chair, and if someone's one piece away, bid against them for it, whatever it costs.
