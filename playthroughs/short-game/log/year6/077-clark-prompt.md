## At the table since your last turn

(Storyteller's note: Provinces 2 and 3 can't buy new land yet: their only new land is the strip below the Wall, which opens only when all four links above each of them stand, and none is built. This year only Province 1 and Province 4 can buy, both on the sea.)

Noah: "Forty percent, as declared. Rich pays 16 on his 40, Reina 8 on her 20, and I pay 8 on my 20. That's 32 to the crown, and I've counted it twice. For the record, this is Year 6 of 8, so three years are left including this one. Reina has started again from nothing, Rich is the closest of anyone to a set and still isn't close, and Province 2 loses about 5 grain in an average year, so I'll be watching my own ledger as closely as everyone else's."

Noah collects 8 from himself, 16 from Rich and 8 from Reina: 32 to the crown. Clark builds the Marble Pagoda on H22 for 20 from the crown; 590 of the works are still owed. Clark buys four squares of sea for Province 1 at F23, F24, G27 and H25, 80 from the crown; each adds 20 to the crown's harvest from next year.

Reina: "Two squares of sea, J25 and K25. That's forty, and on the crown's three quarters I pay ten and Clark pays thirty. Thank you, Your Majesty. That's the first roof anyone has helped pay for in this game, and I'll make sure the table hears about it."

**Reina whispers to you** (passed on by the Storyteller): "Agreed. If Noah or anyone else starts building a set aimed at you, you'll hear it from me before it reaches the auction table. In exchange, Province 4 is fed and the Scholar's salary stays at seven."

Reina buys two squares of sea for Province 4, at J25 and K25, paying 10 of the 40; the crown pays 30. Each adds 20 to her harvest from next year.

Noah: "The Customs House goes up at 150, and I'll tell you why, because I'm not going to pretend otherwise. Rich, you have the Jar and the Vase, and you once told me a Brush means nothing to you without the Customs House. That makes this the piece that puts you within reach of my chair. So I'm pricing it where nobody will pay, and the rules say it comes up at 2 next year. That's the one year's hold the rules allow me, and I'm using it in public."

Noah puts up the Customs House at a minimum of 150.

Rich: "Noah, you've taught us all how it's done, and I'm an eager pupil. The Bowl goes up at one hundred and fifty, the same figure as your Customs House, so nobody can say Province 3 priced a piece to suit itself. I kept my word to the letter all game, and I'll take the one year's hold the rules allow me, in public, the same as you."

Rich puts up the Wooden Bowl at a minimum of 150.

Reina: "I'm putting the Vase up at ten, which is an honest price for something nobody at this table needs, and I'm keeping the Brush in its box for now. I'll also take two more squares of sea, I25 and J26, on the same terms as before if the crown is willing. Ten from me, thirty from Clark, and the Scholar's province eats through whatever the next card brings."

(Storyteller's note: Noah, now the Official, needs the Vase: his set is the Wooden Bowl, the Vase, the Land Deed and the Bamboo Brush, and he holds the Bowl and the Deed.)

Reina puts up the Vase at a minimum of 10, keeping the Brush in stock. She buys two more squares of sea for Province 4, at I25 and J26, paying 10; the crown pays 30. The auctions: three lots. Noah's Customs House at 150, Rich's Wooden Bowl at 150, Reina's Vase at 10. Noah, Rich and Reina each give a limit on every lot at once; the Emperor doesn't bid.

Noah: "The Customs House stays at 150, as I announced. I'm passing on the Bowl, since I already have one. The Vase I'll take, and my ceiling is 20. That's double Reina's minimum and as much as I'll pay for a piece I can live without. Here's the count: if I win it at 10, I'm left with 83 and a half, and Province 2 loses about 5 an average year, so I'm fine to the end of Year 8."

Rich: "One hundred and fifty for the Customs House, Noah, and I have a hundred and thirty-seven and a half, so I can count too and I'll bow out with my compliments. The Bowl stays at one-fifty, as promised. As for Reina's lovely Vase, I'm a collector at heart, and a man can't have too many."

Reina: "Pass, and pass. A hundred and fifty twice over is more than my whole purse, and I'd rather keep my grain than buy pieces I can't use. The Vase stays at ten, as I said. Bid as you like, gentlemen. And note that the Brush is still in my stock, and both of you want it."

The auctions, settled in order: nobody meets Noah's 150 for the Customs House or Rich's 150 for the Wooden Bowl, so both stay in stock and must go up again next year at 2. Rich wins Reina's Vase at 21: Noah stopped at 20. Reina keeps a quarter, 5. Grain now: Reina 64, Clark 118, Rich 116.5, Noah 93.5.

Rich: "Twenty-one, and worth every grain. A matched pair of Vases beside the Jar, Your Majesty, so Province 3 has the finest mantelpiece in the empire. Noah, you stopped at twenty, just as you said you would. I do admire a man who keeps to his figures."

**Rich whispers to you** (passed on by the Storyteller): "Noah was one Vase and one Brush from your chair, and I paid twenty-one to make it two pieces again. I'll remember the Wall link over Province 3 on the terms you named."

Rich puts the Vase on L14. Salaries: Clark pays Noah, the Official, 5 and Reina, the Scholar, 7. Year 6 ends. Grain: Clark 106, Rich 116.5, Noah 98.5, Reina 71. Stock: Province 2 (Noah) the Customs House and Province 3 (Rich) the Wooden Bowl, both unsold and reopening at 2 in Year 7; Province 4 (Reina) the Bamboo Brush. Works still owed: 590.
