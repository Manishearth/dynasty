diary: Two years, and I've gone from the man with the Scroll to the man with two Vases and a Bowl nobody can afford. Noah paid my ninety-five without a murmur, as he'd said he would, and then he used it on Reina instead of on me or the crown. That was the cleanest knife-work I've seen at this table, and I had a hand in sharpening it. The Reservoir cost me twenty-five and paid me twenty in its first harvest, which is the best bargain I've made all game. The second Vase cost twenty-one, bought only to keep Noah a piece further from the throne, and I've made sure Clark knows whose grain that was. Province 3 is safe, a hundred and sixteen grain in the barn, and I hold the seat I drew. Next year the Customs House and my Bowl both come up at two, and the Brush is in Reina's box. A cheap House and a Brush would give me a set and the throne in Year 8, so I'll go on smiling at Noah while I count what Reina's Brush will cost me.
