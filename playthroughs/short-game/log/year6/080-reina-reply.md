diary: I told myself I would out-talk what I couldn't outbid, and then I had seventy-four grain against a ninety-five scroll and nothing to say. I bought the Brush to keep it from Rich, and congratulated Noah on his Deed as though he owed me something. He didn't, and he said so to my face before he took my chair, my pieces and my thirty-four grain. It was the cleanest robbery of the night, and he did his sums out loud while he did it. So I've been an Empress, then an Official, and now I'm a Scholar in Province 4 with nothing on the board. Oddly, it's the first seat where somebody else pays for my roof: Clark bought three quarters of four squares of sea, and he wants an informant against Noah, which is a fair price for being fed. The table has stopped racing for sets and started hoarding them behind hundred-and-fifty minimums. Now Noah wants the Vase Rich has just taken off me, and Noah and Rich both want the Brush I'm holding. The Brush is the last card I have, so it goes up when it does the most good, and to whoever has done the most for me. Two years are left. I won't get back to the throne by Year 8, so I'll play for a full granary and decide who sits on it.
